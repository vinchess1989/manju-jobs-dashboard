Are you passionate about football (soccer) and interested in building something of your own?

Stranger Soccer is expanding globally and is looking for a License Owner to launch and grow Stranger Soccer in Helsinki.

This is not your traditional management job— it is designed for entrepreneurial individuals who want to build, operate, and grow their own local football operation while creating a meaningful impact in their community.

Interested in learning more?

Visit www.strangersoccer.com and explore the Bring Stranger Soccer to Your City section to learn more about the opportunity. If it feels like the right fit, complete the interest form to start the conversation. We also encourage you to download the Stranger Soccer app and experience the platform firsthand.

Responsibilities


Launch and grow Stranger Soccer in Helsinki
Build and manage a local team, including staff and Official Hosts
Secure suitable football venues
Drive marketing and customer acquisition to grow the player community
Oversee game operations and deliver an outstanding player experience
Work closely with our Singapore HQ, supported by operations and technology teams


You should have


A passion for football and involvement in the local football community
Experience in management, operations, or customer service
An entrepreneurial mindset and desire to build a business
The ability to take ownership and grow the Stranger Soccer brand locally
Strong drive, resilience, and a results-oriented attitude


About Stranger Soccer

Football is the world's most popular sport, and Stranger Soccer exists to make playing football simple, accessible, and convenient.

Through the Stranger Soccer app, players can discover games, book a spot, and simply turn up to play—making football as easy as going to the gym.

Founded in Singapore, Stranger Soccer is expanding across cities worldwide, delivering thousands of games and building a growing international community. We are looking for passionate individuals who want to bring Stranger Soccer to their own city.

Our Mission

To make playing football as easy as going for a jog.

Our Vision

A world where playing football is as popular and accessible as watching football.

Ready to make an impact?

If you share our vision and want to bring Stranger Soccer to more communities around the world, we'd love to hear from you.
