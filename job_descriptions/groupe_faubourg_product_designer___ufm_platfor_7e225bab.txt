As The Second Designer In The UFM Programme, You Will Work Under The Guidance Of The Design Lead And Collaborate Closely With The Various Product Pods. You Will Contribute To The Governance Of The Design System, The User Experience Of Business Modules, And The Driver Application, Within a Model Where Product Builders Prototype Continuously And Where You Will Ensure a Consistent User Experience Across The Entire Platform


Extend and guard the UFM design system across all module user interfaces
Design module flows with the pod builders — fast, in-product, validated with early-adopter customers
Own key surfaces end-to-end (driver app, operator worklists) as the number of pods grows
Bring design review into the AI-speed delivery loop: patterns and guardrails rather than pixel-policing.


Profile


Strong product design portfolio; B2B / SaaS experience a plus. Systems thinker
Comfortable designing in code and prototypes, not only in Figma
Multi-persona empathy: drivers, fleet managers, suppliers, back-office operators
Fluent English; another European language is a plus.
