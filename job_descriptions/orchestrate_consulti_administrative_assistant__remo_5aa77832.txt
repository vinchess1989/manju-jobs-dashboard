Tallenna
Orchestrate Consulting's mission is to help job seekers access carefully coordinated opportunities that match their career goals. We are not a staffing firm or agency. Orchestrate Consulting does not hire for these roles—we curate and validate them from verified employers.

We are seeking a highly organized, detail-oriented, and proactive Remote Administrative Assistant to support our team with a variety of administrative and clerical tasks. This position is ideal for individuals who excel at multitasking, have excellent communication skills, and enjoy working in a fast-paced remote environment. You will play a key role in ensuring daily operations run efficiently while providing professional administrative support to management and team members.

Responsibilities


Provide administrative support to managers and team members.
Manage calendars, schedule meetings, and coordinate appointments.
Prepare, edit, and maintain reports, documents, spreadsheets, and presentations.
Respond to emails, phone calls, and other correspondence in a timely and professional manner.
Organize and maintain digital files, records, and confidential documents.
Perform accurate data entry and maintain company databases.
Coordinate virtual meetings and prepare meeting agendas and minutes.
Assist with travel arrangements, expense reports, and other administrative tasks as needed.
Monitor office supplies and coordinate vendor communications when required.
Support various departments with special projects and general administrative duties.
Ensure confidentiality of sensitive company and employee information.


Qualifications


High school diploma or equivalent required; Associate's or Bachelor's degree is a plus.
Previous administrative, office support, customer service, or clerical experience preferred but not required.
Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
Strong organizational and time management abilities.
High level of attention to detail and accuracy.
Ability to prioritize multiple tasks and meet deadlines.
Self-motivated with the ability to work independently in a remote setting.
Professional, dependable, and customer-focused.
