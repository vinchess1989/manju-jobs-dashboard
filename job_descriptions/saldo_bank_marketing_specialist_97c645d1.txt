About Us

Saldo Bank UAB is a specialised bank licensed by the European Central Bank and regulated by the Bank of Lithuania. Saldo Bank offers fully automated lending solutions for consumers and SME customers and term deposit account services for consumer customers. The company's advanced scoring system ensures responsible lending, and its own lending system supports business growth also internationally.




Role: Marketing Specialist

We are seeking an ambitious, proactive, and creative Marketing Specialist to join our international marketing team in our Helsinki office. In this role, you will play a hands-on part in executing our digital marketing initiatives, driving customer engagement, and supporting our growth across target markets (Finland, Sweden, Lithuania).

This is an exceptional opportunity for a rising marketing professional to develop their skills within a regulated neobank.




Key Responsibilities

Content & Copywriting: Draft compelling marketing copy and create engaging content for our website, blog, and customer communications. As well as localization of content.
CRM & Email Marketing: Planning, building, and executing email marketing campaigns and automated customer journeys within our CRM system.
Social Media Management: Maintain and grow our organic and paid social media presence, keeping our channels active, compliant, and engaging.
Website Updates: Manage day-to-day website content updates, ensuring information is accurate and up-to-date.
Partner Relations: Help maintain active relationships with our key external sales and marketing partners.
Collaboration & Compliance: Work closely with the local marketing team, product managers, and compliance to ensure all creative materials align with banking regulations and brand guidelines.
Responsibilities can be modified based on personal experience and ambitions.




Required Qualifications

Previous experience in digital marketing, content creation and/or UX/product copywriting.
A natural talent and interest in writing clear, engaging, and persuasive copy in Finnish. Fluency in Swedish is highly valued.
A Bachelor’s, Master’s degree or equivalent in Marketing, Communications, Business, or a related field.
Fluency in English and Finnish is required. Proficiency in Swedish is a strong asset.




Desired Qualifications

Experience in fintech, banking, insurance, or financial services.
Basic familiarity with email marketing platforms, or Content Management Systems (CMS).
Comfortable working with data and interested in learning how to measure and optimize campaign performance.
A driven, proactive, and curious personality with a strong desire to learn and take ownership of projects.
Experience in coordinating with different stakeholders.




What We Offer

The unique opportunity to grow based on your personal strengths and marketing interests.
Work alongside seasoned fintech marketing professionals in a fast-paced, innovative international digital bank.
A competitive salary and benefits package.
Edenred Employee Benefits: Including a lunch benefit for each working day and a recreational benefit of 400 EUR per year.
Private Health Insurance: From your very first day of working together.
Professional Development Budget: An annual budget of up to 1,500 EUR for training/conferences and 150 EUR for books to help you level up your skills.
Team Culture: A collaborative, supportive environment with regular team buildings and company events!
