Permitting Manager – Data Centre Development (Finland)

A leading data centre owner-developer is seeking an experienced Permitting Manager to take ownership of regulatory approvals across projects in Finland, with the flexibility to support wider European locations when required.




The Role

You will be responsible for driving all permitting activities from early-stage site selection through to construction readiness. This position plays a key role in ensuring projects progress on programme by securing all necessary approvals efficiently and proactively managing regulatory risk.




Key Responsibilities

Lead the full lifecycle of permitting, including preparation, submission, coordination, and approval tracking
Engage directly with local authorities, regulators, and external consultants to navigate planning and compliance requirements
Provide early-stage input to development and acquisition teams, identifying risks and advising on feasibility
Oversee zoning, environmental, demolition, building, and operational permits, as well as infrastructure crossing approvals
Maintain clear permitting schedules aligned with project timelines across multiple live developments
Act as the central point of contact for permitting matters across internal project stakeholders

Requirements

Fluency in both Finnish and English is essential
Approximately 10+ years’ experience within permitting, planning, or regulatory approvals
Strong background in data centre, infrastructure, or large-scale construction projects
Solid understanding of Finnish planning frameworks and environmental regulations
Degree in construction, engineering, land management, or a related discipline
