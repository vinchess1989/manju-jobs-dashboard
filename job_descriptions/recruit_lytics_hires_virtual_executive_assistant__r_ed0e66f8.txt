Räätälöi ansioluetteloni

We are looking for a stellar Account Executive to join our Amsterdam team. Interested? Read on to find out more information about the role and the fantastic benefits available to take advantage of here at Clarity!




You are a reliable point of contact for our clients and colleagues. You handle the day-to-day PR, communications and administrative work, so the clients you work for know everything is running smoothly. You're part of an energetic and creative team. Together, you come up with ideas for getting clients into the news, help with smart angles, maintain media relations, advise on social media approaches, and above all, find that extra something to surprise your clients.




Your responsibilities

Monitor and report on relevant media discussions and client publications
Create media selections for press lists, so media outlets receive relevant news
Translate or (re)write and send press releases and articles
Create social media content
Pitch relevant angles to the media for interviews and articles
Conduct research to make creative ideas even better
Help with project coordination, from content creation to media interviews
Maintain contact with your clients about project progress
Build and maintain relationships with journalists and editors, prepare media briefings, and build your own network with all relevant journalists until you're on first-name terms




Your skills

You're on top of the news and enjoy diving into the papers each morning
You're incredibly organised and keep an overview of everything you do
You're strong at networking and building relationships
You're curious and eager to learn
You speak and write flawless Dutch and English
You're a natural organiser and can work well on multiple projects at once
You know how important deadlines are and can manage them well
You work precisely, both in your coordinating tasks and when writing or proofreading a text
Ability to adopt AI tools and technologies to maximise efficiency and effectiveness across daily tasks




Nice to have:

You've learned the fundamentals of the trade through a course in marketing or communications
You find technology genuinely interesting and understand that almost every company today is a technology company
You're tech-savvy enough to work with tools like Slack and Google Workspace
You know how to use social and digital media effectively to engage with the right audience(s)

Clarity Academy: Continuous learning and development, built into your day-to-day.

Global Peer Groups: Connect and learn alongside colleagues across our offices worldwide.
