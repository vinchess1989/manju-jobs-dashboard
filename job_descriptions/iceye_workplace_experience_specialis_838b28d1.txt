Why this role matters:
 

As a Workplace Experience Specialist, you’ll own the everyday physical and social experience of our Espoo

workplace: decor and seasonal touches, kitchen and pantry supplies, convenience items upon request, and

shared amenities like our gym and terrace. You'll bring our workplace standards to life locally, sourcing,

purchasing and installing what's needed, and you'll gather employee feedback to shape what comes next.

 
Who We Are


ICEYE is the world leader in sovereign intelligence from space. We deliver persistent monitoring capabilities to detect and respond to changes in any location on Earth.

 

ICEYE owns the world's largest and most advanced SAR (synthetic aperture radar) satellite constellation. To our customers we provide intelligence with unmatched quality, latency and revisit times, in any weather, day or night. To governments who choose to operate their own constellation we provide this proven capability as a sovereign system.

 

ICEYE-built constellations serve customers in defence and intelligence, environmental monitoring, insurance and emergency management. We enable fast decisions that contribute to a safer future.

 

Founded and headquartered in Finland, ICEYE operates globally with over 1000 employees across Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.


Your day-to-day responsibilities
 

Implement decor and seasonal updates across our Espoo offices, in line with our workplace standards.

Source, purchase and coordinate the installation of decor, furniture and amenities directly with local suppliers.

Manage kitchen and pantry supplies, and keep convenience, gym and terrace equipment stocked and in good shape.

Run employee feedback activities and turn the results into concrete improvements.

Own our office perks program and other everyday moments that make the workplace better.

 
What we’re looking for:

1 to 3 years of experience in workplace experience, facilities coordination, hospitality, or office management.

Practical, hands-on and detail-oriented, comfortable with physical tasks such as installing decor and organizing office supplies storage and the print room, and with ordering and coordinating vendors for furniture purchase and assembly.

Experience managing vendors and purchases within a set budget.

Experience running or supporting employee feedback activities (surveys, pulse checks).

Fluent Finnish and English, needed for day-to-day vendor coordination
