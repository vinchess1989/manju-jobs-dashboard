Räätälöi ansioluetteloni

Superb opportunity to join Crystal Air at an exciting time in the company’s growth and development. The company has built a strong reputation as an innovative quality driven business, partnering with the world’s best product providers to offer service and maintenance, HVAC Installation and heat pumps. Our clients comprise a wide range of prestigious local and international brands within Ireland and Europe. You will join a motivated and inclusive team, with access to senior decision makers, study support, wellbeing resources, opportunity to work internationally and avenues to grow your career in line with the company’s ambitious growth plans.




Crystal Air is an equal opportunity employer




The Role

Responsible for co-ordinating all project documentation; records must be maintained, kept up-to-date and archived correctly.
Ensure that all safety documents are well checked and submitted on time prior to the document’s submission due date.
Secretarial services as required, taking meeting minutes, filing & emailing.
Booking Travel such as Flights, Hotels, Car hire
Completing timesheets for Subcontractors
Booking and maintaining accommodation across Finland, Sweden & Norway
Arranging ID06 appointments in Sweden
Arranging Tax office appointments in Finland
Booking Inductions for Sweden
Arranging pre-employments medical appointments




Skills & Experience

Highly organised individual with the ability to work autonomously.
IT literate - particularly with MS Office suite.
Strong administration skills.
Possess a positive “Can do” Attitude.
Excellent communication skills, both written and verbal English essential.
Highly organised with strong attention to detail essential.
Ability to multi-task and work well under strict deadlines essential.
Ability to work on own initiative.
