Työn kuvaus

Would you like to explore the creative industry — or see what it is actually like to work alongside a creative entrepreneur?

I am looking for someone interested in marketing, communications, digital content and creative business to join my small creative business through a work try-out (työkokeilu).

I work across visual art, art products and spatial design, with projects ranging from my own artwork and online shop to exhibitions, cultural projects, events, collaborations and client work. This means you will get to see how different parts of a creative business connect — from developing ideas and creating content to presenting, marketing and selling creative work.

What you could explore and learn:

The exact focus of the work try-out can be shaped around your interests, existing skills and learning goals. Depending on what you would like to learn, this could include:

Instagram content: posts, Reels and Stories
photography and documenting artwork, projects and events
website content and updates
online shop content, including product images, descriptions and presentation
newsletters and email communication
basic SEO and digital marketing
looking at website, social media and online shop analytics
identifying potential customers, collaborators and B2B opportunities
supporting communication and outreach
preparing content around exhibitions, events and other creative projects
documenting spatial design and creative projects
learning how a small creative business approaches marketing, sales and communication
exploring how an artist, designer and entrepreneur balance creative work with the practical side of running a business

You would work alongside and with guidance from me, rather than simply being given a list of tasks to complete independently. There will be opportunities to observe how decisions are made, ask questions, try things out and discuss what works and what does not.

Who this could be for:

This opportunity could suit you if you are:

curious about working in the creative, cultural, design or arts sector
considering a career in marketing, communications or digital content
thinking about becoming a freelancer or entrepreneur
already developing your own creative practice or small business and want to see how another creative entrepreneur works
interested in learning more about online sales, social media or digital marketing
looking for practical experience to complement your existing studies or skills

You do not need to be an experienced marketer. Curiosity, reliability, organisation and willingness to learn are more important.

Experience or interest in Instagram, photography, video, graphic design, websites, online shops, SEO, digital marketing or analytics would be useful. A visual eye and an interest in creative work are a bonus.

What you can gain:

This is an opportunity to gain practical experience while getting a closer look at the realities of running a small creative business.

You can develop skills and material for your CV and portfolio, test whether this type of work suits you, build professional connections and learn from the day-to-day decisions, challenges and opportunities that come with creative entrepreneurship.

It is also a two-way learning experience: I can share what I have learned from working as an artist, architect, spatial designer and entrepreneur, while your skills, questions and perspective can bring something valuable to the business too.

About the work try-out:

This is a work try-out (työkokeilu), not an employment relationship. During the work try-out, you continue to receive the unemployment benefit you are personally entitled to through Kela or your unemployment fund, subject to their rules. You may also be entitled to an expense allowance for participation days.

The work try-out must be agreed with your employment services before it begins. If you are interested but are unsure whether a work try-out is possible in your situation, you can discuss the opportunity with your employment-services contact.

Hakuohjeet

If this opportunity sounds interesting, please get in touch and tell me briefly:

what interests you about the opportunity
what you would like to learn or explore
what experience or skills you already have
whether you are particularly interested in marketing, the creative industry, entrepreneurship, or a combination of these

A CV, portfolio or links to previous work are welcome if you have them, but you don't need to have an impressive portfolio or years of experience to apply.

Im happy to answer questions and have an informal conversation to see whether the work try-out could be a good fit for your goals.

Lisätietoja yrityksestä

Paloma Bautista is a small creative business based in Finland, combining visual art, spatial design and creative project work.

The business operates under two areas: Art by Pulu, offering original artworks, prints and other art products, and Design by Paloma, providing spatial and interior design for exhibitions, events, brands and other creative projects.

The business also includes exhibition and cultural project production, event-related work, creative collaborations and commissions. Projects range from creating and selling artwork to designing spaces, producing exhibitions and developing creative concepts for organisations, events and brands.
