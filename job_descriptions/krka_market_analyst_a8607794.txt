WOULD YOU LIKE TO HAVE A DYNAMIC CAREER?




Take up a challenge as a




MARKET ANALYST

(Espoo, on-site)




YOUR CONTRIBUTION

Conduct technical and organizational tasks to collect and organize market and sales data
Process, interpret and prepare analyses and reviews of marketing and sales data
Prepare presentations and reports of processed data
Act as a local admin user for the CRM and internal educational systems
Participation in hospital and dose-dispensing business




WE EXPECT

Education: Bachelor’s degree in Pharmacy or Economics or similar
Experience: Experiences with market analysis, preferably in pharmaceutical business
Languages: Proficiency in Finnish and English
Good PC use skills with regard to Word-processing, spreadsheets, database application, and internet
Excellent skills in excel and interest for further development
Capability for independent development of the processes and analyses in the area of responsibility




WE OFFER

Employment for indefinite time
Initial in-depth training
Work in international business environment
Professional development, career growth
Great colleagues and support from the team




For suitable candidate the job description can be developed in the direction of more responsibility in strategic market analyses and interpretation, and tasks connected with Market Access responsibilities




If you have good analytical skills with ablity to manage priorities and adapt to change, have good communication skills and are proactive and flexible, we are looking forward to your application letter and CV in English language via Linkedin.




The position is on-site in our office in Otaniemi, Espoo.




You can apply for this role until 31.07.2026.




For any further questions you can contact General Manager Sanni Sjöblom (sanni.sjoblom@krka.biz), due to summer holiday season please send an email and we can agree for time for a phone call if needed.
