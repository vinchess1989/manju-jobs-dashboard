Company Description

Santen is a specialized life sciences company with a proud over 130-year heritage focused exclusively on ophthalmology. As a Japan-originated, global company with a footprint in over 60 countries, we are dedicated to delivering innovative treatments and digital solutions for eye health and addressing vision-related social issues for people around the world.

Work Location: Tampere or Helsinki, Finland

Job Description

The Commercial Operations Manager ensures operational excellence across the commercial organization by translating data into actionable insights, optimizing demand planning and product availability, and driving efficient cross-functional processes. The role enables informed business decisions, regulatory compliance, and seamless collaboration across Commercial, Supply Chain, Finance, Market Access, and Regulatory functions to maximize business performance and ensure continuous product availability for patients.

Key Responsibilities & Accountabilities


Manage demand forecasting using sales, market, and customer data (SAP, IQVIA) to improve forecast accuracy and product availability.
Collaborate with Finance, Supply Chain, and regional teams to align IBP processes, optimize stock levels, and support supply allocation decisions.
Monitor and mitigate out-of-stock (OOS) risks through safety stock reporting, excess & obsolete tracking, stock transfers, and authority notifications.
Analyze commercial, market, and inventory data to identify trends, prepare reports and dashboards, and provide actionable business insights.
Ensure accurate product data submission to authorities and maintain regulatory reporting across the Nordics.
Lead cross-functional projects and continuous improvement initiatives in Sales Operations Excellence.
Act as the key liaison between Supply Chain, Sales, Finance, Market Access, and Regulatory to ensure alignment on products and operations.
Lead and coordinate Nordic product meetings, Country Reviews, IBP participation, and operational forums, ensuring effective governance and follow-up.
Oversee operational processes, including invoicing, contracts, and business tools (e.g., Coupa, SharePoint), to support efficient business operations.
Manage product-related communications and administrative activities to ensure business continuity and operational effectiveness.


Qualifications

Essential


Bachelor's or Master's degree (Business, Supply Chain, Life Sciences, or equivalent)
Fluent English; Nordic language(s) (e.g. Swedish) desirable.
Proficiency in SAP, IQVIA, Microsoft Office, Veeva, Coupa, TrackWise, and GLAMS
Experience in the pharmaceutical industry across commercial, supply, or regulatory functions
Experience working with authorities and regulatory compliance processes
Proven cross-functional collaboration experience
Strong understanding of demand planning and sales operations
Strong analytical and data-driven mindset
Structured and detail-oriented approach
Customer and service-oriented mindset
Proactive problem-solving with a strong sense of ownership
Strong collaboration and stakeholder management skills


Desirable


Experience in an international/EMEA environment
Expertise in ophthalmic products
Ability to perform effectively under pressure
