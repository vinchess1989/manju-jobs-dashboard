1. About Our Client:

The organization operates within the AI technology sector, focusing on enhancing AI agent capabilities by connecting large language models (LLMs) to reliable, high-quality web content. This connection supports real-time reasoning and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) in AI systems. The company serves a diverse client base, from startups to large enterprises, enabling the deployment of AI solutions such as sales assistants, research copilots, and internal knowledge tools.




2. About the Opportunity:

The Events Specialist is responsible for leading the planning and execution of the organization’s participation in industry conferences, tradeshows, and corporate events. The role ensures effective event logistics management and drives measurable return on investment from each event. This position supports the organization’s broader goals by facilitating brand presence and lead generation through strategic event participation.




3. Responsibilities:

• Plan and manage end-to-end tradeshow and event logistics including booth design, shipping, setup, and staffing

• Maintain the annual events calendar and oversee budgets for 10 to 20 events annually

• Coordinate with vendors, exhibitors, and internal stakeholders

• Manage pre-show promotions and post-show lead handoff to the sales team

• Track event performance metrics and report on pipeline impact

• Negotiate contracts with venues, vendors, and event organizers




4. Requirements:

• 2 to 4 years of experience in event coordination or field marketing

• Strong project management skills with experience using tools such as Asana, Monday.com, or similar

• Ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously under tight deadlines

• Willingness to travel up to 30% of the time

• Preferred experience with CRM systems (Salesforce, HubSpot) for lead tracking




5. Pay Range and Compensation Package:

• Base Compensation Range $121,100 to $151,300 USD




6. Benefits & Perks:

• 100% company-paid medical, dental, and vision insurance coverage for employees and families

• 401(k) plan with up to 4% company match and immediate vesting

• Paid parental leave: 20 weeks for primary caregivers, 12 weeks for secondary caregivers

• Remote work reimbursement up to $85 per month for mobile and internet expenses

• Company-paid short-term, long-term disability, and life insurance coverage
