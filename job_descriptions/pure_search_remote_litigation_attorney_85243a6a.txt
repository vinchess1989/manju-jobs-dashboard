WHY THIS OPPORTUNITY IS DIFFERENT

Most plaintiff-side firms talk about going to trial. At Berger Montague, we actually do it — at the highest level of the plaintiffs’ bar.




We have a busy national practice spanning antitrust, securities, consumer, employment, environmental, and civil rights litigation. We are looking to add a trial attorney with significant trial experience and the technical knowledge to prepare for and lead trials in complex class action matters. Our cases are large-scale, our opponents are sophisticated, and our outcomes change lives.




This is a position for someone who is ready to be in the courtroom — not on the sidelines. You will lead or substantially assist trial teams, working alongside some of the most experienced class action litigators in the country.




This is a rare opportunity to apply deep trial skills at scale on matters that matter — fighting for individuals and classes whose outcomes depend on what happens in the courtroom.




Learn more about our practice areas: BMPC Practice Areas




ABOUT BERGER MONTAGUE

Berger Montague PC pioneered the use of class actions in American civil litigation. Founded in Philadelphia in 1970, the Firm has spent more than five decades at the vanguard of plaintiff-side litigation, recovering more than $55 billion for clients across antitrust, consumer, employment, securities, civil rights, and more.




Our 100+ attorneys take on high stakes matters where the legal issues are hard, and the outcomes change lives. Associates are given genuine responsibility from day one because our cases demand it.




The firm is recognized by Chambers USA, The Legal 500, and Law360 as a national leader in class action litigation.

THE ROLE

This is a senior litigation role focused on trial preparation and courtroom advocacy. You will be entrusted with leading or substantially assisting trial teams across the firm’s national docket of complex class action matters.




Responsibilities include:

Prepare cases for trial and arbitration; lead or substantially assist trial teams.
Lead existing complex litigation, including discovery and motion practice.
Take and defend depositions.
Participate in all aspects of pre-trial discovery.
Draft and respond to motions; draft briefs.
Present case strategy at trials in complex class action litigation.







QUALIFICATIONS

J.D. from an accredited law school; admitted in at least one U.S. jurisdiction
8+ years of meaningful trial and deposition experience
Strong academic credentials as well as robust analytical, research, writing, and organizational skills
Strong work ethic and an ability to manage and thrive in a fast-paced work environment
Licensed to practice law in at least one United States jurisdiction; candidates licensed in Washington, D.C., California, Minnesota, New York, Pennsylvania, or Illinois are particularly encouraged to apply, though licensure in these jurisdictions is not required




COMPENSATION & BENEFITS

$200,000 base annual salary plus an annual discretionary bonus
Profit sharing
Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision insurance; HRA contribution
Short/long-term disability and life insurance
401(k) with profit sharing
Bar dues and CLE reimbursement; pre-tax transportation benefits




HOW TO APPLY

Submit the following in a single email to Candice Enders, Director of Recruiting, at careers@bergermontague.com:

Cover letter describing your trial experience and interest in plaintiff-side litigation and why Berger Montague specifically
Resume
Law school transcript (unofficial acceptable)
Writing sample (10-20 pages; ideally a brief or motion you drafted)
Three professional references




Subject line: "Trial Attorney Application – [BMPC Office Location Preference] [Your Name]"
