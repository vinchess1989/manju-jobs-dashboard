Hae työpaikkaa
Työn kuvaus

We are looking for an experienced Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations expert for a long-term assignment with a large industrial-sector client.

The tasks involve, among other things, importing data into the ERP system. Clients data team handles the data preparation and related work, but we see that loading the data into the ERP requires specialized expertise.

The ideal candidate brings broad and hands-on experience with D365 Finance & Operations, along with the ability to work effectively in a complex enterprise environment. Finnish language skills are considered an advantage but are not required.

Please apply with your updated CV in English, that clearly demonstrates your skills and experience relevant to this role. We offer continuously, which means that we sometimes remove the assignments before deadline. If you are interested, we recommend that you apply immediately.

Start time: As soon as a suitable candidate has been found
Allocation: Preferably 100%, but lower allocation may also be considered
Location: Remote work is possible

We offer you: We offer a versatile assignment where every day is different and interesting. We also offer a very competitive salary and good employee benefits.

Are you interested in becoming a part of our dynamic team? The position will be filled as soon as a suitable person is found, so please be quick and submit your application!
