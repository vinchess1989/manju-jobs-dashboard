English Writing Generalist – Quality Review (AI Training)

About The Role

We're looking for sharp, detail-oriented English writing professionals to help evaluate and improve AI-generated content. Your editorial instincts will directly shape how AI systems write, communicate, and express ideas in English — making a measurable impact on the quality of content that reaches millions of people.


Organization: Alignerr (Powered by Labelbox)
Type: Hourly Contract
Location: Remote
Commitment: 10–40 hours/week


What You'll Do


Review AI-generated English content for grammar, tone, clarity, and style
Identify subtle errors, inconsistencies, and awkward phrasing that undermine quality
Improve readability and flow without altering the original intent or meaning
Apply consistent editorial judgment across a wide range of content types and topics
Provide structured, actionable feedback using defined quality rubrics
Work independently and asynchronously on your own schedule


Who You Are


Based in the United States
Bachelor's degree in English, Journalism, Humanities, Communications, or a related field
Strong proofreading and editing instincts with a sharp eye for linguistic detail
Able to evaluate large volumes of content efficiently without sacrificing accuracy
Consistent, reliable, and self-motivated — comfortable working independently
No prior AI experience required


Nice to Have


Professional editing, copywriting, or content review experience
Familiarity with style guides (AP, Chicago, MLA, etc.)
Experience working with AI tools or content quality workflows


Why Join Us


Work on cutting-edge AI projects with top research labs
Fully remote and flexible — work on your own schedule
Freelance perks: autonomy, variety, and meaningful work
Contribute to improving how AI communicates with the world
Potential for ongoing work and contract extension
