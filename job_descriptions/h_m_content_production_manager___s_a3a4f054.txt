Job Description



WHAT YOU'LL DO



As a Content Production Manager at H&M, you plan and adapt marketing and in‑store communication content and collaborate with media and production partners to deliver high‑quality assets. You ensure effective store communication by coordinating materials, digital content needs, and feedback from store teams.



You will:



Plan and oversee adaptation and delivery of globally initiated activities in all media and in‑store communication channels, ensuring alignment with brand and campaign goals.
For local tactical activations: plan and coordinate asset production and/or adaptation across multiple channels, based on the local marketing plan.
Stay immersed in the local daily plan to anticipate asset needs for paid media and store communications, while keeping up‑to‑date with global channel playbooks, media formats, and store formats, and maintaining a clear understanding of global store communication strategies and placements.
Collaborate closely with Media Leads, Media Agencies, Production partners and vendors to brief, track, and approve the production and adaptation of assets.
Act as the main point of contact for Media agencies regarding formats, assets, and campaign materials.
Coordinate production of store material, in collaboration with Store Visual teams.
Brief the Store Visual team on POS plans and campaign quantities, feed local digital‑screen content needs into production, and collect in‑store feedback on communication materials and asset allocation to share with relevant stakeholders.

WHO YOU ARE

You have a background in marketing, communications, or business, with strong paid‑media expertise, analytical ability, and stakeholder‑management skills. You bring years of experience in digital or performance marketing, proven success managing media budgets and ROI‑driven campaigns, and solid experience collaborating with agencies.



Degree in Marketing, Media, or Production Management (or equivalent experience)
Strong understanding of paid media formats (TV, OOH, OLV, Display, Social, Print, etc.)
Knowledge of retail store communicatons formats and production
Excellent organizational skills with attention to detail
Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities
Fluent in English; additional languages are an advantage
Minimum 3–5 years of experience in marketing, content production, or media roles (agency or client side)
Experience working in fashion retail or similar
