Räätälöi ansioluetteloni
HydraSpecma is a global company within the hydraulic industry, and we are growing and constantly need top employees to join us!

We are now looking for self-driven and energetic MARKETING MANAGER to our Finnish headquarters in Espoo.

In the role as Marketing Manager, you are responsible for driving market visibility, demand generation, and brand consistency in the Finnish market, in close alignment with Nordic and global marketing strategies. The role combines responsibility for strategic planning with hands-on execution across digital channels, versatile content creation, campaigns, and events.

This role is a new role in Finland, and you will have the opportunity to put your touch into the role, in collaboration with the Finnish team as well as the global Marketing team. You will work closely with Sales, Product Management, and Group Marketing to support commercial growth and strengthen the company’s B2B brand position.

In this role you will drive marketing activities that support sales growth in the Finnish market and set, execute, and follow up on the local marketing plan in alignment with Nordic strategy. You will drive cross-functional initiatives, such as marketing automation, lead generation strategies, and SEO optimization. In this role you will also plan, execute, and optimize digital marketing campaigns across relevant platforms. You will also plan and execute global product and brand campaigns, and coordinate and execute local events and fairs.

To Be Successful In This Role You Should Have


A Master or B Sc Degree in Marketing, Communication, or equivalent
Experience from a B2B marketing role, preferably within a technical or industrial environment
Project Management and leadership skills with the ability to drive projects and work independently
Passion for digital marketing and staying updated with industry trends
Experience to plan, execute and optimize digital marketing campaigns.
Knowledge and documented experience of Umbraco, WordPress, Wix, or any CMS, and strong understanding of SEO, SEM, PPC, social media content marketing, and analytics
Strong graphical skills and experience with InDesign, PowerPoint and video editing, and also proficiency in analytics tools such as Matomo, Google Analytics, SEMrush, HubSpot, and digital reporting
Fluent in both Finnish and English. Proficiency in Swedish is a plus.


As a person you have a high level of energy and drive, and you are self-driven and development oriented. You work in a structured way, and you have ability to handle multiple tasks at the same time. You are communicative and service minded, and you have ability to work both independently as well as together with a team.

We Offer You


An exciting role in an international company where you will have the opportunity to grow within the role and within our organisation
A close collaboration with both marketing globally as well as sales and product management teams in Finland and Sweden
Opportunity to influence and develop our local marketing initiatives
A strong community where we help each other and celebrate our successes This is a permanent position, and you will have the possibility to partial remote work.

About HydraSpecma

HydraSpecma is a global specialized manufacturing, trading, and engineering company with our core business within hydraulic solutions, fluid Conveyance, electrification and renewable energy to a wide range of different industries and markets. We are located in 11 countries, and with our headquarter in Skjern, Denmark, we are a strong Nordic player and a versatile company with high ambitions. We are approx. 1500 employees worldwide (around 90 in Finland) and are owned by the conglomerate Schouw & Co.
