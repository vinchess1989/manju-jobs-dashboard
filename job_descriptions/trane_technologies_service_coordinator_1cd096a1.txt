Where Is The Work

On-Site (5 days)- Helsinki / office in Espoo.




What’s In It For You

Service Coordinator will lead and support a team of Service Technicians within assigned jobs across Finland. Plan and coordinate daily activities, ensure smooth communication between customers and technicians, and support in achieving operational goals. Provide administrative support to service operations, maintain strong team cooperation, and ensure consistent service quality and customer satisfaction.

What You Will Do

Plan and allocate workloads for Service Technicians, ensuring efficient use of resources and meeting productivity targets.
Supervise technical and safety aspects of service work, ensuring timely, high‑quality completion.
Review service reports, worksheets, and technical documentation for accuracy and completeness.
Identify and implement process improvements to enhance productivity and service quality.
Collaborate closely with Sales and other internal teams.
Lead regular coordination meetings and provide updates on backlog, forecast, and priorities.
Schedule service jobs, materials, subcontractors, and ensure timely completion of all interventions.
Prepare work orders, dispatch technicians, and communicate with customers.
Plan and track preventive maintenance activities according to contracts or operational needs.
Manage customer orders and documentation in planning systems and Oracle.
Oversee end‑to‑end procurement processes: issuing POs, ordering parts, receiving goods, and completing transactions.
Maintain accurate databases and administrative records (orders, contracts, sold equipment, claims, invoices, etc.).
Ensure all work follows safety rules, perform site safety follow-ups, and escalate issues when needed.
Support customer satisfaction by addressing concerns and coordinating responses.
Handle warranty claim collection and reporting.
Log service activities, technical data, and required documentation in tracking systems.
Monitor worksheets, quotations, orders, and equipment deliveries; prepare monthly invoicing packages for Service, Parts, and Equipment.
Perform quarterly stock checks with the Spare Parts team and reconcile with Oracle.

What You Will Bring

Minimum high school education (preferred University education)
Minimum 3 years of experience in Coordination of work in service
Experience in leading or supervising a small team welcome
Customer orientation
Ability to build long-term relationship both with a customer and within the team
Very good organizational skills and attention to details
Ability to plan and set up priorities
Assertiveness and courage to speak up
Proactiveness and ability to take initiative
Ability to work under pressure
High computer literacy (ability to work in different systems and programs and good knowledge of MS Office)
English knowledge – communicative level and fluent Finnish
