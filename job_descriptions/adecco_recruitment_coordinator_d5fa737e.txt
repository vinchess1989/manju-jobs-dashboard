Talent Acquisition Coordinator

Location: Fully Remote (EST Hours)

Duration: 6-Month Contract with Potential to Extend

Pay Rate: $30–35/hour (based on experience)




Our client, a well-established and mission-driven leader in the publishing and education industry, is seeking a Talent Acquisition Coordinator to join their recruiting team. This role is ideal for someone who is highly organized, detail-oriented, and passionate about creating an exceptional candidate experience. Working closely with recruiters and hiring managers, you'll support recruiting efforts in a fast-paced environment while helping ensure a seamless hiring process from start to finish.




Responsibilities

Provide a best-in-class candidate experience throughout the recruitment process.
Coordinate and schedule phone, virtual, and in-person interviews in a timely manner.
Maintain candidate records and recruitment activity within Workday ATS, ensuring accuracy and compliance.
Support offer creation and background check coordination.
Post job openings across various job boards and recruiting platforms.
Screen candidates against hiring manager requirements and conduct initial qualification calls.
Assist recruiters with full-cycle recruiting activities and manage a small requisition or sourcing workload as needed.
Source passive candidates through LinkedIn, networking, professional organizations, and other creative sourcing channels.
Partner closely with recruiters and hiring managers to understand hiring needs and develop sourcing strategies.
Provide administrative support to the Talent Acquisition team throughout the hiring process.




Qualifications

High school diploma with 2+ years of administrative experience, or
Bachelor's degree in Human Resources or a related field with 1+ year of administrative experience.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
Exceptional attention to detail and follow-through.
Proficiency with Microsoft Office and experience using an Applicant Tracking System (Workday preferred).




Preferred Qualifications

Experience supporting Talent Acquisition or Human Resources teams.
Recruiting coordination or sourcing experience.
Experience within publishing, education, or a related industry is a plus.

If you're looking to join a collaborative recruiting team and gain experience supporting a nationally recognized organization, we'd love to hear from you!
