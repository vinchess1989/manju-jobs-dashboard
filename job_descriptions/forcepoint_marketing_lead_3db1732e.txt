The Marketing Lead for Network Security builds and articulates the messaging, positioning and uniqueness of Forcepoint Network Security security solutions, drives go-to-market campaigns, develops and provides sales training, produces sales tools, collateral and competitive profiles.

The role includes using AI-enabled tools and data-driven insights to strengthen customer understanding, improve campaign effectiveness and accelerate content development. The person will also lead selected event marketing activities, ensuring that events, webinars and customer-facing engagements support technology positioning, lead generation, customer engagement and brand visibility.







Description

Network Security messaging and positioning for sales, and sales engineering teams, as well as partnership with channel and direct customer
Drive overall go-to-market strategy, planning and execution for the Network Security product family in support of awareness, market-share and revenue growth objective
Develop and implement high impact and differentiated positioning and messaging for Network Security across vertical and horizontal market segments. Apply generative AI tools when relevant while ensuring quality, accuracy and brand consistency
Develop high-impact sales tools such as white papers, presentations, case studies, business cases, TCO/ROI analysis, competitive profiles and sales playbooks for Network Security
Deliver product demonstrations of Network Security to varied constituents including sales, sales engineering, industry and financial analysts, industry analysts, customers and prospects, partners, and others
Develop and implement route-to-market plans for Network Security and partnerships
Provide direction and support to the Global Sales Enablement department to develop and deliver sales and channel training covering the industry, market opportunity, product value proposition, selling strategies, and competitive position
Plan and execute selected product-related event marketing activities, including webinars, seminars, customer events, trade fairs or partner events
Perform other duties and projects as assigned




Education and Experience

Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience related to information technology
Demonstrated years of product marketing or product management experience, ideally with cyber security industry
Experience using AI tools to support product marketing, content creation, AI based Acoount Based Experience
Ability to critically evaluate AI-generated outputs and refine them for accuracy, audience relevance and business impact
Ability to use AI based technologies to position and demonstrate in messaging desired customer outcomes
Experience in B2B event marketing, such as webinars, customer events or partner activities, is considered an advantage




Soft Skills

Proactive communicator with strong analytical skills
Strong business acumen and decision-making ability
Exceptional interpersonal, leadership, communication and writing skills
Proven ability to work closely with sales to enable their success
Proven project marketing skills and the ability deliver sales tools and required content
