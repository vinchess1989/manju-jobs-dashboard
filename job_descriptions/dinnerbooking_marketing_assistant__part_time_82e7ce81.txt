Do you have a passion for digital marketing, and want to create marketing campaigns for some of the best restaurants in Finland ? Then this is the position for you.

 

DinnerBooking provides a market-leading online booking system for restaurants in Nordics, 

The DinnerBooking guest platform, is part of the service we provide for restaurant, where thousands of guests can explore all the nice restaurants using DinnerBooking. This position will be focused on creating even more awareness and generate traffic to the guestplatform and the restaurants that are listed here. Its all about the guest experience – and your job is to make it the best in the market!

Does it sound intriguing? Then prepare yourself for a journey building the best marketing & digital experience in the restaurant industry. 




Your role

As our new Marketing Assistant, you will work hands-on and run our B2C (targeting guests) content & marketing strategy in Finland and help us with creating content & campaigns for various channels addressing hungry guests.

You will join our office in Helsinki and work together with the local team for the Finnish market. 




Responsibilities

Create engaging stories and campaigns for different platforms – especially social media, email campaigns, and blogposts.
Following the 'tone of voice' and Brand Guide and make sure that the content can be converted into measurable results.
Collaborating with our Sales and CS team, so new restaurants joining DinnerBooking are converted into inspiring restaurant stories for guests.




Your profile

Have preferrrably 1-2 years of relevant experience. Student jobs count as well.  
Speak fluent Finnish and can create campaigns for different channels
Are self-driven and proactive, good at starting and finnishing projects
Possess English skills at a professional level in writing and speaking.
Have a creative and aesthetic eye for content and know what performs well on the different channels - and you love to test out new ideas.
Have knowledge for SoMe and FB Business / Ads Manager.
Are able to think performance into content and improving results
Have an eye for structure and is a finisher.
It’s also a big plus if you have experience or insight from the restaurant industry, but it’s not a requirement.

If you can check these boxes, you could most likely be our new colleague.    




Our team

Currently, DinnerBooking consists of nearly 40 employees spread across Europe. You will become a part of our B2C Marketing team, which currently consists of dedicated employees from Denmark, Norway, and Estonia. 

You will refer to the Marketing team in Denmark, which will consist of regular online meetings.

Besides, you will work closely with our collegaues in the Finnish office.. 




Our offer

An exciting role in a company where there are lots of exciting projects on the menu.

You will be part of a company with dedicated colleagues, and where you get the opportunity to have an impact on which direction we should move towards.




Part-time position  (20-30 hours). Salary according to your qualifications.

 

Deadline

We hope that our new colleague can start as soon as possible. Therefore, we encourage you to send us your CV in english today.




Make sure to merge your portfolio in the same document so we can see what you have created recently.
