Based in central Helsinki, Boulevard Tech builds AI systems for professionals in highly regulated industries. We tailor AI models and agents to highly regulated fields – from law and regulation to policy and public affairs – and build the workflows to go with them, working closely with end users at every stage.

We're looking for curious, motivated people to join us in this fast moving space.




What you'll work on




You'll help us apply AI to real, specialized workflows across a range of industry verticals and domains. In practice, you'll work hands-on with:




AI-assisted legal, regulatory and policy research
Building AI-native workflows and structuring legal and policy knowledge for AI systems
Analysis of regulatory decisions, markets, policy and governance models




Why this role is different




This is a full-time position where you'll have the chance to take real ownership early on. As an early-stage company, responsibilities evolve quickly. You'll have the opportunity to shape both the role and the way we work. The role grows with you: you'll move between different types of work, build new skills, and grow into roles and responsibilities that fit your strengths – which over time may include client-facing and customer work.




You'll get direct exposure to:




How AI is actually being built and deployed in these fields
How legal and policy expertise is translated into AI-ready data, models and tools
How client needs are turned into real AI solutions – from understanding a professional's workflow to shaping an offering that fits it




Who should apply




Students nearing graduation, early-career professionals, researchers and doctoral candidates
People who are genuinely curious about AI and motivated to learn how new AI tools work
An interest in regulation, policy and public affairs
Sociable, collaborative people who feel comfortable working closely with a team and, over time, with clients and customers
Strong analytical and interpersonal skills
Ambition and intellectual curiosity
Technical experience is not required, although familiarity with AI tools is a plus. Curiosity and willingness to learn are what matter most




Practicalities




Full-time role
Start date: August–September 2026 – flexible for the right candidate
Location: Helsinki. We work mostly from the office because we value teamwork and collaboration, with the flexibility to work remotely when needed
Working language: English (Finnish not mandatory)
Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.
