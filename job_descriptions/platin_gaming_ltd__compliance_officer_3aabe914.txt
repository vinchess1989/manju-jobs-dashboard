Ensure regulatory compliance with MGA and other gaming jurisdictions

About The Role

Monitor and ensure compliance with gaming regulations across multiple jurisdictions. You'll implement compliance procedures, conduct audits, and liaise with regulators to maintain our gaming licenses.

Responsibilities


Monitor compliance with MGA regulations
Conduct regular compliance audits
Implement compliance policies and procedures
Liaise with gaming regulators
Review marketing materials for compliance
Train staff on compliance requirements
Investigate compliance incidents
Prepare regulatory reports and submissions


Requirements


3+ years of compliance experience in iGaming
Deep knowledge of MGA regulations
Understanding of AML and responsible gaming
Experience with compliance management systems
Strong attention to detail
Excellent written communication
Legal or compliance background preferred
Professional compliance certification a plus


Benefits


Salary €35,000 - €45,000
Health insurance
Professional certifications support
Career progression
Work with regulators
Stable role

Frequently Asked Questions

As a Compliance Officer at Platin Gaming Ltd, you will be part of our General department, contributing to our iGaming products and services. The role involves working with cutting-edge technology in a fast-paced, international environment based in Malta.
