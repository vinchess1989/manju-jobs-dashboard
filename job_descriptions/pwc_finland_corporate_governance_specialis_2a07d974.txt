We are seeking proactive and detail-oriented Corporate Governance Specialist to strengthen our partner affairs and corporate governance operations. In this versatile role, you will support senior executives, the Board of Directors, and various committees, ensuring effective governance practices and smooth execution of partner matters.




What will you do?

As a key liaison between management, the Chair of the Board, General Counsel, and People leadership, you will be responsible for preparing, executing, and following up on board and committee meetings as well as managing partner-related processes.




Your main responsibilities include:

Preparing and coordinating Board of Directors, Partner Committee, Nomination Committee, partner and shareholder meetings
Managing partner affairs processes, including contracts, shareholder registry maintenance, network quality and reporting, and share transfers
Developing and maintaining corporate governance and compliance functions in collaboration with relevant stakeholders
Leading and coordinating projects and processes, ensuring timely implementation and accurate documentation as part of the company’s quality system
Handling sensitive and confidential matters with discretion and integrity




What do we expect from you?

Proven experience as an executive or management assistant combined with exposure to corporate governance
Strong organizational and multitasking skills, with the ability to work proactively and manage multiple priorities
Excellent communication skills in English and Finnish; Swedish skills are a plus
Ability to work independently and as part of a team, demonstrating responsibility and a customer-oriented mindset
High level of discretion, reliability, and strong attention to detail
Flexibility and adaptability to thrive in a fast-paced, international work environment
Commitment to working regularly on-site at our Helsinki office to ensure close collaboration and effective team communication




Interested? Great! How does the recruitment process proceed?

Application period ends by 13.9.2026.
We will interview candidates at our office in Ruoholahti.
The recruitment process will include 2-3 interviews at our office, and online aptitude tests with a feedback discussion via Teams. We will also ask selected candidates to provide contact information for two referees.
