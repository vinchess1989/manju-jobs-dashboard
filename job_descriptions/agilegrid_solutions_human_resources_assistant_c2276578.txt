About The Role

The Human Resources Assistant at INDI Staffing Services plays a vital role in supporting the HR department's daily operations. This position involves providing administrative support across various HR functions, including recruitment, onboarding, employee records management, and employee relations. The ideal candidate is a detail-oriented professional capable of handling multiple tasks efficiently while maintaining confidentiality and professionalism. Under the guidance of HR leaders, the HR Assistant will help streamline processes, ensure data accuracy, and foster a positive employee experience. This role offers an excellent opportunity for individuals seeking to develop their HR career in a fast-paced, innovative environment that values creativity, autonomy, and continuous learning.

Qualifications


Minimum of 1+ year experience in Human Resources or administrative support roles.
Basic understanding of HR functions and administrative procedures.
Proficiency in MS Office applications and HR information systems.
Strong organizational skills with meticulous attention to detail.
Ability to handle sensitive information with discretion and confidentiality.
Excellent communication skills in English, both written and verbal.
Flexible work approach, with the ability to adapt to different working environments and schedules.


Responsibilities


Assist in the administration of recruitment activities, including scheduling interviews and coordinating communication with candidates.
Support the onboarding process for new hires by preparing documentation and facilitating smooth integration into the company.
Maintain accurate and up-to-date employee records and HR databases to ensure data integrity.
Support payroll preparation and benefits administration, ensuring timely and accurate processing.
Respond to basic HR-related inquiries from employees, providing guidance or directing them to appropriate resources.
Help organize HR events, training sessions, and company activities to promote engagement and professional development.
Participate in continuous process improvements within the HR department to enhance efficiency and employee satisfaction.
