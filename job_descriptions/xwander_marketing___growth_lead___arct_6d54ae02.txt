Salary: €3,800–4,800/month gross, depending on experience. Accommodation in Ivalo can be arranged for someone working locally full-time and for hybrid employees during agreed on-site periods.




BUILD GROWTH FROM THE ARCTIC




We’re looking for an experienced, hands-on marketer to marketing and growth across Xwander Group.




You will set the direction, make the work happen and use commercial data to decide what happens next. This is for someone comfortable making decisions, shipping work quickly, testing it in the real world and continuously improving it.




ABOUT XWANDER




Xwander Group is based in Ivalo, Finnish Lapland. Xwander Nordic creates premium small-group and tailor-made Arctic travel experiences. Xwander Productions provides services for international film, TV, commercial and brand productions. Xwander Academy delivers professional outdoor, wilderness safety and first-aid training.




Our work also includes destination marketing through Visit Ivalo, and Nordic MICE, incentive travel and events through NordicMice.




Winter is the most demanding period in our travel business, serving primarily Central European guests. MICE and events continue throughout the year, while film and TV productions create intense periods of work on different schedules.




Through Xwander Productions, we have hosted major international clients and productions, including Apple TV+’s Constellation, MrBeast and Alone Finland.




THE ROLE




This is a senior, hands-on individual-contributor role and the first position with end-to-end responsibility for both marketing and growth across the group.




You will guide how Xwander’s business areas, brands, products and services are positioned and brought to market. You will also be accountable for the quality, accuracy and commercial performance of our websites, partner and distribution platforms, social channels and YouTube presence.




This does not mean producing every asset alone. It means setting priorities and standards, making sure the work ships, using AI and external resources effectively, and ensuring that every important marketing surface is actively managed.




Our working style is closer to a startup than a traditional travel company. We use growth-hacking principles in a practical way: identify a bottleneck, form a hypothesis, ship a focused experiment, measure the outcome and act on what we learn.




The variety makes the role interesting, but we also want to be straightforward: at peak times, the work can be genuinely stressful. Priorities can change quickly because of bookings, client deadlines, events, production schedules or new commercial opportunities.




You need to remain calm, reset priorities and keep the most important work moving without losing quality or follow-through.




YOUR WORK WILL INCLUDE




Setting marketing and growth priorities across Xwander’s brands, products and services.
Managing positioning, content and conversion performance across our websites and relevant partner and distribution platforms.
Leading Xwander’s social media and YouTube presence, including channel priorities, publishing standards, content planning and performance.
Running paid acquisition across Google, Meta and other relevant platforms, including budgets, targeting, creative testing and optimisation.
Managing the funnel from advertising and search to landing page, HubSpot, booking and attributable revenue, including relevant email and lifecycle marketing.
Using market, competitor and customer research to identify opportunities and turn them into practical experiments.
Improving websites and landing pages continuously using AI and Analytics like GA4, Google Search Console and conversion data.
Planning and coordinating Xwander’s presence at relevant travel trade shows and industry events, such as Matkamessut, from objectives, messaging and materials to lead capture and systematic follow-up.
Writing and editing premium-positioned English content and directing work produced with AI tools, partners and external specialists.
Working with sales, customer service and operations to connect marketing activity with availability, bookings, project leads and revenue.




Success will be measured through conversions, attributable revenue, cost per lead, funnel performance, search visibility and commercially relevant audience growth—not by content volume or vanity metrics.




Working as part of the core team in a small company also means responsibilities do not always fit neatly inside individual job descriptions. During peak season or a colleague’s absence, you may occasionally need to help cover customer service or back-office responsibilities.




This is not the primary focus of the role, but a willingness to step in when the team genuinely needs support is important.




WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR




You do not need every point below. If you are strong on most of the essentials and ready to grow into the rest, we would like to hear from you.




ESSENTIAL




Demonstrable experience taking hands-on responsibility for marketing performance and commercial results.
Hands-on paid acquisition experience across Google Ads, paid social or other relevant platforms.
The ability to use Analytics tools like GA4 and Google Search Console independently and turn findings into concrete actions.
A practical understanding of conversion tracking, funnel attribution, CRM handoff and lifecycle marketing.
English copywriting and editorial judgment suitable for premium international brands.
Experience managing content across websites, social media and other digital channels. You understand what each channel is expected to achieve.
An experimental mindset: you can turn a business problem into a hypothesis, run a useful test and act on the result.
Decisive execution, attention to detail and the ability to ship work without waiting for approval on every point.
Strong organisation and composure under pressure. You can manage multiple brands and changing priorities while keeping critical work moving.
Regular use of AI in your workflow and the ability to direct, verify and improve AI-produced work.




NICE TO HAVE




SEO, CRO or A/B testing experience
HubSpot and email or lifecycle-marketing experience.
Social media and YouTube/video production depth.
Premium travel, hospitality or destination-marketing experience.
MICE, events, trade-show marketing, B2B marketing, film, television or media-production experience.
Startup, scale-up or founder-led company experience.
French language skills and experience marketing to Central European customers.
Knowledge of the Finnish or Nordic markets. Finnish is useful but not required.
Availability to start immediately or at short notice.




WHAT WE OFFER




€3,800–4,800/month gross, depending on experience, growing with experience and results.
Accommodation in Ivalo for full-time or agreed hybrid working periods can be arranged.
Permanent, full-time employment after trial period
End-to-end responsibility for building Xwander’s marketing and growth function.
An unusually varied portfolio spanning Arctic travel, destination marketing, outdoor education, MICE, eCommerce, events and international film and TV productions.
A small, startup-like team with short decision paths, modern AI tools and the freedom to move useful work forward.
Ivalo or hybrid working is preferred. Remote work within Finland or elsewhere in the EU may be possible for the right person.




The role includes an initial onboarding period in Lapland to become familiar with the team, products and services.




HOW TO APPLY




Send your application to joni@xwander.com.




We review applications as they arrive and will fill the position as soon as we find the right person.




Please include:




Your CV or LinkedIn profile.
Two or three examples of campaigns, websites or growth projects you personally ran. Explain what you changed and the measurable result where this can be shared without disclosing confidential information.
Relevant work samples or links.




Please also answer these questions:




1 . You join Xwander six weeks before winter high season. Paid campaigns are running, attribution through HubSpot to bookings is unreliable, product information differs across channels, Matkamessut travel-fair materials are due in ten days, and content publishing is inconsistent.




With limited resources, what are your first three priorities during your first ten working days? What do you postpone, and what evidence would show that you are on the right track?




2 . One of your first assignments will be to recruit and develop one or more Marketing Interns, with the possibility of longer-term roles as the marketing function grows.




How would you define the internship profile or profiles, find and assess candidates, and structure their first month? What would you continue to own yourself, what could the interns take responsibility for, and what would tell you the setup is working?
