About Huawei Finland R&D

With 28 sites across Europe and over 2000 researchers, Huawei’s European Research Institute (ERI) oversees fundamental and applied technology research, academic research cooperation projects, and strategic technical planning across our network of European R&D facilities. Huawei Finland Research Center was established in 2012. At our sites in Helsinki and Tampere, you will have an outstanding opportunity to be part of the leading research and development of future smart devices. We offer a career in a forward-looking industrial research team with a solid track record in innovation and publication and delivering its result as factual contributions to on-the-market devices.

HR Specialist

Huawei Finland Research Center HR team is looking for a professional HR Specialist to join our team. As an HR Specialist, you will be responsible for a variety of HR-related activities such as onboarding & offboarding, communication and events. You will report to the HR Manager and work closely with the HR team, as well as other departments both locally and globally. We are looking for motivated and enthusiastic individuals to join our team. If you're eager to learn, enjoy working with people, and want to be part of a supportive and dynamic environment, we'd love to hear from you!

Main tasks will include:


Responsible for the day-to-day HR operations within your assigned areas of responsibility.
Employee lifecycle management from Onboarding to Offboarding, especially new employee integration.
Manage internal HR communications, including company newsletters, HR policy updates, and employee communications.
Update and communicate HR policies while gathering employee feedback to support continuous improvement.
Maintaining HR databases and other personnel records, ensuring accuracy and compliance with GDPR.
Conducting and analyzing annual staff surveys, producing clear reports and insights for managers
Organizing and conducting HR related staff trainings together with HR team.
Respond to employee queries and resolving issues regarding HR matters in a timely and professional manner.
Responsible for planning, organizing, and successfully delivering internal company events.
Support additional HR initiatives and responsibilities based on your expertise, interests, and business needs.


Qualifications & skills requirement:


Relevant educational background: Bachelor’s degree or higher in Human Resources, Business Administration or a related field
2 – 4 years of previous experience in similar HR responsibilities preferably in the technology industry, with the ability to independently manage and take ownership of assigned areas.
Knowledge of Finnish labor law, collective agreements (especially TIPAL), and GDPR requirements is considered an advantage.
Excellent data management skills, with a high level of accuracy, attention to detail, and the ability to handle confidential and sensitive information.
Ability to manage multiple priorities and collaborate with a wide range of cross-functional stakeholders and establish efficient processes.
Excellent interpersonal and collaboration skills, with strong written and spoken English. Finnish language skills are considered as beneficial.
Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, particularly Excel and PowerPoint, and the ability to quickly learn new HR information systems.
Experience from working or studying in multicultural environments is considered beneficial.


What We Are Looking For:


People person with a positive attitude, a “customer service” -mindset and passion for HR
Ability to adjust to changing situations, take responsibility, manage complex responsibilities and work smoothly with different stakeholders
A proactive, solution-oriented professional and proactive working methods and sense of ownership
Motivated team player, who works well with others and is motivated to work in a multicultural environment.


Contract Type, starting date & Location:

This is a full-time consultant position, the consultant shall be employed by Huawei's supplier. We expect you to start as soon as possible and the position will be filled as soon as a suitable candidate is found.

Location: On-site in Helsinki, Finland.

Why join us？

Growth Opportunities: We have accomplished PhDs and researchers, creating an environment where you can collaborate, exchange ideas, and learn from each other while advancing your professional skills and career.

Multicultural Team Environment: We are a team of multinational background, creating a unique working environment for developing imaging technologies.

At Huawei, innovation focuses on customer needs. We invest heavily in basic research, concentrating on technological breakthroughs that drive the world forward.

We seek and reward talent! At Huawei, if you are dedicated to creativity, engagement of technical risks and delivery of target-driven results, your efforts will be rewarded with outstanding career prospects.
