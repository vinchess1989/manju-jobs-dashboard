Roschier is looking to hire Associate Trainees to join its practice teams in March 2027.

Roschier is a leading law firm in the Nordic region with offices in Helsinki and Stockholm. We specialize in business law and cover a broad range of practice areas. The firm is well known for its excellent track record of advising on demanding international business law assignments and large-scale transactions. In total, Roschier currently employs some 500 staff members.

Are you a law student interested in business law, and want to gain experience from working at a law firm? We are now looking to hire law students for the role as an Associate Trainee for a period spanning from March 2027 to September 2027.

Working as an Associate Trainee at Roschier

In the Associate Trainee program, you have the chance to apply your legal knowledge in practice in a challenging and ever-changing business law environment. All our trainees are part of the team from day one, taking responsibility and actively assisting our lawyers in client-related work and other matters. Our Associate Trainees have a similar job profile to Associates in the early stages of their careers, as the tasks may include drafting documents, making legal translations, or preparing problem-based analyses of the application of different laws.

In the role as an Associate Trainee, we offer you practical experience from working at a business law firm and the opportunity to develop on a personal and professional level. We offer you an inspiring and developing environment at one of the leading law firms in the Nordics.

We invest in you

At Roschier, you will be a part of a collaborative and stimulating environment where you can expect to grow on both a personal and professional level in a truly international setting. To support your development, we offer an extensive learning program through Roschier University, tailored to support you at every step of your professional development and equip you with the professional skills you need to excel in your role as an Associate Trainee and in your career. You will be assigned a tutor and a mentor whose role is to support you in learning and navigating your new role, getting to know the team, and welcoming you into Roschier's culture of teamwork, professionalism and client centricity.

Your profile

We are looking for candidates interested in business law, who have completed at least 180 law school study credits by March 2027.

Working at Roschier means collaborating with leading corporations on complex assignments. We seek colleagues with unique skills and perspectives who share our commitment to excellence in client service. As an Associate Trainee, you will enjoy teamwork across borders, share challenges and accomplishments, and ensure service quality. You will have the opportunity to grow both personally and professionally by working on assignments that allow you to develop your expertise. Strong oral and written communication skills, along with fluency in both Finnish and English, are required to excel in this role.

Please apply by 25 August 2026 through the application link and attach your CV, cover letter, university transcript and matriculation examination certificate.

Candidates are evaluated based on their academic and professional qualifications. The recruitment process consists of one interview and an online assessment package including ability and self-evaluation tests. Please note that the final candidates will complete a conflict check before joining.
