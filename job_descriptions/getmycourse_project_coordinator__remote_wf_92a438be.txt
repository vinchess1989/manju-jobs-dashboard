Project Coordinator

Full-Time | Australian Day Shift | Remote/WFH (Philippines)




Join a Growing Civil Infrastructure Team!

An established and rapidly growing Australian civil construction company is seeking multiple Project Coordinators to support its expanding operations. Whether you're a recent graduate looking to kick-start your career or an experienced project coordinator seeking your next opportunity, we'd love to hear from you.




This is an excellent opportunity to join a collaborative team where you'll receive comprehensive training, ongoing mentorship, and clear career progression within the civil infrastructure industry.




Why Join Us?

Be part of a growing Australian civil infrastructure business
Receive full training and ongoing mentorship from experienced professionals
Gain exposure to a variety of large-scale civil and infrastructure projects
Long-term career development and internal growth opportunities
Work with a supportive, collaborative, and high-performing team
Stable full-time position with Australian day shift hours
Remote work opportunity for professionals based in the Philippines
HMO on Year 1




About the Role

As a Project Coordinator, you'll play a vital role in supporting field operations by ensuring daily project activities are effectively planned, coordinated, tracked, and communicated.




Working closely with field teams, supervisors, and internal stakeholders, you'll help keep projects running efficiently while maintaining high standards of quality, safety, and compliance.




Key Responsibilities

Coordinate, allocate, and monitor daily field activities and project tasks
Liaise with field supervisors to coordinate pre-field workflows, resolve enquiries, and validate project information
Maintain accurate records within internal and client systems, including scheduling updates and job claims
Review project documentation and artefacts to ensure compliance with client requirements
Collaborate with internal teams and external stakeholders to support successful project delivery
Assist in ensuring projects are delivered safely, efficiently, and on schedule
Maintain high standards of accuracy, reliability, punctuality, and professionalism




What We're Looking For

We're looking for individuals with strong organisational skills, excellent communication abilities, and a willingness to learn. Industry experience is beneficial but not essential.




Ideal Skills & Experience

Prior experience in administration, project coordination or project management is HIGHLY REGARDED
Strong written and verbal communication skills
Excellent attention to detail
Strong organisational and time management skills
Confident using Microsoft Office, particularly Excel
Proactive, collaborative, and solutions-focused mindset
Familiarity with NBN, telecommunications, utilities, or civil infrastructure projects is advantageous but not required
