Company Description

Alaro is an AI-native law firm founded in 2025 by veterans of Paul Hastings and Monzo. Backed by tier-one VCs and SRA-regulated, Alaro has already processed $5bn of legal work for elite real estate developers and international banks. They combine top-tier legal expertise with proprietary technology to automate high-volume, process-heavy workflows end to end.

Job Description

As a Lawyer at Alaro, you will sit at the intersection of high-stakes execution and legal automation. You’ll handle complex client matters to top-firm standards while simultaneously codifying workflows into precise steps plans for AI agents to execute. This is an opportunity to automate yourself out of repetitive tasks and build the future of legal services.

Location

New York, USA or London, UK

Why this role is remarkable


Work at an AI-native firm that has already processed $5bn of legal work for elite real estate developers and international banks.
Founding team includes experts from Paul Hastings and Monzo, backed by top-tier VCs to redefine traditional legal delivery.
No timesheets or billable targets—Alaro prioritizes output and automation over the traditional law firm grind.


What You Will Do


Execute high-volume client legal work end-to-end to the standard of an elite global law firm.
Codify legal workflows into precise, repeatable steps plans that allow proprietary AI agents to take over manual tasks.
Collaborate directly with engineering teams to monitor agent output and refine the feedback loop for product development.


The ideal candidate


Qualified lawyer with 3-8 years of PQE at a top-tier firm like Paul Hastings, Kirkland & Ellis, or the Magic Circle.
Proven transactional background with a process-obsessed mindset and a natural drive toward legal automation.
Commercially pragmatic professional who can pick up unfamiliar matter types and translate them into scalable, automated processes.
