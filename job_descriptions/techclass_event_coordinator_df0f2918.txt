About TechClass

TechClass is a leading eLearning provider, dedicated to delivering high-quality, engaging, and accessible educational content to learners worldwide. Our mission is to empower individuals and organizations to achieve their learning and development goals through our innovative and user-friendly platform.

Job Overview

We are looking for a dynamic and highly organized Event Coordinator to join our team. You will be the driving force behind our live webinars, in-person events, and expert content sessions. In this multifaceted role, you will source industry experts, manage the setup and coordination of their sessions in our in-house recording studio, and manage our social media channels to maximize our reach, build our community, and promote our educational content.




Key Responsibilities

Event & Webinar Management: Plan, organize, and seamlessly execute in-person events and live online webinars from start to finish.
Expert Outreach & Coordination: Research, contact, and build relationships with industry experts, inviting them to participate in our events and educational content.
Studio Setup & Coordination: Schedule recording sessions and take hands-on responsibility for setting up the physical studio space (arranging the set, ensuring a tidy and professional environment) to guarantee a seamless and welcoming experience for our guest speakers.
Social Media Management: Transform the insights and content gathered from webinars and studio sessions into engaging social media campaigns, managing our channels to build an active online presence.




Requirements

Event Experience: Proven experience organizing, hosting, and managing logistics for both virtual (webinars) and in-person events.
Social Media Savvy: Experience managing corporate or professional social media accounts, with an understanding of how to build digital communities and promote events online.
Hands-on Approach: A proactive, "roll-up-your-sleeves" mentality, comfortable with the physical preparation and setup of event and studio spaces.
Communication Skills: Excellent interpersonal skills with the confidence to pitch, negotiate, and communicate effectively with high-level industry experts and guest speakers.
Organizational Skills: Exceptional attention to detail and the ability to manage multiple event timelines and expert schedules simultaneously.
Language: High proficiency in English, with excellent written and verbal communication skills. Proficiency in Finnish is considered an optional advantage.
