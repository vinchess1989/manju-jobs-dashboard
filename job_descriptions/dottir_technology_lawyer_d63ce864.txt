Head of People & Culture at Dottir Attorneys

Dottir is a modern business law firm for future-oriented organisations. We were founded ten years ago with a focus on technology companies – and while we've grown well beyond that since, tech is still where our deepest bench sits. Our top-ranked Technology team advises companies through technology transactions, from commercial agreements, licensing and complex outsourcing arrangements to business-critical IT system procurement. They cover the regulatory side too: AI, data, privacy, data protection, cyber security and all other aspects of digital regulation.




Now, we are looking for an experienced technology lawyer to join the team.




What you bring:




At least 5 years of post-graduation experience in a law firm or as an in-house lawyer is a must. We want someone who can move across the whole field – from transaction support, contract drafting and negotiations to regulatory work.
Rock-solid experience drafting and negotiating technology contracts – and commercial contracts generally
Strong command of the regulatory landscape around emerging technologies
Not a stranger to IT outsourcing or business-critical IT system projects, whether on the customer or supplier side, in the private or public sector
You're a mentor at heart – or ready to become one. You make time for junior colleagues: reviewing their work, answering their questions, helping them to find their feet
Excellent spoken and written Finnish and English




What’s in it for you:




You'll get your hands on interesting cases, clients building things their industries haven’t seen yet, and responsibility from day one – plus a real say in where the practice goes next
Team members that win awards but work as a unit – here people support each other and split the credit. We are serious about the work, not too serious about ourselves.
We're open to new ideas, other points of view, and pushback. We argue for a living – so here, naturally, the best argument wins.
Competitive salary, benefits and performance-based incentives, plus flexible hybrid working
We will support you with networks, courses, conferences, and events you choose. Your development, your call.
