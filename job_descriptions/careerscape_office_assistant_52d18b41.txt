Careerscape is recruiting on behalf of our client for a motivated entry-level Office Assistant to support daily administrative operations in a fully remote capacity. This is a great opportunity for someone early in their career to build a strong foundation in office administration while working with a supportive, established team.




Day to day, you will juggle scheduling, correspondence, data entry, and light project support, moving between tasks as priorities shift. People who thrive in this role are organized, detail-oriented, and comfortable communicating professionally over email, chat, and phone without needing constant oversight.




COMPENSATION

The salary range for this role is $65,000 - $85,000 per year, based on experience and qualifications.




Additional compensation and benefits details will be shared during the hiring process.




Responsibilities

Manage and respond to incoming emails and phone calls in a timely, professional manner
Schedule meetings, appointments, and coordinate calendars across teams
Maintain and organize digital files, records, and databases
Enter and update data accurately across internal systems
Prepare correspondence, memos, reports, and basic presentations
Order and track office supplies and vendor invoices
Assist with onboarding new hires and coordinating remote team logistics
Support basic bookkeeping tasks such as expense tracking
Coordinate travel arrangements and expense reports as needed
Provide general administrative support to managers and team members




Requirements

High school diploma or equivalent required, associate degree preferred
Strong written and verbal communication skills
Proficiency with Microsoft Office and Google Workspace
Ability to manage multiple tasks and prioritize effectively
Excellent organizational skills and attention to detail
Reliable internet connection and dedicated home workspace
Comfortable learning new software and virtual collaboration tools
Prior office, internship, or customer service experience a plus
Self-motivated with strong time management in a remote setting




Benefits

Medical, dental, and vision insurance
401k retirement plan with company match
Paid time off and paid holidays
Remote-friendly stipend for home office setup
Flexible scheduling within core business hours
Paid training and onboarding support
Opportunities for growth and professional development
Employee wellness resources
Collaborative, supportive team culture
Annual performance reviews with merit increases
