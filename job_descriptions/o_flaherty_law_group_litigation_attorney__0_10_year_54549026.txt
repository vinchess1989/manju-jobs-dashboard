Job Description

Remote

Springfield, IL

$75K - $150K/yr

We are a growing, multi-state law firm seeking an Attorney (0–10 years experience) to handle family law and/or civil litigation matters in Central Illinois (Peoria, Bloomington, Champaign, Springfield).

This is a primarily remote position, with in-person court appearances (typically 2–3 times per week).

We are open to both experienced attorneys and junior attorneys seeking mentorship and hands-on experience.

Why This Role Stands Out


Set your own billable target — control your workload and income
Consistent case flow — no requirement to generate business
Remote flexibility with local courtroom practice
Strong mentorship and support
Full support staff


What You’ll Do


Handle a caseload in family law and/or civil litigation
Attend court locally (approx. 2–3 times per week)
Manage cases with administrative and legal support
Grow into increased independence over time


We Are Seeking

We are open to candidates at different experience levels:

Experienced Attorneys (3+ years):


Background in family law and/or civil litigation
Ability to manage cases with increasing independence


Junior Attorneys (0–3 Years)


Licensed or awaiting bar results
Interest in litigation and courtroom experience
Willingness to learn and grow with mentorship
