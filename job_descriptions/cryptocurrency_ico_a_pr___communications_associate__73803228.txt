Core Tasks


Media Relations & Pitching
Pitch CMC products, articles, research and thought leadership to journalists
Secure CMC quotes/commentary in news pieces, especially around market, macro and crypto trends
Position CMC data as a go-to source for reporters (price action, market caps, trend data)
Track macro/market news daily to spot reactive ("newsjacking") opportunities
Build and maintain media lists and a contacts database/CRM
Events & Speaking
Identify and pitch CMC spokespeople for speaker slots at conferences and side events
Track relevant event calendars and submission deadlines
Support logistics and briefing for confirmed speaking engagements
Podcasts, TV & Online Presence
Pitch CMC for podcast appearances, TV interviews and online features
Target tier-1 (Bloomberg, CNBC), crypto-native (CoinDesk, Decrypt) and independent shows
Build relationships with bookers/producers
Other tasks
Press releases & announcements — drafting/editing releases, managing distribution, embargoes, and the press inbox/inbound inquiries
Data storytelling — turning CMC's proprietary data into pitchable stories, reports and data points (a big differentiator for a data company)
Coverage tracking & reporting — monitoring mentions, building clip reports, share-of-voice, and basic KPI reporting
Spokesperson support — prepping talking points, briefing docs, Q&A prep
Awards & rankings — identifying and submitting nominations
Cross-team coordination — working with marketing, social, research and product; amplifying earned media on social
Crisis/reactive comms support — assisting senior staff on sensitive/breaking issues


Requirements


~1–2 years in PR, comms, journalism or media (agency or in-house)
Strong written English; able to draft pitches and releases quickly
Genuine interest in crypto/Web3, fintech and markets
Comfortable tracking news and working at the pace of a 24/7 market
Organized, proactive, able to juggle multiple pitches/deadlines
Nice-to-have: existing media/podcast contacts, additional languages, data literacy
Remote working
