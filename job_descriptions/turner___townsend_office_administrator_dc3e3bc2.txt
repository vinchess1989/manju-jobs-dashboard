Job Description



Purpose of the Role:

The Office Administrator will oversee office administration and support Business Units in Helsinki, Finland. This role requires a highly organized, detail-oriented individual capable of multitasking in a fast-paced environment.



Key Responsibilities



Provide administrative support to the Operations Manager.
Assist with onboarding new employees, including preparing documents and coordinating inductions.
Order and manage office supplies and equipment.
Coordinate IT equipment requests and setup for new starters.
Support office events, team activities, and internal communications.
Coordinate with suppliers and service providers.
Arrange travel bookings and logistics when required.
Maintain office documentation, templates, and filing systems.
Coordinate courier shipments, deliveries, and incoming mail.
Provide administrative support for company-wide initiatives and activities.
Handle general office enquiries and provide support to employees.
Assist with day-to-day operational and administrative tasks as assigned by the Operations Manager.
Onboarding of all new employees.


Qualifications



Requirements:



Fluent Finnish speaker (essential)
Highly organized and detail-oriented
Strong knowledge of the full Microsoft suite (Excel, Word etc)
Ability to multitask in a fast-paced environment
Strong administrative skills

Join our team and contribute to the smooth running of our Helsinki office while supporting various business units. Apply now to be part of a dynamic and supportive environment!
