Do you want to join CTS as Project Coordinator/Data Analyst?

About The Role

This position is based on the construction site in Seinäjoki. You will be responsible for ensuring that digital tools, project information, and reporting systems are actively used and maintained on site—not just set up, but actually working in practice.

You will work closely with site management, engineers, and discipline leads, helping translate on-site activities into structured data and clear project insight. Your role connects what is happening in the field with the digital systems used for coordination, tracking, and reporting.

You will also contribute to improving project workflows, data quality, and reporting processes, making sure information is consistent, reliable, and ready for decision-making.

What you will do


Coordinate and follow up on weekly and monthly reporting inputs from different site disciplines
Ensure project data is updated, complete, and submitted within agreed deadlines
Create and maintain structured trackers and SharePoint lists for reporting topics such as progress, actions, risks, and quality
Check and validate incoming data for accuracy, logic, structure, and completeness
Prepare clean and organized datasets for dashboards and client reporting
Act as the on-site point of contact for questions related to digital workflows and reporting updates
Support site teams in accessing drawings, models, and project information through digital platforms
Attend coordination meetings, capture action items, and ensure follow-up is tracked and closed
Document reporting routines and continuously improve templates, checklists, and update rules


Who this is for

This role suits someone who wants to work close to construction and take ownership of how project information is coordinated, controlled, and reported. You enjoy bringing order to complex environments and making sure everyone works from the same, up-to-date information.

You likely


Have a background or early experience in project coordination, data analysis, engineering support, reporting, or digital construction
Are comfortable working with structured data in tools like SharePoint, Excel and Power BI
Understand the importance of data quality and consistency in project reporting
Are proactive with follow-ups and not afraid to remind stakeholders about missing input
Communicate clearly with site teams and engineers, even when explaining structured requirements
Pay close attention to detail and take responsibility for the quality of your work
Are motivated to learn project systems and improve how things are done in practice


What you get


A key role on an active construction site with direct impact on project control and reporting
Early responsibility and ownership of critical coordination and data processes
Daily collaboration with site management, engineers, and construction teams
Practical experience with digital project execution and structured reporting
A strong learning environment within digital construction and data-driven project work
Experience from a major construction project in Finland


Location: Seinäjoki, Norway. Site-based role with daily presence on site required.
