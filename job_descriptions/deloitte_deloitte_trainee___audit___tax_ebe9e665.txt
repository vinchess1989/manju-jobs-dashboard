Why Deloitte?




💚 As a Trainee, you will have a meaningful impact on the development of the industry and your career path according to your own interests and strengths.




💚 You will be part of a supportive and inclusive work environment where your people leader, buddy, and experienced colleagues guide and inspire you.




💚 Our flexible working conditions allow you to work in a hybrid model at our office, client sites, and remotely.




What do we hope for from you?




A recent master’s degree or ongoing master studies
Strong drive to deliver results and enthusiasm for teamwork
Fluency in English (Finnish is a plus but not required for all roles)
Previous relevant work experience is a bonus, but motivation and eagerness to learn matter most




Please note that, to be considered for our Trainee positions, you must currently have the legal right to work in Finland, including holding a valid residence permit if required.




Application process




Apply by Sunday, 13 September 2026, by submitting your CV, cover letter, and transcript of records or degree certificate via our recruitment system. Interviews are preliminarily scheduled take place during Weeks 39-40, but we may start interviewing earlier and look forward to receiving your application soon.
