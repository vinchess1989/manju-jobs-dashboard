Would you like to combine HR, people support and compliance coordination in an international project environment?




We are currently looking for a Regional Compliance & People Coordinator to join FiveNines Group in Finland.




FiveNines Group is an international data centre infrastructure company delivering complex, mission-critical projects across Europe. With a strong focus on safety, quality, sustainability and innovation, the company combines deep industry expertise with a collaborative approach to deliver world-class infrastructure projects. As a growing organisation, FiveNines offers an opportunity to work alongside industry experts and contribute to projects that help power the digital world.




As a Regional Compliance & People Coordinator, you will play a key role in supporting both project teams and central services by ensuring that employees, subcontractors and visitors are mobilised efficiently, compliant with local requirements and well supported throughout the project lifecycle. This is a varied and hands-on role combining compliance coordination, HR administration and employee support in an international environment. You'll be part of an international business environment, with the opportunity to contribute both globally and, in the future, from Fivenine's Loviisa-based operations.

You will coordinate onboarding and mobilisation activities for employees and subcontractors coming to Finland, support work permit and right-to-work processes, organise accommodation and local logistics, and help ensure compliance with Finnish employment legislation and site access requirements. In addition, you will support HR administration, employee onboarding, mandatory training and day-to-day employee queries while acting as an important link between local teams, international colleagues and external stakeholders.




To succeed in this role, you have previous experience in HR, administration, compliance coordination or a similar support function. Experience within construction, engineering, infrastructure or international project environments is considered an advantage. You are comfortable managing multiple priorities, coordinating different stakeholders and ensuring that processes run smoothly.

We are looking for an organised and proactive professional with strong communication skills and a service-minded approach. You are fluent in both Finnish and English, enjoy working with people and are able to handle sensitive information with professionalism and discretion. Attention to detail, flexibility and strong problem-solving skills will help you succeed in this role. We are looking for someone with good Finnish and English language skills.




At FiveNines Group, you will become part of an ambitious and highly skilled team that values capability, curiosity, collaboration, compliance and commitment. The company combines the agility of a growing business with the expertise and experience needed to deliver large-scale mission-critical projects. You will have the opportunity to develop your career in an international environment while making a meaningful impact on employee experience, compliance and project success.




Submit your application or contact Eilakaisla’s Recruitment Consultant Josefiina Kuusiniemi for further information. Josefiina Kuusiniemi can be reached by phone at +358 50 409 5955 on August 21st at 13-15 and August 26th at 14-16 or by email at josefiina.kuusiniemi@eilakaisla.fi.
