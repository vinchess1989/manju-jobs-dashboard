About The Company

Milliman is a global leader in actuarial, consulting, and technology services, with a history spanning over 75 years. Renowned for delivering innovative solutions to complex business challenges, Milliman serves clients across diverse industries including healthcare, insurance, financial services, and employee benefits. The company is committed to fostering a culture of continuous learning, diversity, and social responsibility. With a focus on integrity, excellence, and client-centricity, Milliman invests heavily in employee development through training programs, mentoring, and employee resource groups. Its Indianapolis office is home to a vibrant team of professionals dedicated to providing expert advice and strategic insights, particularly within the life sciences and healthcare sectors.

About The Role

We are seeking an experienced Executive Assistant to join Milliman's Indianapolis Health practice, supporting our Life Science leadership team. This pivotal role involves providing high-impact administrative and operational support to ensure the smooth functioning of the practice. The ideal candidate will demonstrate exceptional organizational skills, discretion, and a proactive approach to managing complex calendars, travel arrangements, and administrative workflows. You will serve as a key liaison between leaders, clients, and internal teams, facilitating effective communication and coordination. Your responsibilities will include managing schedules across multiple time zones, preparing expense reports, supporting onboarding processes, and coordinating meetings and events. This role offers an excellent opportunity to work in a dynamic environment where your organizational expertise and service orientation will directly contribute to the success of our client projects and internal operations.

Qualifications


Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Office Management, or a related field
Demonstrated experience supporting senior leaders or executive teams, preferably within a professional services, consulting, or healthcare environment
Proficiency in Microsoft 365 suite (Outlook, Teams, Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
Experience coordinating complex travel arrangements and managing expenses
Strong calendar management skills, including scheduling across multiple time zones
Excellent written and verbal communication abilities
Highly organized with keen attention to detail and the ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously
Ability to handle sensitive information with discretion and confidentiality
Experience supporting meetings and events logistics, including vendor coordination and materials preparation
Comfort with adopting new digital tools and improving administrative processes
Basic understanding of AI systems and their application in enhancing productivity


Responsibilities


Partner with practice leaders and team members to anticipate needs and streamline administrative workflows
Manage and proactively resolve calendar conflicts, scheduling internal and client meetings efficiently
Coordinate travel logistics for individual and group trips, including itineraries and last-minute changes
Prepare, submit, and track expense reports and support invoice processing as needed
Assist with onboarding new hires by coordinating schedules, sharing key information, and connecting team members to resources
Support client onboarding processes, including system setup, information collection, and reporting
Plan and coordinate team meetings and events, handling logistics such as invites, venue arrangements, catering, and materials distribution
Provide administrative support for annual reviews, role evaluations, and leadership meetings
Manage and triage communications for leaders, ensuring priority requests are addressed promptly
Contribute to continuous improvement of administrative processes and adopt new tools to enhance efficiency


Benefits


Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision insurance coverage for employees, dependents, and domestic partners
Employee Assistance Program (EAP) offering confidential support services
401(k) retirement plan with company matching and profit-sharing contributions
Discretionary bonus program recognizing employee achievements
Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA) for dependent care, transportation, and medical expenses
Paid Time Off (PTO) starting from the first day of employment, with accrual based on full-time or part-time status
Observed holidays, with a minimum of 10 per year
Family Building Benefits, including adoption and fertility assistance
Paid parental leave of up to 12 weeks for eligible employees
Life insurance and accidental death & dismemberment (AD&D) coverage, fully paid by Milliman
Short-term and long-term disability insurance, fully paid by the company
