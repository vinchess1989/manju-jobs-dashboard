VaasaETT is a data-driven market analytics and consultancy business, specializing in complex end-user issues in energy markets across six continents and over 90 jurisdictions worldwide. We serve a global client base of energy companies, governments, regulators, innovators, investors, and enterprise consumers. Our insight is based on the most advanced data science, market-leading data sets, outstanding experience, and an unrivalled network of sources developed over more than two decades. VaasaETT assists clients with market assessment and entry, innovative development, and M&A, and carries out public research projects. Our goal is to support our clients in their efforts to transform and develop for the benefit of consumers, society, and sustainability. VaasaETT is headquartered in Helsinki, Finland, with additional offices in Greece and key partners across EMEA, APAC, and the Americas.




See www.vaasaett.com for more information.

        

As our team keeps growing, we are looking to hire a Marketing Lead - Energy Markets.




In this role, you will get to work with international clientele and contacts. You will plan, coordinate, and execute marketing campaigns across channels (LinkedIn, email, website, webinars, sponsorships, events, etc.) to increase brand awareness and lead generation. You will be in contact with data and energy analysts to produce insightful content across main channels. You will be leading a growing team toward success and growing client base. 




If this sounds like something you would enjoy, keep reading!




The role will include:




Campaign Strategy, Coordination and Execution: Leading and executing B2B marketing campaigns, coordinating with internal analysts’ team for marketing content.

 

Events’ planning, coordination and Execution: Identifying and evaluating events, sponsorships and webinars opportunities to enhance brand awareness and leads’ opportunities.




Identifying and evaluating B2B marketing partnerships: Identifying and evaluating partnerships and opportunities with marketing companies, events’ organizers and industry associations. 




Website & Social Media Ownership: Scheduling and organizing social media posting (primarily LinkedIn), managing the company’s website, driving website and social media upgrades for increasing brand awareness.




Reporting Marketing & KPIs Performance: Reporting internally and to management marketing campaigns, events, webinars and sponsorship results through short reports or slides. Assessing quarterly marketing KPIs’ achievements, originally set by the management.




Coordinating internal marketing training sessions: Educating the analysts’ team in marketing and posting, relevant for marketing campaigns across social media.




Review and standardize deliverables’ format: Review and standardize deliverables’ format and graphics. The main deliverables consist on Power Points, Excel files and report deliverables.




We require you to have:




2+ years of experience in a B2B marketing position – ideally in the energy field.
Interest in building a career in marketing within the energy sector.
Natural inclination for design, messaging, and storytelling – and enjoy shaping content that’s both visually appealing and aligned with strategy.
Clear understanding what makes good B2B content work and are comfortable helping to create and coordinate formats like blog posts, LinkedIn content, or case studies.
Excellent communication and presentation skills in English.
Interest in a leadership and coordinating role.




We value:

 

Curiosity, focused and proactive attitude
Strong adaptation and multitasking skills
Strong interpersonal skills
Interest in joining a growing and fast-paced environment
Previous experience in consultancy or industry sectors
Additional language skills
