WHO WE ARE

Combient Foundry is one of Europe's leading venture service providers, working with top industrial corporations to engage with the world's most ambitious startups through a proven venture-client model. Backed by a strong industrial network and partnered with AWS, NVIDIA and EQT Ventures, we're at a pivotal stage of growth.

Europe's industrial strength lies in collaboration. In a world where speed and scale decide who wins, no single company — however large — can innovate fast enough alone. We help Europe's leading corporations work together, learn from each other, and partner with the startups shaping tomorrow.

Our international team combines expertise in corporate innovation, venture clienting, technology and business development. We're ambitious, quality-driven and genuinely passionate about impact. We take the work seriously — not ourselves.




YOUR ROLE

As a Venture Associate, you'll support venture-client projects for some of Europe's leading industrial companies — working alongside experienced team members across the full cycle: identifying business and technical needs, scouting and evaluating startups, and helping prepare pilots that deliver measurable results.

This is a hands-on entry point into corporate innovation and venture clienting — for someone who wants to understand complex industrial challenges, work with emerging technologies, and turn ideas into concrete action. Based in Helsinki, Stockholm or Munich, you'll work closely with colleagues and clients across the Nordics, DACH and the wider European ecosystem.




YOUR IMPACT

Support venture-client projects from needs identification through startup scouting, evaluation and pilot preparation
Help translate industrial and technical challenges into clear startup search and evaluation criteria
Research emerging technologies, startups and market developments
Support startup evaluation together with corporate stakeholders and subject-matter experts
Coordinate with stakeholders across business units, procurement, legal and IT
Prepare workshops, decision materials and project documentation
Support pilot definition — objectives, success criteria, timelines, next steps
Collaborate with colleagues across regions to share learnings, tools and best practices




WHAT YOU BRING

University degree, ideally with technology-oriented background — engineering, applied sciences, industrial technology or technical innovation are a plus.
Experience in consulting, project management, corporate innovation or startup ecosystems is a plus — not a requirement.
Passion for emerging technologies, industrial innovation or corporate–startup collaboration
Strong analytical skills and structured thinking
Clear, confident communication in English
Performance and ownership mindset, willingness to take initiative to make our clients happy
Comfort working in an international, entrepreneurial environment




WHY JOIN US

Create tangible impact with Europe's leading industrial companies and most ambitious startups
Work with emerging technologies across a wide range of industries
Join an international team spanning the Nordics and DACH - and beyond
Combine startup agility with the stability and access of an industry-backed platform
Take real responsibility early and grow into broader project ownership
Learn directly from experienced corporate innovation and venture-client professionals




OUR COMMITTMENT TO INCLUSION

We believe diverse teams build better things. We're committed to an inclusive environment where people of all backgrounds, identities and experiences feel welcome and valued, and we actively work to ensure our hiring process is fair and accessible to all.
