Title: IFRS 9 Experts – Talent Pool (Finland)

Location: Helsinki or Oulu, Finland (50% Remote)

Contract Period: 01 Oct 2026 – 01 Oct 2027 (Future Assignment Pipeline)

Seniority: Junior to Senior Level

Languages: English (Proficient)




Recann is acting as an Employment Business in relation to this vacancy.




About the Role:




We are proactively building a network of IFRS 9 Specialists in Finland to support anticipated client demands within the banking sector. Qualified candidates will join our talent pool to be directly matched with upcoming consulting and project assignments as needs arise.




Key Focus Areas:




IFRS 9 implementation, compliance, and financial reporting disclosures.
Expected Credit Loss (ECL) methodology, credit risk modeling, and impairment calculations.
IFRS 9 data analysis, model validation, development, and governance.
Alignment with relevant banking regulatory requirements.




Requirements:




Hands-on experience within financial services, banking, or credit risk management.
Practical exposure to IFRS 9 framework standards.
Fluency in English.
Based in or able to work in Finland (Helsinki/Oulu with 50% remote flexibility).
