Who We Are

Lenox Advisors brings together sophisticated solutions in wealth management and insurance services to high-net-worth individuals and their families. As a wholly owned subsidiary of NFP Corp., an Aon Company, we combine the culture of a boutique firm with the stability and backing of a large corporation to create an environment that's truly unique. NFP is a multiple Best Places to Work award winner in Business Insurance. We’re a recognized Elite Agency award winner and a leading property and casualty broker, benefits consultant, wealth manager, and retirement plan advisor. NFP provides solutions enabling client success through the expertise of over 8,000 global employees, investments in innovative technologies, and enduring relationships with highly rated insurers, vendors and financial institutions. To learn more, please visit: https://www.NFP.com.

Position Summary & Overall Purpose

Lenox Advisors is seeking a highly organized and detail-oriented Event Coordinator who is passionate about creating exceptional experiences and seeing complex events successfully come to life. This role combines independent ownership with a highly collaborative, team-focused environment. The ideal candidate is someone who can gather input from collaborators, conduct research and site visits, develop creative event concepts, and confidently present recommendations for feedback and approval. This is an excellent opportunity for an experienced event professional who is ready to take the next step in their career and fully lead the planning and execution of impactful events that support business growth, client engagement, and organizational objectives.

This hybrid role will be based in either Stamford, CT or New York, NY and requires regular travel between both office locations, as key leaders and event stakeholders are based in each office. The standard schedule is at least three days per week in the office, with willingness to support events on site outside of business hours.

Key Responsibilities

Event Planning & Execution


Coordinate end-to-end planning of events including conferences training sessions, corporate meetings, and dinners.
Develop timelines, run-of-show documents, and detailed logistical plans for each event
Lead on-site execution including setup, vendor coordination, and issue resolution to ensure excellent delivery


Vendor & Venue Management


Source, evaluate, and manage relationships with venues and vendors (AV, catering, travel, etc.)
Negotiate contracts in conjunction with manager and COO and ensure all services align with budget and event objectives
Coordinate site visits and oversee logistics such as audiovisual needs and space planning in coordination with Lenox IT and the EA team.


Registration & Attendee Management


Manage event registration, RSVP tracking, and attendee communications together with the marketing team
Prepare registration reports and maintain accurate participant data (e.g., tracking RSVPs and headcount for events)
Coordinate event communications (invitations, registration follow ups, “Know Before You Go” emails, etc.)


Stakeholder Collaboration


Partner with executive leadership, executive assistants, event host, marketing, and external partners to align on event goals and messaging
Serve as a central point of contact across internal teams and vendors
Collaborate with marketing on event materials, branding, and communications


Budget & Reporting


Support event budget creation and track expenses to ensure cost efficiency
Process invoices and vendor payments
Deliver post-event reporting including attendance, feedback, and recommendations for improvement


On-Site Operations


Oversee onsite execution including registration, vendor coordination, and troubleshooting and coordinate with the event management vendor if one is hired
Ensure all event elements run according to plan and meet Lenox quality standards
Provide a high-touch, professional experience for advisors, clients, and partners
Travel in the NYC & tri-state area required approximately 25% of the time
Events will sometimes fall outside of normal business hours


Qualifications


5+ years of experience in event coordination, preferably in corporate, financial services, or professional services environments
Proven experience managing multiple events simultaneously with strong attention to detail
Strong organizational, communication, and problem-solving skills
Experience working with executive leadership, senior stakeholders and external partners
Proficiency with Microsoft Office and event management tools/platforms
Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Communications, Hospitality, or related field preferred


Key Competencies


Ability to manage multiple priorities and deadlines in a fast-paced environment
Strong client-service mindset and professionalism
Excellent interpersonal and communication skills
High attention to detail and execution excellence
Adaptability and ability to troubleshoot in real time
