Käytämme evästeitä tarjoamamme sisällön ja mainosten räätälöimiseen, sosiaalisen median ominaisuuksien tukemiseen ja kävijämäärämme analysoimiseen. Lisäksi jaamme sosiaalisen median, mainosalan ja analytiikka-alan kumppaneillemme tietoja siitä, miten käytät sivustoamme. Kumppanimme voivat yhdistää näitä tietoja muihin tietoihin, joita olet antanut heille tai joita on kerätty, kun olet käyttänyt heidän palvelujaan.

We are looking for IFRS Experts (9) – Talent Pool (Finland).

We are proactively building a network of IFRS 9 professionals in Finland to support anticipated future hiring needs within the banking sector. While there is no active assignment at this time, we expect demand for IFRS 9 expertise in upcoming client projects and are looking to connect with qualified professionals interested in future consulting opportunities.

Objective:
Identify and engage experienced IFRS 9 professionals for future consulting and project assignments. Qualified candidates will be assessed and added to our talent pool, allowing us to match them with relevant opportunities as client demand arises.

Target Profile:
We are looking for professionals with experience in one or more of the following areas:

IFRS 9 implementation and compliance
Expected Credit Loss (ECL) methodology
Credit risk modelling and impairment calculations
IFRS 9 financial reporting and disclosures
IFRS 9 data analysis, validation, and reporting
Regulatory requirements related to IFRS 9
Model development, validation, or governance (where applicable)

Location: Finland (Mandatory)
Seniority level: Junior-Senior

We offer you: We offer a versatile assignment where every day is different and interesting. We also offer a very competitive salary and good employee benefits.

Are you interested in becoming a part of our dynamic team? The position will be filled as soon as a suitable person is found, so please be quick and submit your application!
