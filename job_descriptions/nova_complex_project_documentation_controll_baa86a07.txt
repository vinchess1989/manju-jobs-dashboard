About Nova Complex

Established in 2023, Nova Complex (“Nova”) is built on a foundation of deep industry expertise and a clear mission: to catalyse the development of next-generation digital infrastructure across Finland and beyond. We are committed to accelerating the energy transition by delivering high-value, sustainable solutions that meet the evolving needs of global hyperscale customers.

As a full-stack, vertically integrated platform, Nova combines local insight with vast experience in designing tailored infrastructure that optimizes both performance and efficiency. We are focused on markets with a strong growth trajectory, ensuring that our projects contribute positively to the local economy and the future of digital connectivity. At Nova, we don’t just manage infrastructure; we build the backbone of a sustainable digital future.







About the Role

We are looking for an organized and reliable Document Controller for a site-based, fixed-term position (12–18 months). Working alongside the QA/QC Manager and site engineers, you will manage the flow of technical information and maintain strict version control across all drawings, reports, and certificates. This is a practical, essential role focused on supporting technical coordination among contractors and consultants, ensuring that all project records remain complete, structured, and easily accessible throughout the construction lifecycle.




What You’ll Be Doing With Us

Document Lifecycle Management: Manage the intake, review, distribution, and archiving of all project documents, including drawings, specifications, RFIs, and change orders.
Database Administration: Maintain the project’s document management system (CDE), ensuring accurate filing and version control for both electronic and physical records.
Quality & Consistency Checks: Review incoming documents to ensure they meet project naming conventions, standards, and Finnish regulatory formats.
Controlled Distribution: Coordinate the timely release of information to internal teams and contractors, ensuring everyone is working from the latest approved revisions.
Records & Auditing: Maintain accurate transmittal logs and conduct regular audits to ensure no documentation is missing or outdated.
Handover Support: Assist in collating the final Operations & Maintenance (O&M) manuals and "As-Built" packages to ensure a smooth transition at the end of the project.
Team Coordination: Act as the point of contact for contractors and consultants regarding document status and submission deadlines.




Qualifications & Experience

Project Experience: 3–5 years of experience in document control, ideally within the construction, energy, or industrial sectors.
Technical Literacy: A basic understanding of construction documents (drawings, specs, etc.) and their role in a large-scale project.
Software Skills: Proficiency in document management tools (e.g., Aconex, Procore, SharePoint, or BIM 360) and Microsoft Office.
Detail-Oriented: A methodical approach to work, with a focus on accuracy and consistency in managing large volumes of data.
Communication: Strong verbal and written communication skills in English. Proficiency in Finnish is a significant advantage for handling local compliance records.
Reliability: The ability to work independently and manage multiple tasks while maintaining high standards of confidentiality.




Why Join Nova Complex?

Career Evolution: This role provides an unparalleled view into the technical makeup of a hyperscale data center. We actively look to retain our top talent; this position provides a natural pathway into our permanent Operations and Asset Management teams as the project matures.
Ownership: Take responsibility for the project's information structure in a culture that values professional autonomy and direct communication.
Stable Growth: Work in a fast-scaling, expert-driven environment that is building the backbone of Finland's digital future.
