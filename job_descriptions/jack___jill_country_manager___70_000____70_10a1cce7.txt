Job Title

Country Manager

Salary

€70,000 + €70,000 Bonus

Company Description

Realadvisor.com is a high-growth PropTech scale-up headquartered in Geneva with €17M in revenue, dedicated to bringing transparency to the €500B+ European real estate market. Operating across four countries with over 1 million monthly visitors, they provide homeowners with data-driven insights while empowering over 5,000 real estate agencies through smart technology.

Job Description

As Country Manager for Italy, you will act as the local CEO, taking full ownership of the P&L and a 15-person team. You’ll scale a market with €1.8M ARR, leveraging a proven playbook to grow the team to 50-60 FTEs while ensuring high-volume sales success across the Italian landscape.

Location

Remote, Europe

Why this role is remarkable


Full P&L ownership of the Italian market, operating as a "local CEO" with the autonomy to build and scale your own high-performance commercial team.
Join a PropTech scale-up with €17M revenue and 28% YoY growth, disrupting a €500B+ market with a framework already proven successful in three other countries.
Scalability-focused role where you will grow an existing 15-person team to 60+ FTEs, directly impacting the company’s expansion and market leadership in Italy.


What You Will Do


Manage the full commercial lifecycle for Italy, overseeing revenue targets, forecasting, and the end-to-end performance of SDR, BDR, and CSM teams.
Execute the local growth strategy by hiring, training, and coaching a high-volume sales force to scale from 15 to 60 team members.
Optimize sales processes and unit economics to maintain CM3 profitability while aggressively expanding the Italian client base of over 400 real estate agencies.


The ideal candidate


4-6+ years of experience in SaaS or digital tech sales, specifically focusing on high-volume, low-ticket SMB markets rather than enterprise-level deals.
Proven track record of recruiting and coaching revenue teams and managing a P&L, ideally within a fast-paced startup or scale-up environment.
Native-level fluency and deep cultural or professional experience in Italy, with the ability to lead a fully remote team based anywhere in Europe.
