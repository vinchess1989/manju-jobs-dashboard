Job Description



As a valued member of our café team, you’ll work in a warm and welcoming Nordic-inspired environment, seamlessly integrated within our store.



Your focus will always be on delivering exceptional quality, outstanding service, and memorable experiences for every guest. With a natural ability to put the customer first and adapt to their needs, you’ll help create a personalised and inspiring café atmosphere rooted in local culture and hospitality.



You’ll have the opportunity to provide high-level service at every touchpoint—from warmly greeting guests to attentively meeting their needs in a genuine and thoughtful way. You’ll be responsible for preparing and serving high-quality food and beverages, consistently upholding our standards of excellence.



In this role, you’ll also play a key part in promoting the café’s values, culture, and concept, while contributing to the smooth daily operations of the in-store café.



Previous experience in food and beverage or customer service is a plus—but not essential. What matters most is your passion for hospitality, your willingness to learn, and your commitment to creating great guest experiences.





Qualifications


Customer-focused: You enjoy engaging with people and are committed to delivering excellent service.
Adaptable & flexible: Adaptable and responsive to changing priorities and customer requests.
Action-orientated: Proactive and driven to take initiative and get things done with quality.
Good communication skills: You have a genuine interest in people, communicate clearly, and actively listen to understand their perspectives and needs.
A genuine passion for food, beverages, and the ARKET brand, with a strong desire to share that enthusiasm and knowledge with customers.
Schedule flexibility: Willing and able to work varied hours, including evenings and weekends.


Additional Information



This is a part-time position with a contract of 10 hours a week, starting as soon as possible.



Apply by sending in your CV in English as soon as possible. Due to data policies, we only accept applications through the career page.
