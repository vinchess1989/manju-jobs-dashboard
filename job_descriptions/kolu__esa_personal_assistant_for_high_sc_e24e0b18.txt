Työn kuvaus

The Personal Assistant provides physical assistance to enable mobility challenged male student to participate in studies and community activities. The position is based in Espoo/Helsinki and averages 40 working hours per week on normal school weeks. Working hours are agreed according to student's individual needs between typically between 7:30 - 17:00. Work will start at 12.8.2026 till 5.6.2027 according to school term.

(School term dates and holidays | City of Helsinki / "General upper secondary schools" 2026-2027 : https://www.hel.fi/en/childhood-and-education/school-term-dates-and-holidays )

Main Duties: The Personal Assistant's responsibilities include, but are not limited to:

Assisting the student when traveling and getting into and out of taxis and other means of transport.
Assisting with wheelchair mobility both indoors and outdoors.
Providing practical assistance with daily activities related to school and studies, including moving between locations, handling personal belongings, and other tasks as needed.
Assisting with personal hygiene needs (e.g. toilet help) in accordance with the student wishes and instructions.
Observe suitability of upcoming learning material and convert proactively to digital as required by student or teachers.
Providing practical assistance with other everyday activities as required by the student.

The assistant works according to the student's instructions and preferences, respecting the student's privacy, dignity, self-determination, and independence at all times.

Qualifications

Reliable, responsible, and punctual.
Respectful and professional attitude.
Good communication skills (Finnish language is not required).
Ability to assist with mobility and everyday physical support. This requires good physical strength from personal assistant.
Suitability to work in high-school environment.
Previous experience as a Personal Assistant or in a similar role is an advantage but is not required. Appropriate orientation will be provided.

This position is intended to support the student's participation in everyday school-life. It is not a healthcare or nursing role, but a position providing practical personal assistance in accordance with the student's individual needs.

Työssä vaadittavat rikosrekisteriotteet
To work with children, a criminal record extract is required under the Act on Checking the Criminal Background of Persons Working with Children (504/2002).
To work with persons with disabilities, a criminal record extract is required under the Act on the Supervision of Social and Health Care (741/2023).
