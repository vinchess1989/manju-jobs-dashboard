Katso, kuka Aurora Live on palkattu tähän rooliin

Aurora Live – Helsinki

Aurora Live is building Europe’s most wanted executive network — and a high-performance commercial engine for B2B tech companies.

We are looking for a Paid Media Specialist to help us scale results with speed across paid channels. You'll directly work with our Growth Marketing Manager — owning campaign execution, diving into performance data, and moving fast on creative experiments.

What You'll Do

Paid campaign execution


Build and optimize campaigns in ad platforms (LinkedIn, Meta, Google) — structuring campaign groups/audiences, setting campaign objectives, bid strategies, conversion tracking, landing pages and UTM parameters.
Monitor daily and monthly spend pacing across campaigns and markets, adjusting bids and reallocating budget within agreed parameters to hit targets.
Plan and optimise landing pages — aligning page messaging to ad creative and running tests to improve form completion rates.


Reporting & analysis


Track core campaign metrics daily/weekly: impressions, clicks, CTR, CPL, conversion rate, and spend pacing.
Identify patterns across channels to inform testing priorities.
Prepare weekly performance summaries and flag anomalies or opportunities to the team.


Creative & content


Create multiple ad copy variants per campaign, tailored to audience segment and funnel stage.
Brief designers on new creative visual concepts and iterate based on performance.
Plan and produce content offers to support lead gen (guides, case studies, short-form assets).
Run fast creative experiments — launch, measure, learn, repeat.


What We're Looking For


1–3 years of hands-on experience in paid media (LinkedIn, Meta, and/or Google), especially in B2B.
Can manage multiple campaigns across channels and markets simultaneously — from initial setup through to ongoing optimisation.
Comfortable pulling and interpreting campaign data — you can spot an anomaly, form a hypothesis, and know what to test next.
Comfortable changing direction fast when data or priorities shift — you find a way forward, you don't wait for a new brief.
