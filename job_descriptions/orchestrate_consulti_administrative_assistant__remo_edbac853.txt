Job Overview

GoldEagleAuctions, a leading online auction and liquidation company, is seeking a highly organised and proactive Administrative Assistant to join our team. We specialise in the sale of consumer goods, electronics, and surplus inventory, providing a streamlined digital bidding experience for our customers. This is an excellent opportunity for a detail-oriented professional to provide crucial support to our operations in a dynamic, fast-paced environment.

The ideal candidate will be a self-starter, capable of managing a diverse range of administrative tasks efficiently while working independently. This role is fundamental to ensuring the smooth operation of our daily business processes.

Job Title: Administrative Assistant (Remote)

Employment Type: Full-Time

Location: Remote

Responsibilities


Provide comprehensive administrative support to the team to ensure efficient operation of the office.
Manage and respond to emails, correspondence, and customer service enquiries in a professional and timely manner.
Schedule meetings, manage calendars, and coordinate appointments for senior staff.
Assist in the preparation of regularly scheduled reports, presentations, and documents.
Perform data entry tasks and maintain accurate digital records related to auctions, sales, and customer information.
Liaise with internal teams to handle requests and queries.
Organise and maintain digital filing systems, ensuring information is easily accessible.
Support the auction and liquidation process by assisting with listing coordination and post-sale administration.


Qualifications


Proven experience in an administrative assistant or relevant office support role.
Exceptional organisational skills with the ability to prioritise tasks and manage time effectively.
Strong written and verbal communication skills with a high level of professionalism.
Proficiency in MS Office suite (Word, Excel, Outlook) or equivalent G Suite applications.
Excellent attention to detail and problem-solving abilities.
The ability to work independently and proactively in a remote setting.
Experience in e-commerce, online retail, or an auction environment is desirable but not essential.


What We Offer


A competitive hourly wage of $25 - $39 USD, commensurate with experience.
The flexibility and convenience of a fully remote working arrangement.
An opportunity to be part of a fast-growing and innovative company in the online liquidation sector.
