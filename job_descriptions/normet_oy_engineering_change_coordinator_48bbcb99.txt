Engineering Change Coordinator

Normet Oy - Iisalmi

Julkaistu 14.8. (4 päivää jäljellä)

Työpaikkakuvaus

We are a global technology company developing innovative solutions for mining, tunnelling, and construction, improving project safety, environmental impact, and productivity. We offer premium equipment, all-round aftermarket services and high-quality chemicals and rock support as our core products to our customers. We operate globally in over 30 countries with more than 1,800 experts. At Normet, we commit to a culture founded in our Values - Caring, Committed & Courageous. We foster your talent, with us you shine.

We are looking for an

Engineering Change Coordinator

to coordinate and control engineering changes to key components, ensuring timely and accurate implementation from the engineering bill of material (BOM) to the production floor. In this role, you will work closely with the Equipment Engineering team and Product Line Management, and collaborate with Sourcing, suppliers, Production, Quality and Service functions globally. You will report to the Director, Global Engineering and Prototype Production. The role is based in Iisalmi, Finland, but does not require full-time presence at the manufacturing site. At the start, we expect the role to involve working from Iisalmi on average 2–3 days per week.

Main responsibilities and tasks:

Coordinate and drive item changes from engineering bill of material (BOM) to the production floor.

Assess and manage change implementation considering machines under build, items in storage, and impact on service/spare parts.

Plan and align change timing with new item lead times, supplier availability and production schedule.

Work in close co-operation with the Sourcing function to proactively identify components becoming obsolete and trigger needed change actions early enough.

Own and continuously improve engineering change / change management process, including ways of working, documentation and implementation governance.

Work in close co-operation with global teams to align engineering changes, timing and implementation across sites.

Maintain clear change communication and status tracking with Engineering, Production, Supply Chain and Service.

To shine in this position, we hope you have:

B.Sc. or M.Sc. in Engineering, Industrial Engineering, Supply Chain or equivalent.

3–5 years of experience in engineering changes in a manufacturing environment (BOM, change notices, implementation timing).

Understanding of production build phases, inventory/material availability and supplier lead times.

Experience working cross-functionally with Engineering, Production, Supply Chain/Sourcing and Service/Aftermarket.

Experience in ERP/PLM systems and BOM structures is an advantage.

Strong coordination skills: able to drive implementation decisions and follow-up to closure.

Analytical mindset to evaluate change impacts (machines under build, stock, service spares).

Good communication and stakeholder management; confident working with multiple functions and suppliers.

Structured way of working, documentation discipline and attention to detail.

Proactive approach to identifying risks such as component obsolescence and initiating mitigation actions.

Positive drive to constantly develop engineering change processes with key stakeholders.

Fluent written and spoken English and Finnish.

As an employer, we support your well-being with benefits such as: ePassi lunch and Flex benefits for sports, wellness and culture, occupational health care and specialist-level treatment through our work ability insurance, access to Auntie’s mental well-being services and more. We also support degree-oriented studies that benefit Normet with 10 paid working days per year.

In addition, we have an active HYVÄ Normet leisure committee, which organizes various leisure and sports-related events throughout the year.

Read more about working at Normet: https://www.normet.com/en/discover-normet/careers

Come and share your courage to shine with us: in a courageous, committed and caring company. Together with our customers, we have courage to lead the way in sustainable underground mining and tunnelling by building the safest places underground. The position offers you an excellent opportunity to work with global cross-functional teams and make a visible impact on the company’s daily operations, change implementation and customer satisfaction.

We are looking forward to hearing more about you. Please submit your application and CV through our career website by Sunday 6 September 2026 through our career website, please click on the apply-button. We will review applications and continue with potential candidates already during the application period, so do not hesitate to apply if this position interests you!

For further information, please, contact Pekka Holopainen, Director, Global Engineering and Prototype Production, (pekka.holopainen(at)normet.com) OR call +358 44 576 0680.
