Change Management Specialist, Helsinki (hybrid)

Tiivistelmä työpaikkailmoituksesta:

You will drive the human side of a global invoice management system rollout as a Change Management Specialist in a hybrid Helsinki role.
You will own end-to-end change management including creating the project narrative, conducting impact analyses, developing stakeholder-specific plans, coordinating communications, and delivering training across approximately 15 countries in Europe and America.
The role requires knowledge of change management methodologies, advanced English, strong communication skills, and a proactive mindset.
It is meritorious to have Source-to-Pay or finance-related technology and corporate experience, and the recruitment by Academic Work includes a personality and a cognitive test.


Tämä tiivistelmä on luotu tekoälyn avulla.



Join a high-impact global technology project where you will shape the narrative of a major system implementation. Our client offers a dynamic, international environment where your proactive approach directly influences organizational success.

About the role

As a core member of the project team, you will drive the human side of a new invoice management system rollout across different stakeholders and teams. You will bridge the gap between technical processes and people to ensure a seamless transition. This role offers the chance to work in a truly global setting with a hybrid work model in Helsinki. You will gain valuable experience in large-scale corporate change management.

Work tasks

You will be responsible for the end-to-end change management process, from initial analysis and storytelling to stakeholder engagement and training delivery.

Create the project story and narrative for the new system implementation
Conduct comprehensive change impact analyses across different business areas
Develop and implement tailored change management plans for diverse stakeholders
Coordinate and deliver communication strategies for end-users and management
Facilitate training sessions and workshops for international teams
Collaborate closely with the project manager, architects, and process owners
Engage with stakeholders across approximately 15 countries in Europe and America

We are looking for

Knowledge of change management methodologies
Advanced proficiency in English as the primary working language
Good communication skills
Proactive mindset with the ability to challenge the project team and take initiative

It is meritorious if you have

Experience with Source-to-Pay or finance-related technology projects
Previous experience working within a corporate environment

Our recruitment process

This recruitment process is handled by Academic Work and it is our client’s wish that all questions regarding the position is directed to Academic Work.

Our selection process is continuous and the advert may close before the recruitment process is completed if we have moved forward to the next phase. The process includes two tests: one personality test and one cognitive test. The tests are tools to find the right talent for the right position, to enable equality, diversity, and a fair process.
