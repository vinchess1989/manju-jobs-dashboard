About the Role:

The Project Planner is responsible for developing and maintaining the project programme for assigned projects. This includes regularly updating schedules based on progress data and modelling changes as they arise, such as variations, delays, or adjustments to logic and durations.

The Project Planner acts as a key support function to the project team, providing accurate performance measurement, identifying programme risks, and ensuring compliance with contractual requirements, particularly in relation to change management.

Key Responsibilities:

Develop and maintain detailed project programmes across all phases (Tender, PCSA, Contract, Design, Execution, Commissioning) using approved planning software.
Attend internal weekly project meetings and planning department meetings.
Gather and validate accurate weekly progress data, including confirmed start and finish dates.
Record and model programme impacts arising from delays, variations, and scope changes.
Analyse and report on critical path, key milestones, and programme risks.
Support senior management through accurate reporting and timely escalation of programme risks and issues.
Attend client-facing programme meetings and workshops, providing clear schedule updates and analysis.
Liaise with subcontractors to integrate their schedules and progress into the master programme.
Prepare and issue regular reports, including progress updates, 4-week lookaheads, critical path analysis, EHRA updates, and other required reports.
Attend site as required to verify progress and programme assumptions (minimum one site visit every four weeks).
Report to Lead Planners and the Planning Manager on overall project and programme status.
Maintain structured documentation and file management in line with company standards.
Adhere to Planning Department procedures, standards, and governance frameworks.
Contribute to departmental initiatives, continuous improvement, and the development of graduate planners.

Key Requirements:

Proven experience using Primavera P6 with a commitment to continuous improvement.
Experience with Microsoft Project and willingness to further develop skills.
Data center background and experience will be seen as an advantage.
Strong understanding of project planning principles, particularly critical path analysis and schedule logic.
Thorough knowledge of assigned project(s), including scope, construction methodology, and risks.
Strong communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to engage with internal teams and external stakeholders effectively.
Highly organized with attention to detail and a methodical approach to problem-solving.
Ability to manage multiple tasks and projects concurrently in a fast-paced environment.

Skills & Competencies:

Integrates sustainable thinking into day-to-day decisions and actions.
Embeds safety into all activities, consistently following and promoting safe systems of work.
Demonstrates a strong teamworking approach, collaborating with colleagues and stakeholders to deliver successful outcomes.
Willingness to learn, adapt, and develop skills, contributing to personal growth and long-term success within the company.
Proactive, manages responsibilities effectively, and approaches challenges with a solution-focused mindset.
