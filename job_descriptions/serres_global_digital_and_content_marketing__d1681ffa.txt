Serres is the world’s leading provider of surgical fluid management solutions. Our products support safe and sustainable patient care and are used in more than 90,000 surgical procedures every day. We employ over 180 professionals and serve customers in more than 50 markets worldwide. For more information, visit our website at www.serres.com.




As our international business continues to grow, we are looking for a Digital and Content Marketing Specialist to drive global digital marketing and support our distributor network.

You will join a collaborative marketing team based primarily in Finland and report to the Head of Marketing. Working closely with sales, product management, distributors, and external partners, you will take ownership of digital channels, content, and campaigns in an international and highly regulated healthcare environment.




The role is ideal for someone who combines hands-on digital marketing expertise with experience in medical devices, pharmaceuticals, or healthcare marketing. You enjoy working independently, collaborating across functions, and managing several projects in a changing international business environment.




What you’ll do

Develop and implement our digital content strategy
Manage and develop serres.com, our social media channels, and the content calendar
Plan, execute, and optimize integrated campaigns across website, SEO and SEM, email, social media, digital advertising, and marketing automation
Create high-quality, SEO-friendly content for our website, LinkedIn, campaigns, and other marketing materials
Support our global distributor network with digital marketing best practices, campaign concepts, product launches, co-marketing initiatives, materials, data, and insights
Ensure consistent brand positioning and messaging across international markets while allowing for local adaptation
Monitor campaign performance and marketing KPIs, using analytics, market insights, and A/B testing to improve effectiveness
Create compelling marketing communications based on approved product claims
Collaborate closely with sales, product management, distributors, and external agencies




To succeed in this role, you must have:

A bachelor’s degree in marketing, business, communications, or a related field
Approximately 3 to 5 years of relevant experience in international digital marketing
Previous marketing or communications experience in the medical device, pharmaceutical, or healthcare industry
Experience in B2B marketing and channel or distributor marketing
Hands-on experience in planning, executing, and optimizing digital marketing campaigns
Experience in creating content for digital channels
Strong project management skills and the ability to manage multiple priorities while maintaining high-quality results
Excellent communication and collaboration skills, combined with an independent and proactive approach to work
Professional working proficiency in English. German or French language skills are considered an advantage
The ability to work at our Espoo office a few times per week. This position cannot be performed fully remotely




Technical skills

We expect you to have:

Experience with content management systems such as WordPress
Experience with LinkedIn Campaign Manager, Sprout Social, Google Ads, Google Analytics, and Google Tag Manager
Knowledge of SEO best practices and experience with SEO tools
Familiarity with email marketing or marketing automation platforms, such as ClickDimensions
Experience with creative tools such as Adobe Photoshop, Adobe InDesign and Canva
Strong analytical skills and the ability to turn data into actionable insights




What we offer

A versatile marketing role with broad exposure to international business
The opportunity to join a growing company that combines innovation with a strong commitment to sustainability
Meaningful work supporting healthcare organizations on their journey towards net-zero emissions and a more sustainable future
Comprehensive occupational healthcare and insurance coverage, as well as lunch, sports, wellbeing, and culture benefits
A salary of €3,600–€4,300 per month, based on your experience, skills, and overall fit for the role
Hybrid working model and flexible working hours, supported by modern office facilities in Pohjois-Tapiola, Espoo. The office is easily accessible by public transportation and offers convenient parking, making commuting convenient regardless of how you choose to travel




The starting date will be agreed with the selected candidate.
