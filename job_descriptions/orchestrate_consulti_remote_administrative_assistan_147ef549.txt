Tallenna
Orchestrate Consulting's mission is to help job seekers access carefully coordinated opportunities that match their career goals. We are not a staffing firm or agency. Orchestrate Consulting does not hire for these roles—we curate and validate them from verified employers.

We are seeking a highly organized and detail-oriented Remote Administrative Assistant to provide administrative and operational support to our team. In this work-from-home role, you will manage schedules, coordinate communications, organize documents, and assist with day-to-day administrative tasks to ensure efficient business operations. The ideal candidate is proactive, dependable, and able to work independently in a remote environment while maintaining a high level of professionalism.

Responsibilities


Manage calendars, schedule meetings, and coordinate appointments.
Answer and respond to emails, phone calls, and other business communications.
Prepare, edit, proofread, and distribute reports, presentations, and correspondence.
Maintain electronic filing systems and organize company documents.
Perform data entry and update databases with accurate information.
Coordinate virtual meetings, prepare agendas, and record meeting notes when required.
Assist with travel arrangements, expense reports, and vendor communications as needed.
Monitor office supplies, subscriptions, and administrative resources.
Support managers and team members with special projects and administrative requests.
Maintain confidentiality of sensitive company and client information.


Qualifications


High school diploma or equivalent required; Associate's or Bachelor's degree is a plus.
Previous experience as an administrative assistant, executive assistant, office assistant, or similar administrative role preferred.
Proficiency in Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, Outlook, PowerPoint) and Google Workspace.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
Strong organizational, multitasking, and time management abilities.
High attention to detail and accuracy.
Ability to work independently with minimal supervision.
Reliable high-speed internet connection and a dedicated home office.
