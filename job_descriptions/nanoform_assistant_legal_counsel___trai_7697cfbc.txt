About Nanoform

Nanoform is an innovative drug particle engineering and nanotechnology company listed on the Premier-segment of Nasdaq First North Growth Market in Helsinki and Stockholm. Nanoform works together with pharma and biotech partners globally to reduce attrition in clinical trials and enhance their molecules’ formulation performance through its nanoforming technologies and formulation services. Nanoform’s capabilities include GMP manufacturing, and its services span the small to large molecule development space with a focus on solving key issues in drug solubility and bioavailability and on enabling novel drug delivery applications.




Assistant Legal Counsel - Trainee

We are seeking an Assistant Legal Counsel to be a part of our journey to build a global changemaker in the pharmaceutical industry. The Assistant Legal Counsel will work together with Nanoform’s Legal Counsels and local counsels across different jurisdictions to support and coordinate the broad range of legal matters arising in our business.




The position is part-time (approximately 16 - 24 hours a week) and for a fixed term until 31.12.2027. We would appreciate it if the chosen candidate could start in January 2027.




Key Responsibilities

Contract review and negotiations.
Corporate governance related matters for a listed company.
Drafting documents, meeting minutes, and corporate resolutions.
Supporting the coordination of IPR prosecution work across jurisdictions.
Taking an active role in administrating the lifecycle of contracts and documents.
Legal research that supports the decision making by various stakeholders at Nanoform.




Skills, Education and Experience We Look for

Law student with at least half of their studies completed.
Interest in business law, technology, intellectual property rights, and the pharmaceutical industry.
Previous working experience in a law firm or a listed company as well as background in life sciences can be an advantage but not required.
Ability to act independently and as part of a team to provide timely and quality work.
Proactive person that shows attention to detail.
Fluent in written and spoken English and Finnish. Swedish is considered an advantage.
Most of all we value a positive can-do attitude and willingness to learn.




What We Offer to You

A great team to join with highly skilled and motivated colleagues that invent world-class solutions for patients around the globe.
A unique opportunity to learn and develop your skill set as part of an international and fast-growing listed company that is continuously creating something new.
Diverse challenges that we enjoy solving together.
Flexible working hours easily compatible with studies.




You will get to be part of a unique growth story in the pharma industry!




For more information regarding the recruitment process or the position, please contact Senior Legal Counsel Juho Jalo (juho.jalo@nanoform.com).




If this sounds like the challenge you’ve been looking for, please apply by submitting your cover letter (including your salary expectation) and CV by 6 September 2026.




We look forward to hearing from you!
