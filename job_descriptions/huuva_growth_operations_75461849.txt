Reinventing restaurants. Building Europe’s fastest-growing food concept.

Huuva brings the most loved city-center restaurants closer to suburban communities — making high-quality dining more accessible than ever.

Our multi-brand food halls let customers mix and match dishes from top restaurants in a single order, whether dining in or ordering for delivery.

Since launching in 2021, we’ve grown from one kitchen to one of Finland’s largest restaurant platforms — backed by world-class investors including General Catalyst and Lifeline Ventures (early backers of Wolt, Oura, and Supercell).

Our team combines restaurant veterans from Michelin-starred kitchens with growth builders from Varjo, VOI, Smartly.io, and Zalando. Together, we’re scaling faster than anyone else in our category — and we’re just getting started.

Our vision: to become Europe’s largest restaurant scale-up.

About The Role

We’re looking for an ambitious Growth Operations hire to join our Helsinki HQ.

Your mission: make every Huuva venue perform like our best one.

You’ll operate at the intersection of operations, growth, and customer experience — analyzing data, running local growth campaigns, fine-tuning menus, and engaging directly with customers to ensure every experience exceeds expectations.

This is a role for someone who learns by doing, acts fast, and finds satisfaction in improving the details that make a business scale. You’ll be close to the data, the customers, and the kitchens — where the real growth happens.

What you’ll do


Analyze sales, demand, and menu performance data to identify growth opportunities
Plan and execute local growth campaigns that drive measurable sales increases
Manage and optimize menus across Wolt, Uber, and in-store kiosks
Collaborate with Kitchen Operations and Business Development to deliver operational excellence
Engage with customers to ensure satisfaction, resolve issues, and surface insights
Turn manual or repetitive tasks into efficient, scalable processes


You’ll thrive here if you


Have a strong commercial mindset — you think in numbers, not noise
Are both creative and systematic — you care about precision and execution
Have demonstrated a can-do attitude by achieving something meaningful in work, studies, or personal pursuits
Learn fast and take initiative instead of waiting for instructions
Are fluent in Finnish and English
Are based in Helsinki and willing to work at the office daily


What we offer


Salary range: 3,500–4,000 €/month, depending on experience
Option package — real ownership in the company’s future
The chance to learn directly from top-tier operators and Michelin-level chefs
A steep learning curve and fast career progression as Huuva scales
Transparent, high-performance culture built on autonomy and accountability


Why this role matters

Every campaign, menu update, and customer conversation you manage shapes how thousands of people experience food, and how fast we grow.

You’ll be part of a team that’s redefining what restaurants can be — building Europe’s fastest-growing food concept alongside people who’ve already done it before.

If you’re ready to roll up your sleeves, execute with precision, and grow faster than you thought possible, we’d love to meet you.
