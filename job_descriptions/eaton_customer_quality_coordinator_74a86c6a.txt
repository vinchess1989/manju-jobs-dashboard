What You’ll Do

We are looking for a Customer Quality Coordinator to our Critical Power Systems EMEA Business. We are developing and manufacturing large UPS Systems for large datacenter and other customers. Quality of products, components and processes is critical for reliable performance of these mission-critical equipment.

You will part of the Customer Quality Team, reporting to a Customer Quality manager, working to support our customers in data analysis, meeting preparations. You will be working in fast-paced environment within the Quality Team, and collaborating closely with the EMEA country organizations, as well as Eaton internal production and R&D departments. Your main role is to support Customer Quality Engineers in their work of analysing and solving Quality problems. Your work will contribute to the continuous improvement, and making Quality a competitive advantage.

Your Responsibilities Include


Gathering & Maintaining Field Quality information in Jira-based platform
Preparing data for meetings, and capture action plans (meeting minutes)
Liaison with Plant manufacturing - carry over of pertinent and relevant information
Liaison between customer quality and:- Sales, Customer Service, Tech support, Sustaining team.
Leading the coordination and follow up for retrieval of ( EEPS/Eprom ) electronic erasable programmable memory. Initial failure mode Statements / findings, Photos. Handling returns of parts.
Coordinating Lab time/activities.
Following up with due dates for Lab investigations
Measurable outcome is Quality engineer is not waiting for information to begin failure analysis.

Master or Bachelor in Engineering or equivalent
