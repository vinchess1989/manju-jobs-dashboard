Räätälöi ansioluetteloni

Helsinki (Kruununhaka) · 6-month paid traineeship · Starts October 2026 · Apply by 27.09.2026




Getting into advertising from the outside can be hard. This traineeship is your way in.




NoA Connect Finland is looking for an SEM Trainee to join our Helsinki team.




You don't need agency experience or a Google Ads certificate. What you need is curiosity about how advertising actually works, a head for numbers, and the drive to learn fast.




The role in short

A 6-month, full-time, paid traineeship, starting October 2026. Our hope is that this becomes the start of something longer-term: most of our trainees go on to join us as full-time SEM Specialists, though we can't promise it in advance.




What you'll learn to do

Build, manage and optimise search advertising campaigns
Run keyword research, competitor analysis and performance reporting
Work daily with Google Ads, Google Analytics and Looker Studio
Use AI on real client accounts with our own in-house AI stack, and learn when to question what it tells you
Get in early on advertising inside AI platforms, including ChatGPT ads, while they're still being built
Contribute to real client strategy and data-driven decisions




You'll start by watching and assisting. By month six, you'll own parts of client accounts and present your own analysis. How fast you get there is mostly up to you.




AI is a real part of this role

We move fast and we're AI-savvy. Our specialists use an in-house AI stack every day, and we extend it most weeks. It connects directly into the platforms we work in, so it reads live account data instead of guessing.




For you that means two things. You learn to use AI on real client data, with the judgement to recognise when its answer is wrong. And you help build it, because the people closest to the daily work are the ones who spot what should be automated next.




The same shift is happening to advertising itself. AI platforms are becoming media channels, and ChatGPT ads are the clearest example yet. Nobody has years of experience here. You'll be learning it at roughly the same pace as everyone else in the industry, which is a rare edge to have this early in a career.




Existing experience with AI tools is a clear plus. Curiosity about them is a requirement.




What we're looking for

Genuine curiosity about how ads work behind the scenes, and what the numbers are actually saying
Comfort with data: you don't need to be an analyst, but a spreadsheet shouldn't scare you
Fluent Finnish and English, spoken and written (client work requires both)
Studying or recently graduated in marketing, business, economics, statistics or something adjacent. Different background? Show us you can do the work and apply anyway
Able to work from our Kruununhaka office most days. Traineeships happen through overhearing, asking and being asked, which is hard to replicate over video. Remote days by agreement once you're up and running




Nice to have, not required: hands-on Google Ads, Excel or SQL experience, familiarity with AI tools, an e-commerce background, or any project where you've spent your own money on ads and learned something from it.




What we offer

Salary: 2 000 € per month
Contract: fixed-term, 6 months, starting October 2026
Location: A newly renovated office space in Kruununhaka, Helsinki
Breakfast at the office every morning, plus other company events throughout the year
Office gym and yoga room free to use 24/7
A lunch benefit
Paid work time for sport and wellbeing activities




Why NoA Connect

We're a small, specialised digital team of talented and ambitious people who support each other. As part of that team, your work matters from week one and won't disappear into some process.

We're also part of the NoA group, working closely with its creative and tech agencies. That's what's worth paying attention to if you're building a career in advertising, not just a job in it. You'll see how work travels from an idea through creative and production into media and measurement. Most specialists go years without ever seeing the whole chain. You'll see it in your first six months, and it's what separates people who run campaigns from people who understand advertising.







How to apply

Send us your CV and a few sentences on why search marketing interests you — no long cover letter needed. We care more about how you think than how well you can describe yourself.

Apply by 27.09.2026 at the latest. We review applications as they arrive and may fill the position before the deadline, so applying early is worth it.




The process: a short call, then a meeting at our office with the team you'd work with, then a decision. We'll tell you where you stand at every stage.
