Description

The DuPage Senior Citizens Council/Kane Senior Council (DSCC/KSC) is a not-for-profit 501(c)(3), volunteer-driven organization committed to the well-being, socialization, and health of seniors. DSCC/KSC is the designated Meals on Wheels and Community Dining provider in DuPage and Kane County.

DSCC/KSC is seeking a Full-Time Administrative Support person to handle day-to-day administrative tasks across multiple departments. Duties typically include a mix of clerical, organizational, and coordinated tasks. Superior organizational & communication abilities – written & verbal a must. Ability to gather data, analyze information, and prepare reports. Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a fast-paced team environment with tight deadlines.

Responsibilities/Tasks


Answer and direct phone calls, emails, and inquiries
Prepare, format, and file documents (digital and physical)
Maintain the organization's filing and record-keeping systems
Enter updates, and maintain accurate records and databases
Respond to routine requests and resolve minor issues
Support volunteer or client relations as needed
Compile reports, spreadsheets, and basic presentations
