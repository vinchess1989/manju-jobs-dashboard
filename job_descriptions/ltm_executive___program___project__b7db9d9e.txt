We are looking for Change Management Expert in Helsinki, Finland with below expertise:





At least 1 year of fulltime experience in a change management in a large IT or business transformation project




Ability to carry out assigned change management tasks under the supervision of the Change Management Lead




Fluent written and spoken communication skills in both Finnish and English




Supporting the Change Management Lead in executing change management activities and maintaining documentation




Assisting in the preparation of stakeholder communication materials and updates




Supporting the coordination and delivery of enduser training activities




Helping to administer change readiness surveys and collate adoption progress data




Maintaining change management logs trackers and filing systems




Basic familiarity with change management concepts eg awareness of structured methodologies such as Prosci ADKAR
