About Numix




Numix is building the finance and tax infrastructure for startups.




We combine software, AI agents, and financial experts to help founders with bookkeeping, tax, R&D credits, payroll, and CFO-level support.




We’ve grown heavily through founder referrals, partnerships, and community, and now we want to build that into a much bigger growth engine.




About the Role




We’re hiring a Community & Events Lead to build the founder community around Numix.




You’ll own intimate founder dinners, panels, ecosystem events, partnerships, and community programs from idea to follow-up.




This is not about getting the most RSVPs.




It’s about getting the right people in the room, creating conversations people remember, and turning those relationships into community, referrals, partnerships, and pipeline.




What You’ll Do




Run intimate founder dinners and events end to end
Build guest lists and personally recruit great founders, investors, and operators
Manage venues, logistics, programming, seating, and follow-up
Build recurring community programs people actually want to join
Turn events into content, introductions, referrals, and pipeline
Work with VCs, accelerators, law firms, banks, and startup communities on partnerships
Track what works and continuously improve the event playbook




Who You Are




You’re an exceptional host and event operator
You know how to get busy founders and executives to show up
You care more about who is in the room than how many people attend
You write invitations and follow-ups people actually respond to
You’re highly organized and comfortable owning every detail
You understand startups, GTM, and the Bay Area founder ecosystem
You’re comfortable working at early-stage speed without a playbook




Nice to Have




3+ years in events, community, partnerships, or field marketing
Existing Bay Area startup network
Experience working with founders, VCs, or accelerators
Strong content, photo, or video instincts
Experience with Luma, LinkedIn, Slack, Notion, CRM, or community tools
Have been founders before




What Success Looks Like




When great founders hear that Numix is hosting something, they want to be in the room.
