Board of WCN Finland 2026-2027

Women’s Career Network Finland is now looking for a new board for Autumn 2026- Spring 2027. Join the global community of ambitious and motivated people in developing the Finnish branch of the Women’s Career Network further.

WHO ARE WE?

Women’s Career Network is a nonprofit organization for female and non-binary students and young professionals who believe in combining passion and hard work, as well as want to support each other to grow. In 2022, Women’s Career Network (WCN) expanded to become its own organization in Finland. Since then, WCN Finland has thrived and been contributing to the important movement towards a higher percentage of women in leadership positions in the workforce.

The WCN Finland Board will be responsible for the association’s operations in Finland, including strategic and internal development, financing, sponsorships, marketing and recruitment. In addition, the Board in Finland will work closely together with the Board in Sweden to develop global cooperation and our members’ international networking opportunities.

WHAT WILL WE OFFER?

This is a great opportunity to gain valuable experience in an inspiring community, create meaningful connections and expand your professional network with top-tier companies, career mentors and like-minded peers. As a part of WCN you will gain professional experience while making a real difference when it comes to gender equality in the workplace.

WHAT ARE WE SEARCHING FOR?

We are looking for committed and motivated university students and graduates in law, business and tech for the following positions:

HEAD OF EVENTS

Would you like to lead the planning of networking events with top companies in Finland?

We are looking for a Head of Events for the 2026–2027 season to lead a team organizing around one event per month. WCN events include dinners, activities, case events, panel discussions, and company visits. Now we need someone to lead it.

In This Role, You Will:


Event organization (hands on): Actively take part in organizing events yourself by reaching out to companies, manage partner communication, handle logistics (e.g. sign ups, venues and catering), collect feedback, and represent WCN at events.
Strategic planning and partnerships: Work with your team to decide which companies to approach, which partnerships to build, and what kinds of events to organize.
Leadership: Lead and coordinate your team by delegating tasks, supporting team members, and reporting progress to the board.


The Ideal Candidate Is Someone Who:


Is a university student or recent university graduate (0–5 years since graduation) in business, law, or a technical field. It is possible to work full time simultaneously, but strong time management skills are required.
Can commit around to 15 hours per week and attending board meetings every three weeks (remote possible).
Leadership experience in student organizations or similar is a plus but not a requirement
Has a strong interest in event planning. Previous experience is a plus but not a requirement
Has strong English skills and is comfortable using Google Tools (Slides, Forms, Sheets, Drive)
Is proactive, organized, and motivated to bring new ideas to life.
Is based in Helsinki or nearby regions


Head of Mentorship

We are looking for a Head of Mentorship for the 2026–2027 season to lead a team responsible for delivering WCN’s flagship Mentorship Programme. Now in its eighth consecutive year, the programme brings together motivated mentees and experienced mentors from fields such as law, tech, and business.

In This Role, You Will:


Programme management : Take an active role in planning and developing the Mentorship Programme, coordinating its execution from start to finish. This includes recruiting and interviewing mentors, selecting and matching mentees, organising events with partner companies, and ensuring a high-quality experience for all participants.
Strategic planning and partnerships: Work with your team to shape the direction of the programme, identify and collaborate with company partners, and design engaging event concepts that align with the programme’s annual theme.
Leadership: Lead and coordinate a team of three by delegating tasks, supporting team members, and ensuring smooth progress throughout the programme. You will also provide ongoing supervision and continuously develop the programme.


We Are Looking For Someone Who:


Is a university student or recent graduate (0–5 years since graduation) in business, law, or a technical field
Can commit around 15 hours per week and attend board meetings every three weeks (remote possible)
Has strong organizational and time management skills
Is proactive, structured, and comfortable working in a dynamic, fast-paced environment
Has strong communication skills in English
Is motivated to lead a team and manage a complex, year-long project
Is based in Helsinki or nearby regions


Experience in leadership, recruitment, or event/project management is a plus, but not a requirement.

What You Gain:


Leadership experience: A unique opportunity to lead a team and manage a large-scale programme from planning to execution
Strong network: You will collaborate with professionals from top companies and connect with ambitious students and mentors across different fields
Personal and professional growth: You will develop valuable skills in leadership, communication, and strategic planning while contributing to a programme that has a real impact on others
Like-minded community: You become part of a team of around 25 ambitious women who support each other, grow together, and also make time to unwind through team activities


This is a unique chance to shape one of WCN’s most meaningful initiatives and create lasting connections between mentors and mentees.

Apply now and be part of making an impact!

Head of Corporate Relations

In this role, you’ll get to combine strong organizational skills with team leadership. The key responsibilities revolve around budgeting, managing relationships with Premium Sponsors, and acquiring new sponsors, as well as organizing the team’s work, collaborating closely with team members, and driving ideation together. This role takes around 5 to 10 hours a week, depending on the season.

The Ideal Candidate Is Someone Who:


Can work independently and make decisions confidently, while also communicating them clearly to the rest of the team
Has strong interpersonal and communication skills in English
Has some flexibility in their schedule to accommodate meetings with sponsors and partners during working hours
Has some knowledge or experience in budgeting/invoicing/finances etc.
Can attend board meetings every three weeks (remote possible)


Head of Operations

Are you interested in operations and wish to take ownership of the processes that help WCN Finland run smoothly?

We are looking for a Head of Operations to join the new 2026-2027 WCN Finland Board. This is a one-person team established last year, whose purpose is to keep everything running smoothly across the organisation.

In this role, you will maintain and improve the team’s processes while collaborating closely with the President and Vice President, as well as other WCN teams. You will get a broad view of everything happening inside the organization and build relationships across the board. Your responsibilities span two areas: team operations and membership management.

On the operations side, you will manage WCN’s shared calendar, arrange the workspace for the board meetings, organise team days, gather and analyse team feedback, and find ways to make internal processes more efficient.

On the membership side, you will keep member data organised, and track statistics to follow the community’s growth.

From time to time, you will also take on creative tasks, such as designing new merch or marketing materials for events.

Since Operations is still a new team, you will have ownership over the role. In addition to the established responsibilities, you and the next board will decide together where to focus your time. Whether that means improving membership management, rethinking internal processes, or finding better ways to present WCN at student events.

You Will Be a Great Fit If You:


Are a university student or recent graduate (0–5 years from graduation) in business, law, or tech
Can dedicate around 5–10 hours a week to this role, depending on the time of year, and can join board meetings every three weeks
Like figuring things out on your own and can work independently
Are organised and reliable
Enjoy talking to people and working across teams
Are based in Helsinki or nearby


What You Will Gain

You will develop practical skills in operations, communication, and organisation, while having the freedom to focus on what you find most meaningful. And you will do all of this alongside a team of around 25 ambitious women who support each other, grow together, and have fun.

Please Include The Following Information In Your Application:


Name
Phone Number
Email address
What you study/have studied, which semester/graduated and school
What position(s) would you prefer
Attach a brief motivation letter with why you are interested in joining the WCN Board and your CV


Send your application to wcnhelsinki@womenscareernetwork.com by 3rd of May. If you have any questions about the position, please do not hesitate to contact WCN Finland by email. Note that these positions are voluntary-based.
