About The Role

As Junior Administrative Assistant in the COO office you take ownership of travel logistics, calendar coordination, and administrative follow-through, so that our Executive Assistant and COO spend their time on decisions rather than bookings. Our COO travels constantly across the UK, US, Japan, Taiwan, and Latin America, often on multi-leg trips that get rebuilt at short notice. A wrong date or a missed invite has visible cost, so getting the detail right, consistently, is the job. In return you sit closer to how a company actually runs at the executive level than almost any other entry-level role will put you.

Your responsibilities


Own flight, hotel, and itinerary bookings for all COO office travel, including multi-country and multi-leg trips
Manage calendars and scheduling across time zones: invites, rescheduling, and catching conflicts before they become problems
Prepare expense reports and keep receipt tracking clean and current
Support meeting prep with agendas and briefing materials, then track and close out action items afterwards
Maintain and organize the team's task tracking system and contacts database
Bring structure to follow-ups that currently live in informal running notes, so nothing falls through


Your key competencies

You are comfortable being trusted with detail that carries real cost, and you would rather build a system than wait for one to be handed to you.


Currently studying or recently graduated. Business administration, international relations, hospitality, or anything else: the field matters less than evidence you are organized.
Prior experience handling logistics, scheduling, or administrative work in any setting, including internships, part time jobs, student organizations, or event committees
Strong written and spoken English, at a level where we would trust you to email an investor
Availability and responsiveness that fit real-time travel support, including occasional early mornings and evenings
Right to work in Finland


Nice to have


Experience in an EA, office management, or travel coordination role, including informal versions of it
Familiarity with Google Calendar, Notion, Slack, or travel and expense platforms
Finnish or another language used across our markets
Exposure to a startup, consulting, or international business environment
