eBrands is looking for a full-time Finance Assistant to join our Helsinki office.




Are you a detail-oriented finance professional who enjoys working with numbers, accounting tasks, and keeping daily finance operations accurate and organized?‍




Born in Finland, eBrands is a platform for growing consumer brands globally through e-commerce and retail channels. We make local brands global by expanding in the US and Europe, driving sales for both our own and partner brands. Our goal is to positively impact a billion lives with sustainable, purpose-driven brands.




We specialize in expanding brands through our commercial network in the US, UK and Europe, from dominant and emerging e-commerce marketplaces to direct-to-consumer and major retail. Our platform enables local brand presence and a robust delivery network. Our portfolio includes over 50 brands and 1000 + products.




We are now searching for a Finance Assistant to join our growth journey.




Location: Hybrid working model, with a minimum of 3 days per week in our Helsinki office




Responsibilities:

Processing incoming invoices, expense claims and payments
Transferring sales invoices to the accounting system, monitoring overdue invoices, and supporting the Sales Team with invoice-related questions
Processing and reconciling bank statements
Assisting with month-end and year-end closing
Maintaining accurate master data
Coordinating the finance inbox and ensuring timely responses to internal and external stakeholders
Handling other accounting-related tasks
Supporting the improvement of finance processes and ways of working
Depending on your experience and interests, the role may include payroll and more advanced bookkeeping tasks
Supporting administrative tasks when needed







Requirements:

Vocational Qualification in Business Administration, business student, or equivalent education
Previous experience in accounting or finance is an advantage, but we are also open to candidates who are at the beginning of their career and have a good understanding of accounting basics
Careful and reliable working style with good communication skills
Fluent in English and Finnish
Experience with Netvisor and payroll is considered an advantage







Perks and benefits:

Extensive healthcare
A supportive environment to develop yourself
Flexible work schedules
Fun and passionate colleagues
A unique opportunity to be a part of a truly international and rapidly growing company







How do you apply?




Please send your application via email to jani.salmela@ebrands.com with the subject line "Application for Finance Assistant" including:

1. Your CV

2. Your salary request

3. Informal application - who are you and why you would like to join us




Or via Easy Apply




If you have questions about the role, you can call Group Financial Controller Jani Salmela, +358 40 727 8911.
