Want to work with the brightest SaaS founders in the region?‍‍




Vendep Capital is an early-stage venture capital investor specializing in B2B SaaS companies. We are currently investing our 80m€ fourth fund in the greatest Nordic and Baltic B2B SaaS founders while our previous investments include over 40 companies with the likes of AlphaSense, Inven, Hostaway and Hopsworks.‍




We are looking for a brilliant Associate to join our eight-person, close-knit team. While we give you the needed support to learn the ropes here, you're expected to be driven, self-motivated and taking ownership of your role below.In our view this is the most exciting time to be in this industry as AI transforms the world around us. If you're just as passionate about this rare opportunity, we have a home for you.




Responsibilities

There's ample amount of work depending on your strong suite, these include:

Deal flow sourcing
Deal analysis
Deal making & management
‍For the technically oriented - building internal tooling




Ideal profile

Go-getter. doer, self-starter, internally motivated, hungry, action-oriented, high-agency, force multiplier, owner... There are many buzzwords to describe the kind of person we are looking for, but if you consider yourself a smart team player that doesn't wait around for others to catch up, we'd like to have a chat.

Prior interest in or exposure to the Nordic startup ecosystem is necessary.

We work as a team on-site at our fresh office in Keilaniemi, Espoo.‍




What we offer

Ownership: Opportunity to work independently, take initiative, and prove your ability to execute.
Culture: You’ll join a flat and transparent team where people feel valued and genuinely enjoy spending time (*cough* competing) together.
Career growth: We want to help you level up into a high conviction, value-add investor over the next few years.
Benefits: Competitive compensation with great local healthcare, wellness, and other fringe benefits.




How to apply‍

Please send your application and CV with a salary expectation to Timo Felin (timo@vendep.com). We will interview immediately and fill the position as soon as we find the perfect match.
