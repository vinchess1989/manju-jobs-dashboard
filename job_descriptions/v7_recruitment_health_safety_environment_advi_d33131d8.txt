HSE Advisor – Hyperscale Data Centre Project




Finland

Start Date: August 2026

12-Month Contract + Strong Extension Potential

Competitive Day Rate

Accommodation Provided




V7 Recruitment are currently supporting a leading civil engineering contractor in the appointment of an HSE Advisor for a major 150MW Data Centre project in Finland.




This is an excellent 'foot in the door' opportunity to join one of Europe's most significant data centre developments programmes and gain valuable experience within the rapidly growing data centre sector.




Responsibilities

Support the implementation and maintenance of site HSE procedures and standards.
Conduct site inspections, audits, and safety observations.
Assist with incident investigations and corrective action tracking.
Review RAMS and ensure compliance with project requirements.
Monitor contractor performance and promote a positive safety culture across site.
Deliver toolbox talks and support workforce engagement initiatives.
Ensure compliance with local legislation, client standards, and company procedures.




Requirements

Previous experience as an HSE Advisor, EHS Advisor, or Health & Safety Advisor.
Strong background in heavy civils, groundworks, concrete, earthworks, infrastructure, or major construction projects.
Experience working for a main contractor or large civil engineering contractor.
Experience on large-scale, high-risk projects such as:
Civil Engineering
Infrastructure
Energy
Oil & Gas
Industrial Construction
Major Capital Projects
Strong knowledge of site safety management, inspections, audits, and contractor oversight.
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
EU Passport required.




What's on Offer

Competitive day rate.
Accommodation provided.
Long-term project with extension potential beyond the initial 12-month term.
Opportunity to work on a flagship 150MW data centre development.
Excellent opportunity for HSE professionals from a civils background to transition into the data centre sector.




Please note: Previous data centre experience is not required. Candidates must, however, have a strong background in heavy civils or major construction projects and experience working within a principal contractor or large civil contractor environment.
