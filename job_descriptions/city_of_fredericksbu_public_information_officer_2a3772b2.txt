Hiring Range: $64,957 - $70,000 DOQ

Full-Time | Benefits | Professional Development

Built on a strong foundation. Ready for its next chapter.

What You’ll Do

The Public Information Officer for the City of Fredericksburg, under the Communications Manager, supports comprehensive communications across City departments. Responsibilities include creating public safety messaging, advising leadership, managing media relations, and contributing to communication operations. The ideal candidate should excel in editorial decision-making, media interactions, and engage tactfully with the community and City employees.

Job duties may consist of:Media and Public Relations

Acting as the City’s spokesperson, distributing press releases, organizing news conferences, and identifying potential public relations issues. Building relationships with media professionals and monitoring media coverage are also key responsibilities to enhance the City’s image.Digital and Social Media Management

Manage the City’s social media accounts and website, ensuring consistent branding, and executing digital marketing campaigns. This includes creating engaging content and serving as the main social media contact for all City departments.Content Creation and Communication

Design and edit various materials such as brochures and press releases, creating graphics and advertisements, and serving as a photographer. The role also includes preparing speeches and offering guidance on public communication strategies.Community Engagement and Event Coordination Present information to the public, organize community events, and support emergency and public safety operations. The role also involves implementing the City’s brand communication strategy and maintaining its public image.

Why Join the Fredericksburg Team?

A Career with Purpose

This is not just a public relations role. It is an opportunity to influence how a full-service local government communicates with its citizens, manages public information, and builds trust within the community.Comprehensive Benefits

The City offers a competitive total rewards package designed to support your health, financial security, and professional growth, including medical, dental and vision coverage; paid leave; professional development and training opportunities; and participation in the Virginia Retirement System (VRS).

Minimum Qualifications


Bachelor's degree in English, communications, journalism, public relations or a related field;
Three (3) years of experience in communications, journalism, media, public relations, public safety or closely related field;
Or equivalent education, and/or experience


Special Requirements


Possession of a valid Commonwealth of Virginia driver’s license
FEMA ICS-100, ICS 200, ICS 300
FEMA IS-700, IS-800
FEMA IS-29 Public Information Officer Awareness
FEMA E0105 Public Information Basics or equivalent
