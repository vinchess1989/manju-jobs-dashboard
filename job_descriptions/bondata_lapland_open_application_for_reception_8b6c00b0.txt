Submit an open application for a receptionist position in Lapland!

We are seeking receptionists for diverse job tasks in various locations in Lapland. We require applicants to have an independent work approach and a genuine desire to serve customers in the best possible way throughout their visit, even amidst the hustle and bustle. Whether you are newly graduated, fresh in the industry, or an experienced professional with a long background, we have positions available at different levels, including responsibility positions. We expect you to be flexible, have good stress tolerance, and possess a passion for the field. The work involves shift work, and we are also looking for night receptionists.

Job opportunities are estimated to be available from October 2026 to April 2027 for varying lengths of fixed-term contracts, in accordance with the needs of our client companies.

We offer you:

- Responsible and diverse work

- Opportunity to develop your professional skills

- Affordable rent for staff accommodation

- Support from Bondata in employment-related matters throughout the duration of your employment

We expect from you:

- More than 2 year of experience from similar tasks or relevant education

- Fluent English skills, other languages are big plus

- Positive attitude and great team work skills

- Ability to work for whole winter season

How to apply:

Please send your application with your resume in english to us with online application form. Unfortunately, we cant accept email or WhatsApp applications.
