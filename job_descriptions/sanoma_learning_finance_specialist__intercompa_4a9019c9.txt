We are looking for a Finance Specialist for a permanent position to manage intercompany processes within Sanoma Learning.

About the role

In this role, you will be responsible for managing and developing intercompany processes across multiple legal entities within Sanoma Group.

Your core responsibility is to ensure accurate and timely intragroup invoicing and reconciliations, supporting the overall financial closing and reporting processes.

On a monthly basis, you will:

Manage intercompany invoicing processes, ensuring correctness and completeness
Perform intercompany reconciliations, investigating and resolving differences in close collaboration with counterparties
Support the month-end closing process, ensuring intercompany balances are properly recorded and aligned
Ensure that intercompany transactions are correctly reflected in reporting systems (HFM)
Perform key controls related to intercompany transactions

In addition, your responsibilities will include:

Managing intercompany master data (customer and vendor data, trading partners) and monitoring data quality
Creating and validating intercompany invoices, including manual postings and corrections, and monitoring billing flows to accounting
Reviewing intercompany reconciliation differences and ensuring timely posting of counterpart transactions
Coordinating and resolving intercompany disputes and inquiries between entities
Supporting period-end closing by performing intercompany accruals and group-level reconciliations ( HFM reconciliation)
Performing and documenting key internal controls related to intercompany processes
Monitoring and managing system interfaces, identifying and escalating errors and exceptions, and supporting system testing and implementations

You will act as a key contact for intercompany matters, working closely with finance colleagues across countries, as well as controllers and other stakeholders.

In addition:

You will support statutory and tax reporting from an intercompany perspective
Act as a contact point for auditors in intercompany-related topics
Proactively collaborate with stakeholders to ensure smooth and transparent processes

You will also play an important role in:

Improving and harmonizing intercompany processes across the organization
Driving efficiency and automation in invoicing and reconciliation workflows
Participating in finance development initiatives, such as system implementations ( SAP S/4HANA)

About you

To succeed in this role, we are looking for:

Bachelor’s degree in Accounting and/or Finance
Minimum 3–5 years of experience in accounting, preferably with intercompany exposure
Strong understanding of intragroup transactions and reconciliation processes
Basic knowledge of accounting principles (local GAAP; IFRS is a plus)
Experience with SAP/S4HANA or similar ERP systems is an advantage
Strong analytical skills and attention to detail
Proactive mindset with a focus on continuous improvement
Strong communication and collaboration skills in English and Finnish
Ability to work effectively in an international environment
