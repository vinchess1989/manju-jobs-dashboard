Räätälöi ansioluetteloni
KONE is a global leader in the elevator and escalator industry.

In a world where cities are constantly evolving and more and more people choose to live in them, we at KONE stand with one clear aim: to shape the future of cities. We make urban life more vibrant and livable. And we do it by enabling safe, sustainable, and effortless people flow for all. We help cities leave a positive mark on the planet - for the next century and beyond. We shape the future of cities.

The People & Communications team enables business performance and competitiveness through engaged and capable people, strong reputation management, impactful internal and external communications and great employee experience. We bring KONE’s strategy to life, supporting competence, capability and culture development as well as strengthening the KONE purpose and company story for all our stakeholders. By joining our team, you will have the opportunity to work across a diverse range of topics, while also working closely with our businesses, to help drive the People and Communications agenda.

We are looking for a

Business Partner, People & Communications (P&C)

to strengthen our Global People & Communications Business Partner team.

Our team supports the eight global / matrix functions at KONE (Commercial & Operations, Supply Chain, Purchasing, Finance, Legal, Strategy & Transformation, People & Communication, Technology & Innovation).

You will partner with selected leaders and leadership teams, employees and local People & Communications colleagues across multiple countries, driving key people initiatives in a complex, fast-evolving matrix environment.

The preferred location for the position is Finland Espoo/ Hyvinkää or Italy Pero.

What You’ll Do


Act as a trusted advisor to leaders, coaching and developing teams
Lead and support global development projects and change initiatives, including org.design and workforce planning
Run core people processes for selected units (performance, talent, rewards)
Drive data-informed decision-making and cross-functional collaboration in the matrix
Travel occasionally together with the leadership team, meet the employees and build networks between local and global


What You Bring


Relevant degree (BSc/MSc) in HR, Business, or equivalent
Experience in a P&C Business Partner / Generalist / CoE role
Strong project and change management skills, and a developmental mindset
Ability to work independently and navigate ambiguity
Excellent communication and influencing skills
Experience in matrix organizations
Fluency in English
Experience in talent mobility / cross-border relocations / seaside employment is a plus


Why join us


A global, high-impact P&C role with broad exposure
Opportunity to shape people practices in a unique business area
Career growth possibilities within KONE People & Communications
A collaborative environment where your work truly makes a difference
