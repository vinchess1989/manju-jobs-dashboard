Biodiversity created by art, culture and cross-sectoral collaboration is a cross-border collaboration project funded by Interreg Aurora. The project brings together actors from Finland and Sweden to strengthen biodiversity through art-based methods, citizen participation and cross-sectoral collaboration. Novia UAS contributes expertise in art-based and participatory methods and supports the development and piloting of new approaches to citizen engagement and cross-sectoral collaboration.

Responsibilities include


Coordinating and following up Novia UAS’s project activities, including planning, implementation, documentation and reporting
Leading Novia’s work on developing, testing and documenting art-based and participatory methods within the project
Planning and coordinating Novia’s pilot activities together with relevant stakeholders
Contributing to research, analysis and methodological development based on the project activities and pilots
Supporting project partners in applying and evaluating art-based methods in different contexts
Participating in cross-border collaboration and knowledge exchange between Finland and Sweden
Building networks and collaborating with researchers, artists, municipalities, educational institutions and other relevant stakeholders
Communicating and disseminating project activities, methods and results


We are looking for someone who has


A relevant higher education degree
Strong knowledge of and experience in art-based methods and participatory artistic processes
Experience in research, RDI and methodological development
Experience in analysing, documenting and communicating research or development processes and results
An interest in sustainability, biodiversity and the role of art and culture in societal development
Strong organisational, communication and collaboration skills
Experience working across disciplines, sectors and stakeholder groups
An ability to work independently as well as in cross-disciplinary and international teams
Previous experience in EU-funded projects and/or international collaboration is considered an asset
Proficiency in English: Swedish and Finnish considered an asset


Employment

For the period 1.10.2026–28.2.2029 or as agreed.

The position is 50% of full-time working hours.

Salary according to the General collective agreement for Universities of Applied Sciences and internal directives. The position includes a probationary period of six months.

Location

Jakobstad or as agreed. We offer flexible working arrangements.
