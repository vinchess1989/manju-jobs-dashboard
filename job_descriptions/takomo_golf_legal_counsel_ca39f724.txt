Legal Counsel

Takomo Golf - Turku

Julkaistu 15.7. (Päättyy 9.8.)

 
Suosikki
Vahdi vastaavia töitä
Hae työpaikkaa
Työpaikkakuvaus

We are looking for a hands-on Legal Counsel to support the global growth of one of the fastest-growing golf equipment brands in the world.

In this role, you will support, structure and improve how legal work gets done at Takomo. You will work across commercial contracts, supplier and manufacturing agreements, partnerships, employment-related legal coordination, governance support, and external counsel management.

We are looking for someone practical, business-minded, and comfortable working close to the business, someone who helps the company move faster, make better decisions, and avoid unnecessary risk.

Overview

Location: Joukahaisenkatu 1 C, Turku, Finland.

Work Model: Flexible hybrid.

Employment Type: Permanent, Full-time.

Start Date: Flexible / As per agreement.

Takomo is a fast-growing team of over 50 professionals globally. However, the heartbeat of our company is right here in Finland, where the vast majority of our team is based. We are a highly collaborative bunch, and we really want our new team member to be a true part of that local community, daily culture, and shared energy. Because of this, we prefer someone who can regularly collaborate with the team in Finland.

We believe that a great brand is built by a team that genuinely enjoys working together. Here is a look at what life at Takomo really looks like:

Work-Life Balance: Flexible working hours and a hybrid work model. While we value the energy of working together at our Turku office, we offer the flexibility to work remotely when needed.

Growth & Culture: A vibrant, supportive, and international work community where you can directly impact a global brand's trajectory.

Core responsibilities

Draft, review, and negotiate commercial contracts and business agreements

Support supplier, manufacturing, partnership, influencer, ambassador, service, and vendor agreements

Own Takomo’s contract workflow from intake to signature and storage

Build standard templates, approval thresholds, fallback positions, and practical legal playbooks

Coordinate external legal counsel and manage legal work efficiently

Support IP, trademark, brand protection, consumer/e-commerce, employment, governance, and compliance matters 3

Help teams understand legal risk and make better commercial decisions

Build a clear contract repository and improve legal documentation across the company

Escalate strategic, high-value, or high-risk legal matters to leadership when needed

Requirements

Must have

5+ years of relevant legal experience

Experience in international business

Master’s degree in law or equivalent legal qualification

Strong contract drafting, review, and negotiation skills

Solid understanding of commercial contracts and commercial risk

Business-minded and practical approach to legal advice

Ability to work independently and bring structure to fast-moving situations

Strong attention to detail

Comfortable working cross-functionally with commercial, operational, finance, marketing, product, and leadership teams

Nice to have

Experience in consumer products, sports, manufacturing, or international business

Experience with supplier/manufacturing agreements, partnership contracts, or service agreements

Experience with consumer law, product compliance, marketing claims, data protection/GDPR, or international commercial agreements

Experience with US-facing agreements or global commercial operations

Experience with IP, trademarks, licensing, or brand protection matters

Genuine interest in golf, sports, consumer brands, or product-led businesses

What we offer

We want you to feel supported, energized, and valued both on and off the clock. Here is exactly what you get when you join the Takomo team:

The Ultimate Golf Setup: Your very own set of Takomo golf clubs and free golf rounds to help you perfect your game.

Health & Safety: Comprehensive occupational healthcare and work capacity insurance.

Wellness: Epassi benefits for your choice of sports and health activities.

Team Traditions: Monthly breakfasts, after-work hangouts, and our annual company-wide golf trip

A chance to help build the legal operating model of a fast-growing global brand

Ready to join the team at Takomo Golf? Send your CV and application in Finnish or English by August 9th.
