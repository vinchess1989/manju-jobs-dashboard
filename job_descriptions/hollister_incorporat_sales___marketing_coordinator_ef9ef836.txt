Summary

The Marketing Coordinator will be supporting the country organization with projects and activities within sales, marketing and tenders to achieve successful outcomes.

A challenging role with the opportunity to be involved in a multitude of projects.

Responsibilities


Support development of communication deliverables including campaigns, advertisements a.s.o.
Support tender applications
Administer marketing campaigns in CRM system, help measure ROI from campaign activities, track impact of marketing campaign activities in conjunction with
Customer Services
Administer returned patient evaluations and prepare and collate survey monkey evaluation results
Assist and review all website activities, edits, changes
Project manage product migration activities for both brands
Coordinate customer logistics and assist with educational meetings
Coordinate exhibitions
Coordinate internal sales and marketing related meetings
Provide support to sales representatives in line with defined requirements and business specific value propositions
Coordinate purchase orders and invoices
Translations of Global/Nordic marketing materials from
English to the local language
Any additional ad hoc marketing support as required
Back Up for local Customer Service to be able to handle calls and orders
Participate in Nordic or local Sales and Marketing meetings
4 Days in the office


Essential Functions Of The Role


Communicate effectively via email, phone, and virtual platforms.
Collaborate across departments to support organizational goals.
Prepare reports and dashboards for internal stakeholders.
Manage multiple priorities and deadlines in a fast-paced environment.


Education & Work Requirements


Associate's Degree with 2-4 years of related experience


Education & Work Preferences

Competencies


Be Agile - Innovates and adapts quickly, approaching change with curiosity while persisting through obstacles.
Be Customer Centric - Considers the needs, experiences and feedback of customers in all we do.
Be People-Focused - Builds trust and collaborates with an inclusive and empathetic approach.
Be Performance Driven - Operates with an ownership mindset, driving meaningful outcomes.
Live The Schneiders’ Legacy, Our Noble Purpose - Passionately serves Our Mission and Vision, while demonstrating the Immutable Principles.
