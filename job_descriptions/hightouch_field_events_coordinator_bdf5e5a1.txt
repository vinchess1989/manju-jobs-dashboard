About Hightouch

Hightouch is an Agentic Marketing Platform powered by the industry-leading Composable CDP. With complete brand context, customer data, and performance history in one place, every marketer finally has the power to build and ship end-to-end campaigns themselves. Teams move faster, stay on brand, and get AI marketing that actually works.

Founded in 2019 and headquartered in San Francisco, Hightouch enables marketing teams to analyze performance, brainstorm ideas, and generate creative at a speed and quality that wasn't previously possible.

Named a Leader in the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Customer Data Platforms, Hightouch is trusted by leading enterprises like Domino's, Spotify, Aritzia, Cars.com, Ramp, and PetSmart.

At Hightouch, our mission is to help our customers leverage data and AI to grow their businesses. The team is ambitious, impact-driven, efficient — and we believe humility, kindness, and compassion are essential to our success. If you're energized by velocity, obsessed with raising the bar, and want to build alongside people who care deeply about each other and our customers, we'd love to meet you.

About The Role

As our Field Events Coordinator, you will be responsible for executing our field marketing roadmap as we scale our investment and team. You will own the production of trade shows, executive events, and intimate gatherings, customer conferences, and other field marketing activities we are working to activate.

You will work closely with field marketing and growth marketing leadership to develop a roadmap and execution plan for all of our field events in North America. You will also collaborate with our demand generation, design, and product marketing teams to develop themes, creative activations, and content tracks while helping organize executive speakers.

What You’ll Do


Own the execution of field marketing events in North America
Produce trade shows, small dinners, creative field events, customer conferences, customer advisory board events, and executive roundtable events.
Work closely with partners to co-brand and co-sponsor activities
Work closely with event coordinators and trade shows to ensure seamless execution
Project manage multiple events simultaneously


What We’re Looking For


2-4 years of experience working in event production and event coordination
Strong work ethic and attitude
Highly detail-oriented and process-oriented
A creative thinker who can develop interesting concepts and activations and elevate the experience at our events
Strong project management background across many projects and partners simultaneously


The salary range for this position is $90,000 - $120,000 USD per year, which is location-independent in accordance with our remote-first policy. We also offer meaningful equity compensation in the form of ISO options and offer early exercise and a 10-year post-termination exercise window.
