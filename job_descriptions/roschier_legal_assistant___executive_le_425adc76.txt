Do you have a positive can-do attitude and a genuine interest in working in an administrative role and in a team of driven colleagues? We are now looking to find an engaged and service-minded Legal Assistant or Executive Legal Assistant for a fixed-term position in our Assistant team in Helsinki.

Working as an Assistant at Roschier

Roschier is the leading law firm in the Nordic region with offices in Stockholm and Helsinki, with approximately 500 employees. We specialize in business law and cover a broad range of practice areas, and we continue to grow.

Our assistants take the lead in supporting the business in various ways, enabling us as a firm to excel in client experience.

As an Assistant, you will work closely with our partners and lawyers, providing high-quality administrative support and services together with your colleagues in the Assistant team. In addition, Roschier has an extensive Business Operations network to collaborate with.

Your role will include proactive assignment support in client projects, invoicing and matter administration, preparing for meetings and presentations, managing calendars and coordinating travel arrangements. You will also manage documents, administrate client data, and arrange meetings and events.

This opportunity is a full-time and fixed-term position, preferably starting early fall 2026. The fixed-term contract is expected to run for approximately one year, ending towards the end of 2027.

We invest in you

At Roschier, you will be a part of a collaborative and stimulating environment where you can expect to grow on both a personal and professional level in a truly international setting. To ensure your professional development, we offer an extensive learning program through Roschier University, tailored to support you at every step of your professional development and equip you with the professional skills you need to excel in your role as an administrative specialist and in your career. You will be assigned a tutor and a mentor whose role is to support you in learning and navigating your new role, getting to know the team, and welcoming you into Roschier's culture of teamwork, professionalism and client centricity.

Your profile

To succeed in this role, we believe that you are a true team player with a positive and proactive can-do attitude who provides first-class service and support to our partners and lawyers. You are detail-oriented, thrive in a high-paced environment, and strive to walk the extra mile to reach the desired results. You recognize and predict the executive's needs and handle sensitive information with confidentiality, professionalism, and discretion. Since you will provide close support and assistance to our partners and lawyers, prior experience of a similar role is required and previous experience from working at a law firm is an advantage.

Additionally, we believe that you have strong organizing skills, a systematic way of working, and a keen eye for detail that you enjoy using in your everyday work. You have fluent oral and written communication skills in both Finnish and English.

Your application

Please apply by 9th of August through the application link and attach your CV and cover letter. Please note that we only accept applications in English and that all applications should be sent via the application form due to GDPR regulations.

Applications are reviewed on an ongoing basis. However, kindly note that due to summer vacations, the response time can be longer.

Candidates are evaluated based on their professional qualifications and motivation towards the role. Final candidates will be asked to complete an online assessment package, including ability and self-evaluation tests. Reference checks will be conducted on shortlisted candidates.

Want to know more?

For more information, please contact Evelina Norén, Senior Talent Acquisition Specialist (evelina.noren@roschier.com, +46 73-069 50 20).
