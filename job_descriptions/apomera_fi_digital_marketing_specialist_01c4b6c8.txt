Digital Marketing Specialist




We are a growing scale-up in the online pharmacy and wellness sector in Finland, located in Herttoniemi. To support our growth, we are looking for a Digital Marketing Specialist to join our small but ambitious team.




This is a hands-on role where you’ll get to work on a wide variety of digital marketing tasks: writing and sending newsletters, building customer journeys, scaling our marketing automation, supporting commercial planning, creating content, posting on selected digital channels, and ensuring our digital paid marketing stays aligned across all touchpoints. It’s a chance to learn quickly and broaden your skills across the full spectrum of digital marketing by taking ownership, trying new things, and learning through hands-on work.




We value your attitude, curiosity, and willingness to learn more than a long list of past achievements. If you are eager to take ownership and grow your skills fast, this role is for you!




Your key focus areas

Help us build stronger customer relationships and encourage repeat purchases through relevant CRM and email marketing.
Plan, write, edit, and send newsletters to different customer groups - making sure each message feels useful, timely, and relevant.
Create and improve automated email flows, such as welcome emails, abandoned cart reminders, post-purchase messages, and re-engagement campaigns.
Use customer and campaign data to understand what content and offers work best, then suggest practical improvements.
Support the customer journey across our digital channels (esp. Google & Meta) by helping ensure that messaging is clear, consistent, and customer-friendly.
Write and review commercial marketing copy in Finnish, with AI tools available to support your work.
Create and edit simple visual content for newsletters and digital campaigns using tools such as Canva, Photoshop, or similar.
Be ready to help with other day-to-day tasks that come up in a small, growing company - no two days are exactly the same!




What we expect from you

Higher education (University / AMK) in business or marketing.
High interest in data-driven marketing and growth.
A fast learner with the ability to pick up new tools and methods independently.
Comfortable working in a fast-paced scale-up with low hierarchies and changing priorities.
Native or bi-lingual Finnish speaker with strong writing skills. Swedish is considered a plus.
At least basic knowledge of image handling (Photoshop, Canva, or similar).
At least 2–3 years of experience in digital marketing, ideally CRM. 
Familiarity with one or more of the following: eCommerce, digital marketing, CRM, marketing automation, SEO and/or web analytics.




What we offer

A chance to join an ambitious scale-up where your work has a direct impact.
Hands-on responsibility of marketing tasks from day one.
The opportunity to grow into a more senior marketing or commercial role as we scale.







To apply, send us a short but effective motivational note in your email, titled “Digital marketing specialist”, along with your CV to our CEO Sanna Haapaniemi (sanna.haapaniemi@apomera.fi). The expected salary range is 3000-4000 EUR/month + benefits, depending on the skills and experience of the candidate. We’ll move forward as soon as we find the right candidate! If you have any questions about the role before applying, you can reach Sanna on the following call slots: Sept 17 at 14-15 or Sept 23 at 13-14 (+358 503 793 442).
