Räätälöi ansioluetteloni
QUINCY, IL | Klingner & Associates, P.C. is seeking an Office Assistant.Do you enjoy working with others? Are you great at multi-tasking? Join a dynamic team where your abilities and dedication are valued. As an Office Assistant, you’ll help keep the Quincy Regional Office running smoothly and contribute to meaningful projects in our community.

Responsibilities


Welcome all visitors at the front desk of the Corporate / Quincy Regional Office in a friendly and helpful manner
Answer and direct incoming calls firmwide alongside Klingner’s other administrative assistants
Provide support to Senior Leadership Executives by managing calendars, scheduling appointments, arranging travel as needed, and various other tasks as needed
Plan, organize, and execute various corporate outings and events.
Prepare memos, letters, reports, presentations, and other correspondence as needed
Assist professional staff and project administrative staff with various project related activities
Assist Fleet Manager, HR Manager, Talent Acquisition Committee, Marketing Department, and Accounting Department as needed.
Assist with scanning, printing, and copying jobs as needed
Organize and maintain digital and physical filing systems
Prepare and archive project related files
Provide backup assistance to office receptionist
Perform other duties as needed


NOTE: This is a full-time position with the hours of 8:00am – 5:00pm.

Qualifications


High School diploma or equivalent required; 2+ years of college is preferred
5+ years of experience in a secretarial, administrative assistant, or project coordinator position is preferred
Proficient in all Microsoft Office applications, particularly Microsoft Excel and Word
Experience working with voice over IP phone systems software is a plus, but not required
Excellent verbal and written communication skills are required (including grammar, punctuation, proofreading, spelling, interpersonal, and telephone)
Strong organizational skills, efficient time management, and a great ability to understand task directions is essential in this role
Ability to work and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment with competing priorities
Must have a professional disposition and present a business casual appearance
Knowledge of the A/E industry is a plus


Salary Range: $19-$23

The above salary range is industry standard and based on candidate qualifications.

This position is not eligible for immigration sponsorship.
