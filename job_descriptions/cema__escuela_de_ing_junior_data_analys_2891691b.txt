🗂 We’re Hiring: Junior Data Analyst

🕒 Employment Type: Full-Time

💼 Level: Entry-Level / Junior

We are seeking a detail-oriented and analytical Junior Data Analyst to join our team. This role is ideal for recent graduates or early-career professionals who are passionate about data analysis, reporting, and transforming information into meaningful insights that support business growth and decision-making.

🎯 Key Responsibilities

• Collect, clean, validate, and organize data from multiple internal and external sources.

• Analyze datasets to identify trends, patterns, anomalies, and business opportunities.

• Prepare reports, dashboards, and visualizations to support operational and strategic decision-making.

• Assist in developing and maintaining business intelligence (BI) reports and analytics solutions.

• Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and business performance metrics.

• Perform data validation and quality assurance checks to ensure data accuracy and consistency.

• Support ad-hoc reporting, data extraction, and analytical requests from stakeholders.

• Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand reporting requirements and business objectives.

• Document data processes, methodologies, reporting standards, and analytical procedures.

• Assist in process improvement initiatives through data-driven recommendations and insights.

• Support data governance, compliance, and data quality management activities.

• Stay informed about analytics tools, reporting technologies, and industry best practices.

✅ Requirements

• Basic understanding of data analysis, statistics, and reporting concepts.

• Proficiency in Microsoft Excel, including formulas, pivot tables, charts, and data analysis functions.

• Familiarity with SQL for querying and extracting data is preferred.

• Knowledge of Power BI, Tableau, Looker Studio, or other data visualization tools is an advantage.

• Understanding of databases, data structures, and data management concepts.

• Basic knowledge of Python, R, or other analytical tools is a plus.

• Strong analytical, critical-thinking, and problem-solving abilities.

• Excellent attention to detail and commitment to data accuracy.

• Good communication and presentation skills.

• Ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines effectively.

• Bachelor’s degree or diploma in Data Analytics, Statistics, Mathematics, Economics, Computer Science, Business Analytics, Information Systems, or a related field is preferred.

🌟 What We Offer

• Hands-on experience working with real-world business and operational data.

• Exposure to business intelligence, reporting, analytics, and data visualization projects.

• Mentorship from experienced analysts and data professionals.

• Career progression into Data Analyst, Business Analyst, BI Analyst, Reporting Analyst, Data Engineer, Analytics Specialist, or Data Scientist roles.

• Training, certification support, and professional development opportunities.

• A collaborative, data-driven, and growth-oriented work environment.

• Competitive compensation and comprehensive benefits package.
