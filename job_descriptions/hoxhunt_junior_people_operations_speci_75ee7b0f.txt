Our mission and why it matters

We are on a mission to make humans the strongest security layer.

Human risk remains one of the biggest vulnerabilities and traditional awareness training is not enough. We take a different approach by combining AI-driven personalization, real threat detection, and behavioral science to actively protect people and organizations.

We don't just simulate risks. We build the tools that detect and stop them.

Why this role matters

It's Monday morning in Helsinki. The coffee is on, the weekly order is in, and a new Hoxhunter is walking through the door for their first day with everything ready for them. On Friday, the team gathers for an office get together you helped plan. None of this happens by itself, and that's where you come in.

As our Junior People Operations Specialist, you will own how it feels to come to work at Hoxhunt in Helsinki. You will keep our new office running smoothly, bring the team together through culture activities and events, and be the first point of contact when colleagues need help.

This role suits someone who enjoys both sides of the job: the structured weekly routines that keep everything running, and the people moments that make it a great place to be. You will do it in a fast growing global scale-up where your ideas make a real impact. As you grow in the role, you will take on more responsibility and ownership across People Operations.

What You’ll Own And Drive


The Helsinki office experience: coordinate daily office operations, handle weekly orders and suppliers, and keep our new office spaces welcoming and working the way they should
Office culture: run our culture group and coordinate office parties, events and activities that make Hoxhunt a great place to be, and be the main voice of our Helsinki office channel
Employee questions & queries: Handle employee questions and queries via Slack or via our People Ops ticketing system
The employee lifecycle: support and coordinate pre-boarding, onboarding and off-boarding activities, and keep employee data up to date in our HRIS
Documentation and task lists: help keep our operations task lists and process documentation clear and up to date
Wider admin team collaboration: work closely with our Finance and IT teams


What Success Looks Like

In your first 3 months you'll: Own daily office coordination and orders, handle questions directed to the People Ops with confidence, and become the Helsinki team's go to person.

By month 6 you'll: Run the culture group and office activities independently, manage the ticketing system and employee questions on your own, and spot office and team needs before anyone has to ask.

You Have

What makes you thrive here


1 to 2 years of experience in office coordination, office assistance, back office work, event or culture coordination, or a similar role
Strong organisational skills: comfortable managing a variety of tasks, and you enjoy structure and attention to detail, including the weekly routines that keep an office running
A proactive, ownership driven attitude: you take things on without being asked and think ahead about what will be needed next
The ability to adapt and think on your feet when plans change
The courage to speak up, suggest ideas and try new things
Strong communication skills, genuine care for people, and you enjoy creating a welcoming atmosphere where others feel at home
Fluent spoken and written English


You don't need previous HR experience to apply. We will teach you the HR admin side. What matters most is that you love making an office and a team work well.

Bonus Points If You Also


Have experience from a fast growing scale-up or startup environment
Have previously worked with HRIS


Who You’ll Work With

You will join our supportive People team, reporting to our People Operations Manager and working closely with our global People Operations team and the wider People team. We are a small, fast moving team that cares a lot about how things are done at Hoxhunt, and we will back you up while you learn the ropes. Your ideas are welcome from day one, and your presence will be at the centre of daily life in our Helsinki office.

What You Can Expect From Us


Compensation: Monthly salary of €3,300 to €3,900 depending on your experience.
Working ways: This is a full time role. We work in a flexible, hybrid setting, but the heart of this role is in the office, so you will be a consistent, welcoming presence in our Helsinki office.
High performance meets high humanity: We bring an incredibly driven, high-impact energy to our work, but we remain deeply humble and team-focused. You will be surrounded by talented, dedicated colleagues in an environment built on kindness, support, and psychological safety.
Authentic trust & autonomy: We hire great people and trust them to do great work. You will find a culture free of micromanagement, giving you the autonomy to take real ownership, drive impact, and shape things early on.
A product you can be proud of: It is rare in cybersecurity to build a product that end users genuinely love. You will join a fast-paced team making a real, measurable impact against cybercrime.
The perks that matter: Alongside this amazing community, you will enjoy extensive healthcare with other benefits and our beautiful new office spaces in Helsinki (complete with a gym and swimming pool!).

About Hoxhunt

Hoxhunt was founded in 2016 by four visionaries. Today we are a global team of +270 amazing Hoxhunters advancing a truly AI-native category leader in human risk management, with key hubs in the United States, the United Kingdom, Singapore, and Finland. We are proud to be an award-winning, fast-growing software company, recognized by G2 and Gartner, named to TIME Magazine's list of the World's Top EdTech Companies, and featured for our innovation in major publications like Fast Company, TechCrunch, Forbes, and Inc.
