Research and Strategy Coordinator﻿

Department: Strategy

Reports to: Head of Strategy

Location: Fully remote (Eastern time zone preferred)

Type: Full-time 

Salary Range: $60-65K/year







ABOUT BELLWEATHER




Bellweather is a creative agency for legacy brands: the nonprofits, cultural institutions, healthcare systems, and government organizations that are part of people’s lives. We’ve spent a decade helping these organizations find what makes them matter and bringing it to life through immersive research, artistic collaboration, and creative work that doesn’t look or feel like anyone else’s.




Our clients include Brooklyn Public Library, MetroPlusHealth, Museum Hue, NYC Votes, and Visit Baltimore, among others. We're a certified woman-owned business and a Webby Award–winning agency. 




Our work starts with immersive research and deep listening. We spend time in communities, collaborating with artists, poets, photographers, and filmmakers to find what makes an organization extraordinary. From there, we build strategic platforms and brand identities that our clients can own and carry forward long after the engagement ends.




ABOUT THE NAME




Our name combines "bellwether" (a leading indicator) and "weather" (constant change). We help organizations find what makes them extraordinary and give it a platform that lasts.




HOW WE WORK




Bellweather is a fully remote company. Our team is distributed across the U.S., with most of our collaboration happening in Eastern time. We've built our workflows, meetings, and creative process around remote work from the start.




______________________________




THE ROLE




Bellweather's in-house strategy team runs the qualitative and quantitative research that shapes our clients' brand and creative work, from public-health-informed interviews and focus groups to large-scale surveys. The Research & Strategy Coordinator supports the Head of Strategy in planning and running these studies day-to-day, keeping projects organized and moving from initial outreach to final findings.




Day-to-day, this person schedules and coordinates interviews and focus groups, manages data collection and recruitment, and keeps research files, transcripts, and survey data organized. They also help build the studies themselves — drafting surveys and interview scripts, coding and analyzing qualitative transcripts, and supporting the data analysis and visualization that inform strategy and creative presentations.




This role works closely with the Director of Research and collaborates with project managers, external quantitative research partners, and the wider strategy and creative teams. Within the first six months, success looks like independently running the logistics of a research study and contributing substantive analysis and synthesis to the findings presented to clients.




______________________________




WHAT YOU’LL DO




Coordinate with project managers and participants to schedule interviews and focus groups
Coordinate with external quantitative research partners, including uploading and managing surveys
Manage data collection and recruitment for research studies
Organize research files and materials, including transcripts, release forms, survey data, and website analytics
Synthesize research findings, including best practices and literature reviews
Support data analysis and visualization for strategy and creative presentations
Develop surveys and interview scripts for focus groups and one-on-one interviews
Code and analyze qualitative transcripts
Contribute to data analysis and report writing across primary and secondary research studies
Contribute to website analytics reports in collaboration with the Digital team
Conduct peer and competitor audits for client projects
Synthesize client-provided documents with primary and/or secondary research to assist with company, consumer, and culture assessments
Attend and participate in internal agency and external client meetings




______________________________




REQUIREMENTS﻿﻿




Minimum BA/BS in psychology, sociology, anthropology, marketing, communication, public health, or a related field
2+ years of experience in a role emphasizing research, writing, and/or analysis
Experience distilling and presenting results, insights, and findings into reports and presentations
Experience with scheduling and coordination as well as data collection and management
Proficiency in Google Suite, MS Word, Excel, and PowerPoint
Excellent writing skills and strong interpersonal, communication, and collaboration skills
Ability to work independently and manage multiple projects simultaneously




NICE TO HAVE




Advanced degree in Health Communications or another behavioral or social science field
Experience with a range of qualitative research methods and analysis techniques
Understanding of quantitative research methods (e.g. surveying and questionnaire design)  and basic statistics
Ability to travel for occasional in-person work and meetings




______________________________




WHAT WE OFFER




Comprehensive health benefits package including medical, dental, and vision coverage.
Unlimited/Flexible PTO
Fully remote work environment with flexible scheduling, subject to change based on organizational needs.
