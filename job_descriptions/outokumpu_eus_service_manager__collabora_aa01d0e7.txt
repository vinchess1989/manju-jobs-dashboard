EUS Service Manager (Collaboration)
Location:  

Helsinki, FI

We are looking for a Service Manager – End User Services, Collaboration to take responsibility for managing and developing collaboration services in our End User Services area.

 

In this role, you will be responsible for the overall service management, continuous improvement and further development of services and solutions within the Microsoft 365 Collaboration area. You will oversee End User Services more broadly, while specializing in collaboration technologies such as Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, Exchange Online and related Microsoft 365 services.

 

You will act as a key point of contact for business stakeholders and vendors regarding IT topics within your service area, ensuring that services are reliable, secure, cost-efficient and continuously improved.

 

Your responsibilities

Managing daily IT service operations, service performance, availability and continuous improvement within End User Services and the Collaboration area
Acting as the single point of contact for business stakeholders and vendors, including vendor management and third-party service contract follow-up
Preparing and following up service budgets, forecasts, actuals, software license optimization and budget compliance
Managing Microsoft 365 tenant administration, including licensing, governance, access management, policies, reporting, security and compliance topics
Planning and supporting project and change deliveries, including rollouts, adoption, communication, user training and CAB participation/chairing
Maintaining service structure, CMDB accuracy, documentation and knowledge articles within the service area
Mentoring specialists, sharing knowledge and building operational capability within the End User Services team

 

What we are looking for

B.Sc. or M.Sc. degree in Computer Science, IT or equivalent industry experience, with at least 5 years of experience in Collaboration Technologies and IT Operations
ITIL Foundation certification or similar practical Service Management experience
Strong Microsoft 365 knowledge, including Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, Exchange Online, Entra ID/Azure AD, FileShares, security and compliance
Fluent English skills, both spoken and written; other languages are considered an advantage
Analytical, proactive and hands-on approach, with excellent cooperation, communication and stakeholder management skills


What Outokumpu offers

A key role in a global company and modern IT environment
Opportunity to develop and improve global end user services
Collaborative colleagues and support from the wider IT organization
Opportunities for learning, knowledge sharing and professional growth
Flexible working practices, including hybrid work

 

For more information about the position, please contact Head of End User Services, Satu Nuutinen via phone on 13 August between 9:00–10:00. +358503442512 

 

Please submit your application by 30 August. We look forward to hearing from you!

 

The selected candidate will be required to pass a security clearance vetting and health check (including drug tests) before commencing employment
