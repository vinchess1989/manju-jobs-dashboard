Flexible Location | Fully Remote or Hybrid | Sydney preferred

Are you a professional with a law degree who is ready to step beyond traditional practice and make a real impact?

Join a team that is reimagining how organisations interpret and apply regulation—combining legal expertise, technology, and industry insight to help customers navigate complexity with confidence.

This is not your typical “back-office” content role. You’ll be front and centre, shaping industry-leading regulatory content, engaging directly with customers, and influencing how the Education sector approaches compliance.

Why this role stands out


Work from anywhere across Australia – Sydney, Brisbane, Canberra or fully remote
True flexibility – flexible hours with autonomy to structure your day
High-impact work – influence how major organisations meet regulatory obligations
Cutting-edge environment – combine law, AI, and innovation to deliver smarter solutions
Global exposure – collaborate across international teams and markets
Career growth – expand into thought leadership, stakeholder engagement, and product strategy


About The Role

You’ll take ownership of regulatory content within the Property, Construction & Infrastructure sector, ensuring it is accurate, practical, and tailored to both legal and non-legal audiences.

You’ll Operate At The Intersection Of


Legal research and interpretation
Content development and product design
Customer and industry engagement


This is a highly visible role where you’ll engage directly with clients, present insights, and shape the future of regulatory content.

What You’ll Be Doing


Own and evolve a portfolio of regulatory content across the Property, Construction, Infrastructure & Environment sector
Translate complex legislation into clear, practical guidance for customers
Monitor legislative change, regulatory developments, and industry trends
Engage with customers, regulators, and subject matter experts to shape content
Lead content projects from concept through to delivery
Support product, sales, and marketing teams with insights and content expertise
Represent the business at events, webinars, and industry forums


Who We’re Looking For


A professional with a law degree (essential) and/or a legal background
Strong legal research and drafting skills
Ability to simplify complex concepts for non-legal audiences
Interest or experience in Property, Construction, Infrastructure & Environment, compliance, or regulatory environments
If you have experience in Commercial Leasing, Environment or Facilities Management - please apply also!
Confident communicator – comfortable engaging with clients and presenting ideas
Highly organised with strong project management skills
A proactive, curious mindset with a genuine interest in the Education sector


You don’t need to be a practising lawyer—many of the team have transitioned from legal backgrounds into impactful content and product roles.

What’s In It For You


Flexible working that actually works – remote-first with optional office access
Autonomy and trust – manage your own workload and priorities
Purpose-driven work – help organisations stay compliant and operate with confidence
Innovative environment – work with AI-driven tools and forward-thinking teams
Supportive, collaborative culture – intelligent, down-to-earth team with diverse backgrounds
Wellbeing and lifestyle benefits, including additional leave, wellness initiatives, and learning opportunities


About The Business

Join a global leader in legal and regulatory information, known for transforming how organisations access and use critical insights to make better decisions and stay ahead of change.
