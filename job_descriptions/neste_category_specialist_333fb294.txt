We are looking for you,

Category Specialist, Chemicals and Catalysts

to our Global Procurement team

You will support end-to-end category management within Chemicals and Catalysts. In this job you will work closely with Neste colleagues such as Category Leads, Legal Counsels and Operations representatives globally.

In our team, you will collaborate on strategic sourcing initiatives, drive supplier performance, and contribute to the overall efficiency and effectiveness of our Chemicals and Catalysts procurement processes.

Your role


Market Analysis : Analyze Chemicals and catalysts markets relevant to our core spend categories.
Supplier Analysis : Evaluate specific companies within these markets, conducting tailored analyses to understand sourcing alternatives, supplier profiles, cost structures, and offerings.
Spend Analytics : Perform historical and predictive spend analysis to identify savings opportunities and develop improvement strategies.
Contract and Pricing Management : Maintain up-to-date category contracts and pricing, develop automation where possible. Manage smaller purchase requisition (PR) to purchase order (PO) processes.
Supplier Relationship Management (SRM) Support : Contribute to SRM activities by providing data on supplier supply, quality, and cost performance. Ensure supply continuity with appropriate quality and pricing, including managing supplier claims.
Strategic Sourcing Participation : Engage together with the category leads in strategic sourcing initiatives, savings projects, contract negotiations, and supplier relationship management, as well as related reporting.
Stakeholder and Supplier Engagement : Build and maintain strong relationships with key suppliers and internal stakeholders jointly with category leads, fostering collaboration and driving continuous improvement.


The best combination to succeed in this role


Analytical and Financial Capabilities : Strong analytical skills, and a solid understanding of basic financial analysis.
Educational Background : A Bachelor's or Master's degree in Engineering or Business.
Relevant Work Experience : Proven experience in roles related to material procurement. Working knowledge of chemicals will be an added advantage.
Language Proficiency : Fluency in English and preferably the local language of the hiring location.
AI tools: Experience to use and adapt with AI.
SAP working knowledge .


Other Strengths We Value


Global Experience : Experience managing global environments and suppliers.
Negotiation and Collaboration Skills : Experience in negotiation management, strong partnership skills, and high ethical standards, coupled with agreement-making and project management abilities.
Proactive and Results-Oriented Approach : A self-starting and completion-focused work style with a strong sense of urgency and a relentless drive for results.
Problem-Solving Abilities : Strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities.
Adaptability : The ability to work effectively in a fast-paced and dynamic environment.


Your location will be in Finland at our Espoo HQ.
