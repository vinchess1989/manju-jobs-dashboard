Company Description

Growthline is an e-commerce growth consultancy. We work with online brands on CRO, SEO, paid ads, email automation and analytics. Real clients, real results, no fluff.




Role Description

Project based, hourly paid role working directly with our clients. You'll do outbound prospecting, help with CRO and growth work on live stores, and jump into paid ads, email flows or analytics depending on what's needed. Real client facing responsibility from early on. Pay is hourly per project, with equity on the table for the right people as we grow.
