Do you want to help bring Nordea’s marketing and communication activities to life for large corporate and institutional customers? We are looking for a Marketing Specialist to join LC&I Marketing & Communication in Helsinki in a fixed-term role starting in September 2026.

At Nordea, we know that great customer experiences start with engaged people. We are building a workplace where people can grow, collaborate and make an impact. We believe that an inclusive workplace is a sustainable workplace, and we welcome people with different backgrounds, experiences and perspectives. So come as you are.

About This Opportunity

You will join LC&I Marketing & Communication, a Nordic team of 14 colleagues working to strengthen Nordea’s position among large corporates and institutions. We create marketing and communication that supports business priorities, builds relationships and delivers relevant, high-quality content across channels.

In this role, you will support the team with hands-on marketing execution and day-to-day coordination across a range of activities. You will work closely with marketing, communication and event specialists across the Nordics.


Create and adapt content for marketing activities, including text, presentations, simple visuals, podcasts and other supporting materials
Publish and optimise content across owned channels, including web and social media
Coordinate marketing activities based on agreed themes, plans and deadlines
Support webinar planning and execution together with internal stakeholders and external partners
Maintain planning documents, shared materials and other administrative support for the team
Contribute to ad hoc marketing and communication tasks as needed


Who You Are

Collaboration. Ownership. Passion. Courage. These are the values that guide us at Nordea, and we hope they resonate with you too.

You are structured, proactive and comfortable working in a role where both quality and coordination matter. You enjoy collaborating with others, managing multiple tasks at once and making sure the details are right. You are curious, eager to learn and motivated by delivering work that supports both the team and the business.

To succeed in this role, we believe you have:


Experience from digital marketing across several platforms and formats
Fluency in English and Finnish, with strong written and verbal communication skills
The ability to create and adapt content in line with brand, tone of voice and channel requirements
Hands-on experience with social media, especially LinkedIn
Confidence working in a CMS and optimising content for different digital channels
A strong eye for detail and a careful approach to publishing and quality assurance
An analytical mindset and familiarity with web analytics or performance follow-up
A flexible, hands-on attitude and willingness to take on both practical and creative tasks


If this sounds like you, get in touch!

Next steps

Submit your application no later than 13/07/2026.
