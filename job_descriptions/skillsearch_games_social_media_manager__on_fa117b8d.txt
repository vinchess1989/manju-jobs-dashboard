Social Media Manager

Location: Helsinki, Finland (onsite)




Start: 1 October 2026

Contract: 12 months

We’re looking for an experienced Social Media Manager to join a leading mobile games team and take ownership of social channels across YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Threads and more.

You’ll be responsible for turning content strategy into platform-specific plans, owning publishing end-to-end, creating reactive and trend-led content, and making sure everything player-facing feels authentic to the brand.




What you’ll be doing:

Own social content calendars, publishing, copywriting and quality control
Create platform-native, trend-focused content, with a particular focus on TikTok
Coordinate the content pipeline from ideas and briefs through production and publishing
Work closely with Community, Marketing, Production and Analytics teams
Align social plans with updates, LiveOps and major in-game beats
Monitor performance, identify trends and experiment with new formats
Feed learnings back into channel strategies and playbooks

What we’re looking for:

5+ years running social channels at scale for a games, entertainment or creator-focused brand
Experience owning channels with millions of followers
Strong understanding of TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Reddit
Excellent English copywriting and a strong instinct for brand voice and internet culture
Comfortable creating and publishing reactive content at speed
Highly organised across multiple platforms and concurrent campaigns
Autonomous, proactive and comfortable working cross-functionally
Genuine enthusiasm for games and online gaming communities

Helsinki | 12-month contract | 1 October start

If you’re interested, or know someone who could be a great fit, feel free to get in touch.
