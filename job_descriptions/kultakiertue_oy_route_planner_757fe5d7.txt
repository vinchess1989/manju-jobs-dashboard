Räätälöi ansioluetteloni
How does a key role in a rapidly growing Finnish success story sound? At Kultakiertue, we are the market leader and pioneer in the purchasing and evaluation of precious metals, especially gold and silver. Our safe, reliable and truly customer-oriented service model will soon operate in all Nordic countries, and later this year also elsewhere in Europe. More than 100 professionals already work within our unique service concept — and our growth is only accelerating. Now is the perfect time to join the Kultakiertue journey!

As our expansion across Europe continues, we are looking for Route Planners to join our team at our office in Käpylä. Previous experience in route planning is not required. What matters most is your willingness to learn, strong logical thinking skills, and motivation to develop in the role. We will provide you with comprehensive training and the support you need to succeed.

Key Responsibilities


Planning and developing routes for our tour vehicles in designated target countries
Taking responsibility for the overall route planning, with a focus on efficiency and optimal use of resources
Managing and understanding complex planning processes while working closely with colleagues operating in the field


What We Are Looking For


Excellent English skills are required. Finnish language skills are considered a plus
Strong logical reasoning skills and the ability to structure and process information effectively
Strong Microsoft Office skills, particularly Excel, including tables, formulas, filters, and pivot tables
Accuracy, a systematic approach, strong organizational skills, and a positive attitude
A genuine willingness to learn and develop new skills
Good mathematical reasoning skills are considered an advantage
Previous experience living or working in one of our target countries, such as the United Kingdom, Spain, or Ireland, is considered an advantage


What We Offer


A supportive and open working environment where everyone's contribution is valued
Comprehensive onboarding and training for the role
The opportunity to make a real impact, as your work directly contributes to the success and growth of the company
Opportunities to develop professionally and progress into roles with greater responsibility
Meaningful work in a respected and trusted company that is committed to long term development and high quality operations


Additional Information And How To Apply

The salary for the position is 3024 € monthly.

Interested in joining us? Send us your CV and application as soon as possible. We review applications throughout the application period.

Application Period and Start Date

The application period closes on 28.9.2026 . The preferred start date is as soon as possible.
