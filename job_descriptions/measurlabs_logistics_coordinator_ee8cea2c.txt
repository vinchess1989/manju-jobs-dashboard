Measurlabs is a growth-stage company delivering scientific laboratory analyses, materials characterization, and testing services to international clients in 70+ countries. We collaborate with a global network of hundreds of partner laboratories, bringing best-in-class testing services within easy reach of our customers. Almost every company developing, manufacturing, or importing physical products relies on laboratory testing—whether for R&D, quality control, regulatory compliance, or certification. At Measurlabs, we simplify this complexity. Our clients gain access to a vast catalog of testing services, supported by approachable, solution-oriented technical experts who help them solve real-world measurement challenges efficiently and reliably.




The Role




We are looking for a proactive Logistics Coordinator to join our team! This is a permanent position based at our office in Vallila, Helsinki. In this role, you will primarily work with receiving and sending various kinds of material and chemical samples. You will receive samples sent by our clients, register them in our system, prepare the samples (including subsampling, labelling and repacking), and ship them for analysis to our partner laboratories around the world. You will take ownership of resolving any shipping issues, including clearing customs. You will collaborate mostly with our Experts team, the shipping partners we use, and sometimes our clients and laboratories. The key to success in this role is having a strong can-do attitude, keeping samples moving and troubleshooting whenever necessary.




We require your presence at our Vallila office from Monday to Friday. The working hours are either from 8:00 to 16:00 or from 08:30 to 16:30 on alternating weeks.
