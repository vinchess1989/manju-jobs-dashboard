Join the team of bold professionals at Enersense! We are now hiring a Project Planner for a permanent position. The preferred work location would be Pori or Rauma but working from other Enersense offices or remotely is also possible.




The open position is within our Energy Transition business unit, where we help our customers improve the reliability of their production facilities and enhance maintenance operations. We deliver demanding project implementations for industrial projects. We develop digital solutions to improve productivity, manage the overall maintenance and reliability of our customers' production plants, and provide resource and contracting services as well as subcontracting chain management services for domestic and international industrial projects.




Job responsibilities:

Create and manage detailed master construction schedules and project control procedures for live projects
Prepare and present progress updates and performance reports to senior management
Manage your assigned project, ensuring progress is on track while assisting other team members as needed
Liaise with project stakeholders to review progress, identify risks, and proactively solve issues
Develop schedules for the pre-construction, construction, and commissioning phases, ensuring progress reporting is consistently updated
Establish resource requirements for project schedules and communicate tools for achieving project goals
Provide performance updates and reports, identifying variances and taking corrective actions as needed




Requirements:

Degree in Engineering, Construction Management or a related technical field
At least 2 years of relevant planning experience in industrial projects
Strong analytical and logical thinking skills, combined with excellent organizational skills
Good IT skills, including proficiency in using project scheduling tools, mainly Primavera P6
Ability to work independently with a strong sense of responsibility
Strong communication skills and the ability to collaborate with project stakeholders
Experience in managing challenging timelines and delivering results under pressure
Fluent English is required, Finnish is considered as an advantage




The application period ends on 30.8.2026. However, please send your application as soon as possible, as we may begin interviews during the application period and fill the position once the right candidate is found.




For more information about the position, please contact: Jonna Katavisto, Talent Acquisition Partner, +358 44 360 6783, jonna.katavisto@enersense.com. Call times Fri 14.8. between 11-12 and Wed 24.8. between 14-15.
