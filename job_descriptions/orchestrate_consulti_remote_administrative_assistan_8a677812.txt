Orchestrate Consulting's mission is to help job seekers access carefully coordinated opportunities that match their career goals. We are not a staffing firm or agency. Orchestrate Consulting does not hire for these roles—we curate and validate them from verified employers.

The Remote Administrative Assistant supports ASI Instruments by managing daily administrative operations, coordinating communication, and ensuring smooth workflow across departments. This role is ideal for a detail-oriented, tech-savvy professional who thrives in a fast-paced environment and can work independently while staying aligned with company goals.

Key Responsibilities

Administrative & Operational Support

Manage calendars, schedule meetings, and coordinate virtual appointments for leadership and project teams.

Prepare, edit, and organize documents, reports, and presentations.

Maintain digital filing systems and ensure records are accurate and up to date.

Assist with procurement tasks such as vendor communication, quote requests, and purchase order preparation.

Communication & Customer Support

Serve as a primary point of contact for internal and external inquiries.

Draft professional emails, memos, and correspondence on behalf of ASI Instruments.

Support customer service by responding to inquiries, routing messages, and ensuring timely follow-up.

Data & Systems Management

Enter, update, and verify data in company databases, CRM systems, and inventory platforms.

Generate routine reports and summaries for management review.

Monitor project timelines and follow up on outstanding tasks.

Logistics & Coordination

Assist with shipping documentation, order tracking, and coordination with logistics partners.

Support inventory management tasks such as updating stock levels and preparing documentation for incoming/outgoing materials.

Special Projects

Provide administrative support for engineering, sales, and operations teams as needed.

Assist with research, vendor comparisons, and documentation for compliance or quality assurance.

Required Skills & Qualifications

Proven experience as an administrative assistant, office coordinator, or similar role.

Strong proficiency with Microsoft Office, cloud storage, and communication tools.

Excellent written and verbal communication skills.

High attention to detail and strong organizational abilities.

Ability to work independently, manage multiple tasks, and meet deadlines.

Comfortable handling confidential information with discretion.

Preferred Qualifications

Experience in manufacturing, engineering, or instrumentation industries.

Familiarity with CRM systems, ERP platforms, or inventory management tools.

Ability to learn technical terminology related to ASI Instruments' products.

Work Environment

Fully remote position with flexible scheduling depending on business needs.

Collaboration through email, video conferencing, and project management platforms.

Occasional availability required for time-sensitive tasks or urgent communication.

Performance Indicators

Timeliness and accuracy of administrative tasks.

Quality of communication and customer interactions.

Reliability in supporting team operations and meeting deadlines.

Ability to anticipate needs and proactively solve problems.
