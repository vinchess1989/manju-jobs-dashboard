Associate Analyst - Consumer Insight




Nepa's Customer Success team helps our clients grow their brands and make better marketing decisions. We take full responsibility for everything we deliver, from insight design and execution to recommendations and follow-through, ensuring that every project creates real, measurable impact for our clients' businesses.

As an Associate Analyst, you are a key member of our client teams and, together with experienced colleagues, take ownership of client projects and insight deliveries that turn consumer insight into business impact.




About the role

As an Associate Analyst, you'll play a central role in delivering high-quality client projects. Combining analytical expertise with project ownership, you'll work closely with clients to understand their business challenges, analyse survey-led insights into consumer attitudes, behaviour, brand and marketing performance, and translate findings into clear, actionable recommendations that help brands grow.

Together with experienced colleagues, you'll lead insight projects and coordinate projects from planning to delivery, ensuring high-quality execution and an excellent client experience. Along the way, you'll strengthen your analytical expertise, consulting skills and project leadership while working with some of Finland's leading brands.

This is a hybrid, two-year fixed-term position based in either Helsinki or Stockholm, working as part of our Finnish Customer Success team. We believe collaboration and learning happen best in person, so we aim to spend around three days a week in the office.




Your Responsibilities




As our Associate Analyst, your key responsibilities will include:

Leading and coordinating client projects and analytical workstreams from planning to delivery.
Designing surveys and research setups that generate meaningful insights and business value.
Analysing and visualising survey based consumer data to uncover actionable insights.
Translating complex findings into clear, commercially relevant recommendations.
Building strong client relationships by understanding business challenges and delivering high-quality solutions.
Collaborating with colleagues across Nepa to ensure seamless, high-quality project delivery.




Your Profile

We are looking for a structured, self-motivated team player with strong analytical skills and a genuine interest in consumer behaviour, brands and marketing. You enjoy solving business challenges, working closely with clients and turning data into insights that drive better decisions.




To excel in this role, you should have:

Proven experience in market research, consumer insights, analytics, consulting or another client-facing role.
A university degree in business, economics, statistics, marketing, behavioural science or another relevant field.
Strong analytical skills and experience working with quantitative data.
Experience coordinating projects and collaborating with multiple stakeholders.
The ability to communicate insights and recommendations in a clear, engaging and business-relevant way.
Advanced Excel skills. Experience with SPSS, R or similar analytical tools is an advantage.
Fluency in Finnish and English, both spoken and written.
Swedish is considered an advantage.




Who we are

At Nepa, we help brands grow by turning insight into action. We connect continuous data with sharp analytics and deep marketing expertise, enabling businesses to act faster, spend smarter and focus on what really drives growth.

Our platform blends Brand Tracking, Marketing Mix Modelling and Campaign Evaluation into one clear direction for marketers. With hundreds of brands worldwide relying on us, we're trusted to bring clarity to complexity—and make insight an engine for growth.




Next Steps

We review applications on a rolling basis, so don't delay, submit your application today to be considered for this exciting opportunity to join our Finnish Customer Success team as Associate Analyst. Please note that this is a two-year fixed-term position.
