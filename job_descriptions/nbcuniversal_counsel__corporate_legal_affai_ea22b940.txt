Job Description



Role Purpose:



The Counsel, Corporate Legal Affairs will provide legal support across a broad range of software and technology licensing, professional services, confidentiality, and related commercial transactions for NBCUniversal, with a focus on corporate operations, information technology, human resources, and other enterprise functions. This role will serve as a practical and solutions-oriented legal partner to internal stakeholders, advising on contract terms, risk allocation, and related legal issues while helping to advance business objectives. The position may also support business unit attorneys on related transactions and assist with efforts to make the delivery of legal services more efficient and effective. This role will report to the division’s Senior Vice President.



Responsibilities:



Provide legal support for a portfolio of agreements and internal stakeholders, primarily for NBCUniversal’s Sourcing and Operations & Technology groups
Draft, review, negotiate, and support a variety of corporate transaction agreements for NBCUniversal and its various business units, including M&A and related activities
Advise clients regarding contractual rights and duties, matters of contract interpretation, and pre-litigation dispute resolution
Coordinate with Privacy, Information Security, and Compliance teams to ensure personal data and confidential information is collected, used, and secured in accordance with company requirements and evolving law


Qualifications



Basic Requirements:



Juris Doctor (J.D.) degree from an accredited law school
Must be admitted to the New York State Bar or eligible to register as in-house counsel in New York
4+ years of experience at law firm or comparable in-house position

Desired Characteristics:



Prior experience regarding M&A, technology, data security, or intellectual property issues is a plus
Excellent drafting, negotiation, communication, and interpersonal skills
Ability to juggle and prioritize a high volume of deals and deadlines
Demonstrated ability to work both independently and in a collaborative environment, as a good-natured and enthusiastic team player
Eagerness and proven ability to adapt and develop new expertise as business needs and technologies evolve
Capable, comfortable with, and committed to providing timely and practical guidance to business team members at all levels

Hybrid: This position currently has a hybrid schedule, which requires contributing from the New York, NY office a minimum of four days per week. The Company reserves the right to change in-office requirements at any time.



This position is eligible for company sponsored benefits, including medical, dental and vision insurance, 401(k), paid leave, tuition reimbursement, and a variety of other discounts and perks.
