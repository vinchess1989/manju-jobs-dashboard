Administrative Assistant

Job Overview

Here at Ohmium, we are at the forefront of the renewable energy sector, leveraging our team's deep knowledge to design and deploy state-of-the-art green hydrogen solutions. We approach challenges with creativity and innovative thinking, with a focus on cost reduction and efficiency to maximise value for our customers. We are currently seeking a highly organised and self-motivated Administrative Assistant to join our dynamic team. This is a fully remote position, offering the flexibility of either full-time or part-time hours. The successful candidate will provide essential support to ensure our daily operations run smoothly and efficiently. The offered salary for this role is $25 - $36 USD per hour.

Responsibilities


Manage and coordinate calendars, scheduling virtual meetings, and appointments across multiple time zones.
Handle professional correspondence, including monitoring and responding to emails and internal communications in a timely manner.
Prepare, format, and proofread documents, reports, and presentations to a high standard.
Organise and maintain digital files and records, ensuring information is secure and easily accessible.
Perform data entry tasks and assist with maintaining company databases.
Provide comprehensive administrative support to team members and departments as needed.
Assist in the coordination of virtual team projects and company initiatives.


Qualifications


Proven experience as an Administrative Assistant or in a similar administrative support role.
Exceptional organisational and time-management skills with the ability to prioritise tasks effectively in a remote environment.
Strong written and verbal communication skills with meticulous attention to detail.
High proficiency in the Microsoft Office Suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook).
A proactive and resourceful problem-solver with the ability to work independently with minimal supervision.
Discretion and the ability to handle confidential information with professionalism.
Previous experience in a remote-working role is advantageous but not essential.
An interest in the renewable energy sector would be a plus.
