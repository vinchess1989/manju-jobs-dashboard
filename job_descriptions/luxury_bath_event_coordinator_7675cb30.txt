About Us

One Day Bath is a leading bath remodeling company specializing in premium acrylic bath and shower systems. As our company continues to grow, we're looking for a motivated Marketing Events Coordinator to oversee our local events program and help generate new business throughout the Downers Grove area.

This is an excellent opportunity for someone who enjoys event planning, team leadership, and community engagement.

Responsibilities
Research and secure home shows, fairs, festivals, retail events, and community events
Negotiate event contracts and coordinate event logistics
Plan and manage the annual events calendar
Recruit, hire, train, and schedule Event Representatives
Coordinate booth setup, displays, and promotional materials
Ensure events are staffed and executed professionally
Generate qualified leads by scheduling free in-home consultations
Track event performance and prepare reports
Work closely with the sales and marketing teams to maximize event success
Qualifications
Excellent communication and organizational skills
Positive, energetic, and outgoing personality
Leadership experience with the ability to coach and motivate others
Ability to manage multiple projects and deadlines
Comfortable working independently and as part of a team
Event marketing, promotions, retail, or customer service experience preferred
Reliable transportation
Ability to lift up to 30 pounds
Ability to stand for extended periods during events
Weekend availability is required
Benefits
Competitive salary
Performance-based bonus opportunities
Paid training
Career growth opportunities
Supportive team environment
Established, growing company

If you're organized, driven, and enjoy creating successful events while leading a team, we'd love to hear from you.
