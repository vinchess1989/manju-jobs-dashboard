About Brevitē

We make design-forward gear for the photographers, filmmakers, and creators of today. Over the last decade, we've built a brand of more than 2.5 million creators united by a single idea: Make Something.




The role

We're hiring a content creator to make short videos for our TikTok. This is a part-time, remote, contract role. You work directly with our team, we hand you clear briefs, and you turn them into videos that feel native to TikTok and unmistakably Brevitē.




One of our favorite things is helping creators get better. You'll work directly with us and get real coaching as you grow.




What you'll make

Short videos for our TikTok, including will-it-fit and packing videos featuring our bags
3 to 5 videos per week to start, with more during launch periods
On-brand content that is ready to post, week after week




Who you are

You already make short videos and post consistently, on your own account or for a brand
You have a feed we can look at
You understand what makes people watch, follow, and stay
You can take a brief and run with it without much hand-holding
You are reliable and hold a cadence for the long run, not just a heroic month




What you'll need

Your own phone or camera, and the ability to edit your own videos




The details

Part-time, remote, contract
Paid per video
Flexible schedule. We care about what gets made, not hours logged
You own the output. We own the direction and the briefs.
