Hey there!




We’re myVA Rocks! We connect busy pros and business owners with amazing Virtual Assistants. Right now, we’re on the hunt for experienced professionals to join our growing team. Are you ruthlessly organized? The calendar guru? The person who makes sure i’s and t’s are dotted and crossed? Then let's talk!

Our VAs serve several Clients simultaneously, making for a very flexible, interesting, and engaging workday! #jugglingpro




Part time contract




100% remote- always!




Flexible schedule-you’ll need about 20 hours a week available during business hours so you’re ready to connect with clients when they need you.




Rate: $20/hr




You’ll be working with busy professionals or business owners who need help- but sometimes don't know where to start.




Our job is to help them identify how we can help and make their lives easier and free up their time. We act as partners, brainstorming and providing solutions rather than simply taking orders for tasks.

We’ll match you with a client based on what you like to do and what you’re good at! For example, if copywriting is your jam, or maybe making order out of a chaotic Google Drive makes you happy, we'll focus there. Other tasks could include calendar management, bookkeeping, research, document/agenda/presentation preparations, travel arrangements, email management, special projects, and more!




Professional Experience Needed:

You've worked in a remote or in-person office setting for 5+ years.
You are proactive and gutsy!
You have better-than-average attention to detail and great grammar.
You're familiar with basic office technologies, including Microsoft Office, G-Suite, Zoom, and LinkedIn.
You're organized, resourceful, and communicative.




Our VAs come from everywhere and are going places. If you have a great attitude, great attention to detail, love hyper-flexibility, and believe it's important to lift as you rise, we'd love to talk to you.




This role is available to candidates working and residing in AL, AZ, AR, CO, DE, FL, GA, ID, IN, IA, KS, KY, LA, ME, MD, MI, MN, MS, MO, MT, NE, NV, NH, NM, NC, ND, OH, OK, OR, PA, RI, SC, SD, TN, TX, UT, VT, VA, WA, WI, WV, WY.
