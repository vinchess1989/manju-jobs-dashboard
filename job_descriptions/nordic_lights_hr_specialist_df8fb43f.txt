Tiivistelmä työpaikkailmoituksesta:

You will support the Finnish operation as an HR Specialist in a global HR team reporting to the HR Senior Manager.
Main duties include day-to-day HR operations such as employment contracts, work permits, onboarding and offboarding, payroll coordination, and HR data and systems maintenance.
Required qualifications are a bachelor’s degree, over three years of HR experience, fluency in English and either Finnish or Swedish, and understanding of employment legislation and collective agreements.
The position is based in Pietarsaari (Pännäistentie 155) and applications with CV are due by Sunday 2nd of August at 23:59.


Tämä tiivistelmä on luotu tekoälyn avulla.



Are you a HR professional who enjoys ensuring effective, accurate and compliant HR processes? Do you want to play an important role in supporting employees and managers in HR related matters as well as the full employment lifecycle in an international industrial company? You might be exactly the person we are looking for to join Nordic Lights as our new HR Specialist.

You will support our Finnish operation and be part of a global HR team of 3-4 persons, reporting to the HR Senior Manager.

Main responsibilities include:

Supporting day-to-day HR operations, including employment contracts, work permits, employment changes, and general HR administration
Providing practical HR guidance to employees and managers on policies, practices, and employment-related matters. You contribute to the HR practices and guidelines being in line with applicable laws and collective agreements (Teknologiateollisuus)
Coordinating onboarding and offboarding processes to ensure a smooth employee lifecycle experience
Maintaining HR data, systems, records, reports, and documentation in several applications
Coordinating payroll administration by supporting supervisors with payroll data, reviewing payroll calculations and payouts, and collaborating with our payroll provider.

Requirements and desired profile:

Bachelor’s degree in a relevant field
+3 years of experience from HR-related tasks
Understanding of employment-related legislation, collective agreements and the importance of compliant HR practices as well as an interest to work with these areas and learn more
Fluent in English and in either Finnish or Swedish. Ability to work in all of the three languages mentioned is highly valued
Good collaboration and communications skills and a will to support our managers in their role with HR-related themes
Digital competence - an ability to work effectively with various HR related systems
Good attention to detail and the ability to manage accurate HR data, documentation, reporting, and payroll-related information.

What we offer:

Competitive job perks and a highly skilled team of experts to back you up in your work role
A flat organization with a hands-on work culture where everyone can contribute and have an impact. Additionally, our growing company offers you an excellent opportunity to further grow your career
Interesting work in a company with world-class products and customers like Caterpillar, John Deere, Liebherr, Sandvik and Komatsu.
