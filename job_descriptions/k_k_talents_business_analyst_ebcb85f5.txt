Lead Recruiter EU/UK at K&K Talents

K&K Talents is an international recruiting agency that has been providing technical resources in the European region since 1993. This position is with one of our clients in Finland who is actively hiring candidates to expand their teams.




Role: Business Analyst

Location: Helsinki, Finland

Employment type: Full time Permanent




Roles and Responsibilities:

Extract and process complex data from primary and secondary sources; clean, filter, and validate data to ensure accuracy.
Analyze current business functions, evaluate operational bottlenecks, and identify areas for cost-reduction or efficiency improvements.
Design, deploy, and maintain interactive dashboards and automated reports for leadership using visualization tools.
Translate complex, technical insights into clear, non-technical business recommendations for management.
Define and monitor Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) to measure the success of business initiatives.




Note: Applicants for employment in Finland should possess work authorization which does not require sponsorship by the employer for a visa.
