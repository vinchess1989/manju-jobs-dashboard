Wellbeing Manager


Wärtsilä is an innovative technology company known for being a forerunner in the Marine and Energy markets. Every day, we – Wärtsilians - put our hearts and minds into turning Wärtsilä's purpose of enabling sustainable societies through innovation in technology and services into reality. Our ultimate aim is to provide increased value to both our customers and society. New, game-changing ideas and continuous improvement have been part of our DNA since 1834. Together, we can create new business opportunities and more sustainable future that we can all be proud of. 

We want to transition the world towards a decarbonised and sustainable future. By pushing the boundaries of engineering and technology, we can make it work. We are always on the lookout for future-oriented talent – want to join the ride?

 

We are always on the lookout for a talent with a growth mindset – are you the one?  

  

We are looking for an experienced Wellbeing Manager with a passion for promoting wellbeing as a foundation for sustainable performance. In this role, you will lead the development and implementation of Wärtsilä Finland's work ability and wellbeing strategy, as well as related practices, partnering with key stakeholders to foster a healthy, engaged, and high-performing workforce. 

 

The preferred location for this position is Helsinki or Vaasa, Finland. We offer flexible hybrid working opportunities. 

 

 

About the position 

  

As a Wellbeing Manager, you will have a unique opportunity to shape the future of work ability and wellbeing management at Wärtsilä Finland. You will lead the development of work ability practices and initiatives, taking an active role in their implementation and day-to-day execution. You will build strong partnerships with internal and external stakeholders, coach and support managers, and use data-driven insights to drive continuous improvement. 

 

Acting as a trusted advisor, you will help strengthen leadership capabilities, modernize ways of working, and promote a sustainable and proactive approach to employee wellbeing across the organization. You will be part of a supportive and dedicated local HR team in Finland. 

 

 

Your work will focus on these responsibilities: 

 

Lead and continuously develop Wärtsilä Finland's work ability management practices and initiatives, while taking an active and hands-on role in their implementation 

Own and lead collaboration with occupational healthcare providers, pension insurance companies, insurance partners, and other key external stakeholders in Finland, ensuring effective cooperation and follow-up 

Collaborate closely with Health & Safety, employee representatives, HR colleagues, and business leaders to ensure a proactive approach to work ability management 

Support and coach managers in work ability-related matters, strengthen their work ability leadership capabilities, and provide guidance in complex employee cases 

Use relevant KPIs, data, and insights to identify improvement opportunities, translate findings into actions, and guide decision-making 

Drive change and development initiatives that strengthen wellbeing, work ability, and organizational performance, while monitoring their effectiveness and driving continuous improvement 

Promote and implement best practices in work ability management, including preventive actions, risk identification, and competence development across the organization 

Foster a proactive and sustainable approach to employee wellbeing across Wärtsilä Finland 

 

 

To be able to succeed in the position, we expect you to have: 

 

Proven track record in leading and developing work ability management, occupational health, or wellbeing initiatives, preferably in a large and complex organization with both blue-collar and white-collar employees 

Master's degree in a relevant field 

Strong capability to independently shape, drive, and develop your area of responsibility with a high degree of ownership and self-leadership 

Experience in building and managing a broad network of internal and external stakeholders 

Strong analytical and data-driven mindset, with the ability to translate insights into actions and measurable results 

Experience in coaching and supporting managers in work ability-related matters 

Excellent communication, presentation, collaboration, and influencing skills 

Strong expertise in change management and a track record of driving long-term development initiatives 

Resourcefulness, resilience, and an innovative, solution-oriented approach to work 

Fluency in Finnish and English, Swedish language skills are considered an advantage
