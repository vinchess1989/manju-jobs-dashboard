About the role

We’re looking for a Legal Counsel to support Verda’s legal and regulatory function as we scale. You’ll work across a broad range of legal areas, with a particular focus on supporting a fast-growing technology business operating at the intersection of AI, infrastructure, and data.

This is a hands-on role in a scale-up environment, where you’ll collaborate closely with technical, commercial, and compliance teams to provide pragmatic legal guidance and help shape our legal foundations in a global, multi-jurisdictional context. You will play a key role in enabling Verda’s growth, including supporting corporate global expansion.

Your responsibilities

Provide legal support across key business areas, including commercial agreements, partnerships, employment, and vendor relationships

Draft, review, and negotiate a variety of contracts, including NDAs, SaaS, infrastructure, and technology-related agreements

Support corporate legal matters, including governance, structuring, and internal documentation

Support and advise on corporate expansion activities, including entry into new markets and jurisdictional setup considerations

Assist in fundraising and M&A activities, including due diligence, transaction support, and coordination with external counsel

Support legal aspects of supply chain and procurement operations, including vendor terms, risk allocation, and commercial structuring

Advise on intellectual property matters, including protection, licensing, and risk assessment

Monitor and interpret relevant regulatory developments across Finland, the EU, and other relevant jurisdictions, particularly in AI, data protection, and digital infrastructure

Support compliance with applicable regulations such as GDPR, AI Act, and other emerging frameworks

Work closely with compliance, security, and product teams to ensure legal considerations are embedded in operations and product development

Assist in developing internal legal guidelines, templates, and training materials

Support external counsel coordination across different jurisdictions where needed

Your key competencies

OTM/LLM or equivalent degree from an accredited law program

2+ years of relevant legal experience (law firm or in-house)

Strong working knowledge of commercial contracting, including drafting, reviewing, and negotiating agreements (e.g., NDAs, SaaS, technology contracts)

Ability to provide pragmatic, business-oriented legal advice in a fast-paced environment

Strong communication skills in English (written and verbal), with the ability to translate legal complexity into actionable guidance

High level of ownership, ethical judgment, and professionalism

Comfortable working cross-functionally with technical, product, and commercial teams

Ability to operate in a multi-jurisdictional context, including knowing when to escalate to external counsel

Nice-to-haves

Experience a technology, infrastructure, or high-growth company environment

Expertise in one or more of: Intellectual Property (IP), Corporate law, and/or AI / emerging technology regulation

Privacy and data protection

Exposure to M&A transactions, fundraising, or corporate structuring

Experience supporting international expansion or cross-border operations

Familiarity with EU AI Act and other digital regulations

Prior experience working with external counsel across jurisdictions

Practicalities

Location: Helsinki

Working mode: Hybrid, 3 days a week from the office.

Start date: As soon as possible

Employment type: Full-time and permanent
