Role & Responsibilities




Provide administrative and operational support to account teams across multiple client accounts
Assist with media monitoring, tracking coverage, and compiling daily/weekly news clips for clients
Help maintain and update media contact databases, ensuring accurate reporter information and beat assignments
Support the drafting and editing of basic PR materials including media alerts, fact sheets, and background documents
Coordinate logistics for media interviews, briefings, and events including scheduling and material preparation
Conduct research on media outlets, journalists, competitors, and industry trends to support campaign planning
Assist with the development and maintenance of media lists for targeted outreach campaigns
Help prepare client status reports, coverage summaries, and presentation materials
Manage filing systems, project documentation, and shared resources to keep teams organized
Support social media monitoring and reporting for client accounts
Participate in team meetings, taking notes and tracking action items
Assist with new business efforts including research, competitive analysis, and pitch deck preparation
Learn PR fundamentals through hands-on experience and mentorship from senior team members
Handle administrative tasks such as expense tracking, meeting coordination, and calendar management
Contribute to agency initiatives and internal projects as needed




Qualifications




1 year of professional experience in public relations, communications, or related field; relevant internships considered
Strong interest in technology and startup ecosystem
Excellent written and verbal communication skills
Exceptional organizational skills and attention to detail
Ability to manage multiple tasks and priorities in a fast-paced environment
Proactive attitude with eagerness to learn and take on new challenges
Strong research skills and ability to synthesize information quickly
Proficiency with Microsoft Office and Google Workspace
Comfortable learning new tools and platforms
Team player with positive attitude and professional demeanor
Ability to work independently and take initiative on assigned tasks




Preferences




PR agency internship experience
Coursework or academic focus in public relations, journalism, or communications
Exposure to technology sector through internships, coursework, or personal interest
Basic understanding of media landscape and news cycles
Social media savvy with understanding of digital platforms
Experience with media monitoring tools (Meltwater, Cision, etc.)
Familiarity with project management tools (Asana, Monday, Trello, etc.)
Previous customer service or client-facing experience
Interest in emerging technologies and innovation
Strong writing portfolio from academic or extracurricular work
Bachelor's degree in communications, journalism, public relations, or related field




About HAUS




HAUS is the world’s first integrated public relations and creative services agency purpose built for deep tech startups and scaleups.




In January 2022, we set out on a mission to accelerate the adoption of technologies that maximize freedom, material abundance, and human flourishing.




Since then, we’ve had the opportunity to support more than 100 startups, scaleups, venture firms, research labs, and other organizations in industries including artificial intelligence, aerospace, biotechnology, climate, defense, energy, advanced manufacturing, quantum computing, semiconductors, robotics, and more.
