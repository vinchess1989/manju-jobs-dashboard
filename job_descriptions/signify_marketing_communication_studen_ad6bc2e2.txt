About Signify

Through bold discovery and cutting-edge innovation, we lead an industry that is vital for the future of our planet: lighting. Through our leadership in connected lighting and the Internet of Things, we're breaking new ground in data analytics, AI, and smart solutions for homes, offices, cities, and beyond.

At Signify, you can shape tomorrow by building on our incredible 125+ year legacy while working toward even bolder sustainability goals. Our culture of continuous learning, creativity, and commitment to diversity and inclusion empowers you to grow your skills and career.

Join us, and together, we’ll transform our industry, making a lasting difference for brighter lives and a better world.

More About The Role

What you’ll do


Assist to deliver the marketing communication for the assigned channel as well as to launch our new products and technologies.
Support the localization of our digital strategy
Supporting on digital & offline marketing campaigns
Collaborate with marketing (product & communication), sales and channel/account management to work with digital tools and programs that make our business effective, sustainable, scalable and measurable with data
Prepare creative briefs and work with external agencies to deliver high-quality marketing materials
Copywriting and content creation for various campaigns, events and sales use.


What You’ll Need


Ability to take initiative and learn new skills quickly
Interest in customer behavior and visual communication especially in digital formats
Experience or interest in digital marketing communication, SEO and activating digital platforms
Good working knowledge of Microsoft Office tools.
Preferably a university student looking for a 1-year part-time opportunity
Fluency in Finnish
