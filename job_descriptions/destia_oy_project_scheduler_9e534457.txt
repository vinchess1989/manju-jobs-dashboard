We're Hiring: Project Scheduler – Energy Projects




MS Project / Oracle Primavera P6




Are you passionate about planning complex infrastructure projects and ensuring successful project delivery? Do you thrive on building realistic schedules, analysing critical paths, and keeping major projects on track?




We are looking for an experienced Project Scheduler to join our growing team and support the delivery of large-scale substation, transmission, BESS, renewable energy and data centre projects across Finland.




If you have strong experience with Microsoft Project and/or Oracle Primavera P6, we'd love to hear from you.




Your responsibilities

Develop and maintain Level 1–4 project schedules.
Create baseline schedules and monitor project progress.
Perform Critical Path Method (CPM) analysis.
Coordinate schedules with engineering, procurement, construction and commissioning teams.
Prepare look-ahead schedules and recovery plans.
Analyse schedule impacts, delays and changes.
Produce weekly and monthly progress reports.
Support project managers with planning and forecasting.
Participate in client planning meetings and project reviews.




What we're looking for

Proven experience as a Project Scheduler, Planning Engineer, or similar role.
Strong working knowledge of Microsoft Project and/or Oracle Primavera P6.
Experience in EPC, energy, infrastructure, industrial or construction projects.
Good understanding of project planning principles and schedule logic.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
Excellent communication skills in English.




It would be an advantage if you have experience with

High-voltage substations
Transmission or distribution networks
Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)
Data centre infrastructure
Renewable energy projects
Earned Value Management (EVM)
Delay analysis and project controls
Power BI or advanced Excel reporting




What we offer

The opportunity to work on some of Finland's most significant energy infrastructure projects.
Challenging and technically demanding projects.
Competitive salary and comprehensive employee benefits.
Flexible working arrangements.
A professional, supportive and collaborative team.
Excellent opportunities for professional development and career growth.




Ready to shape the future of energy?

If you're looking for a role where your planning expertise directly contributes to the success of major energy projects, we'd love to hear from you.

Apply today by sending your CV and application. Applications will be reviewed on an ongoing basis, and the position will be filled as soon as the right candidate is found.

Join us and help deliver the next generation of energy infrastructure. ⚡




Apply through the “Apply for this Position” section and submit your application and CV as soon as possible. We review applications continuously and may proceed with interviews before the application deadline.




The location of the position can be agreed based on your expertise, place of residence, and project locations across Finland.
