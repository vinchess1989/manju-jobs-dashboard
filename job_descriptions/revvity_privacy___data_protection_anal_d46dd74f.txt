Räätälöi ansioluetteloni
At Revvity, we're on a mission to improve human health through science and innovation - and protecting the data that powers that mission is critical. We're looking for a Privacy and Data Protection Analyst to join our global Data Protection Office and help us build a world-class privacy compliance programme.

This is a hands-on, high-impact role where you'll work at the intersection of law, technology, and business - supporting teams across our Life Sciences and Diagnostics segments worldwide. If you're passionate about data protection, thrive in a fast-moving environment, and want your work to genuinely matter, this could be the role for you.

Please note that this is a hybrid role, offering 3 days onsite and 2 days remote working.

What You'll Be Doing

Commercial Privacy Support


Partner with Legal and commercial teams on privacy questions arising from tenders, RFPs, and customer due diligence processes
Prepare compliance responses, complete privacy questionnaires, and compile supporting documentation
Escalate complex or novel issues to the Data Protection Officer with clear, well-reasoned analysis



Regulatory Monitoring


Monitor evolving European privacy legislation and identify developments relevant to Revvity's operations
Translate regulatory changes into clear, actionable summaries for the Data Protection Officer and key stakeholders



Compliance Programme Support


Conduct assessments to identify compliance gaps across Revvity's global operations
Maintain and update records of processing activities, compliance tracking tools, and programme documentation
Work with regional teams to gather data and drive continuous improvement in data protection maturity



Data Processing Agreement (DPA) Review & Negotiation


Review DPAs from customers, vendors, and partners against Revvity's standard positions
Negotiate standard contractual terms and flag material deviations to the Data Protection Officer
Manage a steady pipeline of agreements with accuracy and efficiency



Cross-Functional Collaboration


Work closely with Information Security, IT, Procurement, and commercial functions to embed privacy requirements into business processes and vendor workflows
Conduct and document Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs)
Support the handling of data subject rights requests in line with regulatory requirements



What We're Looking For


Experience in a privacy, legal, compliance, or data governance role
A solid understanding of GDPR and European data protection law
CIPP/E, CIPM, or similar privacy certification preferred
Confidence reviewing and negotiating data processing agreements
Fluent business English is essential and proficiency in a second European language would be desirable
Strong written communication skills - you are skilled at making complex topics clear and concise
A proactive, organised approach with the ability to manage multiple workstreams simultaneously
Comfortable working in a global, cross-functional environment



Please note that Revvity Turku Site is a drug free site and a medical examination for new employees includes a drug test.
