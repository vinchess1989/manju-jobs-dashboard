Are you an experienced and development-oriented HR professional looking for new challenges? Join our effective People & Culture team at Bassadone Automotive Nordic as an HR Business Partner & Sustainability Coordinator, as our current talent moves into new internal opportunities. We are looking for someone to support the business and people managers of our own retail brand – Auto Bassadone – while also taking ownership of Sustainability reporting and strategy implementation across Finland and the Baltics.




The automotive industry is going through a major transformation, and we are ready to shift into a new gear. This role combines HR annual cycle processes with business-driven development projects, shaped by your skills and interests. Our key project for the year ahead is developing our payroll and compensation processes (Mepco & Automaster). On the sustainability side, the Group-level strategy is owned by our HQ Sustainability team – your role is to implement it locally and collaborate closely with them.

 

Your role in a nutshell:




People Manager Support: You actively support Auto Bassadone’s 16 managers and leadership across all employment lifecycle matters, from recruitment to exit, ensuring consistent people management practices throughout the organisation.
Process Development: You develop and optimise HR processes and operating models to meet business needs. You also implement Group-level HR processes (e.g. performance reviews, salary and development discussions, talent reviews, employee surveys) within your business area according to the annual HR calendar.
Occupational Health & Wellbeing: You support managers in managing work ability, coordinate occupational health processes in collaboration with occupational healthcare, pension and insurance providers, and other stakeholders. You also act as the Group’s Health & Safety Manager for Finland.
Employment Law & Collective Agreements: You interpret and apply collective bargaining agreements (automotive retail, workshop operations, and automotive sector white-collar agreements) as well as employment legislation in practical situations, ensuring HR practices support both business needs and compliance.
Stakeholder Management: You manage the relationship with our external payroll partner and support managers on questions related to compensation, salaries and related practices.
Group Sustainability Coordinator: You hold overall responsibility for Sustainability reporting and the implementation of the Group Sustainability strategy across Finland and the Baltics. The Group-level strategy and framework are owned by the HQ Sustainability team – your role is to translate these into local action, monitor and report on progress, and work in close collaboration with HQ. Strong English communication skills are essential for this part of the role, as most of the collaboration happens in English across functions and borders.

 

What we are looking for:




Broad generalist HR experience, for example as an HRBP or HR Manager, approximately 5 years, ideally from the automotive sector or manufacturing/industrial environment
Ability to manage extensive responsibilities and develop processes in line with business needs
Familiarity with the MepcoHRM system. Knowledge of Automaster is a significant advantage
A networked professional who actively follows changes and trends in the HR field and brings insights on how to leverage them
Strong written and verbal communication skills in both Finnish and English – English fluency is essential for the Sustainability Coordinator responsibilities
Willingness to travel to retail locations across Finland approximately once a month
An interest in or understanding of ESG and sustainability reporting is an advantage

Throughout your journey, you will be supported by your future manager, Group P&C Director Essi, your predecessor Head of Talent Development Emmi, and your Baltic counterpart Maarika.

 

What we offer:




A meaningful and responsible role where you can make a genuine impact
A relaxed and friendly working atmosphere, both in the business and within the team
Modern and comfortable offices in Petikko, Vantaa
Flexible working hours and hybrid work options – please note that the role cannot be performed fully remotely, as we want to be where our people are
Competitive compensation and comprehensive employee benefits (occupational healthcare, ePassi, and the possibility of a company car)

About us:




Bassadone Automotive Nordic Oy is part of the international Bassadone Automotive Group, which operates in several European countries. Bassadone Automotive Nordic represents a portfolio of well-known and respected automotive brands and is responsible for their import, sales, and aftersales operations in Finland and the Baltic countries. In Finland, the company represents the following brands: Alfa Romeo, Alpine, Citroën, Dacia, DS, Fiat, Hyundai, Isuzu, Jeep, KGM, Nissan, Opel, Peugeot, Renault, and Suzuki. The Group also includes its own retail dealership network, Auto Bassadone.

The Group employs more than 400 automotive professionals and offers a stable yet agile working environment that combines the resources and support of an international organization with the flexibility and close collaboration of a local business.
