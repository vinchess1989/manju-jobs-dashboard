We are looking for a Marketing Specialist!




📍 Location: Helsinki, Finland (Hybrid)




Help us activate organisations and employees to make mental wellbeing part of everyday worklife and build the mental skills needed to thrive in a changing world of work.




At Auntie, we're tackling one of the biggest challenges in today's worklife: helping organisations and people build the mental wellbeing and mental skills they need to navigate constant change, pressure and complexity.




Our service is trusted and valued by organisations globally, with customers and Auntie users around the world. To keep growing our impact, we're looking for a Marketing Specialist to join our marketing team.




This is a hands-on B2B marketing role where you'll help turn Auntie's expertise and point of view into content, events and campaigns that build demand and support our sales pipeline.




You'll join a marketing team of three working across an international, multi-market environment — Finland, Norway, Sweden, Germany and beyond. You'll have ownership of your own areas and the support of the team, and you'll collaborate closely with colleagues across Sales, Customer Success and Product.




What you'll do (and enjoy doing)




Content creation & social media

Create engaging B2B content for campaigns, web, social media and sales materials across our markets
Write and adapt content that brings Auntie's positioning, expertise and tone of voice to life
Turn Auntie's expertise and point of view into thought leadership content that builds awareness, engagement and demand
Create content across the customer and buyer journey, with an eye for what moves audiences from interest to action
Own and drive all Auntie's social media channels, from planning and content creation to posting and engagement
Track performance across content and channels and use insights to continuously improve what you do




Sales support

Produce and maintain sales materials including decks, one-pagers and templates that help our sales team engage buyers and move opportunities forward
Support sales and pipeline activities with timely, relevant content and follow-up materials
Work closely with Sales to understand what buyers need at different stages of the buying journey and turn those needs into effective marketing and sales assets




Events & communities

Plan and run Auntie's live and digital events, including Auntie HR Circle and Auntie Leader Circle webinars, roundtables and selected third-party events
Shape event concepts, speakers and content to showcase Auntie's expertise and create valuable experiences for attendees
Turn events into a source of qualified leads and pipeline through effective promotion, activation and follow-up
Track event performance and use insights to continuously improve our approach




We think you'll thrive in this role if you have:

3-5 years of relevant experience in B2B marketing, ideally across content, sales support and events
Are a strong writer and communicator who can turn complex topics into clear, engaging content
Understand B2B buying journeys and how marketing can contribute to demand generation and pipeline
Are comfortable working with data and use insights to guide your decisions
Enjoy being hands-on, writing, creating, coordinating and getting things done
Are proactive and organised, and comfortable planning and driving your work independently and enjoy collaborating with different teams and people
Enjoy working in a growing scale-up, where things move fast, roles evolve and you get to shape how things are done
Experience with paid advertising (LinkedIn, Google, Meta) is a plus
Familiarity with HubSpot is a plus




Language skills

Fluent in Finnish and English (must-have)
Fluent in Swedish is an advantage




Why join Auntie?

At Auntie, you'll be part of a team making a real difference in how people feel and function at work and in life. You'll have room to take ownership, develop your expertise and grow within your role.

You'll work in a marketing team where you can see the impact of your work, collaborate closely with different parts of the business and have the freedom to drive and develop your own areas.




What We Offer

The opportunity to work with a beloved product and drive real impact on the wellbeing of thousands of people
Salary: approximately 4000€ depending on your experience
Flexible hybrid work environment, team will meet weekly at the office in Helsinki
Unlimited use of the Auntie service
Occupational healthcare service
Lunch, exercise and cultural benefits
Wellness hour, internal events and other Auntie tools geared to help you feel your best
A mission-driven culture that values autonomy, learning and work-life balance




About Us

Auntie provides mental wellbeing services and mental skill development to help organisations build sustainable performance and workforce capability.




Our services focus on strengthening employee mental wellbeing while developing capabilities such as resilience, adaptability, focus, self-leadership and people leadership — driving sustainable performance and effective leadership in a continuously changing work environment.




We're a 40-person B2B2C health scale-up, headquartered in Finland and working with organisations and Auntie users globally. Our services are highly valued by customers and users, supporting HR and business leaders in building a healthier, more resilient and more capable workforce.
