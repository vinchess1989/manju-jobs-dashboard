About The Company

AVEVA is a global leader in industrial software, dedicated to creating innovative solutions that empower industries to operate more efficiently, safely, and sustainably. With a workforce of over 6,500 employees across more than 40 countries, AVEVA serves a diverse range of sectors including energy, infrastructure, chemicals, and minerals. The company's cutting-edge software platforms are trusted by over 90% of leading industrial companies worldwide, enabling them to optimize their operations, improve safety standards, and achieve their sustainability goals. AVEVA is committed to embedding sustainability and inclusion into its core business strategy, striving to meet ambitious 2030 targets that focus on responsible growth and social impact. The organization fosters a collaborative and inclusive culture, emphasizing innovation, integrity, and respect for diversity.

About The Role

The Executive Assistant at AVEVA plays a vital role in supporting senior leadership by providing high-level administrative and operational assistance. This full-time position, based in Lake Forest, CA, involves working four days onsite and demands exceptional organizational skills, keen attention to detail, and sound judgment. The successful candidate will serve as a trusted partner to executive leaders, proactively managing complex schedules, coordinating high-impact meetings and events, and building strong relationships with internal teams and external stakeholders. The role requires a proactive approach to anticipate needs, resolve scheduling conflicts, and ensure seamless day-to-day operations. Additionally, the Executive Assistant will assist with travel arrangements, office management, onboarding new staff, and supporting special projects, contributing to the overall efficiency and effectiveness of the leadership team.

Qualifications


Proven experience as an executive assistant or in a similar supporting role for senior management or SVP-level executives
Exceptional organizational and multitasking skills
Strong attention to detail and accuracy
Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook) and other productivity tools
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
Discretion and confidentiality in handling sensitive information
Ability to take initiative and work proactively
Flexibility and adaptability to changing priorities and deadlines
Experience coordinating travel arrangements, meetings, and events
High school diploma or equivalent; additional education or certifications in administration is preferred


Responsibilities


Manage and prioritize executive calendars, including scheduling meetings, appointments, and travel arrangements, proactively resolving conflicts
Serve as the main point of contact for internal and external communications, screening emails, calls, and correspondence
Draft, review, and prepare correspondence, reports, and presentation materials for leadership review
Organize and coordinate meetings, conferences, and special events, ensuring all logistics such as room reservations, catering, and audiovisual setup are handled efficiently
Attend meetings when necessary to record minutes and track key action items
Oversee office management tasks, including handling mail and deliveries, managing supplies, maintaining office facilities, and coordinating maintenance requests
Assist with onboarding new staff members and provide administrative support across departments as needed
Arrange domestic and international travel, including flights, hotels, transportation, and visas, while preparing detailed itineraries aligned with executive preferences
Maintain organized and confidential electronic and physical filing systems for documents, reports, and records
Track and reconcile expenses, process reimbursements, and liaise with the finance department
Support special projects by conducting research, data analysis, and preparing presentations
Collaborate with the leadership team to ensure timely completion of assigned initiatives


Benefits


Flexible work hours and hybrid working arrangements
20 days of paid time off (PTO), increasing to 25 days with tenure
Three paid volunteering days annually
Primary and secondary parental leave
Well-being support programs
Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision insurance
401(k) retirement plan with company contributions
Supportive and inclusive workplace culture
