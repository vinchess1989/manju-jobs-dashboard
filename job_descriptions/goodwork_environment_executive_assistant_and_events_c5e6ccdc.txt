About Recycle Coach

While it’s true that most municipalities start using Recycle Coach so residents know when it’s garbage day, we’re so much more than just a calendar.

Recycle Coach provides solid waste education, recycling education, and communication solutions across 1,700+ cities in North America and Australia. Our mobile app creates engaging and interactive learning experiences that help solve local environmental challenges, teaching residents not only how to recycle correctly, but why it matters.

At Recycle Coach, we’re looking for top talent who get excited about using technology to solve environmental challenges. We’re a forward-thinking organization that has led for 20+ years in waste and recycling information and education through technology.

About The Role

We're seeking a detail-oriented, organized professional to support our CEO and support our trade show operations, providing hands-on coordination and execution support for employee engagement initiatives and industry conferences. This hybrid role splits time between executive assistance and events coordination, offering the opportunity to impact multiple areas of the organization.

Key Responsibilities

Executive Assistant (CEO Support)


Manage CEO calendar, meetings, and scheduling
Prepare meeting agendas, materials, and follow-up communications
Handle correspondence, emails, and administrative requests
Coordinate travel logistics and itineraries
Assist with ad-hoc projects and priorities as needed


Event Management


Coordinate and execute logistics for conferences, roadshows, and company-wide events
Research and manage event vendors, venues, and contracts, ensuring seamless delivery
Manage invoices and support financial processes related to events
Oversee comprehensive quality check of all owned assets and facilitate maintenance as necessary, before and after events
Facilitate printing, packing, and shipping for programs from the warehouse, and is the first line of defense for any logistics issues
Prepares necessary documentation (BOLs, customs forms, etc.)
Collaborate with internal stakeholders and external partners to deliver high-quality outcomes


Qualifications


Experience in operations, events, or program coordination
Strong organizational and interpersonal skills
Attention to detail and follow-through
Comfortable operating in a fast-paced, multi-stakeholder environment
Ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously
