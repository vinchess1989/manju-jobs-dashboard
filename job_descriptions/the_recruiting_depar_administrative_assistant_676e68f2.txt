The Recruiting Department is actively building our network of exceptional Administrative Assistants for current and upcoming opportunities with business owners, executives, and growing organizations. We're looking for professionals who enjoy bringing structure to busy environments, creating exceptional client experiences, and becoming trusted partners to leaders who need someone they can rely on.




If you're someone who naturally anticipates needs, loves organizing people and processes, and takes pride in making life easier for others, we'd love to meet you.




About the Opportunity

Our clients are busy business owners and executive leaders who need more than someone to answer phones or manage a calendar. They need a proactive administrative professional who can create organization, improve efficiency, and help keep their business moving forward.




Every client is different, but these roles often include executive support, meeting coordination, client communications, CRM management, event planning, project coordination, and process improvement. The ideal candidate enjoys variety, works well independently, and finds satisfaction in creating order from complexity.




What You'll Do

Responsibilities may include:

Manage executive calendars and scheduling
Coordinate meetings, travel, and event logistics
Prepare meeting agendas, notes, and follow-up communications
Maintain CRM systems and client information
Assist with client onboarding and ongoing communications
Support billing, invoicing, and administrative processes
Coordinate projects and special initiatives
Track important client milestones and follow-up activities
Schedule social media content and newsletters
Develop and improve administrative systems and workflows
Provide exceptional customer service while representing executive leadership professionally




What We're Looking For

The strongest candidates are:

Highly organized and detail-oriented
Proactive and resourceful
Excellent communicators
Comfortable working independently
Able to manage multiple priorities
Professional, polished, and trustworthy
Naturally service-oriented
Confident interacting with executives and clients
Passionate about helping others succeed




Qualifications

3+ years of administrative or executive support experience
Experience supporting business owners or executive leaders preferred
Experience with CRM platforms, preferably HubSpot
Strong organizational, calendar management, and project coordination skills
Excellent written and verbal communication
Experience coordinating meetings, events, or executive logistics
High attention to detail with exceptional follow-through
Proficiency with Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, and modern business technology
Experience with billing, invoicing, or bookkeeping is a plus
Experience with social media scheduling or marketing support is a plus




Schedule

Part-time
Approximately 15–25 hours per week
Primarily remote positions with occasional in-person responsibilities depending on the client
Flexible weekday schedule during normal business hours




Why Work with The Recruiting Department?

At The Recruiting Department, we partner with organizations that value people, culture, and long-term success. We take the time to understand both our clients and our candidates so we can make thoughtful matches that benefit everyone involved.




If you're looking for an administrative role where your organization, initiative, and professionalism will truly make a difference, we'd love to connect.
