Marketing Specialist, Consumer Experience




At Fazer Bakery Finland, we're looking for talented digital marketing professional to join our marketing team. Whether your passion lies in creating engaging content and building digital communities, or in driving consumer engagement through strategic marketing, data and digital development, we'd love to hear from you.




As a Marketing Specialist, Consumer Experience (Digital Marketing Specialist), you will join Fazer Bakery Finland’s Consumer Experience team and take responsibility for planning, producing and managing digital marketing activities and social media content for some of Finland's most loved bakery brands, such as Reissumies and Puikula.




The role combines hands-on content creation (photography, video production and editing, graphic design and copywriting), social media planning and management, while supporting broader digital execution across websites, campaigns and influencer collaborations. The role also includes website and landing page maintenance, SEO support and digital channel optimisation, email marketing, MyFazer communications and consumer activities, digital activations, competitions and gamification initiatives, as well as performance monitoring, analytics and reporting. You will be a key contributor in bringing our brands to life across digital touchpoints while ensuring a consistent and engaging consumer experience.




This is a fixed-term full-time position until the end of May 2026. We work mainly onsite at our office in Vaarala, Vantaa.




Why choose Fazer?




At Fazer, you'll have the opportunity to work with some of Finland's most iconic brands and help shape the future of consumer and brand experiences. We offer an inspiring and supportive working environment where you can grow professionally while making a meaningful impact every day.




The base salary of this position is between 3 350 € – 4 100 € / month depending on the knowledge and professional experience of the selected candidate. In addition, we offer a range of benefits including for example discounts to Fazer products and well-being benefits such as ePassi.




What we're looking for




We are looking for curious, proactive and commercially minded professional who thrive in a fast-paced environment and enjoy combining creativity, data and consumer understanding to create outstanding marketing. You are passionate about brands, embrace new technologies and digital trends, and enjoy working closely with colleagues, agencies and partners to bring ideas to life.




For this role we are looking for someone with 1-3+ years of digital marketing and/or content creation experience, strong understanding of social media platforms and content formats, hands-on content production skills, and an analytical mindset and interest in digital performance. We are expecting fluent Finnish and excellent English skills.




Apply now to create experiences that matter




If you'd like to help shape the future of Fazer Bakery's iconic brands and engage consumers through meaningful experiences, we'd love to hear from you. Please submit your application via link by 13 September 2026.




Interviews will be held during week 38 at Fazer’s office in Vaarala, Vantaa.




Any questions? Please contact the hiring manager Annika Paakkinen (Senior Manager, Marketing) by phone +358 50 404 3822, preferably on Thursday, 3 September, from 9–10 or Monday, 7 September, from 15–16.
