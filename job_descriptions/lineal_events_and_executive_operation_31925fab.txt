Events & Executive Operations Manager

Location: Remote

Travel: Approximately 40–50% (Domestic & International)




About the Role

Lineal is seeking an Events & Executive Operations Manager to support both our Marketing and Executive Leadership teams.

This role is approximately 70% focused on event and field marketing execution and 30% focused on executive operations support. The ideal candidate is highly organized, detail-oriented, and comfortable balancing multiple priorities while working across teams, time zones, and leadership levels.

On the marketing side, this person will own the planning, coordination, and execution of Lineal's global events program, including flagship events, executive events, conferences, sponsorships, and client experiences. They will also support event marketing activities, creative production, and related campaign execution.

On the executive operations side, this person will provide scheduling, coordination, meeting support, and follow-through assistance for the Chief Client Officer and Executive Leadership Team, helping ensure strategic priorities move forward efficiently.

This is a highly collaborative role for someone who enjoys making things happen, solving problems, and bringing structure to fast-moving initiatives.




What You'll Do

Event & Field Marketing (Approximately 70%)

Own the planning and execution of Lineal's global event portfolio, including:

Flagship owned events
Executive and client events
Industry conferences and sponsorships
Internal company meetings and events




Manage all event logistics, including:

Project plans and timelines
Budgets and forecasting
Venue sourcing and management
Vendor coordination
Swag and promotional materials
Onsite execution




Partner with sales, leadership, and external stakeholders to align events with business objectives and client engagement goals.

Travel to and represent Lineal at events, conferences, and client engagements.




Create and manage event-related marketing assets, including:

Booth graphics and signage
Presentations and collateral
Event invitations and email communications
Landing pages and registration experiences




Support event promotion and attendee communications before, during, and after events.

Manage post-event follow-up processes, including attendee feedback, reporting, and recommendations for future improvements.

Support broader marketing initiatives as needed, including digital asset creation, campaign execution, and project coordination.




Executive Operations Support (Approximately 30%)

Provide scheduling and coordination support for the Executive Leadership Team.

Manage calendar scheduling and prioritization for the Chief Client Officer, including coordinating meetings across internal teams, clients, and external partners.

Assist with calendar adjustments, meeting logistics, and scheduling conflicts.

Attend meetings and leadership calls to document notes, decisions, action items, and next steps.

Draft follow-up plans and communicate action items to stakeholders.

Track progress against commitments and proactively coordinate with team members to help drive tasks to completion.

Assist with preparation for executive meetings, client engagements, presentations, and strategic initiatives.

Help maintain organization, visibility, and accountability across key business priorities.




What We're Looking For

5+ years of experience in event management, field marketing, marketing operations, executive support, or a related role
Proven experience managing events from planning through execution
Strong project management and organizational skills
Experience supporting senior executives or executive leadership teams
Exceptional attention to detail and follow-through
Experience managing multiple projects and competing priorities simultaneously
Strong interpersonal skills and confidence interacting with clients, executives, and senior stakeholders
Experience managing vendors, budgets, and external partners
Proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign) and Canva
Experience creating event signage, booth graphics, presentations, and marketing collateral
Familiarity with HubSpot or similar marketing automation platforms
Comfortable working independently in a fast-paced, remote environment
Willingness and ability to travel approximately 40–50%, both domestically and internationally
Comfortable supporting a global organization across multiple time zones




Nice to Have

Experience in legal technology, B2B technology, or professional services
Experience working within a global organization




Why This Role Matters

Events play a critical role in how Lineal builds relationships, strengthens its brand, and creates opportunities for growth. At the same time, strong executive operations help ensure leadership teams stay organized, aligned, and focused on strategic priorities.

This role directly impacts both. The person in this position will help deliver exceptional client and event experiences while providing the operational support needed to keep key initiatives moving forward across the business.
