Hae paikkaa

Do you want to work with innovative technology and collaborate with leading mining industry customers?

At our Sandvik's Underground Drilling Division, we are looking for a Project Manager to lead product development projects that support the future of intelligent automation, digitalization and sustainable mining solutions. This permanent position is based in Tampere (Myllypuro), Finland.

Your mission

As Project Manager, you will be responsible for leading cross-functional product development projects from planning through execution. You will work closely with internal and external stakeholders to ensure successful project delivery in line with business objectives.

You will play a key role in bringing people together around a common goal, creating enthusiasm and commitment, and helping teams succeed. Guided by Sandvik’s core values, you inspire and guide teams through challenges, help remove obstacles, and create an environment where people can perform at their best.

Your main responsibilities

Lead and drive product development projects

Create and maintain project plans, schedules, budgets and resource plans

Manage project scope, risks, opportunities, dependencies and changes

Coordinate and collaborate with cross-functional teams and stakeholders

Ensure projects are delivered according to agreed objectives and timelines

Your background and what we find important

Experience managing projects in a complex environment

Strong leadership, communication and stakeholder management skills

Ability to build commitment, foster collaboration and help teams overcome obstacles

A proactive and solution-oriented mindset

Relevant higher education or equivalent experience

Fluent English

Experience in product development in engineering or the mining industry is an advantage, but not a requirement

What’s in it for you?

We offer an impactful role in an international environment where you can contribute to the future of mining technology while developing your professional skills and career.

We believe that diversity of experience, perspective and background will lead to a better environment for our employees, our business and, thereby, our customers. Sandvik has an inclusive company culture where our values are Winning together, Curiosity, Responsibility, and Customer focus.

About us

Sandvik is a global, industrial technology group providing solutions that enhance productivity, profitability and sustainability for the manufacturing, mining and infrastructure industries. We are at the forefront of digitalization and focus on optimizing our customers’ processes. Our world-leading offering includes equipment, tools, services and digital solutions for machining, mining, rock excavation and rock processing. In 2025 the Group had approximately 42.000 employees and revenues of about SEK 121 billion in more than 150 countries.

The Underground Drilling Division within Business Area Mining is an industry leading global provider of drilling equipment for underground mining and tunneling industry with focus on technology development and future innovative solutions to enable sustainable operations for our customers. Sandvik Mining and Construction Oy’s Tampere site is a global technology and manufacturing center for Underground Drilling equipment.
