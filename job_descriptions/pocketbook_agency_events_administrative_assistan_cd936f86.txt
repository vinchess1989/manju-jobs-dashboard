An event management company is looking for an Events Administrative Assistant. You will support a fast-paced events and production team through administrative work behind the scenes. This role handles the operations that keep the business running: invoicing, scheduling, contractor payments, insurance, and calendar management.




Travel is required. You will be onsite for events in New York and Los Angeles, and other various locations.




This role is open to candidates who are based in NYC.




Responsibilities

Manage invoicing and billing across active client accounts
Handle 1099 processing for contractors
Track and file insurance documents (auto, comp, and event-specific policies)
Support tax filing tasks tied to team operations
Manage calendars across multiple team members and time zones
Schedule some client-facing meetings, including dinners tied to events
Travel to on-site events when needed
Adapt quickly when pulled into tasks outside your core scope




Requirements

1 to 3 years of experience in office management, executive assistant work, agency support, or event production
Strong organizational skills. You notice gaps and fix them without waiting for instructions
Comfort with fast pace and pressure. Events move fast, and one mistake can cause a chain reaction
Thick skin. You take feedback well and don't take it personally
Flexibility outside standard business hours, especially around event dates
Familiarity with insurance terminology (auto, comp) and event logistics is a plus, even at an internship level
Willingness to travel for on-site event days




Compensation and Structure

Base salary: $45,000
Day-rate bonus for on-site event days
Some events require multiple days on-site: setup, event day, and strike, each paid separately




Location

Open to candidates in NYC and requires travel to events.




Who Thrives Here

You want a role with real ownership, not a 9-to-5 routine. You stay calm under pressure and don't get rattled by last-minute changes.
