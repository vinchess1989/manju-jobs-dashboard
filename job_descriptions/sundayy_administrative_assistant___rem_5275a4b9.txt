About The Company

Gallagher Bassett is a leading provider of claims management services dedicated to supporting clients through some of their most challenging moments. With a strong reputation for integrity, professionalism, and innovative solutions, Gallagher Bassett helps individuals and businesses navigate complex insurance claims processes efficiently and effectively. The company values a culture of teamwork, curiosity, and purpose-driven service, fostering an environment where employees are empowered to make a meaningful impact. Gallagher Bassett's commitment to excellence and client-centric approach has established it as a trusted partner in the insurance industry, offering comprehensive claims solutions across various lines of coverage.

About The Role

The Administrative Assistant at Gallagher Bassett plays a vital role in supporting the Claims team, primarily focusing on professional liability lines such as Healthcare, Errors & Omissions (E&O), Directors & Officers (D&O), Cyber, and Employment Practices Liability (EPL). This position involves providing comprehensive administrative and clerical support to ensure smooth operations within the claims department. The role requires meticulous attention to detail, excellent organizational skills, and the ability to handle sensitive information with confidentiality. As a key member of the team, the Administrative Assistant will assist in claim documentation, reporting, and coordination among internal teams, insureds, clients, brokers, legal counsel, and third-party vendors. This position offers the flexibility of remote work, making it an ideal opportunity for professionals seeking a dynamic and supportive work environment.

Qualifications


High school diploma or equivalent required
2 years of related administrative experience required
Working knowledge of Microsoft Windows applications and Microsoft Office Suite
Preferred: 3–5 years of administrative experience, preferably within insurance or claims
Prior exposure to professional liability claims terminology strongly preferred
Strong organizational skills with exceptional attention to detail
Ability to handle sensitive and confidential information effectively
Excellent written and verbal communication skills
Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment
Experience working with claims management systems
Basic understanding of claims workflows, including indemnity vs. ALAE tracking
Proven ability to meet deadlines in a high-volume, time-sensitive setting
Strong follow-up skills and thorough documentation habits


Responsibilities


Provide day-to-day administrative support to claims professionals and executive leadership
Receive and process incoming Notices of Loss, including First Reports, Supplemental Reports, and Closing Notices
Track administrative activities, follow-ups, and deadlines to ensure timely completion of tasks
Assist with claim system entries and updates to maintain accurate records
Help maintain organized and comprehensive electronic claim files
Prepare and coordinate written and verbal communications to insureds, clients, brokers, attorneys, and third-party vendors under the direction of claims professionals
Assist with payment processing for indemnity and expense transactions, including invoice tracking
Prepare claim-related reports, loss summaries, loss runs, and bordereaux as needed
Ensure compliance with internal claims guidelines, procedures, and documentation standards
Support the claims team in administrative tasks that facilitate efficient claims handling and reporting
