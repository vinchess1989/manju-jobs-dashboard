Hey lawyer with a couple of years of work experience!




Do you want to help create a healthier world – and you know that in a growth company that means hard work, shifting challenges, and the perseverance to see things through? Do you see yourself thriving as a member of a team that wants to create something new (rather than to just repeat and secure the old)? Are you committed to keep learning and growing professionally to be able to help shape better healthcare for the world?




If you responded YES, please keep reading, because you could be the one we are looking for to join Nightingale Health as their new Legal Counsel!




Chronic diseases cause around 75% of deaths globally and place a growing burden on healthcare systems. The way to address this is preventive healthcare, but until recently the tools to detect disease risks at population scale have not existed. Nightingale Health has built them. The Nightingale Health Check is a blood-based risk detection test that identifies risks for multiple chronic diseases, including cardiovascular disease, type 2 diabetes, kidney disease, and fatty liver disease, from a single blood sample. It is designed to be applied population-wide, so healthcare systems can identify the people who would benefit most from preventive action before disease develops.




Nightingale Health's technology is validated in over 900 peer-reviewed scientific publications and is used in many of the world's leading health studies, including the UK Biobank. In Finland, the Nightingale Health Check is in nationwide healthcare use, and over 200,000 people have already received their results. Nightingale Health operates globally with a parent company in Finland and subsidiaries in countries such as Japan, the United States, Singapore, and the United Kingdom, and has customers in more than 34 countries across healthcare and medical research. The company's Series B shares are listed on the Main Market of Nasdaq Helsinki and on the OTCQX International market. Read more about Nightingale Health here.




At Nightingale Health, you will join an agile and commercially focused Helsinki-based Legal Team of two lawyers, and report to the General Counsel. You will be based in Nightingale Health’s bright and modern office located in Tilkka, Helsinki. The team works flexibly, but frequent office presence is highly appreciated.




In your day-to-day work, you will work with a wide range of legal topics covering the entire geographical footprint of Nightingale Health. For example, you will provide legal advice in various fields of law, such as intellectual property, data protection, healthcare and medical device laws and regulations, as well as draft and negotiate different types of agreements, such as sales, R&D and service agreements. You will also manage intellectual property rights and develop and oversee internal legal policies and educate your colleagues on legal topics. In addition, you will manage corporate governance and legal matters relating to the listed parent company, as well as legal matters for the group’s nine subsidiaries.




You could be the candidate we are looking for, if:




🔹 you have an LL.M degree from Finland or another EU country and a couple of years of post-graduate experience from business law e.g. from a law firm or an in-house legal position – previous exposure to health care sector is an asset but not a requirement;




🔹 you have a growth mindset and enjoy constant learning – at Nightingale Health, things move fast and every team member is expected to grow along with the company;




🔹 you identify yourself as a generalist rather than a specialist – agile allocation of tasks is crucial in a small legal team;




🔹 you are eager to take an active role not only when it comes to legal topics, but to learn and consider commercial aspects, as well; and




🔹 you are pragmatic, solutions-oriented, and communicate fluently in both Finnish and English.




Nightingale Health offers you:




A unique work environment: you get to join an international working environment and to work with a multidisciplinary team of scientists, business professionals, developers, designers as well as biotech business experts who have developed a pioneering health check that provides risk detection for multiple chronic diseases from a single blood sample.




A commercial legal role: there are lawyers who enjoy sitting on the sidelines reading the rulebook, and then there are lawyers who want to join the game and play alongside the business. We are looking for the latter type!




A team with a shared mission: you will work with trusting, helping and people-oriented colleagues whose mission is to build sustainable healthcare and reduce health inequalities.




An opportunity for professional growth: Nightingale Health’s high-paced environment offers you an opportunity to constantly develop professionally – we can guarantee that you will not feel stagnated!




Benefits to support your well-being: besides industry-standard salary and eligibility for Nightingale Health’s stock option program, you will receive a wide scale of benefits to support your health and well-being – of which we are happy to tell you more in our conversations.




Interested? Send us your CV and cover letter no later than on 3 September 2026. If you would like to discuss the position in more detail, feel free to reach out to Minjon’s recruitment consultant Vilma Suomi (+358 50 360 5134 or vilma.suomi@minjon.fi).
