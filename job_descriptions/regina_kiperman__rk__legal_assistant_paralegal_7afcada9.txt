Tallenna

RK Law PC is a fast paced, boutique Trusts and Estates and Elder Law Firm in lower Manhattan. We focus on Probate & Estate Administration, Estate Litigation, Mental Hygiene Law Article 81, 17, and 17a Guardianship, Estate Planning, Special Needs Planning, Elder law, & Medicaid planning. We try to maintain a great working environment and love to help people. 




We are committed to providing excellent service to our clients while maintaining a collaborative, professional, and congenial working environment. We enjoy helping people through important and often difficult legal matters, and we are looking for someone who shares that commitment.




We are growing and seeking a full-time Trusts & Estates Legal Assistant / Junior Paralegal with an interest in Trusts & Estates and Elder Law.




This is an excellent opportunity for someone who wants to develop substantive legal skills, gain hands-on experience, and grow into a more advanced paralegal role.

Responsibilities




The Legal Assistant / Junior Paralegal will assist attorneys and staff with the day-to-day management of client matters, court filings, case organization, and office operations.




Responsibilities include:

Drafting and revising legal correspondence, affidavits, affirmations, subpoenas, and other legal documents;
Preparing routine legal documents, including Notices of Settlement, Notices of Entry, subpoenas, Affidavits of Legal Services, and related filings;
Preparing documents for filing with New York State courts, including electronic filing through NYSCEF and other applicable court filing systems;
Traveling to Surrogate’s Court and other courts, when necessary, to file documents, check the status of matters, or complete other court-related tasks;
Maintaining and organizing electronic and physical case files;
Scanning, filing, saving, naming, and organizing client documents and correspondence;
Calendaring court appearances, filing deadlines, conferences, appointments, and other important dates;
Monitoring deadlines and proactively following up with attorneys regarding upcoming obligations;
Communicating professionally with clients, courts, opposing counsel, government agencies, financial institutions, medical providers, and other third parties;
Answering certain incoming telephone calls in a courteous and professional manner and directing calls appropriately;
Assisting with new-client intake, including gathering information and documentation and opening new matters;
Coordinating clients with remote or traveling notaries for document execution;
Preparing and organizing exhibits, document productions, hearing and trial materials, and attorney binders;
Organizing financial records, bank statements, medical records, and other case-related documents;
Assisting with discovery and document collection;
Scheduling meetings, calls, depositions, court appearances, and client appointments;
Preparing correspondence and following up regarding outstanding documents, signatures, records, responses, and other pending items;
Mailing documents through FedEx, UPS, USPS, and other delivery services and maintaining accurate tracking information;
Assisting with service of legal documents and tracking affidavits or proof of service;
Maintaining accurate client and matter information in the firm's case-management systems;
Performing data entry and maintaining accurate records;
Assisting attorneys with billing, time-entry follow-up, and other matter-management tasks as needed;
Maintaining office supplies and assisting with general office organization;
Providing general administrative and clerical support to ensure the efficient operation of the firm; and
Performing other related administrative and legal support tasks as the needs of the firm require.
Qualifications




College graduate; Bachelor’s Degree preferred.
Prior experience working in a law firm or legal office is strongly preferred.
Prior experience with Trusts & Estates, Surrogate’s Court, Elder Law, guardianship, estate administration, or related practice areas is a plus.
Strong interest in Trusts & Estates and Elder Law is highly desirable.
Familiarity with NYSCEF and New York court filing procedures is preferred.
Strong written and verbal communication skills.
Excellent proofreading and attention-to-detail skills.
Strong organizational and time-management abilities.
Ability to manage multiple assignments, deadlines, and priorities simultaneously.
Comfortable using Google Docs, Google Sheets, Microsoft Office, Adobe Acrobat, and other common office technology.
Ability to learn new software and case-management systems quickly.




We Are Looking for Someone Who Is




Highly organized and detail-oriented;
Punctual and dependable;
A strong communicator;
Able to multitask effectively;
Professional and courteous with clients and other professionals;
Flexible and willing to assist wherever needed;
A fast learner;
A self-starter who takes initiative;
Comfortable following detailed procedures while exercising good judgment;
Proactive about deadlines and follow-up;
Able to complete assignments accurately and follow through without repeated reminders; and
Interested in learning and taking on increasing responsibility over time.




Opportunity for Growth




This position is intended for someone at the legal assistant or junior paralegal level. For the right candidate, there is meaningful opportunity to learn the substantive aspects of Trusts & Estates, Probate and Estate Administration, Estate Litigation, Guardianship, Estate Planning, and Elder Law and to grow into a more advanced paralegal position.




Compensation & Benefits

Salary Range: $60,000–$75,000 per year.

The actual salary offered will be determined in good faith based on a variety of factors, including the candidate’s relevant experience, qualifications, skills, and education.
