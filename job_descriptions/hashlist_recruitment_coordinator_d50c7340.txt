People Operations Manager @ Hashlist - From Chip to Vehicle | Global Automotive Embedded & IVI Recruitment | US & EMEA | Talent Acquisition | DEI

Hashlist helps high-performing engineering teams execute critical integrations and large-scale programs by delivering expertise and tools across vehicles and physical AI. We are working with the world's largest OEMs and Tier 1 suppliers.




2026 has been a turning point. We are currently growing 4x annualized, demand from our clients is surging and we're only getting started. We will invest a lot of effort in the physical AI space and will therefore need a recruitment coordinator to join our core team and help us source, screen, and support our customers in finding the talent they need.




What you'll actually do

Source and screen engineers across embedded software, automotive, robotics and physical AI.
Work directly with clients to understand their technical needs and match them with the right talent, fast
Manage candidate pipelines end-to-end - from first outreach to signed contracts
Coordinate across time zones - our clients and engineers span Europe, the U.S., and Asia
Improve our processes - if something's broken or slow, you fix it. No waiting for permission
Pick up the phone - cold outreach, warm outreach, whatever it takes to find the right people




Who we're looking for

A high-energy, self-starting operator with a strong work ethic
Someone who thrives in fast-paced, sometimes chaotic startup environments
Comfortable working across multiple time zones and juggling competing priorities
Strong communicator - you write well, you speak well, you follow up relentlessly
Not afraid to reach out to strangers and have real conversations
Strong sense of ownership - you don't wait to be told what to do
You put delivery and the customer first, always
Fluent with AI tools - using them to research, draft, and automate is your second nature




What gives you an edge

Startup experience - you know what "figure it out" means
Additional languages: French or Italian
Background in recruitment, staffing, or operations




What we offer

A front-row seat in a fast-scaling company backed by serious automotive industry investors (former Continental and IBM Automotive executives, and superangels from Finland's top startup ecosystem)
Real responsibility from day one - no corporate ladder, no waiting your turn
A small team of eight that moves fast, ships fast, and doesn't do politics
Competitive compensation + benefits
The chance to work at the intersection of automotive and physical AI - one of the most exciting spaces in tech right now
