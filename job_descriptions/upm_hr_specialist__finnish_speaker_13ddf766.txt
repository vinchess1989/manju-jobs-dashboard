We are currently looking for an HR Specialist, Finnish Speaker to join one of our teams at UPM HR Services in Kraków, Poland. We offer both permanent and fixed-term opportunities.

 Greetings from your future manager: 

"If you are looking for a job in international team with great atmosphere and open-minded professionals, where you can grow your HR career, then UPM HR Services is the place to be. We provide high-quality HR services to all employees across UPM organization globally. Collaboration and support are at the heart of what we do – we always support each other, making sure our UPM employees get the best service experience. - Irina Stepanova, Manager, HRS Develop & Perform

What you will do 

 In HR Services, our work is organized across multiple teams, each with a slightly different scope of responsibility. In one of our teams, you will help create a positive HR experience for employees by ensuring HR information and processes are accurate and up to date. As part of your role, you will take part in additional HR projects and initiatives, with the opportunity to implement your own ideas.

 Join team is supporting end-to-end recruitment and hiring activities across UPM. You will:

Co-operate with stakeholders at all levels of the business including HR business partners, managers, candidates, and recruitment agencies
Ensure great employee and candidate experience by maintaining clear and consistent communication
Post jobs in selected recruitment channels, schedule interviews, background checks and assessments

Develop & Perform team is supporting local UPM business units with learning-related activities. You will:

Support employee growth and development by coordinating engaging classroom and digital training programs
Guide employees and managers on HR processes and policies
Ensure smooth learning experience through training administration, stakeholder communication, Workday data management, reporting, and invoice verification

 Rewards and Career Changes team is supporting employee data management. You will:

Perform various tasks related to employment life cycle administration and reporting.
Maintain employees’ personal, employment, and compensation data in our HR system
Prepare documents, administer benefit processes, prepare various reports, and answer queries related to our HR system and employment.

 Who you are 

You are fluent in Finnish and have an excellent command of English, both spoken and written 
You are able to work also in a third language - Spanish or German is considered an asset
You are interested in HR and eager to learn how it is organized in a global company 
You can systematically approach a high volume of diverse assignments and handle competing priorities 
You demonstrate accuracy and attention to detail 

This is what we offer to you  

Meaningful job: We are proud of what we do. Our work highlights the expertise of our personnel, the high quality of our products and the company's values.    
Inspired and motivated teams: We have highly skilled workforce. We are a multifaceted, international organization that works as a team to achieve our shared goals.    
Responsibility for people and environment: Our ethical principles embrace people as well as the environment. We are always aiming to build a more sustainable future.    
Comprehensive onboarding and development opportunities: We provide you with the knowledge you need to perform well in your role and to feel like a part of the team, you can also take part in additional assignments and learn new valuable skills.    
Support with relocation to Kraków: We offer flight tickets and support with accommodation. Kraków is an international and affordable city offering a wide range of attractions as well as great transport connections to the Tatra Mountains and other parts of Poland!    
Attractive benefits package: UPM’s benefits package includes private medical health care, life insurance, sport’s card, budget for external training, and company phone also for private use.    
Flexible work arrangements: employing a hybrid model, our employees can split their working time between their home and the office. We support flexible working hours within agreed frame. Our office and ways of working are adjusted to meet the highest safety standards.    

Additional information about the position

We are located in Eximius Park in Zabierzów, Kraków area. 

This position offers a hybrid working model, combining remote work from home with regular on-site collaboration.
