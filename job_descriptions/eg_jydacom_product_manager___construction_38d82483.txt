PRODUCT MANAGEMENT · JYVÄSKYLÄ, MATTILANNIEMI
Product Manager - Construction
Own the strategy and roadmap for Finland’s leading construction SaaS, turning customer insight into smarter cost control, stronger project margins and Nordic growth.
Apply

EG Jydacom - the leading financial management solution for construction in Finland

For years, Jydacom has been the trusted partner for Finnish construction companies managing project economics, cost control, and profitability. Now we're looking for a Product Manager to take it further - strengthening our position at home and laying the groundwork for growth across the rest of the Nordics.

You'll shape how leading Finnish construction firms manage costs, projects, and margins in an increasingly digital industry.

Ready to own a role where deep industry insight meets real impact?

As part of the department
EG Jydacom is an ERP solution built for construction companies, supporting the entire project lifecycle – from tendering and cost calculation to project management, site control, and financial management. The solution helps our customers make better decisions and improve the financial results of their projects throughout the construction projects. EG Jydacom’s customers are the largest construction companies in Finland, with high demands, which makes working with them interesting and motivating.

Our Business Unit is called 'EG Construction', and here you'll be part of a team of more than 400 colleagues, all sharing the same focus and passion: We want to simplify our customers’ world. With a deep understanding of the customer value chain, the market, and a relentless focus on customer success, we're driving growth, streamlining processes, and ensuring a seamless experience for all. With offices in Finland, Norway, and Denmark, we are proud to say that we are market leading within the Construction industry in the markets we are in, and we are working hard on digitizing, optimizing and lowering the CO2 footprint every day.

Your Role: Own the Strategy. Drive the Roadmap. Scale Beyond Finland.
We're looking for a Product Manager with construction industry knowledge and a SaaS background to take EG Jydacom further.

You'll own the product direction for construction SaaS - working with engineering, marketing, sales, and customer success to turn customer insight into features that matter, and shaping a roadmap grounded in real market understanding.

You'll drive the product vision, set the benchmark for excellence, and keep us ahead of the industry, treating AI as core to the product, not an add-on - backed by EG's product management framework, sharp analytical thinking, and evidence-based decisions.

Key Responsibilities:

Own and drive the product strategy for Jydacom — spotting where construction companies are bleeding margin on projects, and turning that into the roadmap that fixes it.

Translate real site-level pain — cost overruns, invisible margins, manual invoicing, fragmented project data — into a product experience that gives finance and project teams control they can trust.

Stay close to our key customers — the construction firms actually running project budgets through Jydacom — and turn their feedback into features that keep us ahead of every competitor eyeing this market.

Lead the roadmap end-to-end: from concept to release, balancing what customers need against where the business needs to grow.

Partner with Product Owners and agile teams to ship releases that work in the field, not just in the backlog.

Team up with sales and marketing to launch new capabilities and take EG Jydacom beyond Finland — building the go-to-market muscle for Nordic expansion.

You Are the Ideal Candidate If you

Have strong financial expertise, combined with an understanding of how long-term construction projects are managed and executed, with particular focus on cost and profitability.

Have proven experience as a Product Manager within a SaaS environment.

Have excellent ability to transform customer needs and market trends into actionable product strategies.

Have data-driven mindset with experience leveraging analytics to guide product decisions.

Have exceptional communication skills in Finnish and in English, and the ability to work effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

What's in it for you?
You'll join EG Jydacom's Product Management team, working alongside skilled Product Managers, Product Owners, and Developers in an agile organization united by one goal: digital solutions that create real value for our customers' businesses.

Real ownership - High responsibility from day one, with room to grow your skills and shape how you work.

Meaningful impact- Your decisions feed directly into products construction companies rely on every day.

Collaborative culture - Engaged, skilled colleagues who take the work seriously — and each other, not so much.

We also back that up with: good health insurance, pension, lunch benefit, and employee activities, plus the possibility of hybrid working.

Our Values:

Lead the Industry — We bring deep industry expertise and help our customers lead their industry.

Make it Happen — We take ownership, move fast and deliver real impact.

Stronger Together — We collaborate across borders with high standards, trust, openness and no politics.

Your working location will be preferably in Jyväskylä, but we support a hybrid workplace, and we are sure we can make it work if you are the right candidate.
