Title: Document Reviewer

Location: - Washington, DC 20530 (Remote)

Hire Type: Contract




Job Summary

We are seeking a detail-oriented Document Reviewer to support litigation and legal matters through document review, legal research, and case preparation. The ideal candidate will have experience reviewing large volumes of documents, identifying responsive and privileged materials, conducting legal research, and supporting attorneys throughout the litigation lifecycle. This role requires strong analytical skills, attention to detail, and the ability to work effectively in a fast-paced legal environment while maintaining accuracy and confidentiality.




Key Responsibilities

Review legal documents for responsiveness, relevance, confidentiality, and privilege.

Perform document review to ensure accuracy and consistency.

Assist attorneys with all phases of litigation, including discovery and case preparation.

Conduct legal research using industry-standard legal research platforms.

Prepare and assist with legal documents, including memoranda, motions, briefs, and other case-related materials.

Execute searches within document review platforms to locate relevant information.

Organize, categorize, and maintain legal documents and case files.

Support document production and ensure compliance with established review protocols.

Collaborate with attorneys, paralegals, and litigation support teams to meet project deadlines.

Maintain confidentiality while handling sensitive legal information.




Required Qualifications

Experience reviewing legal documents in support of litigation or investigations.

Knowledge of federal and state court systems and litigation procedures.

Experience conducting legal research using tools such as Westlaw and Lexis.

Strong written and verbal communication skills.

Experience using Microsoft Office applications, including Word and Excel.

Familiarity with document management and document review platforms.

Excellent analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills.

Ability to manage multiple priorities while meeting deadlines.




Preferred Qualifications

Experience using Relativity or other eDiscovery/document review platforms.

Experience with document review quality control (QC) and privilege review.

Knowledge of eDiscovery processes and litigation support workflows.

Experience performing keyword, Boolean, and advanced document searches.

Previous experience supporting complex litigation or government legal matters.
