Description

Amazon’s Last Mile legal team is seeking a talented attorney to support our rapidly growing and evolving Last Mile logistics teams. Amazon’s goal is to exceed the expectations of all our customers by ensuring that their orders, no matter how large or small, are delivered where and when they need them, as quickly, accurately, and safely as possible. To meet this goal, Amazon’s Last Mile logistics teams continually strive to innovate and provide best in class service through pioneering new products, programs, and services. The Last Mile legal team supports these efforts by providing practical legal advice, developing scalable compliance mechanisms, and helping the business to inspect their programs and look around corners to manage risk proactively.

Key job responsibilities


Serve as lead counsel on a diverse array of projects, including product counseling, commercial and technology contracts, marketing, privacy, and regulatory compliance matters.
Anticipate and address strategic legal, regulatory, and reputational risk.
Develop strategies for handling legal issues in creative, business-centric ways.
Build processes that address risk with flexibility and freedom to move quickly.


Basic Qualifications


5+ years of legal experience
Juris Doctor and membership in one state bar or equivalent


Preferred Qualifications


Experience of a mix of corporate law firm and in-house work
Experience working cross functionally across several teams
Experience managing multiple projects and priorities across teams in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment
Experience in product counseling or commercial transactions a plus
Ability to work effectively and demonstrate sound judgment in fast-paced, ambiguous situations
Strong written and oral communication skills
