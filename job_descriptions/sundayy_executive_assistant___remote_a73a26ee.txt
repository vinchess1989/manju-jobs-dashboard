About The Company

Solar Landscape is a leading provider of solar energy solutions dedicated to promoting sustainable and environmentally friendly power generation. With a commitment to innovation, quality, and customer satisfaction, we serve a diverse range of clients across multiple regions. Our company prides itself on fostering a collaborative and inclusive work environment where employees are empowered to contribute to meaningful projects that make a positive impact on communities and the planet. Operating in multiple locations including Asbury Park, New York City, Boston, Chicago, and Baltimore, Solar Landscape continues to expand its footprint in the renewable energy sector, driven by a passionate team of professionals dedicated to advancing clean energy initiatives.

About The Role

We are seeking a proactive and ambitious Executive Assistant to join our New York City office. This pivotal role involves providing comprehensive support to our leadership team, particularly assisting an Executive Vice President and three senior executives within the Business Development division. The ideal candidate will demonstrate exceptional organizational skills, resourcefulness, and the ability to manage a wide array of administrative responsibilities efficiently. This position requires excellent communication skills, a high level of discretion, and the ability to thrive in a fast-paced, dynamic environment. The Executive Assistant will serve as a key point of contact for internal teams and external partners, ensuring seamless operations and effective coordination across various functions. The role offers an exciting opportunity to contribute to the growth and success of Solar Landscape while gaining valuable experience supporting senior leadership in a forward-thinking organization.

Qualifications


Minimum of 2+ years of experience in an administrative or executive support role
Exceptional organizational and time management skills with strong attention to detail
Proven ability to manage multiple competing priorities effectively
Excellent written and verbal communication skills
High level of discretion, judgment, and integrity in handling sensitive information
Proactive, resourceful, and solutions-oriented mindset
Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (Outlook, Excel, PowerPoint)
Experience supporting multiple executives or a growing team is highly desirable
NYC-based role requiring in-office presence five days a week, with occasional travel to the Asbury Park headquarters


Responsibilities


Manage calendars for Business Development leaders, prioritizing high-impact meetings and client interactions
Coordinate communication and follow-through across the Business Development team and cross-functional partners
Arrange travel logistics including flights, accommodations, and transportation aligned with team schedules
Organize internal and external meetings, including client engagements and team sessions
Track and manage key documents, approvals, and signatures in collaboration with internal departments such as legal
Provide administrative support for Business Development initiatives and projects as needed
Process and reconcile expenses accurately and in a timely manner
Oversee day-to-day office operations, including ordering supplies and maintaining office equipment
Assist with ad hoc tasks and additional responsibilities as they arise within the team


Benefits


Training and professional development opportunities
401(k) plan with a 4% company match
Summer Fridays for enhanced work-life balance
Flexible remote and hybrid work arrangements
Paid parental leave to support family needs
Team lunches, social events, and well-stocked kitchens
Modern, collaborative office spaces in multiple locations including Asbury Park and NYC
Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision insurance coverage
Company-paid life insurance and long-term disability coverage
