About the position in details:




Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is a world-leading IT services, consulting and business solutions organization that has been partnering with many of the world’s largest businesses in their transformation journeys for over 50 years. We believe innovation and collective knowledge can transform all our futures with greater purpose.




TCS is a truly global company with over 600 000 employees worldwide including 153 nationalities with the revue of over 7,2 billion dollars. In Finland, TCS has been present since 1990’s and currently we have over 6000 employees supporting Finnish customers. We at TCS are committed to playing our part for a greater, sustainable future and we are responsible for every life we touch.

A part of the Tata group, India's largest multinational business group, TCS shares Tata Group’s values – Integrity, Responsibility, Excellence, Pioneering and Unity.




Student, are you looking for an internship in the field of sales or marketing for this upcoming spring-term? Do not miss this opportunity to get a unique experience in one of the world’s most recognized IT organizations!




We at Tata Consulting Services Finland (TCS Finland) are looking for a sales intern to focus on two main projects: Conducting market research as well as doing customer profiling.

This is an exciting, full-time, ideally a 6-month paid internship. In this role you will get a great overview of the Finnish ICT market, and you will be introduced to truly global company’s ways of working. You will get a real-life experience in delivering growth and value to the customers. Your main workplace will be at our Finnish headquarters in Ruoholahti, Helsinki, but you will also have a possibility to work remotely as well as occasionally travel in Finland.

Your typical workdays will consist of the elements of the key responsibilities of the role:

Conduct in-depth Finnish market research across industries and key clienteles to aid the growth aspiration of TCS
Collaborate with Market Sales and wider TCS team to understand TCS offering, capability and map to the whitespace
Contribute to actionable content, market reach out of TCS prospects in the Finnish market
Participate in TCS market events, Customer forum, Technology webinars and round tables
Orchestrate and Amplify TCS brand leveraging TCS channels and process




Whom are we looking for?




To be successful on the above mentioned role, we are looking for a candidate with right skills and mindset:

Excellent analytical skills to conduct in-depth market analysis.
A result-driven growth mindset and the passion to learn more about the IT field and its solutions.
Being a real team player with strong stakeholder and relationship management attributes across internal and external key stakeholders.
Excellent communication (both verbal and written) and presentation skills to deliver your messages and convince the management.
Fluency in English, which is our organizational language.
Finnish language skills are considered as a plus, as this position requires the capability of analyzing a Finnish market.
Creativity is seen as an advantage.




Apply now!




Send your CV together with the motivation letter to Recruitment.FI@tcs.com as soon as possible, but the latest by 15th August 2026. We will go through the applications on an ongoing basis.

The internship has been planned to start in the beginning of September 2026, and it is 6 months fixed term paid internship until something else is agreed upon.
