Why Jackson

Jackson Health System is a nationally and internationally recognized academic medical system offering world-class care to any person who walks through our doors. For more than 100 years, Jackson has evolved into one of the world's top medical providers for all levels of care, no matter if it's for a routine patient visit or for a lifesaving procedure. With more than 2,000 licensed beds, we are also proud of our role as the primary teaching hospital for the University of Miami Miller School of Medicine.

Here, the best people come together to deliver Jackson's mission for our diverse communities. Our employees are committed to providing the best CARE by demonstrating compassion, accountability, respect, and expertise in everything we do.

Summary

The JHF Events Coordinator provides day-to-day, hands-on support across all phases of Jackson Health Foundation events. This role ensures smooth logistics, strong vendor relationships, and professional execution from initial planning through post-event wrap-up. Working closely with the Director of Events, the coordinator provides logistical support and onsite needs to help deliver high-quality events that advance the Foundation's mission.

Responsibilities

Assist with planning, logistics, and coordination for all Foundation events.

Support vendor communication and maintain accurate tracking of contracts, Certificates of Insurance (COIs), invoices, and related documentation.

Help maintain event timelines, run-of-show documents, production schedules, and other planning tools.

Participate in venue walkthroughs, vendor meetings, and pre-event planning sessions.

Provide onsite event support, including setup, breakdown, vendor coordination, and guest assistance.

Support post-event follow-up, including documentation, reporting, and file organization.

Maintain organized digital files and ensure all event documentation is stored accurately and consistently.

Work evenings and weekends as needed to support event operations

Experience

Perform all other related job duties as assigned.

Generally requires 1 to 3 years related experience.

Education

Bachelor's degree in related field is preferred.
