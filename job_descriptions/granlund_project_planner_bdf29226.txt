Räätälöi ansioluetteloni

We are now looking for a Project Planner to join Granlund’s industrial and data center projects!

Granlund is a rapidly growing expert organization in the real estate and construction sector. Together with our clients and stakeholders, we are building a sustainable and intelligent future. Our more than 1,600 professionals serve across 30 locations throughout Finland.




Would you be our new Project Planner?

As a Project Planner at Granlund, you will take responsibility for project schedules and project controls in complex construction, data center, and industrial projects. Acting as the Project Manager’s right hand, you will play a central role in supporting project leadership, coordinating stakeholders, and ensuring that projects progress according to plan throughout their lifecycle.




You will work closely with Project Managers, Project Engineers, clients, designers, contractors, and other key stakeholders. Your responsibilities include developing and maintaining project schedules, supporting decision-making through project data and reporting, and ensuring that information flows effectively between different parties. Within projects, you will also guide the work of project engineers and help keep project teams aligned around common goals and timelines.




The role combines project planning, coordination, and leadership. Depending on the project portfolio, you may be responsible for one major project or several smaller projects simultaneously. Projects range from commercial developments to large-scale data center projects, with durations varying from a few months to several years.




The position is a full-time and permanent role, and you will be employed directly by Granlund. The role offers a highly flexible working model with the possibility to work remotely from anywhere in Finland. You may also work from any Granlund office according to your own preference. Occasional travel to project sites, typically around once per month, is expected as part of the role.




What are we looking for?

We are looking for a construction professional with a strong understanding of how projects move from planning to execution. You have experience in project management, project planning, site management or another project-oriented role within the construction industry. Most importantly, you understand how construction projects are delivered and how different stakeholders, phases, and dependencies come together throughout the project lifecycle.




A relevant educational background in construction or engineering is preferred. You may hold a degree as a Construction Engineer, Bachelor of Engineering or Master of Science in Engineering. You communicate fluently in Finnish and have a good working knowledge of English.




As a person, you are honest, collaborative, and proactive. You are able to communicate confidently with stakeholders at different levels and are comfortable taking ownership of your work. You combine a humble attitude with a structured and solution-oriented approach and enjoy working as part of a professional team where knowledge is shared and success is achieved together.




Why join us?

At Granlund, you will work in a company that has been a pioneer in Finnish building services engineering for decades. You will contribute to demanding and large-scale projects such as data centers and industrial environments, where your work truly matters. As one of the leading data center design and consulting companies in the Nordics, we offer long-term career opportunities in a rapidly growing field, where projects are becoming increasingly significant in scale and impact. You will have strong opportunities to grow and develop your expertise, with career paths that can be shaped around your strengths and interests.




We place real emphasis on employee well-being, continuous learning, and a supportive, professional work environment. You will be part of a collaborative and ambitious team where people take ownership, share knowledge, and work together toward common goals.




Are you interested?

Could you be our new colleague? Please submit your application via the link either in English or Finnish as soon as possible, but no later than 16.8.2026. We will begin interviews during the application period.




The recruitment process is carried out in cooperation with Biisoni. For more information, please contact Biisoni’s Recruitment Consultant Nea Repo (nea.repo@biisoni.fi).
