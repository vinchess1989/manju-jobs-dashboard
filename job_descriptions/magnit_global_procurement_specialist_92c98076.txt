Who We Are




Magnit is the evolution of work. Serving hundreds of the world’s most recognizable brands for the past 30+ years, Magnit offers the industry’s first holistic platform for the modern workforce. Magnit’s integrated workforce management (IWM) platform supported by data, software, intelligence, and best-in-class services team is key to our clients’ success. It can adapt quickly to regional or industry economic shifts, and provides the speed, scale, flexibility, transparency, and expertise required to meet an organization’s contingent workforce management, talent strategy and broader organization goals. At Magnit, you’ll work with passionate colleagues who collaborate and deliver meaningful results that positively transform the largest companies around the globe.




About the Role




A a Procurement Specialist within contingent workforce, you are responsible for ensuring an efficient and value-driven delivery of Magnit’s services to our clients. You will work closely with suppliers, hiring managers and cross-functional teams, using the VMS platform to drive compliant, cost-effective and high-quality sourcing processes.




You will play a key role in strengthening procurement practices, supplier performance and overall service delivery.




What You Will Do

Take ownership of the day-to-day delivery of Magnit’s services with a strong focus on procurement excellence
Ensure high-quality service delivery aligned with contractual obligations, KPIs and cost efficiency targets
Act as a key point of contact for stakeholders across multiple business units and EMEA countries
Drive continuous improvement initiatives within sourcing, supplier management and procurement processes
Apply a consultative and data-driven approach to support hiring managers in making optimal purchasing decisions
Maintain and develop Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) to ensure scalable and efficient operations




Within contingent workforce procurement:

Manage and facilitate the sourcing of temporary workforce through the VMS system in alignment with procurement strategies
Actively engage with hiring managers to define requirements and ensure demand planning and specification quality
Collaborate with and manage staffing suppliers, focusing on performance, compliance and value creation
Conduct supplier dialogue and ensure competitive, transparent and compliant sourcing processes
Identify risks, resolve operational and commercial issues, and ensure timely follow-up with all stakeholders
Monitor supplier performance and contribute to supplier development and optimization




What You Will Need

3+ years of experience within procurement, MSP, or contingent workforce management
Strong understanding of sourcing, supplier management and/or service procurement
A business-driven mindset with focus on cost, quality and efficiency
Strong communication skills, with the ability to influence stakeholders across all levels of the organization
Professional fluency in English and Finnish
Analytical mindset with the ability to interpret data and support decision-making
A proactive, solution-oriented attitude with strong ownership
Experience working with VMS systems and procurement tools is highly beneficial
Understanding of billing processes within MSP/procurement environments is a plus
Advanced skills in MS Office (especially Excel)
Bachelor’s degree in business administration, procurement, or similar field
