About this opportunity


A professional responsible for technical documentation of a large-scale data centre construction project. This role ensures that all project documentation is accurate, up-to-date, and accessible to relevant stakeholders, supporting the successful delivery of projects.

This role requires being on-site during the work week. No rotation or fly-in/fly-out arrangement applies.

Responsibilities include but are not limited to:


Maintaining and updating document control procedures and systems, ensuring all documents are correctly filed and easily retrievable.
Coordinating with the project management team to ensure timely submission and approval of documents.
Monitoring document workflows and ensuring compliance with company standards and project requirements.
Providing training and support to the project team on document control processes and systems.
Conducting regular revisions to ensure document control processes are being followed and identify areas for improvement.
Liaising with the client, contractors, and other stakeholders to manage document submissions and approvals.
Preparing and distributing document control reports to the project management team.
Verifying and checking for standards compliance
Remaining in the remit of your role.

Proven experience in document control management within the construction industry.
