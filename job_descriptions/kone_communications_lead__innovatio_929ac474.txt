The People & Communications team enables business performance and competitiveness through engaged and capable people, strong reputation management, impactful internal and external communications, and great employee experiences. The team plays a critical role in bringing KONE’s strategy to life, strengthening the company story and supporting business growth.

Within this, the Global Strategy Communications team is responsible for defining and driving KONE’s global strategic narratives aligned with business priorities and key topics. The team develops overarching storylines and thought leadership positioning, translates complex priorities into clear, insight-led narratives, and enables consistent and compelling storytelling across channels and stakeholders. With a primary focus on external communications and thought leadership, the team also provides strategic advisory to senior leaders on change & transformation, risks and reputational considerations, while offering selective guidance on internal communications to ensure alignment between internal and external narratives.

We are now looking for a

Communications Lead, Innovation

to play a critical role in this model, owning and driving KONE’s external narrative on AI, technology and innovation to support growth, differentiation and market impact.

The role reports to the Head of Strategy Communications and works in close partnership with C-suite leaders across KONE Technology & Innovation, Service business line and Major Projects, as well as with Marketing and global communications teams.

This is a permanent position located in Espoo, Finland.

What You Will Deliver

Drive thought leadership and external communications (:70%)


Own KONE’s innovation narrative, thought leadership, executive positioning and product relevance in AI, technology and innovation
Develop distinctive, insight-led points of view that strengthen KONE’s positioning as an innovation leader
Lead and activate global thought leadership platforms, aligned with strategic priorities and external trends
Drive top-tier global media visibility, strengthening share of voice and quality coverage
Build compelling external narratives that elevate KONE’s customer value and innovation story
Partner closely with Marketing to connect product launches to external trends and market relevance
Translate complex technical topics into clear, engaging and scalable storytelling
Ensure strong product, service and portfolio relevance in external communications
Apply data, insights and AI to continuously improve content effectiveness, reach and efficiency


Strategic advisory & leadership enablement (:30%)


Act as a trusted senior advisor to C-suite and senior leaders across KONE Technology & Innovation, Service and Major Projects
Provide guidance on strategic positioning, messaging, tone and reputational considerations
Support and develop senior spokespersons, including selected EXB-level leaders
Lead internal and change communications related to key initiatives, transformation and sensitive topics
Enable leaders with clear narratives, communication tools and coaching
Support key corporate moments (e.g. quarterly results, leadership forums) as relevant to the topic


End-to-end delivery & global enablement


Drive hands-on development and delivery of global communications programs and campaigns
Create scalable narratives, toolkits and assets for Area and frontline teams
Ensure alignment and consistency across global and local communications
Act as a content and narrative engine, feeding into media, marketing, business and regional teams
Work seamlessly across Global Communications to break silos and maximize impact


What You’ll Bring


15+ years of experience with a focus on strategic communications in a multinational or listed company environment and/or similar
Ability to combine strategic advisory with hands-on delivery
Extensive experience in strategic communications, thought leadership and external positioning
Proven ability to challenge, influence and convince senior stakeholders and C-suite leaders
Strong track record in driving global external communications and media impact
Experience in AI, technology, digital or innovation-related topics
Strong understanding of market dynamics, competition and external trends
Experience working cross-functionally, including with Marketing and business leaders
Excellent storytelling, writing and content development skills
Strong judgment in managing sensitive and high-stakes topics
Ability to operate with agility across priorities and stakeholders
Strong analytics skills and understanding of how to use data-led insights to drive thought leadership


Success in this role looks like


KONE is recognized as a credible, differentiated thought leader in AI, technology and innovation
Increased external visibility, share of voice and quality coverage
Clear, consistent market narratives adopted across the organization
Senior leaders are confident, visible and impactful external voices
Communications is seen as a trusted strategic partner driving business outcomes
Thought leadership and communications contribute to demand generation and growth


How You Will Work


Partner closely with KONE Technology & Innovation, Service and Major Projects leadership
Collaborate with Marketing, Media Relations, Investor Relations and regional teams
Contribute to a topic-led, insight-driven communications model focused on thought leadership, external visibility and market impact
