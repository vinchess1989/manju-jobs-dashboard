SRIYOG is an information technology consulting organization focused on helping clients address complex digital and technological challenges.

The company provides advisory and implementation support across areas such as systems integration, data-driven solutions, and process optimization.

Team members collaborate with clients to analyze requirements, design tailored IT strategies, and enhance operational efficiency.

SRIYOG values innovation, analytical thinking, and continuous learning, making it a supportive environment for emerging professionals interested in technology and research.

Role Description

The Research Assistant internship at SRIYOG is a remote role focused on supporting technology consulting projects through structured research and data-driven analysis.

Day-to-day tasks include gathering and reviewing information from technical and business sources, organizing findings into clear reports, and assisting in the evaluation of IT tools, platforms, and methodologies.

The Research Assistant will help compile datasets, perform basic data analysis, and contribute to the preparation of presentations or documentation for internal stakeholders and clients.

The role also involves collaborating with team members, participating in virtual meetings, and maintaining accurate records of research activities and project progress.

Qualifications


Candidates should possess strong Analytical Skills and Data Analysis abilities to interpret information, identify patterns, and support evidence-based recommendations.
Candidates should possess effective Communication skills, including clear written and verbal communication for reports, documentation, and remote collaboration.
Candidates should possess solid Research skills, including literature review, information sourcing, and synthesizing technical and business insights.
Candidates should possess relevant Laboratory Skills where applicable, such as handling data collection tools or experimental procedures in technology-related studies.
Basic understanding of information technology concepts or consulting practices is beneficial.
Strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and the ability to manage multiple tasks in a remote environment are preferred.
Proficiency with productivity tools (e.g., spreadsheets, presentation software, collaboration platforms) is an advantage.
Current enrollment in or recent completion of a degree program in a relevant field (e.g., Information Technology, Computer Science, Data Science, Business, or related disciplines) is preferred.
