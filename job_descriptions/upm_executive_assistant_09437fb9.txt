Would you like to join a global, growing company that innovates for a more sustainable tomorrow? UPM could be the team for you. We are frontrunners in developing renewable alternatives to fossil-based materials. Besides enabling both businesses and consumers make more sustainable choices, we strive to create a positive UPM experience for all our employees around the world. We live by our values – trust and be trusted, achieve together, renew with courage. Together we can renew the everyday, for a future beyond fossils!

Learn more about us as a workplace upm.com/careers

We are now looking for an experienced Executive Assistant to join in UPM Plywood to be a key stakeholder to the President and CEO, the Board of Directors and the management of WISA Group Plc, a publicly listed company to be established as a result of the partial demerger of UPM-Kymmene.

Greetings from Group Leadership Team

“As WISA Group takes shape as an independent listed company, you’ll have a unique opportunity to work at the heart of the business, partnering closely with our leadership team and helping build our future success. We are looking for a proactive, positive, and trusted professional who enjoys creating structure, driving things forward, and making a real impact in a dynamic environment. You combine excellent organizational skills and attention to detail with a can-do attitude, professionalism, and a genuine enthusiasm for taking ownership and delivering high-quality results.”

What You Will Do

In this role, you will provide proactive support to the President and CEO, the Board of Directors and the Group Leadership Team, ensuring smooth coordination of leadership activities, effective communication flows, and well-organized decision-making processes. You will plan and coordinate management meetings, workshops, visits, and events, prepare materials, and manage day-to-day administrative activities with professionalism and attention to detail.

You will handle confidential information with discretion, coordinate travel and key administrative processes, support the use of digital tools, and help maintain efficient daily operations. Acting as a trusted partner to the leadership team, you will create structure in a dynamic environment and ensure that important priorities are progressed effectively.

Key Responsibilities


Act as executive assistant to President and CEO, while cooperating closely also in relation to the Board of Directors and the Group Leadership Team members
Support the President and CEO and the Group Leadership Team with planning, coordination and preparation of meetings and materials
Coordinate travel and meeting arrangements, as well as other key administrative processes, and serve as a key user for selected tools
Manage communication flows and handle confidential information with professionalism
Plan and execute leadership meetings, workshops, visits and events


Who You Are


You have solid experience as an executive assistant or in a similar senior coordination role
You possess strong organisational skills and the ability to manage multiple priorities with accuracy
You have the ability to work with senior leadership of a listed company
You have excellent communication skills in Finnish and English
You are confident in using digital tools and have a willingness to learn new systems, including the use of AI
You bring a proactive, solution-oriented mindset and the ability to work independently
You have a high level of discretion and sound judgement
Experience with board material portals and/or insider management tools is considered an asset


Success in this position calls for excellent organizational skills, sound judgment, and the ability to manage multiple priorities with accuracy. If you are proactive, positive and solution-oriented, we’d love to hear from you.

Additional information about The Position

This position is located in Helsinki, Finland.

The position holder will report to WISA Group’s President and CEO.

 

For further information about the role, please contact Pia Helminen, VP HR, Plywood on August 7 between 9:00–11:00 or on August 14 between 14:00–16:00, tel. + 358407675326.
