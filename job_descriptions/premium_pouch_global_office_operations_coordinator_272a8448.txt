Office Operations Coordinator – Premium Pouch

Premium Pouch is a fast-growing, Finnish nicotine pouch company that sells and manages its own Greatest and Puff&Pouch brands.

As our business continues to grow, we are looking for an experienced and proactive Office Operations Coordinator to join our team in the Helsinki Finland. In this role, you will be a key support to management and work closely with colleagues across the business. You will coordinate a broad range of operational and administrative activities that keep the business running smoothly every day. This position offers a high degree of independence and responsibility, and is well suited to someone who enjoys creating structure, solving problems quickly, and supporting a growing business in a practical, hands-on way.




Your Responsibilities

Finance & Documentation

Prepare and send proforma invoices, customs and other export/import documentation
Put together internal reports
Maintain contract archives and basic documentation

Partner & Commercial Support

Prepare materials and presentations for partners
Coordinate logistics for trade shows and events
Arrange shipment of samples and materials to partners and customers
Handle day-to-day communication with suppliers and partners

Office & Operations

Coordinate flight, hotel and car rental bookings for management and the sales team
Coordinate office administration and supplier agreements
Organise internal meetings, travel arrangements, catering and other practical office activities
Handle practical arrangements for new employees (access cards, equipment, credentials) and help organise team days
Continuously look for ways to improve administrative routines and ways of working




About You

You are a self-starting, dependable professional who enjoys taking ownership and figuring things out. You think about why something is done, not just how, and you find the fastest, most sensible way to get it done. You manage several priorities at once without needing to be told what's important, and you're reliable down to the small details. You thrive in a fast-changing, high-growth environment and can move quickly when the pressure is on.




To Succeed In This Role, You Likely Have

Strong organisational and coordination skills, with a hands-on, get-it-done attitude
Confident use of Microsoft Office / Google Workspace, especially Excel and Word/Docs
Working knowledge of Canva
Comfort using AI tools to work faster and smarter
Professional fluency in Finnish and English, written and spoken. Swedish, Spanish or German is an advantage




What We Offer

The chance to take real ownership in a fast-growing company where your work matters from day one — and a permanent role built for someone who wants to grow with the business, not a short-term placement.




You Will Have The Opportunity To

Take ownership of a broad and varied role
Work closely with management in a fast-paced, high-growth environment
Shape how administrative and operational processes are built as we scale
