1. About Our Client:

The organization operates in the renewable energy sector, focusing on the design and deployment of green hydrogen solutions. It addresses the challenges of cost reduction and efficiency in renewable energy by employing innovative approaches to maximize value for customers.




2. About the Opportunity:

The Administrative Assistant role supports the team by ensuring daily operations proceed smoothly and efficiently. This fully remote position, available as full-time or part-time, is vital for maintaining organizational effectiveness through managing communications, scheduling, and administrative tasks.




3. Responsibilities:

• Manage and coordinate calendars and virtual meetings across time zones.

• Monitor and respond to professional correspondence promptly.

• Prepare, format, and proofread documents, reports, and presentations.

• Organize and maintain secure and accessible digital files and records.

• Perform data entry and assist with company databases.

• Provide administrative support to team members and departments.

• Assist in coordinating virtual projects and company initiatives.




4. Requirements:

• Proven experience as an Administrative Assistant or similar role.

• Strong organizational and time-management skills for a remote environment.

• Excellent written and verbal communication with attention to detail.

• Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook).

• Proactive, resourceful, and able to work independently.

• Ability to handle confidential information professionally.

• Remote work experience is beneficial but not required.

• Interest in renewable energy is a plus.




5. Pay Range and Compensation Package:

• The offered salary for this role is $25 - $36 USD per hour.
