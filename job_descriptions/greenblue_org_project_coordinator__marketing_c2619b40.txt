About GreenBlue:

GreenBlue is an environmental nonprofit whose mission is to accelerate the transition to a regenerative, just, and sustainable materials economy. Our key projects include the Sustainable Packaging Coalition and the How2Recycle on-pack labeling system. 

 

The Opportunity

GreenBlue’s Marketing and Communications team is seeking a Project Coordinator, Marketing Communications and Events to join its team. This is a temporary position lasting approximately 90 days. When this position ends, a full-time, regular position, with benefits, may be offered at the full discretion of the organization.  This role will work closely with the rest of the Marketing and Communications team, the GreenBlue team at large and a variety of external stakeholders supporting the production of GreenBlue Marketing Communications initiatives and events. This is a temporary, remote position based in the Continental US.




Please read through the end before applying.  

 

The Project Coordinator, Marketing Communications and Events will: 

Be the lead on Cvent event registration and related systems development, implementation and troubleshooting for all GreenBlue events 
Be the lead in Event Tech Stack including RSVP systems, Event App, event website updates and webinars 
Support Associate Director Marketing Communications in the implementation of the Social Media strategy for the organization 
Support Associate Director Marketing Communication on external marketing partnerships fulfillment 
Be the liaison with the event AV company including preparing ROS, stingers and coordinating timelines 
 Support event logistics, procurement and fulfillment as needed 
Prepare Social Media Graphics and related copy for speakers and sponsors to showcase their event participation 
Take an active onsite role as part of the Marketing Communications and Events team at GreenBlue events  
Support the Marketing Communications team as needed. 

 

You…

Are creative, have an outstanding work ethic, a can-do attitude and are a problem solver 
Have experience talking about and writing about climate and sustainability topics 
Are incredibly organized, assertive and self-motivated and self-reliant 
Are a strong writer and communicator 
Enjoy people and are good at building relationships with stakeholders (virtually and in person) 
Enjoy designing event experiences and networking events  
Thrive implementing new event tech solutions 
Get excited about developing and implementing new digital marketing strategies 
Possess exceptional attention to detail 
Enjoy working in collaboration with a team in a fast-paced environment 
Are flexible and adaptive 
Enjoy both planning and event execution 

 

What you will bring:

Bachelor’s degree in marketing, communications or event management and minimum 5 years of related work experience or an equivalent combination, including at least 4 years’ experience in a similar role. 
Experience working at or with a nonprofit organization 
Experience in stakeholder engagement and event logistics  
Excellent written and verbal communications skills 
Proficiency with Cvent Registration Systems, Event Apps and other Event Tech 
Proficiency in Microsoft Office, Adobe Suite, Canva and event tech stack including presentations (slide decks) management 
Experience using Clickup is a plus. 
Experience with website management, especially with Wordpress  
Experience cultivating, negotiating, and managing relationships with key stakeholders and vendors. 
Experience writing event marketing copy (long and short form) 
Experience fostering an environment of creativity and professional growth. 
Experience working in a matrixed environment and collaborating and implementing multiple projects.

 

This role will be assessed at 30 and 60 days.  




To apply for this position please send over: Cover Letter, Resume, Writing and Social Media Samples; and Events Portfolio to MK Moore, Associate Director Marketing Communications at mk.moore@greenblue.org  




Applicants invited to the second round of interviews will be asked to complete a project.
