About The Company

Evolution Sports Group is a dynamic and innovative company dedicated to delivering exceptional sports-related services and solutions. With a focus on excellence and customer satisfaction, our organization has established a strong presence in the sports industry. We pride ourselves on fostering a collaborative and inclusive work environment that encourages professional growth and development. Our commitment to innovation and quality makes us a preferred partner for clients and stakeholders in the sports sector. As we continue to expand our operations, we are seeking talented individuals to join our team and contribute to our ongoing success.

About The Role

We are looking for a highly organized and detail-oriented Data Entry Clerk/Virtual Assistant to become an integral part of our remote team. This full-time position offers the flexibility of working from your own home while supporting various administrative and data management functions. The ideal candidate will possess excellent data entry skills, strong attention to detail, and the ability to operate independently in a fast-paced environment. This role involves maintaining accurate data records, managing communications, and assisting with scheduling and research tasks. The position provides an opportunity for professional growth within a vibrant company that values initiative and dedication.

Qualifications


High School diploma or equivalent
1-2 years of experience in data entry or administrative support roles
Proficient in Microsoft Office Suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint) and Google Workspace (Docs, Sheets, Slides)
Strong attention to detail and accuracy in data handling
Excellent communication and organizational skills
Ability to work independently and meet deadlines consistently
Previous experience in the sports industry is a plus but not mandatory


Responsibilities


Enter, update, and maintain accurate data within company databases and systems
Organize and manage electronic and physical files to ensure easy retrieval and security
Respond promptly to emails and phone inquiries, providing professional communication
Assist with scheduling appointments, meetings, and calendar management
Conduct research and compile relevant information as required for projects and reports
Prepare and format reports, presentations, and other documentation
Support various administrative tasks and special projects as assigned by management


Benefits


Competitive hourly pay ranging from $35 to $48, depending on experience

Health, dental, and vision insurance coverage
Retirement savings plan to support long-term financial security
Paid time off for rest and personal needs
Opportunities for professional development and career advancement
Company-provided workstation and equipment for a productive home office setup
Flexible work schedule options, including full-time and part-time roles
