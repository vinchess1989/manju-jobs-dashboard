We are looking for a Professional Content Creator who can create engaging, educational, and high quality content about AI tools, productivity, and digital workflows.

Responsibilities


Create high quality content for blogs, social media, and marketing campaigns
Develop educational and engaging posts about AI tools and productivity
Write SEO optimized articles and website content
Create scripts for short videos, reels, and educational content
Collaborate with the marketing team to develop content strategies
Research trends in AI, SaaS, and technology to create relevant content


Required Skills


Strong content writing and storytelling ability
Experience creating content for social media platforms
Basic understanding of SEO and digital marketing
Ability to simplify complex topics like AI and technology


Preferred Qualifications


Experience working with SaaS or AI products
Experience with content tools such as Canva, CapCut, or video editing tools
Familiarity with AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, or other AI assistants
