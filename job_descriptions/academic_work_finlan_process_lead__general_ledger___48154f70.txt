About the role

You will lead the General Ledger and Asset Accounting teams, ensuring efficient daily operations and timely period-end closings. This role is pivotal in harmonizing regional processes and successfully navigating a major ERP implementation.

You are offered
- A unique opportunity to fast-track your career and gain heavy ERP implementation experience in a high-impact role.
- A collaborative and supportive environment where your expertise genuinely matters.
- Comprehensive occupational health care services to support your well-being.
- Lunch benefit and hybrid work flexibility.

Work tasks

As the operational lead, you will balance team management with high-level process development and system implementation tasks to ensure financial accuracy and efficiency.
- Lead and coordinate General Ledger and Asset Accounting teams globally.
- Oversee, maintain, and develop GL and Asset Accounting processes and documentation.
- Ensure period-end closing tasks are completed accurately according to global schedules.
- Participate in the D365 ERP implementation, including data migration and configuration.
- Manage work distribution, holiday planning, and substitutions within the teams.
- Drive digitalization and automation initiatives across the process cycle.
- Support the finance organization with change management and problem-solving.

We are looking for

- Around 5 years of proven experience within General Ledger and Asset Accounting
- Practical experience with ERP systems and system development
- Degree in Finance or Accounting
- Professional proficiency in English
- Strong skills in resolving discrepancies and maintaining accurate records

It is meritorious if you have
- A proven track record of leading senior accountants or finance specialists is a major plus.
- Knowledge of Finnish
- Experience with Microsoft Dynamics 365 (D365)

To succeed in the role, your personal skills are:
- Change oriented
- Social
- Responsible
- Respectful

Our recruitment process
This recruitment process is handled by Academic Work and it is our clients wish that all questions regarding the position is directed to Academic Work.

Our selection process is continuous and the advert may close before the recruitment process is completed if we have moved forward to the next phase. The process includes two tests: one personality test and one cognitive test. The tests are tools to find the right talent for the right position, to enable equality, diversity, and a fair process.
