Job Description

Job Title: Government Policy Consultant

Job Type: Contract

Location: Remote

Job Summary: In this role, you'll apply your expertise to help train next-generation AI systems. Your work will shape how models learn, reason, and perform through high-quality, real-world input.

Key Responsibilities


Analyze, draft, and review policy documents, legal contracts, proposals, and research papers using advanced Word and PDF skills.
Provide strategic consultation and actionable recommendations on regulatory, legal, healthcare, academic, or finance-related matters.
Conduct thorough document reviews, ensuring compliance, clarity, and adherence to industry best practices.
Support clients with precise document formatting, tracked changes, form filling, and data extraction.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to synthesize complex data into succinct, actionable policies and procedures.
Stay abreast of evolving regulations and guidelines relevant to your field of expertise.
Communicate findings and recommendations in clear, compelling written and verbal formats.


Required Skills And Qualifications


Minimum 3+ years of professional experience in government policy operations.
Advanced proficiency with Word and PDF tools, including tracked changes, formatting, document review, and data extraction.
Exceptional communication skills, with a strong emphasis on clear, professional written and verbal interactions.
Demonstrated expertise in drafting, analyzing, and reviewing complex policy or regulatory documents.
Proven ability to manage multiple projects and meet stringent deadlines in a fast-paced remote environment.
Keen attention to detail and commitment to delivering high-quality work.


Preferred Qualifications


Experience working with Fortune 500 clients or on high-profile regulatory, legal, or policy projects.
Track record of collaborating with multidisciplinary teams to achieve impactful outcomes.
