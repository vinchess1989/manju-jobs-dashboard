We are looking for a Creative & Communications Specialist Intern to join our product team. This role is for someone interested in solving client problems and creating high-quality content across gaming, marketing, and communities.




You should be comfortable communicating with clients and external partners and have some experience using AI tools for creative work.




Do these questions sound like you?




Can you turn client needs into clear briefs and high-quality deliverables?
Can you create strong marketing, social media, game, or brand content?
Are you comfortable communicating with clients, teammates, and online communities?
Can you use AI tools effectively while applying your own creative judgment?
Do you generate ideas, identify repetitive work, and find practical ways to improve it?




This is an internship, so it is completely fine if you do not yet have good answers to many of these questions. We are not looking for someone who already knows everything. The goal is to help you develop these abilities while solving real-world problems faced by Odd Latent and its customers.




In this position, you will have room to grow in several directions. You may develop toward B2B go-to-market work, client communication and customer success, social media and community, creative production, or applied AI. You do not need to have chosen one path already.




What you will do




Support client communication, gather feedback, and help ensure a strong customer experience.
Produce and iterate on marketing creatives, game assets, social content, and brand materials.
Plan and publish social media content and support community activities.
Use AI-powered tools and automated workflows while applying human judgment to final outputs.




What we are looking for




Strong written and verbal communication skills.
Creative judgment and excellent attention to detail.
The ability to understand client goals, ask useful questions, and communicate progress clearly.
Experience in some combination of social media, community, marketing, design, games, video, writing, or content production.
A problem-solving mindset and comfort moving between creative and communication tasks.
Curiosity about AI tools, automation, and technology.




What we offer




Paid employment with a salary of 2,000€ to 3,000€ gross per month, based on experience.
Occupational health care.




About Odd Latent




Odd Latent is a Finnish applied AI company redefining how games and entertainment are created. Founded in 2025, our team brings together 25 years of game-making experience and a decade of work in AI and machine learning.




We develop advanced AI tools that improve game production, live operations, and marketing workflows. Our current products include AI assistants, agentic workflows, and automated systems for live operations and marketing production.




We also operate our own game studio, Odd Latent Games, which publishes games and uses our technology in real production environments. By combining applied AI with hands-on game development, we are reinventing the studio model for the next generation of game developers.




Information every applicant should provide




A current CV. Please add in the CV links to relevant work you would like to show us.
Confirmation that you are able to work primarily on site in Espoo.
Confirmation that you have, or will have by the agreed start date, a valid right to work in Finland for the full duration of the employment.




Hiring process




Application review.
Short introductory meeting with a founder.
Final in person interview with the team.
Offer and written employment contract process.




Equal treatment and privacy




Odd Latent evaluates applicants based on role related skills, potential, and demonstrated work. We welcome applicants from different backgrounds and do not discriminate on the basis of personal characteristics protected by Finnish law.
