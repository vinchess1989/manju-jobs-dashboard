Räätälöi ansioluetteloni

Winthrop Technologies is Europe's only dedicated data centre delivery partner, offering full turnkey solutions from design through to construction and commissioning. Headquartered in Dublin, we deliver projects across Europe and have become the most trusted name in our sector.

 

Reporting to the Planning Manager, the Project Planner will be responsible for developing, maintaining, and reviewing project schedules and controls procedures. This role requires strong analytical, technical, and communication skills, with a focus on proactive planning, risk management, and performance tracking to ensure successful project delivery.


Responsibilities:

Develop, maintain, and review detailed master construction programmes and project controls procedures for live projects
Prepare and present progress reviews and reports to senior management
Liaise with key stakeholders to review project progress, identify risks, and support proactive problem-solving
Establish and manage project schedule resource requirements across all phases — pre-construction, construction, and commissioning
Create and maintain progress reporting tools to monitor the achievement of project goals and milestones
Provide performance updates and variance reports, identifying corrective actions where required
Represent the Planning function in client and subcontractor meetings, ensuring clear communication of schedule status and planning issues
Support account management and business development by building and maintaining long-term client relationships
Take ownership of assigned projects and assist colleagues on other projects as needed
Ensure project planning practices align with company standards and contribute to continuous improvement initiatives



Requirements


Degree in Engineering, Construction Management, or a related discipline
Minimum 2+ years' relevant experience in the construction industry, preferably within a main contracting environment
Experience working on large-scale projects, ideally within the data centre or high-tech industrial sectors
Strong knowledge of construction methodologies, sequencing, and planning processes
Proficient in Primavera P6 and/or Asta Powerproject; experience with BIM / digital construction tools and workflows is highly desirable
Strong analytical and logical thinking skills, with a sound understanding of programming techniques and typical production rates
Excellent communication and presentation skills, with the ability to convey complex planning information clearly
Capable of working to defined, and often challenging, timescales
Strong commercial awareness and understanding of contractual and project management principles



Benefits


Opportunity to work with a Market Leader in the Data centre Industry
