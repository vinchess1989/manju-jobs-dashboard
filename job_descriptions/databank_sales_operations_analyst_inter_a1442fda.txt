Sales Operations Analyst Intern

The ability to take ownership of projects from inception through execution is essential. The ability to multi-task and prioritize while managing multiple projects and maintaining keen attention to detail on tight timelines is also a key ingredient for success in this position. The personality needed for success in this role is open and friendly with the ability to develop strong relationships. Sharp problem-solving skills and the ability to build efficient processes, plus comfort with multiple demands, shifting priorities, ambiguity, and rapid change are a must. A results-oriented oriented mentality, with an underlying sense of urgency and drive to win are also necessities.

Responsibilities


Assist with maintaining/updating current reports and dashboards as needed.
Creation of new reports or dashboards as needed.
Create the Overage Bandwidth report after each month and send to the sales teams.
Create the Changes without Orders report each Monday and create any necessary opportunities.
Review the closed won report each month and add the Opportunity Region information on any opportunity where this information is missing.
Create new prospects in Salesforce for the sales reps.
Maintain sales handbook and/or playbooks.
Assist on special programs (i.e. interactions work, sales incentives, sales target programs tracking and management etc.)
Assist with sales training documentation (creation, updating, publishing)
Assist with maintaining sales SharePoint site.


Qualifications


Proficiency in MS work, Excel, PowerPoint & Visio
Phenomenal communication and presentation skills (must be an advanced PowerPoint user).
