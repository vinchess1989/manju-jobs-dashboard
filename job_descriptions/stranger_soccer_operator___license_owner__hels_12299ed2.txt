Räätälöi ansioluetteloni
Are you passionate about football (soccer) and interested in growing a local football community?

Stranger Soccer is expanding globally and is looking for an Operator & License Owner to launch, manage, and grow our football platform in Helsinki.

This is not a conventional management job—it's an opportunity to build and operate your own local football operation while making football more accessible in your city. It's ideal for entrepreneurial individuals who are passionate about the game and driven to create meaningful, lasting impact.

⚽ About The Opportunity

As an Operator & License Owner, you'll lead the growth of Stranger Soccer in Helsinki—from building local partnerships and managing operations to growing an active community of players.

You'll have the freedom to shape the local operation while benefiting from the systems, expertise, and support of our Singapore headquarters.

💼 Key Responsibilities


Launch and grow Stranger Soccer in Helsinki
Build partnerships with football venues and secure quality playing locations
Recruit, coordinate, and support Official Hosts and local team members
Drive marketing initiatives to attract players and grow the community
Oversee game operations and deliver an outstanding player experience
Identify opportunities to improve and expand the local operation


You'll receive support from our Singapore headquarters, including:


A technology platform for app, payments, and operations
Proven operating systems and processes
Ongoing guidance across operations, marketing, and growth


🎯 Who We're Looking For


Passion for football and a strong understanding of the local football community
An entrepreneurial mindset with a strong sense of ownership
Experience managing operations and delivering measurable results
Background in operations, community management, or customer experience
Strong leadership, communication, and relationship-building skills


💡 Why This Opportunity?


Build and grow Stranger Soccer in your city
Make a lasting impact on the local football community
Lead a growing network of players and Official Hosts
Benefit from the support of an established international brand
Enjoy a performance-driven opportunity where your success drives your rewards


🌍 About Stranger Soccer

Stranger Soccer makes playing football simple, flexible, and accessible. Through our mobile app, players can discover nearby games, book a spot, and simply turn up to play.

Founded in Singapore, Stranger Soccer now operates in 10+ cities worldwide and has hosted over 100,000 games. We're looking for passionate individuals who want to bring this experience to their local communities.

🚀 Interested in Joining Us?

Visit www.strangersoccer.com and explore the "Bring Stranger Soccer to Your City" section to learn more.

If you're excited to help shape the future of football participation, we'd love to hear from you.
