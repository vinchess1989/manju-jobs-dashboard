We are looking for a Procurement and Supply Chain Advisor to joins us building better ways of working. This is a fixed-term position, starting as soon as possible and ending during fall 2027. The PSC Development Advisor is responsible for driving the development and continuous improvement of procurement and supply chain capabilities in the function and within key stakeholder groups in Merima. This role will lead the coordination of the overall functional development roadmap, in alignment with strategic goals, and collaborate with stakeholders to develop and implement enabling tools, systems, and standardized processes.




Key Responsibilities




Strategic Development & Roadmap Coordination

Work within the function and with cross-functional leaders to identify capability gaps, prioritize improvement initiatives and drive adoption. Coordinate the development and implementation of roadmap activities, ensuring alignment across stakeholder groups and the overall organisational development plan.
Support in the global ERP program from procurement and supply chain perspective, coordinates within the team the support for the company program.
Lead the identified Contract Management program and act there as a subject matter expert in procurement and supply chain related matters.




Process & System Design and implementation

Develop and standardize core procurement and supply chain processes and supporting tools
Define performance benchmarks and best practices for process efficiency, compliance, and quality and track performance.
Collaborate with IT to define functional requirements and ensure effective deployment of digital solutions
Develop training materials and change management plans to support tool and process adoption across the organization.
Act as a subject matter expert and trusted advisor for transformation initiatives.
Establish a culture of continuous improvement by identifying and implementing lessons learned and improvement opportunities.
Collaborates with different functions and cross-functional leaders in development programs that are connected to procurement and supply chain
Conduct trainings for procurement team about procurement core activities and support the team overall in procurement related questions and tasks from strategic sourcing perspective.




Qualifications and Experience Required

Bachelor’s degree in Supply Chain Management/Procurement, Business, Engineering, or a related field.
6+ years of experience in procurement, supply chain operations, or a similar role.
Proven experience in process improvement, system implementation, and cross-functional project management.
Strong knowledge of ERP, procurement tools and other functional systems
Experience in global or multi-site supply chain environments.
Excellent communication skills in English and Finnish; knowledge of other languages is a plus.
