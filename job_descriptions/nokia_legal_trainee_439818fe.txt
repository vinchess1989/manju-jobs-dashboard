Job Description

We are looking for a Legal Trainee to join us from August for six months. While our preference is for a full-time trainee, we are open to considering candidates interested in part-time work as well.

In this role, you will support global stakeholders in contract signing processes, review corporate governance documents, assist in managing Nokia ’ s entities, and provide guidance on related rules and policies. This position offers an excellent opportunity to learn and grow in a truly international environment. You will have the chance to work independently while collaborating closely with our team, connect with our global legal network, and strengthen your English and communication skills. Working with us will give you valuable hands-on experience to support your future legal career.


Duration: 6 months
Target start date. August 2026
Location: Espoo


How You Will Contribute And What You Will Learn


Provide support and assistance to global stakeholders throughout the contract signing process
Respond to inquiries regarding sales contract management from the global organization
Address matters related to legal entity management and participate in additional assigned projects


Key Skills And Experience


A background of 1-3 years in law studies at a university with basic understanding of contract law
Good O365 -skills
Fluency in both written and oral English and Finnish
Attention to detail and ability to take initiative


Kindly note that to be able to work as a trainee in Nokia Finland, you must hold active student status and be located in Finland since international relocation support is not available for trainee positions.
