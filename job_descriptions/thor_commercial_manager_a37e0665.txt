Commercial Manager - 12 Month Initial Contract - Hyperscale Data Center Project




Partnered with a global industry-leading EPC contractor who are ramping up their team particularly in Finland. Their proven track record of delivering quality on data center projects they continue to win exciting hyperscale projects in a competitive market. With this boom in the Finnish market they are proactively expanding their team to increase their capabilities with the best talent in the market.




Leading their project commercial team and driving strategies that supports profitable growth and strong project performance. You will oversee the commercial aspects of their contracts, ensuring the projects are delivered with commercial diligence and compliance.




Freelance/ Contract position
Flexible Working Model
Long-term Contract (open to extensions)
Competitive Hourly rate (negotiable based on experience)




Key Responsibilities:

Strategic Leadership & Team Management
Commercial Strategy & Business Growth
Contract Management
Project Commercial Oversight




Key Requirements:

Minimum of 10 years' experience with engineering, construction, or infrastructure sectors
3 years at Commercial Management level
Strong understanding of commercial, financial, and contractual principles, including experience with standard forms of contract (e.g., NEC, JCT, FIDIC)




This role is an opportunity to influence major renewable, data centre, and utility projects while growing your career within an innovative, people-focused organisation committed to continuous improvement and sustainable success.
