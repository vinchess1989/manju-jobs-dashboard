At Polsinelli, What a Law Firm Should Be is not just our tagline, it is what we live every day. We strive to create an environment where our team members are encouraged to bring their creativity and professional passions into the workplace so that they can thrive. Are you a people person who has a keen eye for detail, thrives in a professional environment and wants to be part of a dynamic team? We have the perfect role for an engaging professional like you!

Polsinelli seeks to hire a Practice Group Assistant to work with their Real Estate Finance group in the New York office. This is a great opportunity for recent college graduates or individuals interested in real estate and the paralegal profession.

Core Responsibilities


Assist with pre-closing, closing, and post-closing of loans
Compile and organize documents: loan documents, signature pages, etc.
Revise/edit documents
Track missing items
Create closing binder indexes and closing binders
Prepare custodial letters and custodial packages
Create and update various checklist
Track post-closing items such as recorded documents and original documents
Work with attorneys, analysts, paralegals, and title companies


Skills


Excellent computer skills, including, but not limited to: Word, Excel, Outlook, and Internet searches
Strong interpersonal skills
Strong written and verbal communication skills
Ability to multi-task and balance multiple projects and priorities simultaneously


Requirements


Strong interest in the real estate lending and the legal profession
Meticulous attention to detail and strong sense of pride in personal work product
Bachelor’s degree strongly preferred
Prior law firm experience or paralegal certificate a plus
Must be able to work individually as well as in a team setting
Must be dependable, dedicated, and able to maintain confidentiality


The budgeted salary range for this position is $50,000 - $60,000.

As part of full-time employment associated with this position, Polsinelli PC offers the following benefits: Paid time off, sick time off, a referral program, medical insurance and benefits, dental insurance, vision insurance, life insurance, AD&D insurance, ID Theft insurance, long-term disability benefits, short-term disability benefits, Parking/Transit reimbursement (varies depending on location), 401(k) benefits, and employee assistance benefits.
