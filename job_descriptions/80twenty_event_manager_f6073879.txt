Senior Recruiting Partner

This is remote outside of CA, with global travel a few times a year. Bay area preferred.

Contract, 20 hours a week, ability to convert FT.




Our client is a modern AI-powered IT management platform unifying ticketing, automation, and monitoring for IT teams. As they scale into a new ICP, their event footprint is growing fast and they need someone senior enough to build the function and manage the logistics, not just run the calendar.




This role owns roughly 20 events a year (excluding webinars), spanning the US and select international markets. Travel is selective, reserved for the highest-impact shows, not every stop on the calendar.




The Opportunity

This isn't a maintenance role. There's no existing playbook, vendor bench, or budget framework to inherit, this person builds it. They are seeking a senior, self-sufficient event contractor who can own the full operational backbone of a global event program — tradeshows, meetups, and activations without requiring day-to-day oversight. This role is 100% logistics, budget, and coordination; it does not include marketing strategy, campaigns, messaging, or lead nurture. They need someone who sees a deadline on the calendar and works backward from it automatically, who flags a risk the moment they spot it rather than the week of the event, and who treats vendor and budget management as non-negotiable disciplines. The founder wants events that people remember and talk about — the brand shows up occasionally with sharp, unexpected activations.




What Success Looks Like

Build and manage a detailed, repeatable event plan and checklist for every show — booth build, shipping, travel, exhibitor basics (A/V, electrical, furniture), signage, and swag with every deadline backed into the calendar the moment an event is confirmed
Own all vendor relationships for a given event: catering, signage/branding, A/V, booth build, and swag, including confirming appointments, chasing deliverables, and resolving issues
Lock in exhibitor basics, vendor orders, and travel/hotel bookings well ahead of deadlines
Proactively flag any timeline risk, vendor delay, or resource gap as soon as it's identified, not after it's already compressed the runway
Maintain a standing punch list per event that is visible to stakeholders without needing to be asked for it
Build a repeatable events function — playbooks, vendor relationships, shipping and storage logistics built from the ground up and ready to scale as the calendar grows
Experience sourcing vendors and agencies as needed
Budget management that earns trust fast enough to shift from line-item oversight to a flexible, outcomes-based model
A brand presence at events that people notice, photograph, and talk about, not just another booth
Someone who already knows the B2B SaaS event landscape well enough to plan a September SF event around what else is happening that week, not discover it after the fact
Ensure post-event lead lists are fully structured, documented, and routed to the marketing/SDR team after each event




Requirements:

6+ years in B2B SaaS, event management, trade shows and events
Fluency in the B2B SaaS event calendar and landscape — who's showing up where, what's competing for attention, what's worth the spend
Proven ability to build a function, not just execute inside one: vendor sourcing, agency relationships, shipping/storage logistics, budget frameworks
Demonstrated ownership from planning through reconciliation without needing day-to-day direction
Experience designing or elevating experiential moments, even if execution today is ad hoc
Proactive and organized
Comfortable operating independently
Global event experience — able to prioritize and travel selectively across a distributed calendar (this takes place in US and EUR)
