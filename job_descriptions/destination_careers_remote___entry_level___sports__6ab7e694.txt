Robert Mari
Robert Mari
Human Resources Business Partner at Versace

Responsibilities include:




General

Instrumental in providing support for the yearly marketing plan that identifies and outlines all activities to drive sales across channels
Manage marketing calendars; Retail/Wholesale/Store Openings/Email Content/Promotions
Responsibility for the “traffic” of all collateral pieces to ensure all materials arrive on time
Liaison with accounting to track invoices, ensure they are in budget and paid in a timely manner
Coordinate retail & wholesale advertising plans, manage advertising budget, material requests and added value
Instrumental in developing events program to support retail and wholesale initiatives
Manage event calendar for retail and wholesale activities
Management of events budget to ensure it is respected
Liaison with accounting to track invoices, ensure they are in budget and paid in a timely manner
Ensure great working relationship with all depts & stakeholders including Milan HQ




Advertising/Promotions/LRA

Ensure media plan is revised on a monthly basis
Manage LRA and advertising budget and show aptitude for ensuring flexibility as business needs change
Work with production teams & Milan HQ from all local publications to ensure materials are supplied on time
Liaise with Milan office to request artwork; track artwork from Milan to magazine’s printer
Coordination with local advertising for added value / marketing opportunities
Track efficiency of advertising
Work with Milan HQ to create robust monthly retail promotion calendar based on Global Omni-Channel calendar
Partner with malls to maximize opportunities through their marketing channels, paid & organic
Explore 3rd party partnerships to promote Versace products




Event/Activation Management:

Identify groups for event partnerships to support retail and wholesale business
Develop concept and execute grand opening events for new boutiques
Identify and maintain relationship with event suppliers (catering, florist, modeling agencies hair / make up, production teams, av, receive estimates and negotiate rates)
Execute in store selling events
Work with buyers and store managers to understand commercial needs
Work with modeling agencies, catering company, tailors, florist, audio / visual to ensure each element is secured and in line with brand
Order / prepare gifts for guests if necessary
Work with charity and hostess to ensure / manage their role at event
Work with graphics designer, printer to create and ensure invitations are sent in timely manner
Manage guest list and RSVPs
Liaison with Milan office to communicate event concept, invitation copy, and event recap
Liaison with the press office to organize samples for selling events
Work with style office to propose looks and run of show for selling events
Develop metrics for events and track success (sales, new client acquisition, client conversion etc.)
Develop a follow up plan to convert event clients to loyal clients in partnership with CRM team
Develop concept and detailed timeline for each event




Experience & Key Competencies:

Bachelor’s degree required
3-5+ years of work experience in marketing and events organization.
Experience within the retail sector preferred.
Ability to develop marketing plans, programs and promotional briefs.
A passionate, energetic team oriented individual with strong analytical, interpersonal, creative and leadership skills.
Experience of managing budgets and invoicing
Strong track record in marketing automation, campaign management, demand generation, direct marketing and database marketing.
Proven entrepreneurial and creative skills to think beyond traditional marketing approaches.
Strong oral and written communication and presentation skills.
