Job Summary

We are seeking a highly organized, proactive, and customer-focused Professional Education Events Specialist to help drive the successful planning and execution of healthcare professional (HCP) education programs across the United States. This role serves as a key partner in delivering exceptional educational experiences that support marketing, medical and overall company wide business objectives.

The Specialist will manage multiple educational initiatives simultaneously, collaborating with Marketing, Medical Affairs, Compliance, Sales, and external vendors to deliver high-quality virtual, hybrid, and in-person HCP educational events. This position offers the opportunity to contribute both operationally and strategically while developing expertise in healthcare professional education and event management.

Event Planning & Coordination


Coordinate event logistics including but not limited to registrations, attendee communications, audio visual production, food and beverage planning, and meeting materials creation.
Lead onsite event execution activities, proactively resolving issues and ensuring a seamless attendee experience.
Manage vendor deliverables and timelines while ensuring event requirements, expectations, and deadlines are clearly communicated and achieved.
Track project milestones and follow up with stakeholders to ensure deadlines are met.
Maintain event planning documents, templates, and event specific budgets.
Support the implementation of established event processes and identify opportunities for increased efficiency.
Build strong working relationships across Marketing, Medical Affairs, Compliance, Procurement, Sales and vendor teams.
Analyze event performance data and develop post-event insights and recommendations to support continuous improvement.
Help identify trends and opportunities for continuous improvement based on event outcomes and participant feedback.


Basic Qualifications


At least 3 years of experience in event management, professional education, healthcare marketing, meetings management, conference planning, or related disciplines.
Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple projects and deadlines simultaneously.
Strong customer service mindset with a commitment to delivering exceptional attendee experiences.
Ability to anticipate challenges and proactively develop solutions.
Demonstrated ability to work independently while managing multiple priorities.


Preferred


Experience in healthcare, pharmaceutical, medical device, or professional education environments.
Familiarity with virtual event platforms and registration systems.
Exposure to working with external vendors or event agencies.
Basic understanding of HCP engagement and compliance requirements.


Additional Requirements


Ability to travel up to 25-30% for event support and onsite execution.
Willingness to work evenings or weekends as required by event schedules.

Insulet Corporation (NASDAQ: PODD), headquartered in Massachusetts, is an innovative medical device company dedicated to simplifying life for people with diabetes and other conditions through its Omnipod product platform. The Omnipod Insulin Management System provides a unique alternative to traditional insulin delivery methods. With its simple, wearable design, the tubeless disposable Pod provides up to three days of non-stop insulin delivery, without the need to see or handle a needle. Insulet’s flagship innovation, the Omnipod 5 Automated Insulin Delivery System, integrates with a continuous glucose monitor to manage blood sugar with no multiple daily injections, zero fingersticks, and can be controlled by a compatible personal smartphone in the U.S. or by the Omnipod 5 Controller. Insulet also leverages the unique design of its Pod by tailoring its Omnipod technology platform for the delivery of non-insulin subcutaneous drugs across other therapeutic areas. For more information, please visit insulet.com and omnipod.com.
