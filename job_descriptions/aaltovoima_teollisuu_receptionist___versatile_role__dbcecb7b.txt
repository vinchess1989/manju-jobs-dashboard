AALTOVOIMA

We are looking for a dynamic and versatile Reception All-Rounder for our client company in Kemijärvi for the upcoming winter season. In this role, you will truly be at the heart of the destination, as your workdays will consist of a wide variety of tasks: reception duties, breakfast service, light breakfast preparation, and accommodation cleaning.

We are looking for individuals who enjoy varied workdays and want to ensure a first-class customer experience for our guests right from the morning. The job starts at the onset of the season and offers a fantastic opportunity to experience the magic of Laplands winter as part of a professional team.

We offer:

* A highly diverse and interesting role in an international travel destination.

* Pay according to the Collective Agreement for the Hospitality Sector (MARA TES). Guaranteed minimum of 30 hours per week.

* Affordable accommodation options: shared room €250/month or private room €450/month.

* The support of Aalto Henkilöstöpalvelut and a reliable employer experience.

We value:

* Fluent English skills. Knowledge of other languages is considered an asset.

* Valid Hygiene and Alcohol Passports (both are mandatory).

* Previous experience in hotel or accommodation cleaning.

* Experience in waitstaff service, as well as a proactive and customer-oriented attitude.

* A driver's license is considered an asset.

Join our team by submitting your application today via the link provided. We review applications as they arrive and will fill the positions as soon as suitable candidates are found.

About Aalto Henkilöstöpalvelut

Aalto Henkilöstöpalvelut is a multi-sector HR services company from Finland connecting work with professionals. We are part of the broader Workteam Group, a corporate group specializing in HR and service sector solutions.

The foundation of our operations relies on reliability and flexibility. Our core values are achieving success together, swift responsiveness, and positive service. Every employee and client is important to us.

Our staff has decades of combined experience in recruitment across construction, logistics, industry, and service sectors within the staffing industry.
