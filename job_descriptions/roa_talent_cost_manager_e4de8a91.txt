Associate Cost Manager | Data Centres / Mission Critical | Helsinki




ROA Talent is supporting a global Construction company with the appointment of an Associate Cost Manager in Helsinki, working across a portfolio of complex construction projects.




This would suit an experienced Quantity Surveyor or Cost Manager who has moved beyond simply managing individual packages and is comfortable taking responsibility for cost planning, financial control, change management and senior-level reporting across multiple projects.




The environment is highly stakeholder-driven, so the ability to communicate commercial information clearly and influence project decisions is just as important as producing the numbers.




What we're looking for

7–8+ years’ experience within Quantity Surveying / Cost Management
Strong preference for experience within a cost consultancy environment
Experience managing costs across multiple projects, programmes or portfolios
Strong commercial management, cost planning and financial control experience
Good understanding of change management and commercial governance
Experience producing and presenting detailed cost reports and financial analysis
Chartered status
Degree or equivalent background in Quantity Surveying, Commercial Management, Construction or a related discipline
Confidence working with senior stakeholders across different projects and regions
Comfortable operating within a remote / hybrid, internationally connected project environment




Experience within Data Centre, Mission Critical or large-scale Infrastructure construction would be particularly relevant, although candidates from other technically complex sectors will also be considered.
