What are we looking for?

Skills Required


Exceptional attention to detail and commitment to accuracy
Strong written and verbal English communication skills
Quick learner with the ability to adapt to new systems and processes
Proactive, resourceful, and self-motivated
Strong administrative and document management skills
Ability to manage confidential and sensitive information responsibly
Experience as a Virtual Assistant, Administrative Assistant, Executive Assistant, or in a similar remote support role
Experience managing calendars, scheduling appointments, and coordinating documents remotely
Experience supporting professionals, executives, legal teams, or small business owners is highly regarded (not mandatory)


Working Hours


Part-time: 20 hours per week to start
Working hours aligned with New Zealand business hours
Potential to increase to full-time as the business grows


What will you do?


Provide comprehensive administrative support
Prepare, format, review, and coordinate business documents
Maintain accurate and up-to-date electronic filing systems
Manage diary appointments, scheduling, and reminders
Update and maintain document precedents and templates
Ensure document accuracy and consistency across all files
Assist with document coordination and workflow management
Handle confidential information with discretion and professionalism
Support process improvements and administrative efficiencies
