1. About Our Client:

The organization is a Florida-based painting company specializing in commercial and residential projects. It focuses on delivering high-quality service and craftsmanship to meet client expectations.




2. About the Opportunity:

The Remote Administrative Assistant role provides essential administrative support to ensure smooth daily operations. This full-time remote position is vital for maintaining organizational efficiency and supporting various teams through effective communication and task management.




3. Responsibilities:

• Act as the primary contact for client inquiries via email and telephone.

• Manage schedules, appointments, and calendars for project managers and painting teams.

• Prepare, issue, and track quotations, invoices, and project documentation.

• Maintain organized digital files of client records and project details.

• Assist with basic bookkeeping tasks including expense tracking and data entry.

• Coordinate with suppliers to order materials and ensure timely delivery.

• Provide comprehensive administrative support to enhance team operations.




4. Requirements:

• Experience in administrative, virtual assistant, or similar support roles.

• Strong organizational and time-management abilities.

• Excellent written and verbal communication skills and professional telephone manner.

• Proficiency with Microsoft Office Suite or equivalent productivity software.

• Self-motivated and able to work independently.

• Customer service oriented with focus on positive client experience.

• Reliable internet connection and dedicated quiet workspace for remote work.

• Experience in trade services or construction is beneficial but not required.




5. Pay Range and Compensation Package:

• Salary ranges from $45 to $59 USD per hour based on experience and skills.
