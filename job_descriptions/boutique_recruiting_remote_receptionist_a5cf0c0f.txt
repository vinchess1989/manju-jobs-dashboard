Position Title: Remote Receptionist

Salary Range: $22–$40 per hour/ DOE

A growing law firm is seeking a polished, dependable Remote Legal Receptionist to serve as the first point of contact for clients, prospective clients, attorneys, and staff. This fully remote position will manage high-volume calls, schedule consultations, collect prospective client information, conduct conflict checks, and maintain accurate records within the firm’s case management system. The ideal candidate is calm under pressure, highly organized, tech-savvy, and committed to delivering exceptional service to clients navigating sensitive and emotionally challenging legal matters.

Responsibilities


Serve as the first point of contact for clients, prospective clients, attorneys, and staff
Answer and manage a high volume of incoming calls with professionalism, discretion, and empathy
Schedule consultations and coordinate availability with the firm’s intake specialists
Communicate with intake specialists through live chat to determine who can accept prospective client calls
Gather essential information from prospective clients, including their name, phone number, and type of legal matter
Create and maintain prospective client profiles and portals within the firm’s case management and CRM system
Open approximately 20 prospective client portals each day


Requirements


At least 3 years of receptionist experience in a professional, high-volume environment
Current or recent experience performing responsibilities closely aligned with a receptionist role
Proven ability to handle challenging clients with patience, professionalism, and sound judgment
Ability to manage several back-to-back calls without becoming overwhelmed
Strong customer service and interpersonal skills
Excellent time-management, organization, and multitasking abilities
Proficiency with Microsoft Word, Excel, and PowerPoint


Benefits


Medical, dental, and vision insurance, Employer-paid life insurance
401(k) plan with a 3% employer match after one year
2 weeks of combined vacation and sick leave
10 paid holidays, including your birthday
$125 monthly work-from-home stipend
$125 monthly health and wellness stipend


This is an opportunity to join a fast-growing, people-focused law firm where strong performance is recognized, and employees have a clear path to grow. If you are calm under pressure, serious about accuracy, and committed to providing exceptional client service, apply today!
