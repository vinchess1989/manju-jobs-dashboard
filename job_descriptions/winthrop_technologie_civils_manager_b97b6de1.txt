Katso, kuka Winthrop Technologies on palkattu tähän rooliin

Hae
Tallenna

Manage the co-ordination of the Civil work packages on this hyperscale project.

• Manage a project team of 20+ including, package managers, design managers, engineers and site supervisors.

• Support your team in managing 10+ subcontractors with a combined package value in excess of €100m.

• Oversee contractor's design elements of the project, ensuring design is carried out and relevant approvals in place ahead of construction on-site.

• Ensure BIM processes are followed by sub-contractors carrying out works under your supervision.

• Management of Site Safety in conjunction with safety personnel (including set-up, management and close out).

• Management of Site QA in conjunction the Projects team.

• Management of Project Schedule in conjunction with Planner, Project Managers, Sub-Contractors and consulting Engineers/Architects to ensure client requirements are adhered to.



Requirements



• Bachelor's degree in Civils Engineering or similar preferred

• At least 7 years of CSA industry experience.

• Previous experience delivering projects from concept to handover.

• Experience in scheduling, programming, managing subcontractors and sequencing.

• Experience in effectively managing teams is essential.

• Data centre experience is advantageous but not necessary

• Experience in the delivery of large-scale projects is necessary
