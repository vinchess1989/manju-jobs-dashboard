Specialist, Asset Accounting
Location:  

Espoo, FI

Country/Region:  FI
Job Area:  Finance
Business Unit:  Group Finance

Are you looking for a career in an inspiring, global environment where your expertise makes a difference? At Neste, you will be working for the world’s leading producer of renewable diesel and sustainable aviation fuel, which enable our customers to reduce their greenhouse gas emissions.

We are looking for you,

Specialist, Asset Accounting

to join our Asset Accounting team in Espoo.

Neste Global Financial Services provides finance shared services globally for Neste Group companies and employs approximately 70+ specialists. Finance processes included in the scope are accounts receivables, accounts payables, payments, general ledger and fixed assets.

As an Asset Accounting Specialist, your responsibilities include controlling and managing fixed asset master data and accounting in our ERP systems. The role also involves interpreting and maintaining IFRS 16 lease contracts as well as accounting entries in applicable systems. Close collaboration with different business units relating to investment projects is a key part of your daily work. Additionally, you play a vital role in performing month-end-closing tasks according to the Neste Group Reporting Schedule. Together with your expertise, we can do meaningful work to drive Neste forward. 

Reasons to join

We operate in an inspiring and evolving business environment as a global leader in renewable fuels. We contribute to Neste’s success by living our values every day: we care, we have courage, we cooperate.

We prioritize safety at all times and in every location. We strive to create a positive and impactful work environment.

Transformation brings new possibilities and fosters our courage and curiosity. As a global  company, we support personal development with versatile learning and career opportunities. Collaboration is key, and we believe in flexible, smart ways of working. We work at the office at least three days a week to ensure seamless teamwork and a sense of belonging.

In addition, our benefits for employees in Finland include, for example, a personnel fund as part of the overall remuneration, good occupational health care, a membership in a sickness or health insurance fund and leisure time accident insurance. We also support leisure time activities, e.g. by offering benefits for sports and culture (ePassi).

More about the job

Your role in our team

 

In your role, you will 

 

Be accountable for ensuring compliant fixed asset and lease asset accounting and reporting within IFRS standards, local GAAPs and tax regulations

Control asset master data in applicable systems and manage asset assignments such as scrapping, sales, additions and transfers

Cooperate actively with project management and other internal and external stakeholders to ensure correct and timely fixed asset activations

Perform month-end and year-end closing tasks according to Neste Group schedules, instructions and guidelines 

Prepare and ensure quality and compliance of annual asset tax reporting

Prepare audit material relating to fixed assets and assist auditors in their inquiries

Participate in Neste Corporation’s system development projects in the area of project management and fixed asset accounting

Train, support and actively share knowledge to colleagues and stakeholders

 

Keys to success in this role 

 

Bachelor’s degree or similar, preferably in Accounting or Finance

Experience in financial accounting and bookkeeping

Understanding of IFRS and GAAP

Fluent in English and Finnish skills are considered as an asset

Good communication skills

Team-oriented person who enjoys collaboration

Ability to work independently, effectively and with high quality under time pressure

Proactive, precise and analytical way of working

 

Other strengths we value

 

Knowledge in SAP 

Previous experience on finance applications and tasks are considered as an asset 

A continuous improvement mindset

Neste in brief

Neste (NESTE, Nasdaq Helsinki) is the world's leading producer of renewable diesel and sustainable aviation fuel (SAF), with production on three continents. The company’s renewables production capacity is expected to reach 6.8 million tons annually in 2027. Neste also produces high-quality oil products at its Porvoo refinery in Finland. The company has a network of nearly 1,000 fuel stations with expanding service offering, such as EV charging, in Finland and in the Baltics.

Neste’s strategy focuses on growth in renewable fuels, which help its customers to reduce their greenhouse gas emissions. Neste is included in many international indices for its sustainability performance. In 2025, Neste's revenue stood at EUR 19.0 billion. Read more: neste.com
