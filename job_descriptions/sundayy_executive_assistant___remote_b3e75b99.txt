About The Company

Solo is dedicated to closing the justice gap by helping individuals resolve debt-related issues efficiently and effectively. With a mission to assist over 70 million people facing collection actions annually, Solo has established itself as a leader in debt resolution services. The company has successfully helped Americans respond to over $2 billion in debt lawsuits, demonstrating its impactful presence in the financial and legal support sectors. Backed by prominent investors such as Kleiner Perkins and Y Combinator—famous for supporting industry giants like Apple, Google, Stripe, and Airbnb—Solo is positioned for rapid growth and innovation. The company has garnered recognition in leading publications including TechCrunch, Business Insider, Bloomberg, NPR, ABC, IBT, and Fast Company, reflecting its influence and commitment to social impact. Solo operates fully remotely, fostering a flexible and dynamic work environment focused on mission-driven objectives. The organization’s core principles emphasize customer-centricity, continuous improvement, data-driven decision-making, and operational excellence, all aimed at making justice accessible for all.

About The Role

We are seeking a highly organized and proactive Executive Assistant to support our CEO in a fully remote capacity. This role is vital to ensuring the smooth operation of the executive’s daily activities, including managing schedules, coordinating travel, and overseeing administrative functions. The successful candidate will serve as a key point of contact for internal and external communications, handle sensitive information with discretion, and facilitate onboarding and offboarding processes for employees and contractors. Additionally, the role involves supporting recruiting efforts, maintaining accurate candidate records, and assisting with personal matters for the CEO as needed. The position requires a detail-oriented individual with excellent communication skills, capable of multitasking and prioritizing in a fast-paced environment. This is an opportunity to contribute to a mission-driven organization that is transforming the debt resolution landscape, with the flexibility of a fully remote work setting and competitive compensation package.

Qualifications


3+ years of experience in administrative, operations, or people support roles
Strong organizational and multitasking skills with keen attention to detail
Excellent written and verbal communication abilities
Proficiency in Google Workspace, Slack, and project management or HR systems
Experience with corporate card platforms such as Brex and travel booking tools preferred
Ability to handle confidential information with professionalism and discretion
Proactive and resourceful problem-solving skills
Reliable, self-motivated, and adaptable to a fast-paced environment
Friendly, professional demeanor with a collaborative approach
Alignment with Solo’s principles: customer-centricity, data-driven decision making, continuous improvement, and pursuit of excellence


Responsibilities


Provide comprehensive administrative support to the CEO, including calendar management and scheduling appointments
Oversee travel arrangements, including booking flights, hotels, and rental cars
Manage the CEO’s email inbox, routing or responding to messages as appropriate
Assist with onboarding and offboarding processes for employees and contractors, ensuring all systems, accounts, and equipment are properly provisioned and deactivated
Conduct recruiting outreach, coordinate interviews, and communicate with candidates
Maintain accurate records of recruiting activities and candidate information within tracking systems
Support the CEO with personal matters, including travel coordination, scheduling, errands, and special requests
Perform miscellaneous tasks as needed to ensure operational efficiency and support the team’s objectives


Benefits


Competitive salary range of $52,000 – $116,000
Quarterly bonuses based on revenue growth
Equity options ranging from 0.001% to 0.1%
Fully remote work environment offering flexibility and work-life balance
Opportunity to contribute to a mission-driven organization making a significant social impact
Collaborative and innovative company culture focused on continuous improvement
