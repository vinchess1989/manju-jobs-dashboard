Job Purpose

At Emirates, we connect the world through our global hub in Dubai, ensuring our customers always 'Fly Better'. As one of the world's most recognised and admired brands, we are driven by innovation and excellence. We are looking for a Sales Coordinator to join our Commercial team in Finland, playing a key role in supporting our sales, marketing, communications, events, and administrative activities across the Finnish market.

Joining us you will experience a fast-paced multicultural environment where your ideas and expertise will help support the continued growth of Emirates in Finland. Working closely with our Commercial team, Head Office in Dubai, trade partners, and other stakeholders, you will contribute to delivering engaging sales and marketing activities while ensuring effective day-to-day coordination and administration.

What will I be doing?


Support the Commercial and Sales team with a broad range of activities that contribute to the successful operation and growth of Emirates in the Finnish market.
Provide support with BSP-related activities, including refunds and the handling of ADA, ADM and ACM cases. Assist the Sales team with the processing and coordination of special tickets, including AD, FAM and PEP tickets.
Act as local marketing support and coordinator between the Finland Commercial team and the Corporate Communications, Marketing and Brand teams in Dubai. Support the briefing process, campaign planning, communications with trade and other partners, translations, proofreading, and approval of advertising and marketing materials.
Coordinate and maintain sales and marketing materials, including images, PowerPoint templates, videos, and other promotional assets. Review press releases prepared by the PR agency and coordinate any required follow-up with relevant stakeholders.
Coordinate and manage events and business promotion activities, including sponsorship activities and joint workshops with tourism boards, hotels, and other partners. Support the full event process, including invitations, RSVP management, event coordination, and CRM updates.
Manage promotional giveaway stock and coordinate the ordering of supplies. Create Prizewinner Letters as requested by Sales Executives and the Sales Manager.
Maintain and update trade contact lists and databases, ensuring relevant commercial and trade information is accurate and up to date.
Provide a link between the local office, Head Office in Dubai, and relevant Nordic contacts. Coordinate cases with the National Consumer Disputes Board and other relevant parties, as well as supporting the team with legal and regulatory-related enquiries when required.
Coordinate hotel reservations for guests arriving in Finland and provide general administrative support to the Commercial team.
Assist the Finance and Administration Manager with various commercial and finance-related matters. Manage local office suppliers and provide general administrative support to the Country Manager, Finance Manager, and Commercial team as required.


Qualification

Qualification

In order to be considered for this role, the essential things we need from you are:


Experience in a sales, commercial, marketing, communications, or administrative support environment
Strong organisational and coordination skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities and deadlines
Excellent written and verbal communication skills in Finnish and English
Strong attention to detail, particularly when working with commercial, marketing, and customer-related information
Experience supporting events, marketing activities, communications, or business promotion activities
Skilled use of Microsoft Office, Teams, SharePoint, and social media platforms
Strong interpersonal and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to work effectively with colleagues, trade partners, agencies, and Head Office teams
Good understanding of the leisure, consolidator, and corporate travel markets
Strong administrative and problem-solving skills
'A' Level standard of education or equivalent
The unrestricted right to live and work in Finland


Your application will really stand out to us if you:


Have experience working within the airline, travel, tourism, or aviation industry
Have experience supporting sales or commercial teams within an international organisation
Have experience coordinating trade events, workshops, sponsorships, or other business promotion activities
Have experience working with CRM systems and maintaining commercial or trade databases
Have experience working with marketing, PR, or corporate communications teams
Enjoy working in a multicultural environment and collaborating with colleagues and stakeholders across different locations
Are proactive, flexible, and comfortable taking ownership of a varied range of responsibilities in a fast-paced commercial environment
