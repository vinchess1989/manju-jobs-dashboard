Join Nestlé Nordic – We are looking for the Power of You

Are you a Finnish-speaking university student passionate about marketing, brands, and consumer trends? Would you like to gain hands-on experience in a global FMCG company while working with some of Finland's most loved coffee brands?

Join our Coffee team in Finland as a part-time Student Marketing Assistant (approximately 2 - 3 days per week) and kick-start your career in an international environment.

Working at Nestlé

We are the largest food company in the world, focusing on nutrition, health, and wellness. Nestlé Nordic is a community where people truly matter. We value every aspect of you, from your talents to your personal experiences.

At Nestlé, we encourage you to embrace your authenticity. When you bring your whole self to work, you unleash your full potential.

Your job

We are now looking for a Student Marketing Assistant to join our Coffee team in Finland.

We offer you a chance to work in an international environment both with Finnish and Nordic colleagues. This role offers a unique opportunity to gain practical experience with versatile tasks in area of marketing and commercial development. Work is versatile, sometimes challenging, but your professional colleagues will always support you. We take our work seriously, but don’t forget humor in the way we work. The position is based in our Finnish Head Office in Espoo Keilaniemi and you will report to the local Commercial Development Manager. Your main responsibilities will include:


Creating and translating marketing materials
Supporting the planning and execution of marketing campaigns
Planning and coordinating events, trade fairs, sampling activities, and product tastings
Creating content ideas for local social media channels
Supporting influencer marketing collaborations
Assisting in trade presentation preparation
Conducting market and competitor analyses
Supporting the Coffee team with various commercial and administrative tasks


We are looking for the power of you

We are looking for a proactive and detail-oriented team player who enjoys working in a dynamic environment. You are able to manage multiple tasks simultaneously, meet deadlines, and take initiative when needed.

You are currently pursuing a university degree in Marketing, Business Administration, Nutrition, or a related field. Ideally, you are in the final stages of your Bachelor's studies or have recently started your Master's degree.

We believe you will thrive in this role if you have:


excellent verbal and written Finnish language skills (fluent)
ability to work in English in international environment
good MS Office, especially PowerPoint skills
strong desire to learn and grow, both personally and professionally
excellent communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to thrive in a team environment.
previous experience in a relevant role or field is preferred, but in the first place this is an opportunity to gain experience and learn


What we offer


A meaningful student position in one of the world's leading FMCG companies
Hands-on experience in brand marketing and commercial development
An opportunity to work with colleagues across the Nordic countries
A supportive and inclusive team environment
Valuable learning and development opportunities
Modern office facilities in Espoo Keilaniemi
Competitive salary and employee benefits


Would you like to know more about us?

Follow us on LinkedIn and Nestlé Finland if you would like to be notified of new Nestlé opportunities and our values. Follow us on Instagram if you want to know more about our company culture and see what it is like to work at Nestlé Nordics.

Join Nestlé Nordic and Make Your Mark:

If you are ready to be part of a supportive team that values your true self, apply already today but no later than August 16th. If you have any questions, don’t hesitate to contact Commercial Development Manager Juliaana Leppänen via LinkedIn. Please note that we will only accept applications that are sent to us via our Careers site.

Salary: 15,30 €/h

Preferred start date: September 2026

Note: Nestlé Nordic is an equal opportunity employer. We embrace diversity and inclusion and encourage individuals from all backgrounds to apply.
