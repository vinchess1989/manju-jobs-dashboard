Räätälöi ansioluetteloni

Chicago, IL | Full-Time | In-Person




Evolv is expanding its Chicago team and looking for an Events Assistant to support the delivery of our in-person client campaigns.




If you enjoy working with people and prefer a role where every day is active and interactive, this is an opportunity to gain hands-on experience across events, customer engagement and campaign representation.




What You’ll Be Doing

Support client campaigns at retail and event locations across Chicago
Welcome and engage members of the public
Represent campaigns and communicate key information
Create positive, professional interactions with customers
Answer questions and help generate interest
Assist with the smooth day-to-day running of campaign locations
Work alongside the team to achieve event and campaign goals
Maintain a professional and engaging presence throughout each event




What We Offer

Weekly base with additional earning opportunities based on performance
Full training before representing campaigns independently
Ongoing coaching and support
Practical experience across events, customer engagement and client campaigns
A social, team-oriented working environment
Opportunities to develop your communication and leadership skills
Increased responsibility available as your experience and performance grow




What We’re Looking For

Confident and comfortable interacting with new people
Strong communication and interpersonal skills
Positive, professional and reliable
Comfortable working in busy, public-facing environments
Able to learn quickly and respond well to feedback
Enjoys working as part of a team
Full-time availability for an in-person position in Chicago




You don’t need a specific degree or career background to be considered. We’re looking for people who can communicate well, bring the right energy to a customer-facing environment and take responsibility for their own performance.




If you’re looking for a hands-on role where you can build practical events and client-facing experience, apply today to join Evolv in Chicago.
