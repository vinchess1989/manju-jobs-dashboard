Tiivistelmä työpaikkailmoituksesta:

The Community Engagement Specialist will act as the day-to-day liaison between the customer, general contractors and the local community by visiting sites, managing community feedback, participating in project meetings, and preparing weekly updates.
The role requires strong local presence and stakeholder-management experience in infrastructure or construction, excellent communication and documentation skills, and fluency in Finnish with strong professional English.
The fixed-term position starts in October 2026 in Espoo and surrounding areas, runs for 12 months with possible extension, and the successful candidate will be employed by Adecco.
Applicants must include a cover letter in English and their salary request by 6/9/2026; the recruitment includes interviews by Adecco and the customer and questions can be sent to mari.torstila@adecco.fi.


Tämä tiivistelmä on luotu tekoälyn avulla.



For a well-known and iconic global ICT customer in Espoo, we are looking for a Community engagement specialist for Datacenter projects.

Tasks and job description

Serve as liaison between general contractors, neighbours and stakeholders

Act as a day-to-day bridge between the customer, its general contractors, and the local community

Visiting construction sites and surrounding areas on a regular basis and assessing potential community impacts

Maintaining regular contact with neighbours and local stakeholders as construction progresses

Participating in relevant project meetings

Managing community feedback

Preparing weekly updates

Monitoring, documenting, coordinating

Qualifications and Experience

Strong local presence, stakeholder management capability, and disciplined execution

Experience in infrastructure development and/or construction

Experience in community engagement, stakeholder relations, or a related external-facing role

Strong ability to build trusted relationships with local stakeholders, including neighbours, municipalities, partners, and contractors

Ability to monitor, document, and escalate issues

Strong written and verbal communication skills

Fluency in Finnish mandatory, and strong professional English language skills

Start: October 2026

End: 12 months + potential extension

Location: Espoo + surroundings

What next?

The recruitment process consists of interviews by Adecco and the Customer. Adecco will employ the chosen person during this fixed-term period.

The recruitment is handled by Adecco and all questions and further information will be provided by: IT&Tech Recruiter Mari Torstila, mari.torstila@adecco.fi

Please include a cover letter in English and your salary request to your application as soon as possible but latest by 6/9/2026. Also note that we will start reviewing applications and potentially conducting interviews already during the application period. We look forward to hearing from you soon!
