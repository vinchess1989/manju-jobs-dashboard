Räätälöi ansioluetteloni
We are looking for a customer-oriented Documentation Trainee to our Technical Documentation team in Espoo, Finland and Porvoo, Finland. Your main tasks are:
Scanning and digitization of physical material.
Ability to follow instructions and guidelines for document management.
Validation skills against standards/guidelines.
Moderate problem-solving and analytical skills.
Follow-up on delivery schedules.
Verifying that the documentation fulfill the quality requirements.
Maintaining document registers.
Requirements:
Good knowledge of computers (PC).
MS Office (especially Excel)
Moderate written and spoken English and Finnish.
Ability to communicate efficiently with multinational contacts.
Ability to work both independently and as part of a bigger team.
We value:
Proactive and “Can do” attitude.
Excellent communication & collaboration skills.
Readiness to learn new things continuously.
Long-term commitment.
We offer you:
International and flexible work environment in a growing company.
Possibility to develop working tools and processes.
Skilled colleagues and friendly atmosphere.
