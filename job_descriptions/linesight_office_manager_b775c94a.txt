Overview

As an Office Manager at Linesight, you will work with highly experienced, culturally diverse, and passionate teams nurturing a strong social dynamic and a shared sense of achievement. You will gain vast industry experience that will open doors for you, extend your skillset and expand your perspective. We will empower, support, and enable you to take real responsibility in your role so that you can take charge of your career progression with us.

The Opportunity

In this role, you will:


Manage the day-to-day operations of the Finland office ensuring a welcoming and efficient workplace.
Act as the main point of contact for employees, visitors, suppliers, and external partners.
Support employee and contractor onboarding, including workplace setup and local orientation.
Collaborate with HR, Mobility, IT, and other teams to deliver a positive employee experience.
Coordinate office supplies, equipment, mobile services, invoices, and vendor relationships.
Assist employees with local administrative processes
Organise meetings, client visits, team events, and employee engagement activities.
Provide administrative support to the country lead, project teams and assist with local business operations.
Handle general office administration, documentation, correspondence, and translation support when required.
Provide local language and cultural support to employees and project teams.
Occasional travel to project sites across Finland



We would love to hear from you if:


Previous experience in Office Management, Workplace Experience, Administration, Hospitality, HR Support, Executive Assistance, or a similar role.
Fluent Finnish and English language skills, both written and spoken.
Local knowledge of Finnish employment, healthcare, governmental, or administrative processes
A highly service-oriented mindset with a professional and approachable manner.
Strong organisational and multitasking skills with a hands-on approach.
Ability to work independently while managing competing priorities effectively.
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
High level of integrity, discretion, and attention to detail.
Proficiency with Microsoft Office 365 and other digital collaboration tools.
Experience supporting employee onboarding, workplace operations, or people-related administrative processes is advantageous



All interviews are conducted either in person or virtually with video required.
