Työn kuvaus

Gastro Hub Oy is looking for two skilled Georgian Cooks to join our kitchen team in Helsinki.

We are seeking two experienced Georgian cooks who are passionate about authentic Georgian cuisine and can help us deliver high-quality food and memorable dining experiences to our guests.

Main Responsibilities

Preparing authentic Georgian dishes according to restaurant recipes and standards
Making traditional Georgian breads, pastries, and dough-based products such as khachapuri
Preparing meat, poultry, fish, vegetable, and sauce dishes typical of Georgian cuisine
Ensuring consistent quality, presentation, and food safety standards
Participating in menu development and introducing traditional Georgian recipes
Managing food preparation, storage, and kitchen hygiene in accordance with Finnish regulations
Working closely with the kitchen team to ensure smooth daily operations
Monitoring stock levels and assisting with ingredient ordering

Requirements

Previous experience as a cook with Georgian cuisine
Strong knowledge of traditional Georgian food preparation methods and recipes
Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced kitchen environment
Understanding of food hygiene and safety requirements
Team-player attitude and willingness to take responsibility
English language skills sufficient for working in an international team
Georgian language skills are considered an advantage
Relevant culinary education is considered an advantage
