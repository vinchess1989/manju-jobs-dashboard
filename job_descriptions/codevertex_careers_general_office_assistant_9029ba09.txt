About The Role

CodeVertex Careers is hiring a General Office Assistant to support daily administrative work, office coordination, documentation, scheduling, and internal team operations. This is a United States remote position with structured expectations for communication, documentation, and ownership.

Responsibilities


Coordinate administrative tasks, internal requests, calendars, files, and daily workflow items.
Maintain accurate records, shared documents, trackers, and standard operating procedures.
Support communication between internal teams, applicants, vendors, and business stakeholders.
Review assigned information for completeness, accuracy, and timely follow-up.
Help improve repeatable administrative processes in a remote-first work environment.


Required Qualifications


Experience in administrative support, office coordination, operations support, or a similar business support role.
Strong written communication, organization, follow-through, and attention to detail.
Comfort using email, spreadsheets, shared documents, calendars, and online business tools.
Ability to manage routine tasks independently while following documented processes.


Preferred Qualifications


Experience working in a remote-first or distributed team environment.
Ability to improve templates, checklists, trackers, or operating procedures.
Comfort working with confidential or process-sensitive business information.


Compensation And Work Environment

The salary range for this role is $41,000 – $61,000 per year. Actual compensation may vary based on experience, skills, location, and business needs. This role is remote within the United States.
