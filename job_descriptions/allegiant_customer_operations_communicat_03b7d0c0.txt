Summary
The Customer Operations Communication Specialist uses specialized knowledge and skills to implement, maintain, and manage internal and external communication efforts as it relates to scheduled, charter, and irregular operations.

Visa Sponsorship Available: No

Minimum Requirements
A combination of Education and Experience will be considered. Must be authorized to work in the US as defined by the Immigration Act of 1986. Must pass a Criminal Background Check.

Education: High School Diploma/GED

Years of Experience: Minimum two (2) years of experience in communications strategy development.



Excellent written and verbal communication skills
Outstanding organizational and time-management skills
Experience and knowledge in copywriting, proofreading and editing
Works well under pressure and meets tight deadlines
Proficient in basic computer software such as Word, Excel, Microsoft, etc
Team-oriented and able to work in a collaborative environment





Preferred Requirements


Bachelor’s degree preferred but not required





Job Duties


Coordinate and monitor communications between the OCC, Stations, Crew, and Customers
Serve as a point of contact for internal media requests and delivery of information
Create, coordinate, and maintain external and internal communications process flows
Create effective communication strategies during scheduled, charter, and irregular operations
Maintain library of approved scripts and liaise with Media and Marketing departments for ad-hoc scripts
Communicate all information proactively and timely with the designated management including Executives and internal/external customers
Communicate service recovery with internal and external customers
Maintain Admin Portal business rules
Maintain Staff Contacts as it relates to the Communications Hub
Provide support to OCC Customer Operations
Other duties as assigned
