Role Overview & Impact

The Human Resources Coordinator provides administrative and operational support across the Human Resources department. This entry-level position supports onboarding, employee records, HR system transactions, recruitment coordination, employee communications, and general HR department operations.

The HR Coordinator serves as an initial point of contact for routine employee and applicant questions and ensures that requests are directed to the appropriate HR team member. The successful candidate will be highly organized, service-oriented, comfortable using technology, and committed to maintaining the confidentiality and accuracy of employee information.

Key Responsibilities

Core Responsibilities

Recruitment & Talent Coordination


Support candidate communications using approved templates
Track candidate status and documentation in the applicant tracking system
Assist with pre-employment requirements
Maintain accurate recruiting records and candidate files
Support job fairs, community outreach, internship activities, and recruiting events
Provide a professional, timely, and welcoming candidate experience


Onboarding & Offboarding


Coordinate pre-employment and onboarding activities after an offer has been accepted
Send new-hire forms, instructions, orientation schedules, and required notices
Track completion of pre-employment requirements, background checks, employment documents, and onboarding tasks
Coordinate the timely completion and retention of Form I-9 documentation in accordance with established procedures
Coordinate with hiring managers, Payroll, Information Technology, Learning and Development, and other departments to support new-hire readiness
Confirm that new employees receive appropriate system access, equipment information, schedules, and introductory materials
Support new-hire orientation logistics and attendance tracking
Follow up on incomplete forms or onboarding requirements
Initiate separation checklists after receiving appropriate authorization
Schedule exit interviews or distribute exit questionnaires


HR Compliance & Documentation


Assist with personnel-file, I-9, benefits, training, and HR data audits
Track required documents, acknowledgments, certifications, and renewal dates
Assist with preparing records for internal and external audits
Follow established HR policies, procedures, record-retention requirements, and confidentiality standards
Escalate incomplete, inconsistent, or potentially noncompliant documentation
Maintain current versions of approved HR forms, templates, notices, and resources
Support the distribution and tracking of required employee notices


Benefits & Employee Support


Route plan interpretation and eligibility questions to the HR Generalist or appropriate team member
Support annual Open Enrollment communications, scheduling, and administrative preparation
Assist with routine employee reminders and documentation follow-up
Maintain confidentiality of medical and benefits information
Support benefits and leave audits as requested


HR Operations & Administrative Support


Provide general administrative support for the HR team, including scheduling meetings, tracking training completion, and preparing reports
Assist with HR initiatives such as employee engagement events, training programs, and performance review cycles
Maintain organized HR documentation and assist with reporting needs as requested by leadership


Success Measures


Accurate and timely recruitment coordination and onboarding completion
Compliance with California labor laws and HR documentation standards
High responsiveness and professionalism in employee communication
Well-maintained, audit-ready personnel records
Efficient support of HR initiatives and department operations

High level of professionalism, discretion, and ability to handle confidential information
