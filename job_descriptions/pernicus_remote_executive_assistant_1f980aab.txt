As the Remote Executive Assistant, your mission is to maximize the productivity of our executive team. You will handle complex administrative tasks, manage high-level communications, and ensure that our strategic operations run without a hitch.

Main Responsibilities


Calendar Mastery: Manage complex, shifting calendars across multiple time zones, prioritizing meetings and resolving scheduling conflicts proactively.
Communication Gatekeeping: Act as the primary point of contact for internal and external stakeholders, managing emails and drafting professional correspondence.
Project Coordination: Track deliverables for ongoing Pernicus ventures, ensuring deadlines are met and follow-ups are completed.
Travel & Logistics: Arrange detailed domestic and international travel itineraries (flights, accommodation, visas) when necessary.
Meeting Support: Prepare agendas, attend key meetings to take minutes, and distribute actionable tasks to the relevant team members.
Data & Document Management: Organize digital files, prepare pitch decks, and assist in the creation of monthly reports.


Job Requirements


Experience: 1–5+ years of experience as an Executive Assistant, ideally within a startup, VC firm, or consultancy environment.
Tech Savviness: Expert proficiency in Google Workspace (Docs, Sheets, Slides), Slack, and project management tools like Notion, Asana, or Trello.
Communication: Exceptional written and verbal English communication skills; ability to interact with high-net-worth individuals and CEOs.
Organizational Skills: A "project manager" mindset with the ability to juggle multiple priorities without dropping the ball.
Discretion: High level of integrity and the ability to handle sensitive, confidential information with absolute professional secrecy.
Education: Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Communications, or a related field.


Perks & Benefits


Truly Remote: Work from wherever you are most productive.
Global Exposure: Gain a front-row seat to the scaling of innovative companies across Africa and beyond.
Growth Opportunities: We value lifelong learners and provide access to workshops and professional development resources.
Competitive Compensation: A salary package that reflects your expertise and the value you bring to the team.
Flexible Culture: We focus on outputs, not hours logged.
