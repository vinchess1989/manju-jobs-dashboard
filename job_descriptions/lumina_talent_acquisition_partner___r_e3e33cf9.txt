Job Title: Talent Acquisition Partner
Department: People & Talent
Location: Remote


Role Overview
We are seeking a Talent Acquisition Partner to own end-to-end recruitment across LUMINA and help shape how we attract and secure talent as we scale. Working closely with hiring managers, you will run live searches across our technical and commercial teams – from autonomy, AI, software and controls engineering through to mechanical, test and development, finance and operations.
The role demands someone equally comfortable delivering hands-on recruitment and improving the systems behind it, sourcing hard-to-find candidates, advising the business on hiring decisions, and bringing structure, data and consistency to a fast-growing talent function. This position is critical to building the team behind LUMINA’s machines: securing exceptional people quickly, protecting a high hiring bar, and giving every candidate an outstanding experience of the company.

What You’ll Do
Key Responsibilities



Manage the full hiring cycle - intake/kick-off
meetings, role scoping, sourcing, screening, interview coordination, offers and
onboarding handover.

Partner with hiring managers and founders to calibrate
requirements, challenge the brief where needed, and deliver against tight
timelines.

Proactively source passive, specialist candidates via
LinkedIn, direct outreach, referrals and networks - often in niche autonomy,
robotics and electrification fields.

Write clear, inclusive job descriptions and manage
targeted advertising across the right channels.

Build and maintain talent pipelines and pools

Own candidate experience with clear, timely
communication and feedback at every stage.

Manage multiple live roles across functions,
seniorities and regions, prioritising effectively.

Brief and manage agency and search partners
selectively, holding them to consistent, high-quality engagement.

Maintain accurate recruitment data and turn it into
insight - time-to-hire, pipeline conversion, source effectiveness and
offer-accept rates.

Use AI tooling to work smarter across sourcing,
screening, JD writing and outreach, while keeping a human, quality-first touch.

Support process improvement, hiring governance and
employer-brand activity as the function matures.


What You’ll Bring
Required Skills and Competencies



Proven ability to manage end-to-end recruitment across
a range of roles and seniorities.

Strong proactive sourcing skills - engaging passive,
hard-to-find candidates, not just managing inbound applications.

Hands-on experience with AI tools such as Claude (and
similar) to support sourcing, screening, JD writing and outreach.

Excellent stakeholder management and communication –
credible with technical hiring managers and founders alike.

Ability to work at pace amid shifting priorities, with
the ownership and self-direction a start-up demands.

Well organised and detail-oriented, confident working
with recruitment data and ATS/tracking tools. 


Experience



3+ years in talent acquisition, ideally having moved
from an agency background into an in-house or RPO environment.

Recruiting within engineering, hardware, robotics,
autonomy, AI or other deep-tech/high-growth environments.

Experience hiring across the USA, Europe or India is an
added benefit – we recruit internationally and value multi-region exposure.


Why Join Us?
We’re building a team of driven individuals who take
ownership, move fast, and hold themselves and each other to a high standard –
and we know the best ideas come from diverse perspectives. Whatever your
background or life experience, if you’re excited by the role and ready to make
an impact, we’d love to hear from you.
