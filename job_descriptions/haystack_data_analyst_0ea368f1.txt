We're working with a highly reputable firm, owned and operated by government veterans, supporting nationwide law enforcement initiatives that dismantle criminal organizations and disrupt their financial infrastructures. They play a critical role in safeguarding national security.

The Role


Receive, gather, analyze, and organize information from multiple sources
Ensure completeness of information and reconcile inconsistencies
Accurately enter required data into databases, documents, or spreadsheets
Establish and maintain physical files and track case file status
Coordinate requests for additional information and prepare documents, reports, and summaries
Perform word processing relevant to cases and provide timely status updates


What You'll Need


High School diploma or GED; undergraduate degree preferred
Ability to grasp the organization and functions of law enforcement customers
Meticulous data entry and excellent communication skills
Competence to review, interpret, and evaluate complex legal and non-legal documents
Strong organizational and prioritization skills with attention to detail
Proficiency with Microsoft Office suite (Excel, PowerPoint, Word) and common software applications


What's On Offer


Opportunity to support the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI)
Comprehensive benefits package with flexibility for health, work-life balance, and professional growth
Work alongside government veterans with extensive backgrounds in Homeland Security, Law Enforcement, Military, and Intelligence
