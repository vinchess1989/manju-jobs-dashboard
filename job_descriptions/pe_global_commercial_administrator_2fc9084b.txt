Commercial Administrator




Location: Vaasa, Finland

Employment Type: Permanent




We are currently recruiting for a Commercial Administrator to join a major data centre project in Vaasa, Finland, working with a leading international data centre contractor.




This is an excellent opportunity for an experienced Commercial Administrator / Commercial Assistant / Project Administrator with strong organisational and administrative skills to join a large-scale construction project and support the commercial team throughout the project lifecycle.




The Role




The successful candidate will provide administrative and commercial support to the project team, helping ensure that commercial processes, documentation and reporting are managed accurately and efficiently.




Key responsibilities will include:

Providing day-to-day administrative support to the commercial and project teams.
Maintaining and organising commercial documentation, records and project correspondence.
Assisting with the preparation and management of purchase orders, invoices and payment documentation.
Supporting the commercial team with subcontractor and supplier administration.
Maintaining trackers, spreadsheets and commercial reports.
Assisting with the preparation of cost reports and other project documentation.
Managing document control and ensuring records are kept up to date.
Liaising with internal teams, subcontractors and suppliers as required.
Supporting the Quantity Surveying / Commercial team with general project administration.
Ensuring company procedures and commercial processes are followed.




Requirements

Previous experience in a Commercial Administration, Project Administration, Construction Administration or similar role.
Experience working within the construction, engineering, MEP or data centre sector would be highly advantageous.
Strong organisational and administrative skills.
Excellent attention to detail and accuracy.
Good knowledge of Microsoft Office, particularly Excel and Word.
Ability to manage multiple tasks and work to deadlines.
Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
Professional working proficiency in English.
Candidates must already be based in Finland and have the right to work in Finland.




What’s on Offer?

Permanent position on a major international data centre project.
Opportunity to work within a large, highly experienced project team.
Long-term career development opportunities.
Competitive salary and benefits package.
The opportunity to gain valuable experience working on a major hyperscale data centre development.




***This is a local-hire position in Vaasa and is not a rotational/FIFO opportunity.***




If you are an experienced Commercial Administrator looking for your next opportunity in the construction/data centre sector, we would be interested in hearing from you.







***Please note our client cannot assist with any visa sponsorship and candidates must have the correct visa to live and work in Finland***
