Do you have a knack for creating compelling content and managing social media accounts with a traditional PR background? Are you able to confidently pitch a story to journalists and content creators? Do you have a love for cars, trucks, motorcycles, or an outdoor and adventure enthusiast?




If so, Kahn Media is seeking a talented Account Executive to join our award-winning team. This position can either be fully remote or hybrid based on location.




As an Account Executive, you will work under the guidance of a Senior Account Executive or Account Director, but will also take ownership of client relationships, participate directly in client meetings, and lead day-to-day PR and social media initiatives. You will be responsible for developing and executing social media strategies, securing media placements, managing content calendars, attending events, and reporting on results.




This role is ideal for someone with 1.5 - 2 years of PR experience who wants to merge their communications skills with a passion for the automotive and outdoor lifestyle industries. (everything from social influencer outreach to creating posts to coordinating publications)




Responsibilities and Duties

Develop and execute comprehensive PR and social media strategies for multiple clients
Draft and schedule posts for platforms including Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, and X
Draft press releases and pitch story ideas to automotive, lifestyle, and related media outlets
Create and manage social media content calendars and campaigns
Build and maintain relationships with journalists, influencers, and industry partners
Collaborate with the creative team to develop original content using client assets or new materials
Monitor and analyze PR and social media performance, providing insights and reports
Represent clients in meetings and at industry events




Qualifications and Skills

Preferred Bachelor’s Degree in Public Relations, Marketing, Communications, Journalism, English or related field.
Required – 1 to 2 years of professional PR or marketing agency experience.
Exceptional multi-tasking skills.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
Knowledge of AP Style as it pertains to writing for PR.
Strong problem solving skills.
Strong knowledge of social media platforms - especially Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube.
Experienced in organizing, creating, and scheduling social media posts.
Basic photography/video and editing skills.
Those with basic technical knowledge of cars, trucks, and off-road driving will be given priority.




About Kahn Media

Kahn Media Inc. is one of the fastest-growing PR/Marketing firms in the automotive, outdoor, and luxury lifestyle spaces thanks to our focus on working with the best brands, the most talented people, and fostering a highly charged creative work environment.




Our specialty is being the bridge between the top brands and their customers, creating fun engaging content and promoting the latest vehicles, products and events. We have an amazing in-house art department with talented photographers, videographers and designers.




Our Account Executives specialize in working directly with the clients and the media by writing new product and news releases, stories for company blogs, newsletters, providing event coverage and generating social media campaigns. We believe in delivering excellence for our clients and creating a fun, positive environment for our team.
