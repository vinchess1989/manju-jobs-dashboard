This is a hybrid role that blends building and operating high-impact hiring programs for thousands of YC startups and some minor hands-on talent sourcing. About 90% will be spent designing, running, and improving the events, playbooks, and operational programs & content that help YC startups hire themselves. About 10% of your time will be spent driving a smooth, high-quality recruiting pipeline for YC’s own internal software team.

TL;DR


Minimum 2+ years of experience, ideally in an operations role, and extra points if there’s an interest talent/startup team building
Prior evidence managing a operations pipeline, from sourcing to hire (ideally evidenced by 5+ past hires)
Prior evidence running events and programs, including university recruiting programs, meetups or other recruiting-related events


If you’re the kind of person who enjoys building teams through recruiting work AND loves building scalable programs, and you want to work directly with founders while shaping how thousands of startups hire, this is a uniquely high-leverage role.

About The Team & Product

Y Combinator runs Work at a Startup (workatastartup.com) — a free hiring platform that connects exceptional candidates with YC startups. In the past few years alone, it has helped 1,000+ people find roles at companies like Stripe, Instacart, Brex, Whatnot, and more. Think of it as a marketplace, in which we need to:


Train thousands of companies a year on how to hire well, through programming, content, product documentation and more
Grow the online candidate pool through content, programming, social experimentation and product ideation
Run repeatable events — job expos, tech talks, and more — for in-person hiring and community building


These efforts serve as the top-of-funnel engine feeding talent into the YC ecosystem — and this role helps run and scale all of it.

What You’ll Do (90% Events & Operations)

Program & Event Management


Own YC’s job seeker programming: Monthly Hiring Meetup, Tech Talks, Career Expos, and more
Develop and iterate on our internal playbooks for all recruiting-related events
Run high-quality in-person events on campus that help founders and candidates connect
Analyze engagement and event performance to understand what’s working and what needs improvement
Grow our candidate ecosystem by building partnerships and relationships with universities, communities, and talent pools


What You’ll Do (10% Recruiting)

Technical Recruiting


Source, screen, and engage technical candidates for YC’s internal software team
Maintain strong pipelines and run structured, consistent interview processes
Coordinate across YC teams to ensure programming is polished, professional, and reflects YC’s standards
Aim to hire 4-5 engineers annually, using Work at a Startup and other YC-specific recruiting tools


What We’re Looking For

Must-Haves


Experience running programs and projects, with an eye for ongoing improvement and repeatability
Past content creation experience — blog posts (professional or personal), video content, and social
Experience leading cross-functional projects or events where multiple stakeholders need tight coordination
A goal-oriented, analytical mindset with a track record of executing projects with measurable outcomes


Nice-to-Haves


Technical recruiting experience including sourcing, pipeline management, and evaluation of technical talent
Experience planning and executing in-person or virtual events
Light design ability (Figma, Sketch, or similar)
Experience in or enthusiasm for the startup ecosystem


Who Thrives Here


Operators who take real pride in their craft and love building repeatable, high-quality programs
People who enjoy recruiting but also want to expand into product, events, and broader ecosystem building
People who exercise excellent judgment and can handle sensitive information with care
Individuals excited about startups — many people in this role have founded companies, worked at them, or plan to


Location & Benefits

Location: This is an in-person role at YC's campus in Dogpatch, San Francisco. This is where our users (founders, partners, and employees) are five days a week, so it’s optimal for you to be here with them. You must live in the SF Bay Area or be willing to relocate. We offer generous relocation support for those who want to move to SF to work here.

Compensation: $90K to $160K base salary, depending on experience.

Benefits: Our full benefits package includes medical, vision, and dental plans, infertility benefit, STD/LTD, life insurance, commuter benefits, flexible spending account, health savings account, 401(k) + 4% matching, generous parental leave, paid holidays, and flexible paid time off policy.

Work Authorization: Y Combinator is willing to sponsor certain employment visas in accordance with company policy.
