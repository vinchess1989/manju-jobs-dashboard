Job Overview

Ready- Contractors, a reputable company that has been serving the eastern North Carolina community with quality workmanship since 1969, is seeking a highly organised and self-motivated individual to join our team as a WFH Administrative Assistant. This is a full-time, remote position based in the United States, offering the flexibility to work from home. The successful candidate will be responsible for providing comprehensive administrative support to ensure the efficient operation of our construction business. We are looking for a proactive professional with excellent communication skills and a keen eye for detail.

Responsibilities


Manage and maintain executives' schedules, appointments, and travel arrangements.
Handle and screen incoming calls and correspondence, directing them to the appropriate person.
Prepare and edit correspondence, reports, presentations, and other documents.
Organise and maintain digital filing systems, ensuring documents are easily accessible.
Liaise with clients, suppliers, and internal staff in a professional and courteous manner.
Assist with basic bookkeeping duties, including data entry and expense tracking.
Provide administrative support for various construction projects as needed.
Coordinate virtual meetings, prepare agendas, and take minutes when required.


Qualifications


Proven experience as an Administrative Assistant, Virtual Assistant, or in a similar role.
Excellent organisational and time-management skills, with the ability to prioritise tasks effectively.
Strong written and verbal communication skills.
Proficient in Microsoft Office Suite (Word, Excel, Outlook, PowerPoint) and other common office software.
Ability to work independently with minimal supervision in a remote setting.
High level of attention to detail and accuracy.
Experience within the construction industry is highly desirable but not essential.
A proactive approach to problem-solving and a 'can-do' attitude.


Benefits


Competitive hourly wage of $40 - $100 USD, based on experience and qualifications.
A fully remote position offering an excellent work-life balance.
The opportunity to be part of a well-established company with a strong reputation for quality and reliability.
A supportive and collaborative team environment where your contribution is valued.
