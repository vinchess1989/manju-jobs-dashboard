Relationship Development Coordinator - Sonset Resolutions

Here at SonSet Solutions, we use technology to spread the gospel of Jesus Christ worldwide. The applicant could use one's networking skills to inspire others to partner with us in the advance of the gospel. Help build strong relationships with donors and friends of SonSet Solutions, deepening their engagement through prayer and financial support.

About The Applicant


Called to serve in missions
Outgoing, service-minded, and professional in demeanor
Gifted in developing and nurturing new relationships
Organized, detail-oriented, and self-motivated
Excellent written and verbal communicator


How One Will Serve


Support funding campaigns and annual giving goals
Grow a recurring donor base through relationship-building
Assist in planning fundraising events and initiatives
Track and report on donor engagement and contributions
Develop and distribute promotional materials


Requirements and Information


A follower of Jesus Christ with a heart for the gospel
Committed to SonSet Solutions’ Statement of Faith
Full-time position, onsite in Elkhart, Indiana
Sonset Solutions is a faith-based nonprofit organization. Staff members are employees of the ministry and their compensation is funded through a financial partnership model in which supporters contribute to the mission of the organization. Staff receive training and support in developing and maintaining ministry partnerships.
