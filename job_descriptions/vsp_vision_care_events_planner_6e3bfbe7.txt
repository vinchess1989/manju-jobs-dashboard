Olenko sopiva tähän työhön?
Räätälöi ansioluetteloni
Plan and execute events that support the goals of the enterprise. Ensure successful execution of event from start to post-event evaluations.

Plans and executes events, such as marketing and sales conferences, trade shows, and customer-related events

Directs logistics, including registration and attendee tracking, presentation and materials support, and pre- and post-event evaluations

Procures vendor services

Works with facility, exhibit set-up, equipment, and catering requirements

Provides on-site event support

Research potential event locations and evaluate alternatives

Prepares and tracks budgets, and provides periodic progress reports for each event project

Conducts post-event evaluations to determine how future events could be improved upon

Regularly exercises discretion and independent judgment in the performance of his/her job duties

Job Specifications

Typically Has The Following Skills Or Abilities

Bachelor’s degree in marketing, hospitality, or related field, or equivalent experience

2+ years in an event planner-oriented position

Keen attention to detail and follow-up/follow-through are a must for this position

Proficiency in Excel, PPT, and Microsoft Office

Experience in budget and planning

Experience managing all phases of multiple projects simultaneously, and adjust to continuously changing responsibilities and priorities

Excellent communications skills

Must be available to travel as needed
