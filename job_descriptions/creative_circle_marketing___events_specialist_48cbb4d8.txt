Our client, a tech startup, is seeking a Marketing & Events Specialist to join their team on a remote contract basis.

This role is primarily focused on coordinating and executing smaller-scale events such as lunch & learns, partner activations, and intimate industry gatherings. These events are frequent, detail-driven, and critical to the company's go-to-market efforts.

During slower event periods, you will pivot to support broader marketing initiatives including email campaigns, social media, and campaign execution.

This is a hands-on role for someone who enjoys managing logistics, keeping multiple moving pieces organized, and staying close to both events and day-to-day marketing execution.

What You'll Do

Event Coordination (Primary Focus):

Plan and execute small-scale, high-impact events (e.g., lunch-and-learns, partner events, field activations)
Manage logistics including scheduling, vendors, materials, registrations, and internal coordination
Support event promotion and post-event follow-up activities
Ensure smooth, detail-oriented execution across a high volume of events

Marketing Support (Secondary Focus):

Manage email marketing campaigns, including setup, scheduling, and coordination
Support social media planning and publishing
Assist with campaign execution and marketing program coordination
Help keep marketing initiatives organized and on track

Team Support:

Jump in wherever needed - this is a startup environment, and priorities can shift quickly
Collaborate cross-functionally to support overall marketing efforts

Requirements

3+ years of marketing experience, with hands-on experience supporting or coordinating events
Experience managing smaller, detail-heavy events or programs (field marketing experience is a plus)
Strong organizational skills and attention to detail
Ability to manage multiple events and tasks simultaneously
Comfortable owning work end-to-end with minimal direction
A proactive, flexible attitude with a "whatever it takes" mindset
Excited by a role that blends event coordination with broader marketing support

 

In this position, you may have access to client or customer systems, confidential and/or proprietary information or data.
