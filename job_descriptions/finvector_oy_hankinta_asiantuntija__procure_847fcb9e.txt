Hae työpaikkaa
Työn kuvaus

We are looking for a professional Procurement Specialist to join our Indirect Procurement team.

In this role, your primary responsibility will be the preparation, negotiation, implementation, and maintenance of supply and service agreements across a wide range of indirect spend categories. These may include engineering and technical services, facility management, maintenance, laboratory and quality-related services, IT solutions, logistics, professional services, and other business-critical support functions.

You will play an active role in supplier relationship management, sourcing activities, contract lifecycle management, and procurement process development. Working closely with internal stakeholders across Production, Quality Assurance, Engineering, Facilities, Finance, and other support functions, you will help identify business needs, execute competitive sourcing initiatives, and ensure that procurement activities align with operational, quality, and compliance requirements.

The role offers an excellent opportunity to contribute to the continuous development of procurement practices, drive cost optimization initiatives, support supplier performance management, and participate in strategic procurement projects. You will also take part in supplier negotiations, tendering activities, risk assessments, and other purchasing-related tasks while collaborating with a global supplier network.

We are looking for a self-directed and proactive professional with excellent interpersonal and stakeholder management skills and a strong team-player mindset. You should be comfortable working in a dynamic environment, managing multiple priorities, and building productive relationships with both internal customers and external suppliers. Strong negotiation, analytical, and contract management skills are essential. Experience working in a regulated industry and understanding of quality systems are considered advantages.

The working language of the organization is English; therefore, excellent written and spoken English skills are required.

In order to succeed in this role, you should have:

Previous experience in procurement or supply chain management in an international environment
Experience in negotiating, preparing, and managing commercial agreements
Knowledge of indirect procurement processes and supplier management practices
Experience with ERP systems such as Microsoft AX/D365 or similar
Strong organizational and stakeholder management skills
Knowledge of pharmaceutical, healthcare, manufacturing, or other regulated industries is considered an advantage
A relevant university degree or equivalent educational background

The employment is permanent and full-time (37,5 h/week). We would like you to start preferably in beginning of September.

What FinVector offers you

Meaningful job in organization where your work truly matters for peoples health and where your work is valued. High competence, quality, teamwork, and transparency are emphasized in everything that is done at Finvector. In international organization you have great opportunities to grow and develop.

In addition to great colleagues and meaningful work, we offer a comprehensive range of employee benefits, including extensive occupational healthcare services, ePassi benefits for sports and cultural activities, a lunch benefit, and participation in our company-wide bonus scheme.

Are you looking for new career opportunities and want to be part of a meaningful success story? Please submit your application via the link below by 9 August 2026 and attach your CV and application, including your salary expectation.

For more information about the position, please contact Hiring Manager Manu Pitkänen (+358 40 939 8913) during office hours in weeks 29–30. During the Hiring Manager's holiday (weeks 31–32), please contact Procurement Manager Mervi Huttunen (+358 50 327 3227).
