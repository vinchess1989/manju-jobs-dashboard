Job Description

We are seeking a motivated and detail-oriented second- or third-year law student to join our legal team as a Law Clerk. This position offers hands-on experience working alongside attorneys and supporting a variety of legal matters. The ideal candidate will possess strong research, writing, and organizational skills and be eager to gain practical experience in a fast-paced law firm environment.




Responsibilities and Duties

Conduct legal research and prepare written summaries and memoranda.
Assist with drafting pleadings, motions, briefs, discovery requests, and other legal documents.
Review and summarize medical records, depositions, case files, and other supporting documentation.
Assist attorneys with case preparation, including discovery, depositions, hearings, and trials.
Organize and maintain case-related documents and information.
Monitor deadlines and assist with ensuring timely completion of assignments.
Communicate professionally with attorneys, staff, clients, and outside parties.
Attend hearings, depositions, mediations, and other legal proceedings when appropriate.
Perform additional legal and administrative duties as assigned.




This position provides an excellent opportunity for a law student to develop practical legal skills and gain meaningful exposure to litigation while working closely with experienced attorneys.
