Role Responsibilities


Leverage advanced Excel skills to structure, analyze, and present data for AI model training.
Collaborate with the customer s team to deliver insightful business intelligence solutions.
Prepare, clean, and validate large datasets to ensure data accuracy for AI systems.
Develop and document BI workflows with clear written and verbal communication.
Translate business questions into actionable data insights for model improvement.
Create detailed reports, dashboards, and visualizations in Excel for AI training.


Requirements


Have strong relevant experience in business intelligence or analytics, focusing on Excel.
Exceptional proficiency in advanced Excel features such as Power Query, PivotTables, VBA, and data modeling.
Strong written and verbal communication skills to convey complex data insights to diverse audiences.
Proven ability to work independently and remotely, managing time and deliverables efficiently.
Solid understanding of business processes and translating business requirements into technical solutions.
Experience cleaning, preparing, and validating large datasets for analytics or AI training purposes.
