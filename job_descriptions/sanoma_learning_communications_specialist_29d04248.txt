At Sanoma Learning, we all share a deep-rooted passion for education and are committed to supporting teachers help all students reach their full potential. We are proud of the impact we make together across Europe, locally as well as internationally.

With learning and media impacting the lives of millions of people every day, Sanoma has a unique purpose. To continue developing our financial and external communications, we are now looking for a Communications Specialist for a one-year, fixed-term employment.

About The Position

In this position, you have a unique chance to make a difference in further developing Sanoma’s financial, sustainability and other external communication. We offer you an opportunity to learn more about the learning and media businesses and to gain extensive experience in communications of a large, listed company, supported by a team of skilled and engaged colleagues.

Key Responsibilities


Owner of Sanoma.com, running and developing the platform (Optimizely), incl. content and usability maintenance and development, content ownership of the Group pages, as well as participating in development and updating of the Sustainability and IR sections
Owner of the Group’s Social Media strategy and channels, incl. strategy development, content planning and creation, coordination with SBUs, publishing posts
Contributing to and publishing of the Group’s external releases and news, key user for release distribution platform
Preparations of the Annual and Interim Reports, e.g. participating in content creation, preparing templates, coordinating the creation of visuals, choosing pictures, translating texts from English to Finnish, transforming the content online
Implementing internal and external IR and sustainability communications, e.g. updating relevant intranet pages, writing to newsletters, writing sustainability news
Maintaining and developing image library for external communications and developing document templates (PPT, Word)
Active role in organising of IR events as part of the team
Coordination and cooperation with different service providers
Active networking and coordination with internal and external stakeholders


About You

We are looking for a digitally savvy communicator, especially skilled in website and social media development, who will show great commitment and enthusiasm to further enhance Sanoma’s external communications across channels.

You have a hands-on way of working, driving and moving things forward independently, using both your general judgement and previous communications experience to make the right prioritisations. You have attention to detail, a business-minded approach and a strong developmental mindset, along with the ability to adopt to changing situations. You have strong interpersonal skills and an ability to network and build relationships that help you navigate a complex organisation.

Formal Requirements


Academic degree in business/economics, law, communications or similar
Relevant work experience in corporate communications or communications consulting, in a listed company setting
Excellent communication skills, in English AND Finnish (both verbally and in writing)
Knowledge in using website platforms (experience in using ‘Optimizely’ as an administrator is considered an advantage)


What Sanoma Offers

As an employer, Sanoma offers a working environment where you are able and encouraged to show your best self, in a work environment that is result-oriented but also very flexible and collaborative. We offer you a key position with interesting and challenging tasks and good opportunities for professional growth, in a renowned market-leading company having a positive impact on the lives of millions of people every day.

Interested?

Please submit your application in English. Due to summer holidays, you may be a strong candidate but still not contacted immediately.

Position Specs

Location: Central Helsinki

Work-type model: Hybrid

Contract: Temporary, full time (Oct 2026 – Sep 2027)

Travelling: Limited (in Europe)

About Sanoma Learning

How do you prepare students for the world, engage their curiosity and inspire them to grow? That is the main question of teachers all around the world. At Sanoma Learning, it has always been our mission to help to find and develop best-in-class answers to this question. If you join us, it means that you embark on an exciting journey to help shape the future of learning for primary, secondary and vocational education. We believe that your journey is our success as a company.

Sanoma Learning is a leading K12 European learning company, operating in 12 countries. Our learning products and services enable and support teachers and schools to develop the talents of every child to reach their potential. By offering printed and digital learning content, as well as digital learning and teaching platforms for primary, secondary and vocational education, we want to grow our positive impact on learning across Europe. With over 3000 employees, we help shape the future of education for 25+ million of students.

So, are you ready to start your journey to help shape the future of learning and help teachers to support all students to reach their potential?
