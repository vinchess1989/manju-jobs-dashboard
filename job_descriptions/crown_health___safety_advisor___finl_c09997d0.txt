Health & Safety Advisor

Mission-Critical Roofing, Cladding & Façade Projects

Location: Vaasa & Helsinki, Finland

Working Arrangement: Rotation-Based

Role Overview

Due to continued growth, we are seeking two Health & Safety Advisors to join our expanding European operations team in Finland.

The successful candidate will support the delivery of roofing, cladding and façade packages on major mission-critical construction projects. Working closely with Project Managers, Site Managers, clients and subcontractors, you will play a key role in ensuring all works are delivered safely, compliantly and in line with Crown's industry-leading standards.

This is an excellent opportunity to join a market-leading specialist contractor and work on one of Europe's most technically challenging projects while developing your career within a growing international business.

Key Responsibilities


Promote and drive a positive health and safety culture across all project teams
Ensure compliance with local legislation, client requirements and Crown's Health &Safety Management System
Carry out regular site inspections, audits and safety observations, identifying risks and implementing corrective actions where required
Prepare and review Risk Assessments and Method Statements (RAMS)
Deliver site inductions, toolbox talks and safety briefings
Support project teams in planning works safely and identifying potential hazards before work commences
Monitor subcontractor performance and ensure compliance with project-specific health and safety requirements
Investigate incidents, accidents and near misses, producing reports and implementing corrective actions
Maintain accurate health and safety documentation and reporting
Liaise with clients, consultants and other stakeholders on all health and safety matters
Support internal and external audits and contribute to the continuous improvement of Crown's health and safety standards
Provide practical advice and guidance to project teams to ensure works are delivered safely, on programme and to the highest quality standards


Requirements


Bachelor's degree in Occupational Health & Safety, Environmental Health & Safety, Construction Management or a related discipline
Recognised Health & Safety qualification such as NEBOSH General Certificate, NEBOSH Construction Certificate or equivalent (NEBOSH Diploma desirable)
Minimum three years' experience in a Health & Safety Advisor role within the construction industry
Experience working on roofing, cladding, façade, building envelope or large-scale construction projects is desirable
Data centre or mission-critical project experience is advantageous
Strong knowledge of EU and Finnish Health & Safety legislation and industry best practice
Experience carrying out site inspections, audits, incident investigations and risk assessments
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills
Full driving licence
Willingness to work on a rotational basis
Currently based in Europe and eligible to work within the EU


Benefits


Competitive salary, commensurate with experience
Rotation-based working arrangement with international project exposure
Opportunity to work on high-profile, mission-critical construction projects across Europe
Clear career progression opportunities within a growing and ambitious organisation
Ongoing training, professional development, and support for further qualifications
Collaborative and supportive working environment with a strong focus on employee wellbeing
Opportunity to work alongside experienced industry professionals on technically challenging projects


Crown is an equal opportunities employer and welcomes applications from all suitably qualified candidates.
