Marketing Lead 




Eleiris builds AI solutions as a natural extension of how organisations work. We integrate AI into core operations and everyday workflows, helping teams reduce manual work, gain clarity, and focus on what matters. 




We’re a fast-growing startup aiming for €10 million in revenue next year and €100 million within three years. 




We’re looking for a hands-on Marketing Lead to become our first dedicated marketing hire. You’ll build Eleiris’ marketing from the ground up, setting the direction and owning the execution. 




What you’ll do 




Build and lead Eleiris’ marketing strategy and operations 
Plan and execute multichannel marketing and paid advertising 
Create compelling content about pioneering AI technology and its real-world business applications 
Turn our expertise, customer insights, and projects into effective marketing 
Grow our visibility and demand through SEO and GEO, LinkedIn, and other relevant channels 
Define audiences, messages, metrics, and ways of working, and continuously improve results 
Collaborate closely with our leadership, commercial team, and technical experts 




What we’re looking for 




A few years of hands-on experience in planning and executing marketing 
The ability to build structure and take ownership in an evolving environment 
Experience with paid advertising, content creation, and multichannel marketing 
Practical knowledge of SEO and GEO, WordPress, LinkedIn, and marketing analytics 
Strong communication, collaboration, and commercial thinking 
Fluency in English; Finnish is highly beneficial 
Experience in B2B, technology, SaaS, or consulting is a plus 

﻿

You don’t need to be an AI expert from day one. However, a genuine passion for AI technology and a strong drive to build deep subject-matter expertise are essential. You’re eager to understand AI technology and its business potential - and communicate it clearly and credibly. 




You’ll thrive here if 




You combine strategic thinking with hands-on execution 
You’re comfortable building where no structure exists yet 
You take ownership and move things forward independently 
You’re curious, resourceful, and eager to learn 
You want to grow with an ambitious company 




Why Eleiris 




Build the marketing function of a fast-growing AI company 
Take real ownership and have a visible impact on growth 
Work on cutting-edge AI projects that are transforming organisations 
Join a small, senior, hands-on team with no corporate layers 
Work full-time in a hybrid setup with regular on-site days in Helsinki 




Interested? 




Send your CV and a free-form application to sanna.rousi@eleiris.ai. 

 

In your application, we’d love to hear what motivates you about this role and see practical examples of what you have built, improved, or driven forward, and how you achieved it. We’re interested in your way of thinking, not just the number of years you have worked in marketing. 

 

We’d also like to hear your thoughts on AI, how you currently use AI in your work, and how you would apply AI in marketing. You do not need to be an AI expert, but we value curiosity, practical experimentation, and a genuine interest in understanding how AI can create value for businesses. 




Please apply by 15. September 2026.
