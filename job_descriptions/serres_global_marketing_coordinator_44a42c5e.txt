Hae 
Tallenna

Serres is the world's leading provider of surgical fluid management solutions. Our products help healthcare professionals deliver safe and sustainable patient care and are used in more than 80,000 operations every day worldwide. We employ over 180 professionals and serve customers in more than 50 markets globally. For more information, visit our website at www.serres.com.




As our international business continues to grow, we are looking for a Marketing Coordinator to support the execution of global marketing activities across digital channels, events, content, and marketing operations.

You will be part of a small and collaborative marketing team based mainly in Finland, reporting to the Head of Marketing. While you will have ownership of your responsibilities and work independently, you will also collaborate closely with colleagues across sales, product management, and other functions internationally. This role offers broad exposure to an international business environment and is well suited to someone who enjoys variety, changing priorities, and keeping multiple projects moving forward simultaneously.




What you'll do

As our Marketing Coordinator, you will support the execution of global marketing activities and help ensure that marketing operations run smoothly across channels and markets.




Your responsibilities include:

Updating websites and other digital marketing channels
Coordinating the production of print and digital marketing materials
Updating marketing materials, including basic copy and image edits
Publishing newsletters
Creating and publishing social media content
Coordinating localization and translation projects with key stakeholders
Coordinating marketing events and exhibitions
Collaborating with internal stakeholders and external vendors
Supporting the maintenance and effective use of marketing systems and tools
Ensuring marketing activities and materials align with brand guidelines and established processes
Monitoring and reporting on key marketing performance indicators (KPIs)




What we're looking for

Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Business, Communications, or a related field
Approximately 3 years of experience in international marketing coordination, marketing communications, or similar marketing support roles
Strong organizational and prioritization skills
Ability to manage multiple tasks simultaneously and deliver high-quality work in a fast-paced environment
Excellent communication and collaboration skills
A proactive, service-oriented mindset and strong attention to detail
Professional working proficiency in English is required. Finnish language skills are considered an advantage




Technical skills and experience

Ability to maintain CMS systems such as WordPress
Ability to use CRM, and Sprout and marketing automation platforms
Basic level understanding of Photoshop, InDesign, Canva, and Google Analytics
Experience in coordinating marketing projects and working with external vendors
Experience in medical devices, healthcare, or other highly regulated industries is considered an advantage




What we offer

A versatile marketing role with broad exposure to international business operations
The opportunity to work in an internationally growing company that combines innovation with a strong commitment to sustainability. As part of our mission, we actively support healthcare organizations in their journey towards net-zero emissions and a more sustainable future
A supportive and collaborative team environment where you can work independently while having the backing of experienced colleagues
Excellent employee benefits including comprehensive occupational healthcare and insurance coverage, lunch-, sports-, wellbeing, and culture benefits
A monthly salary of 3400-3900€, based on your experience, skills, and overall fit for the role.
Hybrid working model and flexible working hours, supported by modern office facilities in Pohjois-Tapiola, Espoo. We value the flexibility of hybrid work and encourage regular in-person collaboration. Therefore, we hope that the successful candidate has the possibility to work from our Espoo office from time to time. The office is easily accessible by public transportation and offers convenient parking, making commuting convenient regardless of how you choose to travel.
