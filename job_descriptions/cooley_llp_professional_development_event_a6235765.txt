Cooley is seeking a Professional Development Events and Operations Coordinator to join the Professional Development team.

Position summary: The Professional Development Events and Operations Coordinator is responsible for coordinating Professional Development events and providing logistics and operational support for the Professional Development department, with a primary focus on the planning and execution of PD Academies and other firmwide learning initiatives. Specific duties and responsibilities include, but are not limited to, the following:

Position responsibilities:


Maintain firmwide Professional Development calendar for PD events and Academies; distribute timely updates and changes
Coordinate scheduling and end-to-end logistics for PD events and initiatives (including PNC/SC)
Manage registrations, attendance, and event rooming lists
Provide onsite event support, including check-in, materials distribution, speaker support, and issue resolution
Liaise with Marketing, Creative, and Technology to coordinate event communications, deliverables, and technical requirements
Develop and maintain templates and tools for PD (e.g., tip sheets invitations, agendas, checklists, and evaluation forms)
Standardize procedures across annual Academies
Process invoices and expenses; support department budget tracking and documentation
Identify opportunities to improve recurring event and administrative processes by developing standardized workflows, templates and documentation and by thoughtfully leveraging firm-approved technology and automation tools.
All other duties as assigned or required


Skills and experience:

Required:


After orientation at Cooley LLP, exhibit proficiency in the Microsoft Office suite, iManage and other firm applications
Available to work overtime, as required
2+ years of direct related experience (e.g., administrative, project coordination or event coordination)
Technical problem-solving abilities


Preferred:


Bachelor’s degree
Experience working in professional services firm
Interest in learning and applying firm-approved AI and automation tools to streamline administrative and event coordination workflows


Competencies:


Excellent attention to detail
Strong verbal and written communication skills
Ability to organize, prioritize and coordinate multiple activities
Strong judgment
Team-player with collaborative spirit
Unwavering ability to handle and maintain confidentiality regarding firm information, projects, client data (if applicable)
High level of professionalism at all times
Continuous improvement mindset, with a natural curiosity for improving processes, adopting new technologies and creating more efficient, scalable ways of working
