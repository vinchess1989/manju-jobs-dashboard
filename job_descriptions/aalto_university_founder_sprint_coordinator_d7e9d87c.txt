Aalto University Founder School operates as a startup within the university: moving fast, delivering results quickly, iterating, piloting, and pivoting. We enjoy working with people who are excited to make things happen on short notice.

Founder Sprint is our flagship program - a selective, blank-page founder program that bets entirely on the person. We are hiring someone to run it, build the system that lets it scale, and grow toward owning it. This is not a traditional role, you are not just organizing but engineering the system around the program.

This role is a great preparation for future work as a startup founder or in hectic and demanding leadership roles. This position suits someone with a strong interest in high-growth entrepreneurship, community-building, events, and communications in a fast-moving environment.

Job duties may change over time. Teamwork and open communication are a must. This job requires physical presence most workdays. Work and collaboration is done in English. There is a possibility to extend the continuation of this role or grow into leading the program.

The full-time Founder Sprint Coordinator runs the daily execution, participant experience, logistics, communications, and operational rhythm of Founder Sprint.

The Coordinator makes sure the program runs smoothly, participants are supported, activities are well-prepared, communication is clear, and the overall cohort experience is strong together with the Founder Sprint Specialist.

This is a growth role. The aim is for the Coordinator to learn Founder Sprint deeply over time and develop toward taking over responsibility for the program in the future.

This role is for someone with high agency, strong execution skills, and ambition to grow. Success in this job can open doors to some of the most attractive entrepreneurial career paths in Finland and beyond.

The Founder Sprint Coordinator will partner with the Founder Sprint Specialist as two co-owners of the program:


Own the full recruitment funnel for each cohort - attracting applications, sourcing, scoring, interviews, selection, onboarding and building the system that runs it.
Build the tools that scale the program: application pipelines, scoring systems, AI-assisted ops, internal automations. We expect you to ship.
Run the weekly program rhythm: schedules, deadlines, deliverables, workshops, mentor sessions, feedback loops, and make it sharper every cycle.
Be the face of Founder School in the ecosystem: events, pop-ups, student orgs, partnerships, ambassador work.
Connect participants to the people, capital, spaces, and next steps that matter, inside and outside Aalto.
Hold the bar: intensity, quality, ambition, and care across the entire cohort experience.
Jump into other Founder School work when it counts: Founder Talks, alumni, ecosystem projects, events.


What’s in it for you?


You get direct access to one of the most ambitious startup hubs in the Nordics.
You get direct access to exceptional students, founders, investors, mentors, alumni, and startup ecosystem actors.
You will gain experience in top-tier program operations, founder development, coaching support, ecosystem building, and venture development
You get real ownership and responsibility from day one.
You will develop skills that are highly relevant for future work as a founder, operator, venture builder, startup program lead, or ecosystem builder.
You will be part of a small, ambitious team with high autonomy building something new. See our current team from here.


Who thrives here?

Qualities that make someone exceptional for this role:


You build. You are a builder-operator. You make things happen that wouldn’t have happened without you.
You are able to use AI tools to build systems that make the program scale with better results.
You run many parallel threads - people, deadlines, logistics - without dropping the bar.
You're comfortable with execution and documentation as much as with strategy.
You want to learn to build and run a world-class founder program
You are passionate about startups and want to help future founders grow
You have strong organizational skills and enjoy making things run well.
You communicate clearly and proactively.
You exhibit high agency and do not wait to be told every next step.
Familiarity with Aalto’s entrepreneurial ecosystem (a plus, not a must).
Previous experience in startups, student entrepreneurship, events, communities, or program coordination (is a plus).
Speaking and writing fluent English (Finnish is a plus).

Assignment

If you are interested in this role, make it yours! Make use of your agency, ambition and energy to proactively produce something that allows us to understand who you are.


Please record a short video (maximum 2 minutes) describing your experience with startups, program management, and community building, along with anything else you feel is relevant to this role. Make sure the video can be viewed through the link.
Founder Sprint exists for one purpose: to become the place where the next decacorn founders emerge from. How would you build Founder Sprint into a program that everyone competes to get into?


The format for this can be anything (such as a strategic proposal, AI agents or anything that you feel is relevant). We look for a sharp mindset, high agency, and achievable vision.
