Title: Recruiting Coordinator / Resume Reviewer

Location: Remote (U.S.)

Compensation: $28–$35/hour

Employment Type: Contract (W2 6-months)




About the Role

We're looking for a detail-oriented and highly organized Recruiting Coordinator / Resume Reviewer to support our growing Talent team. This is an excellent opportunity for someone early in their recruiting career who is eager to learn, thrives in a fast-paced environment, and is passionate about delivering an exceptional candidate experience.




You'll play a key role in reviewing applicants, supporting interview scheduling, managing agency-submitted candidates, and assisting with recruiting operations to help ensure hiring processes run smoothly and efficiently.




What You'll Do

Review incoming applicants and agency-submitted candidates against role requirements
Bucket and organize candidates within the ATS based on qualifications and hiring criteria
Ensure timely responses to agency submissions and help maintain a 4-hour SLA
Coordinate and schedule interviews across multiple stakeholders
Communicate professionally with candidates, agencies, and hiring teams
Maintain accurate candidate records and hiring workflows in the ATS
Support recruiting operations projects and process improvements
Assist recruiters with pipeline management and candidate tracking
Help ensure a positive and seamless candidate experience throughout the hiring process




What We're Looking For

Start up experience a plus
1–2 years of experience in recruiting coordination, talent acquisition, HR, or a related field
Strong attention to detail and organizational skills
Excellent written and verbal communication
Ability to manage multiple priorities and deadlines simultaneously
Comfortable working in a fast-paced, high-growth environment
Coachable, eager to learn, and receptive to feedback
Strong sense of ownership and follow-through
Experience with applicant tracking systems (ATS) is a plus
