We are hiring in Kouvola!




We’re continuously building strong teams to support the design, delivery, sustainability and operation of our data centers across the Nordics. We’re always interested in hearing from experienced professionals who want to be part of a fast‑growing and critical infrastructure industry.




We hope you’ve had the chance to learn a bit about atNorth and already have an idea of the areas where you could build your career with us.




If you’re motivated by technically complex environments, operational excellence, and close collaboration with multidisciplinary teams, we’d love to get to know you. By submitting an open application, you’ll be considered for current and future opportunities that match your background and interests.




We are mainly looking for talents to work from our sites, and options for remote work are limited.
