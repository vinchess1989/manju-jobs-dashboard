Junior Consultant, Outsourcing

We are currently seeking a professional at Junior level to grow our outsourcing team and embark on this exciting start-up venture. If you are looking for a dynamic opportunity to excel in a multicultural environment while shaping the future of financial services in Finland, your journey begins here.




What will you do?

Accounting: Processing various aspects of clients' accounting, including supplier invoices, sales invoices, inventory movements, fixed assets, bank transactions, travel expenses and preparing reconciliation files.
Financial and Management Reporting: Assisting with preparing statutory financial reports and Management reports for clients.
Tax Reporting: Preparing VAT returns and Corporate Income Tax returns with the help of a senior consultant.
Statistical Reporting: Preparation of statistical reporting, such as Intrastat.
Collaboration: Coordinating with other Forvis Mazars departments such as tax department and external auditors.




What are we looking for?

An agile and business as well as people-oriented outsourcing professional with an international mind-set and the following competences, qualities and qualifications:

Education: A high school or university degree in economics.
Language Skills: In addition to fluent Finnish, proficiency in English (spoken and written). Knowledge of other languages such as Swedish or German is a plus.
Local Expertise: Knowledge of Finnish accounting and tax regulations.
Experience: 1-2 years (Junior) professional experience in a similar position.
Technical Skills: Proficiency in MS Office, especially Excel. Experience with accounting software like Netvisor is considered a plus.
