Internal Communications & Engagement Coordinator

Location: Remote (PST Preferred)

Duration: 12 months

Compensation: $55 – 70 (DOE)




Description

The Sage Group’s client’s Ads Growth Operations team creates communications, experiences, and programs that connect employees to our business, culture, and one another.




We’re looking for an Internal Communications & Engagement Coordinator to support the execution of communications and employee engagement initiatives across Ads. Working under the direction of the Growth Operations team, you’ll help bring projects to life by supporting communications, presentations, digital content, events, and managing project workflows. The ideal candidate is a highly organized communications generalist who enjoys creating high-quality deliverables, managing details, and supporting multiple initiatives and colleagues in a fast-paced environment.




Responsibilities

Support the execution of internal communications and employee engagement initiatives across Ads.
Edit, format, and publish communications across multiple channels—including emails, newsletters, presentations, Slack channels, websites, and digital content—using internal tools.
Execute on and maintain provided project plans, workback schedules, documentation, and status updates to support successful project delivery.
Work on communication assets including Keynote presentations, simple graphics, templates, and supporting materials while partnering with comms professionals on larger initiatives.
Maintain internal websites and digital content using the client’s content management systems, ensuring consistency, accuracy, and quality across channels.
Support communication and event operations by tracking deliverables, managing project tools, and following established workflows and processes.




Key Qualifications

Minimum of 3 years of experience supporting internal communications, employee engagement, project coordination, marketing communications, or related work.
Excellent writing, editing, and communication skills with experience executing content across a variety of formats and channels.
Strong project coordination and organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple concurrent priorities and independently execute work within established processes.
Experience working in content management systems to publish digital content and project management platforms to manage work (Wrike, Airtable, Asana, Jira, or similar).
Strong collaboration and communication skills with the ability to build effective working relationships with the team.




Bonus Qualifications

Experience supporting internal communications, events, or employee engagement programs.
Experience working in a technology company or communications, creative, or experiential agency.
Experience using AI tools to improve content creation, documentation, or project execution.
Experience creating simple visual assets using Adobe Creative Suite, or similar design platforms.
