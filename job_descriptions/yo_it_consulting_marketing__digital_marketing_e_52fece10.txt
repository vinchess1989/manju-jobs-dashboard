Job Title: Marketing Specialist

Job Type: Contractor, Part-Time

As a Marketing Specialist, you'll apply your expertise to help train next-generation AI systems. Your work will shape how models learn, reason, and perform through high-quality, real-world input. No prior experience in AI is required — your domain knowledge is what matters.

Key Responsibilities


Design and execute comprehensive digital marketing campaigns to drive brand growth and engagement.
Analyze campaign performance data, delivering actionable insights and optimizing strategies for maximum ROI.
Create compelling, original content tailored to diverse digital platforms, ensuring consistent messaging and tone.
Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams to align marketing initiatives and project timelines.
Track, report, and present marketing metrics, identifying opportunities for improvement and innovation.
Manage multiple projects simultaneously, balancing deadlines and quality standards.
Contribute marketing domain expertise to advance the effectiveness of AI-powered solutions in a dynamic project environment.


Required Skills And Qualifications


Minimum 5 years of proven experience in marketing, digital marketing, or campaign execution.
Expertise in data analysis, performance tracking, and translating insights into actionable marketing strategies.
Exceptional written and verbal communication skills; attention to detail in messaging and reporting.
Demonstrated ability to manage projects and coordinate with cross-functional teams.
Strong content creation skills for digital channels including web, email, and social media.
Proficiency with marketing analytics tools and platforms.
Ability to prioritize tasks, meet deadlines, and thrive in a fast-paced, remote environment.


Preferred Qualifications


Experience with AI-driven marketing tools or training data for machine learning projects.
Background in supporting technology or SaaS products with marketing initiatives.
Advanced certifications in digital marketing or data analytics.
