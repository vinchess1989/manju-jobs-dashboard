About the Role

We are seeking a highly organized, coachable, and enthusiastic PR Account Coordinator to join our fast-paced agency team specializing in the beverage and hospitality space. This is an entry-level role designed for a self-starter who is passionate about public relations, media relations, and storytelling. You will support multiple account teams by handling daily account operations, media tracking, and client reporting.




Key Responsibilities:

Media/Influencer Relations & Outreach

Media List Building: Research and build accurate, targeted media lists for specific clients using PR databases (e.g., Cision, Muck Rack). 
Media Monitoring: Monitor daily news cycles and client coverage; compile and distribute daily or weekly coverage reports and clip books. 
Pitching: Assist senior staff by drafting and distributing press releases, media alerts, and targeted pitches.

Account & Client Support

Meeting Coordination: Schedule client calls, draft agendas, and take detailed meeting notes with clear action items.
Reporting: Create timely campaign wrap-up reports and assist in evaluating media metrics.
Administrative Support: Maintain account trackers, client files, and sample inventories to keep teams organized. 

Research & Content

Industry Research: Conduct competitor and industry research to support new business pitches and campaign brainstorming.
Copywriting: Draft first drafts of supporting content, such as bylined articles, award submissions, and social media captions.




Qualifications:

Age Requirement: Must be 21 years of age or older to legally handle and manage alcohol samples.
Education: Bachelor’s degree in Public Relations, Communications, Journalism, Marketing, or a closely related field. 
Experience: Relatable PR agency internship or previous communications experience preferred. 
Writing Skills: Excellent writing and editing skills with a strict attention to detail. 
Tech Savviness: Proficiency in Microsoft Office 365, Google Workspace; familiarity with PR tools like Muck Rack, Cision, or Coverage Book is a plus.
Soft Skills: Exceptional organizational and time-management skills; ability to juggle multiple clients and deadlines in a dynamic environment. 




What We Offer:

Competitive base salary
Health care benefit stipend
Generous Paid Time Off (PTO) and company holidays
Opportunities for mentorship, training, and career progression
