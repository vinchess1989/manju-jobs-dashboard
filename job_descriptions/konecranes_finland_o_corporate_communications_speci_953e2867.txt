Job Description
Ready to move your career forward?

Join Konecranes as our new Corporate Communications Specialist.

The Konecranes Brand & Communications team is an integral part of the company's global operations. We work closely with Business Areas and global functions in planning and delivering effective corporate communications and brand marketing programs and continuous communications.

Location: You can be based in Espoo or Hyvinkää, with Espoo as the main location for the team. We work in a hybrid model, with an expectation to be on-site at Espoo a couple of days per week.

Key responsibilities

Planning and implementing Konecranes' corporate communications activities in line with the Konecranes strategy and priorities
Project management independently and as a part of a team, varying from process development to single projects
Producing content from concept through publication for primarily internal but also external, social and other media
Supporting strategic and transformation projects
Collaborating across Konecranes' Business Areas and functions
Qualifications
What we expect:

Bachelor's degree or equivalent in relevant field
Minimum 3 years of experience in corporate communications; preferably in a listed company
Native level Finnish and excellent fluency in English
Strong written and verbal communications skills, ability to adapt the communication style according to the situation and culture
Digital literacy, comfortable using MS office, digital tools, social media and AI
Strong project management skills, capable of managing multiple projects in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment
Analytical mindset, data driven approach to brand marketing and communications
Excellent networking skills
Service minded and flexible working style
Able to work independently as well as a member of a team
Passion for learning, willingness to take on new challenges and share knowledge
Additional Information
Why join us?

At Konecranes, you will have the opportunity to work in a truly global environment where your ideas and expertise make a real impact. You will join a team that values creativity, collaboration and continuous improvement, and where your voice matters.

In this role, you will have the opportunity to influence corporate communications on both strategic and practical levels, work closely with leadership and collaborate across international teams. You will benefit from the scale and stability of a global organization while having room to grow and advance your career according to your ambitions.

In addition, we offer:

Flexible and hybrid working with the possibility to work remotely.
Competitive salary and performance-based incentives, together with a comprehensive benefits package, including ePassi welfare benefit, free gyms at our Hyvinkää and Espoo offices, and an insurance fund.
The opportunity to participate in our Employee Share Savings Plan.
Konecranes cottages available for rent.
Multiple sports and leisure activities through Koneen Kerho ry, such as sports, networking, and cooking classes.

Want to learn more about Konecranes and what your future colleagues have to say - visit our Career pages !

Interested?

Please submit your CV and application, along with a relevant work portfolio by 20 September 2026, using the Apply link below. You can add additional attachments, such as your application and portfolio, on the second page of the application form. We look forward to hearing from you!

For further information about the role, you are welcome to contact Niina Suhonen, VP, Brand & Communications (+358 40 843 9438), during the following times: 8 September at 13-15 or 16 September at 10-11.30.

Olemme suomalainen teknologiayritys, jolla on maailmanlaajuinen läsnäolo ja työmahdollisuuksia.

Konecranes Suomesssa
Olemme suomalaiset juuret omaava teknologiayritys, joka on laajentunut vuosien saatossa maailmanlaajuiseksi nostolaite- ja huoltopalvelutoiminnan suunnannäyttäjäksi. Konsernimme pääkonttori sijaitsee Suomessa Hyvinkäällä ja Suomesta meiltä löytyy sekä nostureiden valmistusta ja huoltotoimintaa että maailman luokan tuotekehitys-, projektinhallinta- ja suunnittelutoimintaa liittyen nostolaitteiden automatisointiin, etäohjaukseen ja reaaliaikaiseen tiedonkeruuseen. Lisäksi Suomesta löydät myös niin paikallista toimintaa kuin globaalejakin operaatiota tukevia ammattilaisia esimerkiksi talouden, markkinoinnin, henkilöstöhallinnon, hankinnan ja IT-palveluiden saralta. Olemme ylpeitä monipuolisesta, eri alojen ammattilaisista koostuvasta henkilöstöstämme, kansainvälisestä toimintaympäristöstämme sekä monikulttuurisesta, lähes 30 eri kansallisuudesta koostuvasta työyhteisöstämme Suomessa.
