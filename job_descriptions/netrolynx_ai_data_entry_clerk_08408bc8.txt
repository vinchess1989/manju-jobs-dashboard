About The Company

GL Inc. is a reputable consulting firm specializing in product testing and product development services. Our mission is to connect consumers with leading brands by providing authentic and actionable feedback through innovative in-home usage testing methodologies. We work closely with national and international companies to help them refine their products, improve customer satisfaction, and gain market access. Our commitment to integrity, privacy, and quality ensures that both our clients and participants receive the highest standards of service. With a focus on leveraging real-world consumer insights, GL Inc. strives to be a leader in the market research industry, fostering transparency and trust in every project we undertake.

About The Role

We are seeking dedicated Product Testers to join our team from the comfort of their homes across the United States. This role involves participating in in-home usage testing projects that provide valuable feedback on a variety of consumer products. As a Product Tester, you will be responsible for following specific testing protocols, documenting your experiences, and providing honest reviews. The position offers flexible hours, typically between 15 to 25 hours per week, making it ideal for individuals seeking part-time, seasonal, or supplementary income. Compensation ranges from $25 to $45 per hour, depending on the project, with weekly payments made via direct deposit or check. No prior experience is required, making this an accessible opportunity for anyone interested in contributing to product development and market research.

Qualifications

The ideal candidate for this role should possess the following qualifications:


Ability to follow detailed instructions accurately
Strong attention to detail and a curious mindset
Reliable internet connection and access to a computer
Access to a digital camera or smartphone capable of taking clear pictures
Excellent communication skills and honesty
Ability to commit to a flexible schedule of 15-25 hours per week
Must be 18 years of age or older


Responsibilities

The primary responsibilities of a Product Tester include:


Properly documenting in-home usage tests according to the provided daily schedule, including screenshots, audio recordings, videos, and journal entries
Handling products responsibly and following safety guidelines
Adhering strictly to the testing instructions, which may involve unpacking, reading instructions, using the product for designated periods, and providing feedback through reviews or online forms
Utilizing mobile testing technology, such as MFour’s innovative smartphone tools, to capture insights and emotional responses during product use
Participating in private chat discussions or focus groups as directed by market research firms
Writing detailed and honest reviews as specified in the daily schedule for each project


Benefits


Highly competitive pay rates ranging from $25 to $45 per hour
Weekly payments via direct deposit or cheque
Flexible working hours tailored to your schedule
Opportunity to learn about new products and industry trends
Work remotely from home or any location with internet access
Most tested products can be kept after testing is completed
No previous experience required, making it accessible for entry-level applicants
