Event Volunteer at NORDEEP Espoo | 16–17 September 2026

Step into the Deep Tech Ecosystem! Expand your network, learn the game!




NORDEEP is the Nordic’s largest deep tech business summit, bringing together academia, capital, and industry at Dipoli, Espoo, to turn science-backed innovations into thriving global businesses. For our 5th Anniversary Edition, we’re looking for motivated Event Volunteers to join the team and help us deliver a seamless, high-value experience for the global deep tech community.




💼 What you’ll do

Depending on your assigned schedule and role, you may help with:

Registration & guest check-in
Building & teardown of the event production
Speaker & session support (stage assistance, mic running, room flow)
Exhibitor & research institution support (booths, logistics, wayfinding)
Matchmaking / meeting area support (facilitating high-level dealmaking)
VIP, investor & academic delegation assistance
Info point & general attendee support




📋 What we’re looking for
A genuine interest in deep tech, startups, science, events, or ecosystem building
Positive, proactive attitude and great communication skills
Comfort in a fast-paced business environment with international attendees
Reliability and punctuality—teamwork is our core foundation
Fluency in English




🎁 What you get
Exclusive networking access and a front-row seat to the Nordic deep tech and research ecosystem
An official Volunteer Certificate to recognize your contribution
A recommendation for top-performing volunteers who show exceptional teamwork and reliability
Official event merch & volunteer perks
A volunteer afterparty to celebrate the success with the whole team
Meals and refreshments provided during your shifts




📅 When & where
Main Event Dates: 16–17 September 2026
Location: Dipoli, Espoo, Finland
Shifts: May vary (morning/day/evening)
