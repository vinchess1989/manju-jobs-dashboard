Join DATAMARK as a Senior Corporate Talent Acquisition Partner and contribute your expertise in a role where your impact truly matters!

The Senior Corporate Talent Acquisition Partner is a highly experienced member of the Talent Acquisition team responsible for leading full-cycle recruitment efforts for professional, leadership, and specialized positions across the organization. This role serves as a trusted talent advisor to hiring managers and executives, driving effective recruitment strategies by leveraging advanced sourcing techniques, market intelligence, talent acquisition technologies, and consultative recruiting practices to identify and attract top talent. This role also serves as a subject matter expert in recruiting best practices, talent acquisition systems, sourcing tools, and market trends while supporting strategic Talent Acquisition initiatives.


Develop and maintain consultative partnerships with the senior management team and hiring managers to understand workforce needs
Lead recruitment efforts for specialized and difficult-to-fill positions through advanced sourcing, networking, and talent-mapping techniques.
Develop and execute proactive sourcing strategies to build diverse talent pipelines for current and future hiring needs.
Partner with business leaders to create recruiting strategies that align with organizational goals and workforce planning initiatives.
Maintain and expand relationships with professional organizations, industry associations, universities, and networking groups to strengthen talent pipelines.
Champion best practices related to candidate experience, employer branding, recruiting technology, and process efficiency.
Provide guidance and knowledge sharing to Talent Acquisition team members regarding sourcing strategies, recruiting technologies, market insights, and corporate recruiting practices



Requirements


Bachelor's degree in human resources or business administration, or equivalent experience required
Minimum of 6 years' experience in a corporate Talent Acquisition environment, recruiting professional, technical, management, and executive level positions



Other Qualifications:


Demonstrated experience partnering with senior leaders and hiring managers to fill complex and strategic roles.
Proven experience utilizing Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS), sourcing platforms, talent intelligence tools, CRM systems, and other recruiting technologies.
Experience conducting passive candidate sourcing and building talent pipelines for difficult-to-fill positions
Experience supporting Director and Vice President level searches preferred
Bilingual in English and Spanish preferred
Previous recruitment experience in a BPO or Contact Center environment is highly preferred
Ability to travel as required


Required Skills:


Exceptional passive sourcing and talent identification skills.
Strong executive presence and ability to influence senior leaders.
Excellent verbal, written, and presentation communication skills.
Ability to translate market data and recruiting analytics into actionable business recommendations.
Strong project management and organizational skills.
Ability to manage multiple high-priority searches simultaneously.
Advanced networking and relationship-building capabilities.
Strong analytical, problem-solving, and decision-making skills.
