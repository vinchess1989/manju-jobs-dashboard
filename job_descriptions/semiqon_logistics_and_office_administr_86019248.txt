Do you enjoy creating a well-organized, efficient, and welcoming workplace? Are you at your best coordinating logistics, managing procurement activities, and ensuring daily operations run smoothly? If so, we invite you to join SemiQon's pioneering team. In this role, you'll support the operations behind our quantum technology development and contribute to the success of a growing deep-tech company.




Role overview

As a Logistics and Office Administrator, you will play an important role in supporting the day-to-day operations of our business. You will be involved in logistics and procurement activities, coordinate shipments and purchasing processes, and help maintain an organized, productive, and welcoming office environment. Working closely with colleagues, suppliers, and service providers, you will contribute to the efficient running of our operations while ensuring that key administrative processes are handled effectively. This is a full-time position with opportunities for growth and development within the company.




‍

Key responsibilities

Manage logistics operations, including shipping, receiving, deliveries, and coordination of domestic and international shipments
Coordinate import and export activities, including customs documentation, shipping paperwork, and communication with freight forwarders, customs authorities, and logistics partners
Monitor international shipments and help ensure compliance with applicable customs, trade, and transportation requirements
Process purchase requests, create purchase orders, and support procurement activities
Coordinate with suppliers, vendors, and service providers to ensure timely deliveries and service quality
Track orders, maintain procurement records, and assist with invoice processing
Help with office organization, including office supplies, inventory management, and workspace coordination
Assist with travel arrangements, travel logistics, and expense reports
Schedule meetings and appointments as needed
Support operational and administrative projects across the organization




‍

Qualifications

Experience in logistics, procurement, office administration, or a similar support role
Understanding of export permit processes and export control regulations is preferred
Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities and deadlines
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (Teams, Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
Ability to work both independently and collaboratively in a fast-paced environment
Fluency in English and Finnish is preferred

‍

*A security clearance vetting may be required for the position.

Join us in shaping the quantum future

At SemiQon, your work keeps a fast-moving, deep-tech company running smoothly as we work to reshape the landscape of quantum computing. If this sounds like the right challenge for you, apply now to join us as our Logistics and Office Administrator.

‍About us

SemiQon’s mission is to realize the promise of quantum computing by delivering scalability through powerful, resilient, and cost-effective silicon-based quantum hardware. Its technology builds upon decades of development and know-how from the semiconductor industry, making its cryo-optimized CMOS electronics commercially competitive and well-suited for mass-manufacturing. SemiQon’s first-in-the-world cryo-optimized CMOS electronics deliver scalability and power efficiency to wide-ranging applications in quantum, space, and beyond.
