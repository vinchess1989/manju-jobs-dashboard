At Verda, we're building a fully featured European AI cloud, covering everything needed to train, experiment with, and deploy AI models at scale.

We are a full-stack AI infrastructure company, meaning we design, operate, and optimize the compute powering modern AI workloads across training and inference. Our infrastructure runs on 100% renewable energy, helping build a more sustainable AI ecosystem.

Join Verda while it’s still being built - not once it’s finished.

About The Role

We are looking for a proactive, organized, and motivated intern to join our team! This is an excellent opportunity for someone who enjoys supporting day-to-day operations, takes initiative, and thrives in a fast-paced environment.

What You'll Do


Support daily administrative and office tasks.
Assist with organizing office supplies and snacks.
Ensure workspaces remain organized and well-stocked.
Assist with ad hoc projects and other duties as needed.
Act as the a first point of contact for employees/vendors for our second office in Helsinki
Take care of our coffee machines and own them when we have a visitor


What We're Looking For


A proactive attitude and willingness to take initiative.
Strong organizational and time management skills.
Good communication and interpersonal skills.
Ability to multitask and prioritize responsibilities.
Attention to detail and a positive, can-do mindset.
Basic computer skills (Microsoft Office or Google Workspace).
A team player who is eager to learn and contribute.
Previous experience as a barista is a huge plus.


What We Offer


Hands-on experience in office administration and kitchen operations.
Mentorship and opportunities to develop practical workplace skills.
A supportive and collaborative team environment.
Exposure to a dynamic business with opportunities to learn across different functions.


Practicalities

Location: Helsinki, Finland

Office Based: 5 days a week at our beautiful Helsinki headquarters in the heart of the city.

Employment type: Internship 6 months

If you're enthusiastic, dependable, and ready to gain valuable experience while making a meaningful contribution, we'd love to hear from you. Apply today and start building your professional experience with us!
