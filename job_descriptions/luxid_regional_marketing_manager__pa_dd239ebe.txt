Are you a hands-on marketing professional who enjoys coordinating projects, bringing people together, and ensuring marketing activities run smoothly? Do you thrive in a role where you can connect marketing, sales, and data into impactful actions? If this sounds like you, you might be the Regional Marketing Manager, we’re looking for! This is a fixed-term parental leave cover position expected to last a little over one year, starting in autumn 2026.

We Luxidians are a team of creative thinkers, strategists, data analysts, writers, designers, filmmakers, virtual world builders, developers and technologists. We use insight-led creativity to accelerate growth for our clients. With 30+ years of experience in delivering marketing and commerce solutions to a strong international client base, we help organizations break silos to build customer journeys that drive sales and support buyers.

As a Regional Marketing Manager, you will coordinate and drive Luxid’s operational marketing activities and ensure smooth day-to-day execution across channels, translating Luxid’s commercial and marketing strategy into effective marketing actions. Supported by the Head of Revenue & Growth and the Global Marketing Lead, you will work closely with specialists across digital marketing, content, design, martech and data to coordinate marketing initiatives, facilitate collaboration, and ensure high-quality execution across the organization. You combine planning with hands-on delivery and play a key role in aligning marketing with sales and business goals.

Main responsibilites:

Marketing Coordination & Execution


Plan, coordinate, support and execute marketing activities across channels
Manage marketing calendars, content schedules and campaign-related activities
Coordinate collaboration between different teams and stakeholders


Content, Brand & Communications


Coordinate and contribute to content creation (website, newsletters, campaigns, events)
Ensure brand consistency across all touchpoints
Contribute to internal communications such as weekly updates and monthly newsletters


Marketing Platforms & Data


Manage and continuously develop HubSpot usage, including data quality, reporting and user support
Optimize platform usage for marketing and sales needs
Support data-driven decision-making through reporting and marketing-related insights


Events & External Activities


Plan and coordinate events and the practical arrangements (for example invitations, registrations and communications)
Ensure high-quality execution and effective post-event marketing activities


Website & Digital Development


Coordinate and develop website content (luxidgroup.com)
Act as a project manager in website development initiatives (scope, timelines, coordination)


Sales Support & Collaboration


Monitor public tenders and identify potentially relevant opportunities
Strengthen collaboration between marketing and sales teams
Work closely with stakeholders across business units


Qualifications that ensure thriving in this role:


Experience coordinating and managing B2B marketing activities, projects or campaigns
Strong hands-on experience with HubSpot is required, including newsletters, workflows, reporting and data management.
Strong project management and coordination skills
Ability to manage multiple priorities in a dynamic environment
Excellent communication and collaboration skills
Understanding of how marketing supports sales and business growth
Fluent English (Finnish is considered an advantage)


What You’ll Gain

A supportive, highly skilled and experienced team:

At Luxid, everyone is encouraged to be themselves and bring their unique strengths to the table. Our daily work is built on close collaboration, open communication, and shared successes. We regularly exchange ideas, support each other, and celebrate achievements together.

Welcoming new team members:

New employees are warmly welcomed and quickly integrated into work. We provide a clear onboarding process and continuous support from experienced colleagues. You’ll have the opportunity to shape your role and bring in your own ideas from the start.

Opportunities for growth:

You’ll work on impactful projects in international environment, develop your skills across marketing domains, and have opportunities for continuous learning through trainings, certifications, and industry events.

Diversity and inclusion:

We value different backgrounds, perspectives, and expertise. Our team is international, English-speaking, and inclusive – and we believe diversity strengthens our work.

Wellbeing and flexibility:

We offer extensive occupational healthcare, wellness and sports benefits, and flexible remote work possibilities. Our offices are located in Helsinki, Turku, London, Austin, and Dayton. For this role, regular visits to Helsinki or Turku office are expected.

Competitive salary:

Salary starting from €4,000 per month, with the final compensation determined based on the selected candidate's experience, expertise, and overall fit for the role.
