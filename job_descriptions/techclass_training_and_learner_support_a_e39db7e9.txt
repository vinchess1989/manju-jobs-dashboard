About TechClass TechClass is a leading eLearning provider, dedicated to delivering high-quality, engaging, and accessible educational content to learners worldwide. Our mission is to empower individuals and organizations to achieve their learning and development goals through our innovative and user-friendly platform.




Job Overview We are seeking a dedicated Training & Learner Support Assistant to ensure the successful delivery of our training programmes. In this role, you will work closely with lead trainers and the programme team to assist during live sessions, facilitate engaging workshops, and support participants throughout their learning journey. By providing hands-on administrative assistance and coordinating activities, you will play a vital role in fostering an inclusive, positive, and highly engaging educational environment.




Key Responsibilities

Training Assistance: Support lead trainers during live sessions and presentations, ensuring smooth delivery and assisting with participant questions.
Workshop Facilitation: Actively facilitate group activities, workshops, and breakout sessions to keep learners engaged and on track.
Learner Support: Act as a reliable point of contact for participants, offering guidance, motivation, and practical support throughout their educational journey.
Programme Coordination: Provide comprehensive administrative assistance by coordinating learning activities, organizing event logistics, and managing programme schedules.
Community Building: Cultivate a positive, inclusive, and collaborative learning environment that drives student success and overall satisfaction.




Requirements

Educational Background: Experience in training, teaching, tutoring, or teaching assistance, with a strong ability to facilitate workshops and group discussions.
Student Success Experience: Background in learner services, academic advising, coaching, mentoring, or career guidance.
Coordination Skills: Proven experience in project coordination, programme administration, or event management, paired with excellent organizational abilities.
Communication Skills: Strong interpersonal and communication skills with the ability to connect with diverse groups of learners.
Digital Proficiency: Confidence in navigating and utilizing digital collaboration tools and modern learning platforms.
Industry Knowledge (Bonus): Familiarity or experience working in healthcare, MedTech, entrepreneurship, innovation, research, or related fields is highly advantageous.
Global Mindset: Experience thriving in multidisciplinary or international environments.
