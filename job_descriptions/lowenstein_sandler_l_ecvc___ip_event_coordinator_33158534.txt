Who We Are:

Lowenstein Sandler is a one of the most sophisticated law firms in the United States, with over 400 lawyers and 300 business services professionals serving clients from offices in New York, Palo Alto, Roseland, Salt Lake City, San Francisco, Washington, D.C., and Wilmington. We recruit candidates who seek a collaborative, entrepreneurial culture that prioritizes our clients, our colleagues, and the communities where we work and live. Committed to career development and to a diverse and inclusive workplace, the firm offers many programs and opportunities for personal and professional growth. We seek professionals for our business services group who share these values.




What You Will Do:

Under the guidance of the ECVC & IP Event Manager, this position supports the planning and execution of client events and business development initiatives for the firm’s West Coast offices, while collaborating closely with the broader ECVC & IP Events team across offices. This role combines hands-on event coordination with strategic business development support to enhance client engagement, strengthen market visibility, and support attorney relationship-building efforts. This position is San Francisco based with occasional travel to our Palo Alto office for events and will offer a hybrid remote work opportunity. This position requires regular on-site presence at events throughout the Bay Area, including Palo Alto and San Francisco, with occasional evening commitments.




Essential Job Requirements:

The ability to work and thrive in a fast-paced environment
Coordinate a wide range of events, including client programs, panels, networking receptions, and dinners
Handle event logistics from concept through post-event follow-up, including:
Venue sourcing (internal and external)
Vendor coordination (catering, A/V, rentals, etc.)
Run-of-show development and on-site execution
Create and manage event timelines, budgets, and checklists
Coordinate across internal teams (IT, Office Services, Marketing, Reception, etc.)
Attend and manage events on-site, ensuring seamless execution and high-quality client experience
List and invite management, including but not limited to:
Creating and sending invites out on behalf of key attorneys to dinners and events;
Create searches, run reports and update information in firm’s CRM system;
Research and update contact information as needed;
Coordinate with attorneys and admins to obtain lists and send invites.
Other tasks, including but not limited to:
Manage multiple projects simultaneously with precision and attention to detail;
Coordinate with building security and firm staff as needed;
Keep and maintain stock and equipment in an organized manner;
Coordinate ordering of event supplies;
Working knowledge of CRM and event management software;
Other tasks as assigned.




Business Development & Marketing Support (Integrated Focus):

Collaborate with Business Development team and attorneys to align events with client targeting
Assist with identifying target audiences and building strategic invite lists using CRM tools
Support development of event-related marketing materials (invitations, follow-ups, collateral)
Track and report on event attendance, client engagement and follow-up opportunities
Assist with post-event follow-up, including coordinating outreach and identifying business opportunities
Conduct light research on clients, industries, and prospects to support event strategy
Brainstorm ideas for new event formats that enhance market presence




Skills, Knowledge, and Abilities:

Minimum 2 years’ experience in event planning, marketing, or business development experience (professional services or law firm experience preferred)
Bachelor’s degree preferred
Strong organizational and project management skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment
Excellent written and verbal communication skills, along with strong interpersonal abilities
Ability to work confidently and professionally with attorneys, clients, executives, and external partners
Experience with CRM systems and event management platforms is a plus
Highly detail-oriented with strong execution and follow-through skills
Strategic thinker with an interest in business development and client engagement
Proven ability to build relationships with a client-service mindset
Adaptable, proactive, and solutions-oriented approach to problem-solving
Ability to work both independently and collaboratively across teams
Willingness to maintain regular on-site presence for events across the Bay Area
Flexibility to travel locally and work early mornings or evenings as needed




Office Location: San Francisco, CA (Hybrid)

Schedule: Full-time, Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM – 5:30 PM, Flexibility to work early mornings or late evenings on occasion

Travel Required: Frequent travel to the Palo Alto office and throughout the Bay Area for events




The expected salary range for candidates meeting the requirements of this position is $75,000 to $85,000. The range provided is the salary that Lowenstein Sandler in good faith believes at the time of this posting that it is willing to pay for the advertised position. Exact compensation will be determined based on individual candidate qualifications and location. Our benefits package includes coverage options for medical, dental, vision, prescription drug, life insurance, disability, FSAs, 401K, and paid time off.
