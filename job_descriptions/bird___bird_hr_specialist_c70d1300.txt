The opportunity




Are you a proactive and curious team player with a strong sense of ownership in your work? Are you interested in the legal sector and motivated by working in an international environment? If so, this could be the perfect opportunity for you.




In this role, you will have the opportunity to contribute to a wide range of HR activities and collaborate with colleagues across different teams and countries.




We are looking for an HR Specialist to join our team in Helsinki, Finland!




The firm




Bird & Bird is an international law firm with a strong focus on helping organisations navigate change, particularly in areas driven by technology and innovation. As part of our Business Services team, you will play an important role in supporting our people and operations.




At Bird & Bird, you will be trusted, supported, and encouraged to develop, while working in a collaborative and international environment alongside colleagues across our global network.




What you'll do




As an HR Specialist, you will play a key role in supporting our people operations and day-to-day HR activities. In this role, you will work in a versatile and responsible position at the heart of the organisation, where HR processes, administration, and supporting our business come together. You will be involved in a wide range of operational and developmental HR and administrative tasks, such as:




Coordinating HR processes across the employee lifecycle
Managing recruitment processes and onboarding activities
Acting as a key contact for partners and managers in day-to-day HR matters
Contributing to the development of HR practices and employee well-being initiatives
Producing internal communications and maintaining HR documentation
Collaborating with Business Services teams (Finance, Marketing, etc.) across Finland, the Nordics, and globally alongside our international HR function




About you




Ideally, you have solid experience in HR and are comfortable working with a wide range of HR processes and day-to-day responsibilities. You bring a proactive and structured way of working, strong communication skills, and the ability to support managers and collaborate across the organisation. You would be a great fit for us if you have:




a relevant degree in Human Resources (e.g. University of Applied Sciences)
~ 5 years of experience in HR, with experience in a professional services environment (e.g. a law firm) considered an asset
hands-on experience with HR processes
excellent communication skills in Finnish and English
strong organisational and prioritisation skills
a proactive, independent, and practical approach to work
an interest in exploring and developing legal tech and AI-driven solutions




What will you be offered?




You will work in a collaborative and supportive environment with skilled and dedicated colleagues. You will have the opportunity to develop your expertise and contribute to meaningful HR work in a professional services environment.




We offer you a full-time position with competitive employment benefits. Salary will be based on your experience and skills and will be discussed with selected candidates.




The role is expected to start as soon as a suitable candidate is found.




Interested in joining us?




If this opportunity excites you, please submit your CV and application through our recruitment portal by 4 September 2026. In your application, we would like to hear about your expectations for the position, how your previous experience would be of value, and your reasons for wanting to work in our firm. We will review applications on a rolling basis and conduct interviews in an agile manner.




If you have any questions, please contact Partner Sakari Halonen by email sakari.halonen(at)twobirds.com.




Bird & Bird is an international law firm with a focus on organizations being changed by or creating technology and the digital world. We combine exceptional legal expertise with deep industry knowledge and refreshingly creative thinking to help our clients achieve their commercial goals. We have over 3600 employees in 34 offices across Europe, the Middle East, Asia-Pacific and North America and a strong presence in the Nordics.




Bird & Bird is committed to creating and sustaining a diverse and inclusive environment. We know an inclusive culture makes us more innovative and agile. We’re committed to treating everyone with kindness and respect, to creating an environment where everyone feels empowered and supported to succeed.




To find out more, please visit our social media channels and website www.twobirds.com/fi.
