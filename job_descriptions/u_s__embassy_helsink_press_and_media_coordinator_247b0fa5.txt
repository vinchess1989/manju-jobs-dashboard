Työpaikkakuvaus

U.S. Embassy in Helsinki is seeking a Press and Media Coordinator to join our Public Diplomacy Section

This is a fixed-term contract to substitute for a colleague on parental leave (until mid-June 2027).

The Public Diplomacy Section works to strengthen understanding of U.S. policies in Finland by engaging with local media, countering misinformation, and supporting transparent, accurate reporting. We are looking for a motivated professional who can monitor Finnish media, advise embassy leadership, prepare press materials, and maintain strong relationships with journalists and media outlets.

In this role, you will help shape how U.S. priorities are communicated in Finland, support media logistics for high-level visits, brief embassy personnel, and contribute to strategic planning for public outreach.

Key Responsibilities

Monitor Finnish media and provide clear summaries and analysis for embassy leadership

Draft press releases, background papers, briefing materials, and other content in English and Finnish

Prepare and coach embassy personnel for media engagements, including background materials and interview guidance

Advise and coordinate with embassy colleagues on media strategies and responses to sensitive issues

Shape the embassy’s media engagement approach and maintain relationships with journalists, editors, and major outlets

Plan and coordinate Finnish and U.S. media coverage and logistics for high‑level visits

Support crisis communications and may serve as an embassy spokesperson when designated

Manage the press/media program budget, oversee related grants as the Grants Officer Representative, and maintain program records

Provide occasional backup for the Digital Production Coordinator, supporting content development for embassy platforms

Requirements

Bachelor’s degree in: Journalism, Communications, International Relations, Political Science, or Economics.

At least four years (4) of relevant experience in media, journalism, communications, public affairs, or related fields.

Fluent in English and Finnish.

Excellent writing and communication skills in both languages.

Strong understanding of Finnish media, journalism standards, and audience trends.

Ability to analyze media content, identify trends, and provide strategic recommendations.

Ability to build strong customer relationships and communicate effectively across cultures.

Strong organizational skills and ability to manage multiple tasks.

Familiarity with digital tools, media platforms, and content production.

Ability to travel within Finland for media engagement activities.

Additional Information

Full-time position: 40 hours per week

Fixed-term contract from October 2026 until mid-June 2027

Salary: 51 468 EUR per year

Benefits

Bi-weekly salary payments

Paid Finnish and U.S. public holidays (20 days per year)

On-site gym

Training opportunities & personal development

Required Documents for the Application:

Proof of right to work in Finland (passport or residency permit)

Copy of Bachelor’s degree diploma or transcript

Please submit your application through our careers site by the indicated closing date. Make sure all required documents are included. We can only review complete applications.

We look forward to your application!
