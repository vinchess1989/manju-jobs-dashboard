Role Responsibilities


Leverage experience in business operations to evaluate and test workflows, systems, and processes.
Assess and validate project management, CRM, and productivity tools using business data.
Identify opportunities for process automation and workflow optimization, providing detailed feedback.
Analyze business data to inform tool performance and usability.
Collaborate with teams by documenting findings and offering actionable recommendations.
Engage in communication to share insights and feedback on business systems.
Contribute to AI training datasets using operational expertise.


Requirements


Proven experience actively operating a business with hands-on involvement in business systems.
Deep expertise in business operations, project management, and workflow optimization.
Extensive use and evaluation of CRM systems, SaaS tools, and process automation platforms.
Strong data analysis skills with an ability to draw actionable insights.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills, focusing on clarity and thoroughness.
Demonstrated ability to document, evaluate, and improve real-world business processes.
Access to real operational workflows and data for authentic testing and feedback.
