We are representing a leading $8Bn+ AUM alternative investment manager in Denver in its search for a Counsel / Senior Counsel.




This is a broad in-house opportunity for an investment management attorney to join a lean legal and compliance team and work closely with senior leadership across the firm. The role will support a range of private fund matters, including fund formation, investor documentation, regulatory matters, and ongoing fund operations across multiple investment strategies.

The team is looking for a practical, commercially minded attorney who can take ownership of day-to-day matters, work closely with the investment and investor relations teams, and provide clear legal guidance without overcomplicating issues.




Responsibilities

Support fund formation, structuring, and ongoing fund matters across open-end and closed-end strategies
Draft, review, and negotiate LPAs, side letters, subscription agreements, PPMs, and related fund documentation
Advise on investor negotiations and ongoing investor-related matters
Support regulatory and compliance matters relating to private funds and registered investment advisers
Advise on AML/KYC matters and related investor onboarding requirements
Work closely with outside counsel on fund launches, amendments, and other fund-related matters
Partner with investment, investor relations, finance, and senior leadership on day-to-day legal matters
Provide practical, business-focused advice across a broad range of legal and regulatory issues
Support broader corporate, governance, and firmwide legal matters as needed




Experience

Juris Doctor (JD) degree and admission to a U.S. state bar
Approximately 4 to 6 years of legal experience, ideally within a leading investment management or private funds practice
Strong experience with private fund formation and ongoing fund matters
Experience drafting and negotiating LPAs, side letters, subscription agreements, and related fund documentation
Familiarity with Investment Advisers Act and private fund regulatory matters
Experience across multiple private fund strategies preferred
Prior in-house experience helpful but not required
Strong drafting, negotiation, and issue-spotting skills
Practical, business-minded approach with the ability to operate effectively in a lean environment




Location & Compensation

Denver, Colorado
Five days per week in office
Relocation support available
Base salary: $225,000-$250,000
Total compensation: approximately $320,000-$350,000
