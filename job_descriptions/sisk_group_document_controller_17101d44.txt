We are seeking a Document Controller to join our project team in Finland, to support the effective management, distribution and quality control of all project documentation.

You will work closely with the design, engineering and site management teams to maintain accurate records, ensure version control and manage compliance with company and project requirements.

John Sisk & Son Ltd is one of Ireland’s leading construction and engineering businesses, delivering landmark projects across Ireland, the UK and Europe.

Document Management


Validate, check, and curate all incoming and outgoing project documentation, ensuring proper receipt, classification, verification, registration, distribution, and retrieval. 
Establish and maintain central site files, uncontrolled drawings, registers, and collate Operation & Maintenance, Health & Safety, Quality, Environmental, and BCAR files. 
Configure and implement change control processes within the CDE. 
Prepare and configure distribution matrices, status reports, and workflows for sample submissions, TQs, RFIs, and other reportable data in compliance with project requirements. 
Attend project meetings to engage with the project team and stakeholders on documentation and IM matters. 



Continuous Improvement


Identify opportunities for improvement, reduction of waste, and mitigation of risks related to document management and information workflows. 
Cover duties of other Document Controllers when required, e.g., during holidays or sickness. 
Support the ISM in maintaining compliance with company-wide DPD procedures and standards. 



Training & Support


Host training sessions for new users of VFP and FV, either individually or in groups. 
Act as the main point of contact for support and coaching of project users, including client teams, design teams, subcontractors, and supply chain partners. 
Assist in developing project file naming protocols and workflows with guidance from the Information Systems Manager (ISM). 
Continuously provide guidance to project teams to ensure strict adherence to DPD protocols. 



Essential


Experience working on projects with large volumes of data and information from multiple sources. 
Ability to communicate effectively with, and educate, all stakeholders on DPD protocols. 
Experience dealing with multidisciplinary design teams, client teams, subcontractors, and supply chain partners. 
Ability and willingness to train and mentor others. 
Knowledge of file naming conventions and standards
Organisational, prioritisation, multitasking, and time management skills. 
Analytical thinker, diligent, and self-motivated. 
Excellent document preparation, formatting, and troubleshooting skills. 



Desirable


Interest in project delivery processes, project administration, and the wider construction industry. 



Essential


Advanced user of Microsoft Office suite. 
Understanding of ISO 19650, BS 1192, and PAS 1192 standards. 
Advanced user of VFP, FV, or similar CDE / DFT platforms. 



Desirable


Experience in configuring and managing systems in compliance with ISO 19650. 
Advanced user of Field View (FV) or similar digital field tools. 



Sisk is a family-owned business with over 160 years of history built on trust, teamwork and innovation. We offer a supportive environment, opportunities for career progression and the chance to contribute to some of Ireland’s most significant construction projects.
