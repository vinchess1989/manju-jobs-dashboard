CapMan is a leading Nordic private assets management and investment company with a large international network. Rooted in the Nordics, we have successfully invested across a wide range of private asset sectors for over 35 years. We invest in real estate and infrastructure assets, natural capital and businesses. As active owners we create growth journeys, success stories, and turnarounds, improve Nordic infrastructure, strengthen societies and make businesses more sustainable.

Our teams are locally based and guided by Nordic values, committed to honesty, teamwork, and action. Driven by our passion for growing businesses, driving sustainable change, and helping to build the society of tomorrow. CapMan employs around 250 professionals in Helsinki, Jyväskylä, Stockholm, Copenhagen, Oslo, London, Düsseldorf, Luxembourg and Paris. We are listed on Nasdaq Helsinki since 2001.

This is an opportunity for you to join CapMan in the role as Sustainability Reporting Specialist as part of the Sustainability team.

About the team

The CapMan Sustainability team consists of seven sustainability professionals based in Helsinki and Stockholm. Most team members work with sustainability value creation and are specialised in our respective asset classes and aligned to one investment team. The Sustainability Reporting Specialist is dedicated to sustainability data and reporting across CapMan Plc and the investment teams. The Sustainability team is responsible for developing and driving the ambitious sustainability strategy across CapMan.

We are now looking to recruit a full-time Sustainability Reporting Specialist to join our Helsinki office.


About the role

You will have the opportunity to run the sustainability reporting for CapMan Plc and across our investment teams and portfolio companies as well as assisting the sustainability team in their daily work. This position provides an ideal opportunity to develop your project management, reporting and data skills in real assets and private equity.

As a Sustainability Reporting Specialist, you will be responsible for various activities related to sustainability data and reporting, including:

Project managing the sustainability statement in CapMan Plc annual report, CapMan Investment Sustainability Report as well as voluntary reporting, such as PRI, UNGC and TNFD

Managing investor and industry data requests, including investor questionnaires and due diligence requests, EDCI and assisting with GRESB reporting

Running the CapMan portfolio company data collection and coordinating with Sustainability team, portfolio companies and the ESG platform, including portfolio company training and assistance, ongoing portfolio company data collection and calculation support, data quality checks and platform corrections

Annually updating/reviewing the portfolio company data points

Assisting the investment teams with annual SFDR disclosures and periodic fund reporting

Responsible for ESG platform administration, including data quality and consistency controls and user support and troubleshooting

Sustainability data management (PLC & Group reporting support)

CapMan Plc GHG data collection, calculation and quality checks

Assisting the Sustainability team in their daily work and in sustainability development projects

Who you are

To be successful in this role we believe that you;

Have strong project management skills

Have a relevant academic degree, for example in Sustainability, Finance, Law, Economics, or related fields

Have between 2-6 years prior experience from in sustainability reporting, preferably in private markets investments or finance

Demonstrate strong drive, initiative, and problem-solving skills, along with a desire for continuous learning

Have an analytical and detail-oriented mindset with strong communication skills, both written and verbal

Proficiency in English and Finnish required
