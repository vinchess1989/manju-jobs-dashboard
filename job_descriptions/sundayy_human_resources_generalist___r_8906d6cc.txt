About The Company

For over 50 years, Modernistic has been a leading provider of exceptional cleaning and restoration services throughout Michigan. As a family-owned business with six locations, we pride ourselves on our commitment to quality, customer satisfaction, and fostering a positive work environment. Our company culture emphasizes going above and beyond for our clients and co-workers, investing in our people, and promoting from within to cultivate long-term careers. With a focus on continuous growth and community service, Modernistic remains dedicated to delivering outstanding service while creating a supportive and dynamic workplace for our team members.

About The Role

Modernistic is seeking a dedicated HR Generalist to join our rapidly expanding team. This key position will be based out of our Grand Rapids or Plainwell office, depending on the candidate's location, and will serve all six of our Michigan locations. The HR Generalist will oversee all human resources functions across the company, including talent acquisition, employee relations, benefits administration, and compliance. This role requires a people-focused, organized individual with a passion for building strong teams and fostering a positive workplace culture. The successful candidate will act as a primary point of contact for HR-related matters, lead recruiting efforts, and support leadership in developing HR strategies aligned with company goals. This is an excellent opportunity for someone looking to make a significant impact within a growing organization that values its employees and promotes internal advancement.

Qualifications


3+ years of HR management or generalist experience
2+ years of recruiting experience
Strong knowledge of Michigan and federal employment laws
Proven ability to manage multiple priorities effectively
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
High level of discretion and confidentiality
Strong organizational and problem-solving skills
Self-motivated, detail-oriented, and results-driven
Valid Michigan driver’s license with a good driving record
Ability to pass a drug test and background check
Experience in the service industry is a plus
Knowledge of the cleaning and restoration industry is a plus
HR certification (PHR/SPHR/SHRM-CP/SHRM-SCP) is a plus


Responsibilities


Oversee benefit administration and act as the primary contact with benefit vendors
Handle employee issue resolution and provide guidance to leadership and staff
Ensure OSHA and safety compliance across all locations
Maintain OSHA logs, manage workers’ compensation claims, and FMLA leave processes
Gather company records as needed for project bids and audits
Set recruiting priorities, manage recruiting budget, and act as a recruiter when necessary
Develop, implement, and maintain HR policies, processes, and best practices
Ensure compliance with employment laws and maintain accurate employee documentation
Partner with leadership to enhance talent retention and employee engagement initiatives
Support onboarding and orientation for new hires
Track and analyze HR metrics to measure success and identify areas for improvement
Travel to other Michigan locations as needed to provide HR support and leadership

Pet insurance plans to keep your furry friends healthy and protected

Flexible work arrangements, including remote work options where appropriate
