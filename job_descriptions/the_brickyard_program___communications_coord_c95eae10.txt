Company Description The Brickyard is a national civic and service organization founded by Veterans and dedicated to strengthening communities across the United States. Formerly known as +More Perfect Union, the organization focuses on bringing people together through social gatherings, community service, and civic engagement. The Brickyard works to bridge divides and foster meaningful connections among individuals from diverse backgrounds. Team members collaborate to design and support programs that cultivate civic responsibility, local leadership, and a shared sense of purpose.

Role Description The Program & Communications Coordinator is a full-time remote role responsible for supporting the planning, execution, and communication of The Brickyard’s civic and service initiatives. The Program & Communications Coordinator is a dynamic, mission-driven professional responsible for coordinating program operations while managing organizational communications and digital engagement. This role serves as the operational backbone for program delivery and helps amplify the organization's impact through compelling storytelling, marketing, and stakeholder engagement.

In a small nonprofit, this individual wears many hats and collaborates across programs, fundraising, operations, and leadership. The ideal candidate is highly organized, tech-savvy, an excellent writer, and passionate about building community through effective communications and exceptional program execution.

This entry-level, non-supervisory position will be responsible for managing social media community management and content, various administrative tasks, tracking the budget, and measuring overall impact in direct support of the day-to-day programming. The Program & Communications Coordinator will report directly to the Sr. Program Manager with a matrixed working relationship with Marketing.




Qualifications

Required:

2-3 years of experience in nonprofit programs, communications, marketing, event planning, project coordination or a related field
Exceptional written and verbal communication skills
Strong project management and organizational abilities with excellent attention to detail
Experience managing and prioritizing multiple projects simultaneously in a fast-paced environment while meeting deadlines within budget
Proficiency with Google Workspace.
Experience using Canva for graphic design and presentation development.
Experience managing professional social media platforms, including but not limited to Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, and TikTok, and scheduling tools (Sprout Social, Hootsuite, or similar).
Experience working with CRM systems, preferably Salesforce, Airtable, HubSpot or similar platforms.
Experience working independently while collaborating effectively across teams.
Embody The Brickyard's Community Agreement
Receive and give feedback as a way to grow professionally




Preferred requirements:

Veteran, military family member or experience working with the military and Veteran community 
Experience working in a startup, nonprofit, civic engagement, education, public service, or mission-driven organization(s). 
Familiarity with email marketing platforms such as Iterable, MailChimp, HubSpot
Experience supporting virtual events and webinars using Google Meet or similar platforms 
Knowledge of basic website content management systems 
Experience with data collection, reporting, and program evaluation
