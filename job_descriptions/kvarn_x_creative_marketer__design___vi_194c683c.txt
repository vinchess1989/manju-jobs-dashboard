Räätälöi ansioluetteloni

Kvarn X is one of the fastest-growing fintechs in Finland and one of the few fintech platforms in the EU licensed across the full range of investment and financial services, all delivered through a modern cross-asset financial app. Our vision is a single app where customers manage their entire financial life: investments, savings, wealth management, payments and everyday finances, side by side, on regulated and supervised infrastructure. AI is reshaping how investment information is consumed and how financial services are personalised, distributed and delivered across channels. We are building for that shift.




About the role

Our marketing is run by a small, hands-on growth team that moves fast and tests a lot, and right now the creative side needs a person of its own. Our main audience is Finnish, so you'll need to know the market you're creating for.

We're looking for a creative marketer who can take a campaign idea and turn it into ads people actually stop for: static visuals, short-form video, motion graphics, and everything in between. You'll work directly with the person running growth and paid media, so you'll see how your creatives perform, iterate on what works, and kill what doesn't. No layers of approval between you and the results.




This is a hands-on role. You'll be making things every week, not briefing an agency.




What you'll do

Concept, design and produce ad creatives for Meta, TikTok, YouTube and display – both static and video
Edit short-form video and build motion graphics for paid social and in-app use
Create landing page visuals, social content, and brand assets for Kvarn X
Use AI image and video tools as a natural part of your production workflow to test more variations, faster
Read performance data together with the growth lead and turn learnings into the next round of creatives
Help keep the Kvarn X visual identity consistent as we grow




What we're looking for

A few years of experience producing creative for paid social
Strong visual design skills and hands-on video editing / motion ability
Real production experience with AI creative tools (image, video, or both) – not just experiments
An understanding of what makes a performance ad work: hooks, formats, pacing, testing
Ability to work independently, own your output end to end, and move quickly
Fluent Finnish is a must – you'll be creating for the Finnish market and need to understand its audience, tone and cultural references
Working proficiency in English
Interest in investing or personal finance is a plus, but great creative work matters more




Why Kvarn X

Your work goes live fast and you see the results – real budgets, real feedback loops
Freedom to shape how creative is done here, with the tools and AI workflows you think are best
A small team where your voice carries weight
Hybrid setup based in Helsinki




We're open to either a full-time hire or an ongoing freelance arrangement. Let us know which setup suits you best.
