Head of Finance & Administration @Frictape Ltd | Finance Leadership | Business Development | Operations & Cross-Functional Collaboration

Location: Vantaa, Finland (headquarters) 

Work time: Part time 2-3days per week (Tue-Thu) | Fixed term

Start: As soon as possible




We’re growing our finance and back-office team as Frictape continues to expand internationally. This is an entry-level role, so you do not need years of experience. What matters most is curiosity, motivation to build your career in an international team, and the ability to learn quickly in a changing environment.




Most of our customers are based outside Finland and are often large international companies. That means the work is rarely one-size-fits-all — different cultures, ways of working and customer needs bring plenty of variety. Our customers mainly operate in offshore oil & gas and offshore wind, giving you a window into both established energy industries and the newer solutions shaping the future.




You would be an important part of our head office team, working closely with the Head of Finance & Administration and supporting sales, R&D and other operations when needed.




What will your tasks look like:

Supporting accounts payable and accounts receivable processes
Managing vendor and customer information
Supporting internal reporting and performance tracking
Customer communication: Quotations, order handling
Registering incoming product samples
Supporting day-to-day administrative activities across different business functions

The role is varied, so it suits someone who enjoys learning new things and supporting different parts of the business.




What we are looking for:

Positive, open-minded team player with “get it done” attitude
Fluent in English
Accuracy and attention to detail
Ability to organize and prioritize tasks
Willingness to learn financial and administrative processes
Experience with D365 Business Central and Power BI is considered an advantage




What we offer you:

Practical experience across finance, sales and administration
International working environment
Direct cooperation with different business functions
Opportunity to develop the role as the company grows




If this sounds like a role for you, we ask you to complete the Culture Index Survey so we can see how it may apply to your work-related needs as well as our company requirements.




Please click the following link to complete the survey: https://go.cultureindex.com/p/azCQGmJkbEMzQ




To apply, complete the survey and send your application, CV and salary request by 13th of September to our Head of Finance & Administration – Tiia Väike (tiia.vaike@frictape.com)
