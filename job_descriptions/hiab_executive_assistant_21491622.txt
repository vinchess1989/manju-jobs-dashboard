Your mission

We are seeking a highly organized and proactive Executive Assistant to support our Chief Financial Officer at Hiab, a stock listed company. This role provides essential administrative and coordination support to our CFO while working closely with our Investor Relations team to ensure smooth execution of financial operations and investor engagement activities.

You’ll manage complex schedules, coordinate travel logistics, prepare materials for meetings, and handle confidential information. This position requires strong organizational skills, discretion, and the ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced environment.

Key Responsibilities

CFO Administrative Support


Manage the CFO’s calendar, scheduling meetings and coordinating appointments
Coordinate communication between the CFO and internal/external stakeholders
Handle travel arrangements and expense reporting
Maintain organized filing systems for important documents and information
Governance of monthly and quarterly meetings for various CFO functions, including agenda drafting, action follow-up and document timeline
Oversee end to end ownership of the Board Audit and Risk Management meeting cycle.


Investor Relations Coordination


Coordinate logistics for quarterly earnings cycles, investor conferences, and roadshows
Manage travel arrangements for CFO/ CEO and IR team members attending investor events
Maintain synchronized calendars between CFO/ CEO and IR department
Track and organize investor meeting materials and documentation
Lead the practical execution of the Annual General Meeting, coordination across several teams and manage all logistics needed (venue, catering, suppliers)


Operational Support


Develop efficient processes for recurring administrative tasks
Build productive working relationships with other administrative staff and department contacts
Offer support for HQ Real estate matters
Ensure compliance with company policies regarding confidential information
Identify opportunities to improve administrative processes.

Calm and efficient under pressure with competing priorities
Quick learner who adapts easily to new systems and processes
Reliable and trustworthy professional.
