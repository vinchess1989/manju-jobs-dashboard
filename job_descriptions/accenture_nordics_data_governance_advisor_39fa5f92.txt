Accenture is looking for a Data Governance Advisor to join AI & Data practice in Helsinki.

Are you ready to take your expertise to the next level with consulting? Do you have passion for helping organizations take the full benefits of data, analytics and artificial intelligence? Then a role as a Data Governance Advisor in our AI&Data practice is a great fit.

This is an extraordinary opportunity to build a rewarding career and play a key role in transforming organizations and communities. Working in highly collaborative teams for world-leading clients, we’ll nurture your talent in an inclusive culture that values diversity.

Job description:



Define and implement data governance frameworks and strategies aligned with business objectives, ensuring data quality, integrity, and regulatory compliance.

Advise clients on governance policies, standards, and operating models, including data ownership, stewardship, and accountability structures.

Establish data quality management practices, including standards, controls, and monitoring across critical data domains.

Lead the development of data policies, documentation, and metadata management, improving data discoverability, lineage, and consistency.

Support risk, compliance, and data privacy initiatives, ensuring alignment with regulatory requirements.

Act as a trusted advisor, translating governance challenges into practical, business-driven solutions.

Contribute to data strategy and transformation programmes, embedding governance across data, analytics, and AI initiatives.



What’s in it for you:



A unique opportunity to build deep expertise in data governance, strategy, and consulting, working on impactful transformation programmes.

Exposure to leading governance frameworks, tooling ecosystems, and global best practices.

The chance to work closely with clients, influencing strategic decisions and enterprise-wide data agendas.

Access to Accenture’s global network, including methodologies, assets, and domain experts in data, AI, and compliance.

A collaborative and inclusive working environment with highly skilled colleagues.

Strong focus on work-life balance, wellbeing, and continuous learning.

A dedicated mentor to support your professional and personal growth.

Competitive compensation and benefits package.



Who are we looking for:



a few years of experience in data governance, data strategy, or data consulting.

Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a business-first mindset.

Ability to translate governance concepts into practical, implementable solutions.

Excellent communication and presentation skills in English.

Proficiency in Finnish.





Nice to have:



Master’s degree in a relevant field.

Experience in Financial Services or regulated industries is considered an advantage.



What next?

We would love to hear from you! Please submit your CV and application to take the next step in your career.
