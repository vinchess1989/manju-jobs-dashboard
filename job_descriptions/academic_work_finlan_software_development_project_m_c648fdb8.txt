About the role

As a Software Development Project Manager, you will lead key R&D initiatives within Mirka Industrial Automation, where software, robotics, process know-how and surface finishing expertise come together to create next-generation automation solutions for global industrial customers.

You are offered the opportunity to work with world-class and unique automation technologies in a stable yet innovative family-owned environment.

Work tasks

This role involves managing the full lifecycle of complex software and automation projects, bridging the gap between technical execution and business goals to bring innovative products to the shelf.
- Lead software development projects from initial concept to final market release
- Coordinate multidisciplinary technical teams within a matrix organization
- Oversee the renewal and modernization of existing automation products
- Manage the end-to-end development of a completely new software product
- Apply agile methodologies to ensure efficient project delivery
- Facilitate communication between technical teams and internal stakeholders
- Ensure software integrations align with industrial automation standards

We are looking for

- At least 3-5 years of project management experience in technology-driven environments
- Solid understanding of software development processes and agile methodologies
- Experience coordinating multidisciplinary technical teams
- Good understanding of industrial automation systems and software integration
- Fluent English communication skills

It is meritorious if you have
- Knowledge of computer vision, robotics, or scanning technologies
- Experience with AI-based automation solutions
- Background in customer-oriented project implementations
- Relevant technical degree
- Proficiency in Finnish

Our recruitment process
This recruitment process is handled by Academic Work and it is our clients wish that all questions regarding the position is directed to Academic Work.

Our selection process is continuous and the advert may close before the recruitment process is completed if we have moved forward to the next phase. The process includes two tests: one personality test and one cognitive test. The tests are tools to find the right talent for the right position, to enable equality, diversity, and a fair process.
