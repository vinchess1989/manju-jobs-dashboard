Programme Responsible Editor & Regulatory Compliance Manager

Part-time | Estonia | Hybrid/Remote within Estonia

MAXSTREAM MEDIA OÜ is seeking a responsible and detail-oriented professional to join our television operations as a Programme Responsible Editor & Regulatory Compliance Manager.

MAXSTREAM MEDIA OÜ is an Estonia-based media company developing a portfolio of thematic television channels distributed through IPTV, cable, satellite, web/OTT platforms and mobile applications.

Role purpose

The selected person will exercise day-to-day editorial oversight over the television programme schedules operated by MAXSTREAM MEDIA OÜ.

Using the company’s internal Programme Approval Portal, the Editor will review future schedules and playlists prepared for each television channel and will have genuine authority to:

approve programme schedules;
approve schedules subject to required amendments;
request changes before transmission;
reject schedules or individual programme items that do not meet applicable editorial or compliance requirements.

Only approved schedules may be released for technical playout.

Main responsibilities

Review weekly programme schedules for the assigned television channels.
Review programme metadata, including:
programme title and category;
genre;
age classification;
proposed transmission time;
country of origin;
European-work status;
language and subtitle information;
rights-clearance status;
accessibility information.
Verify compliance with:
the channel profile;
applicable licence conditions;
programme-structure commitments;
protection-of-minors requirements;
internal editorial and compliance procedures.
Approve, conditionally approve or reject weekly schedules.
Provide clear written reasons when requesting amendments or rejecting content.
Review and approve material schedule changes or emergency replacements before transmission.
Monitor monthly programme-structure and compliance reports.
Maintain accurate and auditable editorial decision records.
Report material compliance concerns to the Management Board.
Support the preparation of responses or documentation requested by the Estonian regulatory authority, where required.

Internal approval process

Programme schedules are reviewed through MAXSTREAM MEDIA OÜ’s internal Programme Approval Portal.

The system displays:

future schedules and playlists;
individual films, episodes, trailers and promotional clips;
age ratings and proposed transmission times;
programme categories and channel-profile indicators;
rights-clearance information;
compliance alerts;
programme-structure percentages;
version history.

For each weekly schedule, the Editor selects one of the following decisions:

Approved
Approved with required amendments
Rejected

After every approval, the system generates a version-controlled PDF approval certificate and sends it automatically to designated editorial, compliance and archive email accounts.

Any later amendment creates a new version and requires a new approval. Previous approval records remain preserved.

Workload

The position is part-time.

Expected average workload:

approximately 8–12 hours per week during normal operations;
additional flexibility may be required during launch periods, regulatory reviews or significant programme changes.

The main review cycle is weekly. Material amendments are reviewed when required, and cumulative compliance checks are carried out monthly.

Candidate profile

We are looking for a person who:

is legally resident and based in Estonia;
has strong attention to detail;
is comfortable reviewing structured programme information;
can make independent and documented decisions;
has good written English;
is able to work with web-based internal systems;
understands the importance of age classification, programme scheduling and regulatory compliance;
can communicate clearly with international programme and technical teams.

Previous experience in television, media, broadcasting, journalism, audiovisual regulation, compliance or programme scheduling is an advantage, but suitable candidates can receive internal training.

Important

This is not a nominal appointment.

The selected person will have genuine authority to approve, request amendments to or reject programme schedules before transmission.

The role does not include preparing the original schedules, technical playout, video editing, subtitling, sales, accounting or rights negotiations.

What we offer

Flexible part-time work.
Hybrid or remote work within Estonia.
Structured internal approval system.
Training regarding the company’s television channels, internal procedures and compliance workflow.
Long-term cooperation with an international media operation.
Workload suitable for a media professional, compliance specialist, graduate or early-career candidate seeking flexible employment.

MAXSTREAM MEDIA OÜ on Eestis registreeritud meediaettevõte, mis arendab temaatiliste telekanalite portfelli. Telekanaleid levitatakse IPTV, kaabeltelevisiooni, satelliidi, veebi- ja OTT-platvormide ning mobiilirakenduste kaudu.

nõuda enne edastamist muudatusi;
lükata tagasi saatekava või üksik programm, mis ei vasta toimetuslikele või regulatiivsetele nõuetele.

Tehnilisse väljastusse võib edastada ainult kinnitatud saatekava.

Kinnitatud

Tagasi lükatud

Oluline teave

Tegemist ei ole üksnes formaalse nimetamisega.

Valitud isikul on tegelik õigus saatekavasid enne edastamist kinnitada, nõuda nende muutmist või need tagasi lükata.

Ametikoht ei hõlma esialgsete saatekavade koostamist, tehnilist väljastust, videotöötlust, subtiitrite valmistamist, müüki, raamatupidamist ega edastusõiguste läbirääkimisi.
