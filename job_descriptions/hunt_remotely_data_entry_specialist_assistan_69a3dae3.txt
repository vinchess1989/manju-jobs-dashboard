We are seeking a Remote Data Entry Specialist - Assistant Administrator to support data management and administrative operations. This role is ideal for detail-oriented individuals comfortable handling both data entry and general administrative support.

Job Responsibilities


Data Entry: Input and maintain data across spreadsheets and internal systems, verify accuracy, and manage digital files.

Quality Control: Maintain standardized formats and perform routine checks to ensure data consistency.


Required Qualifications


Strong attention to detail and accuracy.
Proficiency with Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets.
Excellent organizational and time management skills.
Clear written communication and ability to follow structured instructions.
Comfortable working independently in a remote environment.
