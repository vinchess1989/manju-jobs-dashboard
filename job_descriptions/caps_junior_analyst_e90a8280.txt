Junior Analyst, CaPS – Join an extraordinary team and network!

 

We combine a purchasing power of private equity-owned portfolio companies across IT, travel, logistics, marketing, and facilities into framework agreements that save our members around 20 % on indirect spend. Today, the CaPS network spans roughly 350 companies and close to 100,000 employees across Finland, Sweden, and the Baltics.

And we're just getting started!

 

Backed by Fidelio, one of Europe's top preforming  private equity investors, we've entered an exciting new chapter, expanding into the Netherlands and Denmark, with one clear ambition: to become Europe's leading procurement network for private equity and investment firms by 2030.

Curious to learn more? Visit www.caps.fi.

 

 

Your chance to grow fast — from day one




We're looking for a Junior Analyst ready to jump in, take on real responsibility, and grow alongside a high-performing team with a strong proven track record.

If you're driven, curious, and want a front-row seat to a fast-scaling international business — this is your opening.

 

Responsibilities

In this role you will take part in different areas of CaPS services. Your responsibilities include:

Conducting cost saving analyses for potential CaPS member companies
Supporting the onboarding of new member companies
Preparing materials for meetings
Collaborating closely with CaPS members and vendors in a fast-paced environment
Supporting CaPS team members and business operations across different ongoing tasks and development projects and contributing to a positive team culture

 

 

Who we are looking for

We are looking for driven and curious individuals who get things done. We value a collaborative can-do attitude, willingness to support our team and ability to connect with different stakeholders.

 

Successful candidates typically have:
You can manage many priorities in a fast-paced environment — and stay precise while doing it
You have a commercial mindset and care about team success
Excellent communication skills in Finnish and English (both required). Swedish is a plus.
Fluency with Excel and PowerPoint
A hard-working, energetic team-player mindset
Adaptability, learning orientation and strong self-leadership
You've shown ambition somewhere in your life — competitive sports, academics, or another field where you've pushed to excel
A degree or ongoing studies in e.g. economics or engineering, with a strong academic track record

 

 

What is in it for you

You will join a team with 21 members in Finland, Sweden and Netherlands. Cultural characteristics that describe our team are passion, commitment, intensity, team effort and laughter. This isn't a role where you sit on the sidelines. You'll be on the front line of a fast-growing international business, where your work has a direct, visible impact on CaPS and its members. With good results you will quickly gain more responsibilities and the chance to drive your own projects. For committed top performers, the path can lead far, all the way to partner possibilities.




We offer you a unique opportunity to work in an exciting industry with great colleagues and a low hierarchy culture at our office located in the heart of Helsinki. As a team member, you will enjoy our comprehensive personnel benefits. The position is a fixed-term traineeship contract of 6–12 months starting in September/October 2026. For exceptional performers the traineeship may lead to a permanent position.

 

Ready to apply?

If you feel that you are what we are looking for, apply by 27 August by emailing your CV and short cover letter to recruitment@caps.fi with the subject line “CaPS Junior Analyst Application”. Please act swiftly as the position will be filled as soon as the right candidate is found.  

For additional information about the position, please contact Heta Korpivaara at +358 405100343 August

10th between 15.00-16.00 or August 17th between 15:00-16:00.

 

Meet the team!

At the end of August, we'll be holding a relaxed group run event. The plan is to go for a short and easy jog, followed by lunch, where we'll tell you more about CaPS and our plans. Further details will be sent by email directly to those selected from the applicants. We are looking forward to meeting you and having a fun event!
