Reservations & Coordination Specialist 

Rovaniemi, Finland (on-site) 

Lapland Private designs fully personalized travel experiences in Lapland for international clientele. Our itineraries range from mid-range to completely private luxury travel, all united with the same passion for seamless Arctic travel. We work in a small, high-trust team where every person carries real responsibility from day one, not a narrow slice of one. 

We're looking for someone to own the operational core of how our clients experience Lapland: reservations, logistics, and day-to-day coordination with our network of trusted local partners. You'll report directly to our Sales Director. This is a role for someone who wants the whole picture, not just their piece of it. If you've been doing excellent work in the travel or tourism industry without the room to grow further, this is that room. 




What you'll own: 

Managing reservations and bookings across a portfolio of premium partners and services. Coordinating logistics so that every detail of a client's trip holds together, before arrival and in real time. Acting as a direct point of contact for partners and, where needed, for clients. Keeping our internal systems and schedules accurate and current. 




What you bring: 

Excellent written and spoken English, non-negotiable. French is a genuine advantage and will be considered a strong plus, but the role is not French-dependent. You come from the travel or tourism industry, with genuine working knowledge of Lapland as a destination. We don't have the runway to train someone on the region from scratch. Sharp organizational instincts and comfort holding several moving parts without dropping any of them. 




What this is: 

A permanent, Rovaniemi-based position with a small, senior team. This is not a remote role. Direct exposure to how a premium travel operation actually runs, not a call-center version of it.Competitive salary and good benefits. Genuine room to grow as the company scales toward its next stage. 

Start: as soon as possible. This position will close as soon as we find the right candidate, so early applications are encouraged. 




Please send your CV and cover letter to janir@laplandprivate.com. Due to volume, only applications relevant to the role will be contacted following review.
