Executive Administrator (Chief of Staff Office to CEO)
Finland (Onsite)

 

The Chief of Staff Office supports the CEO and leadership team by ensuring smooth day-to-day operations and effective coordination across priorities. This is a fast-paced, high-visibility environment where organisation, responsiveness, and attention to detail are critical.

We are looking for a  Executive Administrator to support the Chief of Staff to the CEO. This role focuses on executive support, coordination, and administrative delivery, helping ensure meetings, communications, and logistics run seamlessly.

This role is ideal for someone early in their career who is highly organised, reliable, and eager to build experience supporting senior leaders in a dynamic environment.

 

Your responsibilities

 

Executive & Administrative Support

Provide day-to-day administrative support to the Chief of Staff
Assist with managing a complex and evolving calendar, ensuring alignment to priorities
Coordinate meetings, including scheduling, logistics, and confirmations
Support travel planning and basic expense coordination where needed

 

Coordination & Organisation

Support coordination across teams by ensuring information is shared clearly and on time
Maintain simple trackers, action logs, and schedules

Help ensure tasks and deliverables are organised and visible

 

 

Stakeholder Support

Build effective working relationships with stakeholders across the business
Work closely with Executive Assistants and team members to ensure alignment
Act as a reliable and responsive point of contact for coordination requests

 

Digital & Ways of Working

Use Microsoft 365 tools (Outlook, Teams, PowerPoint, Excel, SharePoint) in day-to-day work
Use AI tools (e.g., Copilot) to support note-taking, summarisation, and drafting
Adopt efficient ways of working and continuously look for small improvements

 

Confidentiality & Professional Standards

Handle sensitive information with discretion and professionalism
Maintain high standards of accuracy, organisation, and reliability

 

Your skills and experience

 

Core Skills

Strong organisational and time management skills
High attention to detail and accuracy
Clear written and verbal communication
Ability to prioritise tasks and manage multiple requests

 

Working Style

Proactive, dependable, and responsive
Positive attitude and willingness to learn
Comfortable working in a fast-paced environment

 

Tools & Digital Skills

Good working knowledge of Microsoft 365 tools
Confidence using digital tools to support productivity (AI experience a plus, not essential)

 

Interpersonal Skills

Strong teamwork and collaboration
Professional approach when working with senior stakeholders
Good judgement and discretion

 

Experience

Experience in administration, coordination, or similar roles
Experience working in a corporate or fast-paced environment is beneficial but not essential
