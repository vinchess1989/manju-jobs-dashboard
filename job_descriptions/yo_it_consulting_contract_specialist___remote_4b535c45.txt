Job Description

Role Title: Contract Specialist

Role Type: Contract

Location: Remote

Scope Of Work


Draft, review, and provide high-quality templates, samples, and annotated examples of the following agreement types:
Master Service Agreements (MSAs)
Lease Agreements
Intellectual Property (IP) Licensing Agreements
Marketing Agreements
Licensing Agreements
Ensure all agreements reflect current legal standards, industry best practices, and practical commercial use cases.
Submit well-structured, high-quality documents that contribute to the project's AI training dataset.
Communicate promptly and professionally, both in writing and verbally, to clarify requirements and deliverables as needed.


Preferred Qualifications


Practicing or recently qualified commercial attorney with exposure to contract drafting and negotiation.
Working knowledge of standard commercial agreements, including MSAs, lease agreements, IP and marketing contracts.
Strong written and verbal communication skills, with attention to clarity and accuracy in contractual language.
Demonstrated ability to create or adapt legal documents for practical, real-world scenarios.
Experience with remote collaboration and delivering assignments autonomously.
Detail-oriented approach to ensure agreements comply with relevant laws and best practices.
Interest or background in legal operations, technology, or AI-enabled platforms is a plus.
