Spare Parts Coordinator - Help Keep Global Deliveries Moving 

Would you like to work in order handling and customer service with a proactive and solution-oriented approach? Our team is looking for a new Spare Parts Coordinator! Tampere Supply Center is a team of around 100 professionals serving global deliveries of Automation Products.  

Your responsibilities 

Manage spare parts orders and ensure accurate order handling throughout the delivery process. 

Monitor delivery schedules and proactively resolve potential delays. 

Collaborate with production, purchasing and shipping teams to support on-time deliveries. 

Communicate with stakeholders and provide updates on order and delivery status. 

Contribute to the continuous development of global spare parts delivery processes. 

Expectations for you 

Degree in engineering, business administration or a related field. 

Good MS Office skills and comfort with digital systems. 

Experience in order handling, supply chain operations or ERP systems is an advantage. 

Experience in Salesforce is considered an asset. 

Advanced English and Finnish language skills, written and spoken. 

Ability to work independently while supporting team goals. 

 

To succeed in this role 

You bring a structured and detail-oriented approach to your work and enjoy solving problems in a fast-paced supply chain environment. You build effective relationships across teams, communicate proactively, and take ownership of delivering high-quality results.  

This position is also suitable for junior talents who are eager to learn and grow with us, even if you don't yet have extensive experience in this field. 

We also expect you to align with Valmet's Leadership Principles-practical behaviours that guide how we lead, collaborate, and succeed together:  

Take accountability - Act with ownership, set clear expectations, and follow through.

Lead with courage - Champion change, try bold ideas, and learn quickly.  

Build on trust - Listen to understand, be open, and create psychological safety.  

Collaborate for growth - Work across boundaries, value diverse perspectives, and focus on customer value. 

We offer 

In return, we provide you with a challenging and interesting position in a global supply chain environment, along with excellent opportunities for competence development. This role offers a great perspective on the growing automation business and an excellent career path within Valmet.
