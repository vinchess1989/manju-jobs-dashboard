Company Description




Parlor Social Club is a professionally curated and personalized network designed to connect a diverse group of accomplished individuals. Leveraging technology and data science, the network offers members ongoing events, exclusive access to partner spaces, amenities, and curated private experiences. By fostering meaningful connections, Parlor Social Club provides a distinctive platform for collaboration and engagement.




Overview




Parlor Hosts are at the core of the member experience. They serve as the social anchor at events, creating a welcoming environment while ensuring each experience runs seamlessly.

This role blends hospitality, social facilitation, and operational execution. Hosts are responsible for helping members connect, managing event flow, and representing the Parlor brand with professionalism and warmth, while staying cool under pressure.

Responsibilities




Foster Community & Connection
Create a warm, inclusive environment for all members
Facilitate introductions and meaningful conversations
Integrate new members into the group
Actively manage group dynamics (e.g., breaking up cliques, engaging solo attendees)
Ensure every member feels welcomed and included
Lead Event Execution
Serve as the on-site point of contact for members, venue partners, and Parlor staff
Manage event setup, flow, and logistics
Ensure perks and partnerships are executed correctly
Troubleshoot issues in real time while maintaining a calm, confident presence
Deliver a Seamless Member Experience
Greet and onboard members upon arrival
Maintain a consistent, high-quality experience throughout the event
Adapt quickly to changing situations and unexpected challenges
Represent Parlor’s elevated, thoughtful brand in every interaction
Capture Insights & Content
Document key member interactions and connections
Capture photo and video content aligned with brand standards
Provide post-event reports with clear, concise insights
Flag standout members, issues, and opportunities for improvement
Communicate Effectively
Use the Parlor app and Slack for real-time communication
Escalate issues appropriately using internal frameworks
Maintain clear, professional communication with members and partners

Why This Role Matters

Hosts are essential to Parlor’s success. They are the bridge between the brand and the community and play a critical role in member retention — especially for new members experiencing Parlor for the first time.

A great host doesn’t just run an event — they create an experience.
