About Nbyula

Nbyula is a German technology brand headquartered in Berlin, Germany. Nbyula believes in creating an open world where borders or bureaucracies do not hinder study and work opportunities. Nbyula aims to create a global mentorship network of international students, professionals and ex-pats to nurture the next-gen “Skillizens without Borders”.

To know more about us, please visit https://nbyula.com/listings.

About The Position

There is a growing interest among the student community in Nbyula to consider Finland as an international study & work destination. As an international student enrolled in a program, you are the best mentors to guide the new generation.

Roles & Responsibilities

As a student mentor, you will:


List ways in which you can mentor international student aspirants. You can offer 1-1 personal guidance sessions around topics like program selection, application filing, SOP assistance, LOR reviews, application interview preparation, and many more.
Service paid mentorship orders from student aspirants across the world.


Qualification & Skills


It would be best if you are an international student presently enrolled in a program at any university in Finland
Speak and write English at least as a second language


Perks


Handsome income from mentoring international students
Mentoring bonus subject to the mentoring level attained
Opportunity to make a difference in the life of students
Meaningful channel to earn a side income while you study
Flexible mentoring schedule as per your convenience
