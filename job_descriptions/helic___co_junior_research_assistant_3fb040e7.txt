Job Summary


We are seeking a motivated and detail-oriented Junior Research Assistant to support research projects and data collection activities. In this role, you will assist with literature reviews, data gathering, analysis, and documentation while ensuring accuracy and adherence to research protocols. This is an ideal entry-level position for individuals looking to build a career in research or academia.



Key Responsibilities

Conduct literature reviews and summarize key findings

Assist in designing and implementing research studies and experiments

Collect, clean, and organize qualitative and quantitative data

Maintain accurate records, databases, and documentation

Support data analysis and interpretation under supervision

Prepare reports, presentations, and research summaries

Ensure compliance with ethical guidelines and research standards

Coordinate with team members and assist with project logistics


Qualifications

Bachelor’s degree (or currently pursuing) in a relevant field (e.g., Social Sciences, Life Sciences, Business, or related discipline)

Basic understanding of research methodologies and data collection techniques

Proficiency in Microsoft Office (Excel, Word, PowerPoint)

Strong analytical, organizational, and critical thinking skills

Excellent written and verbal communication skills

Attention to detail and ability to manage multiple tasks


Preferred Skills

Familiarity with statistical tools (e.g., SPSS, R, Python)

Knowledge of qualitative and quantitative research methods

Experience with academic writing and citation styles (APA, MLA, etc.)

Exposure to survey tools or data collection platforms
