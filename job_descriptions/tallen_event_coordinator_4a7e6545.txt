Company Overview: 

Tallen offers meeting planners, exhibit houses, exhibitors, and corporations AV technology and production services that enable them to deliver their message to audiences of all sizes in venues across the U.S. Europe, Asia, and Latin America.

 

Summary:

The Event Coordinator serves as the point of contact for Clients and Vendors while maintaining accurate calendar maintenance in the most organized and efficient manner. This role is fully remote and based on US East Coast hours.

 

Key Responsibilities: 

Responsible for ensuring all customer and vendor orders are executed on time, in an organized and efficient manner to the satisfaction of the customer/vendor.
Responsible for determining the best course of action to meet client and vendor needs, delegating work to and managing support staff to ensure client needs are met and oversee the successful execution of delivered technology to corporate events and trade shows.
Responsible for identifying and solving any problems with the fulfillment of orders.
Processes all customer and vendor orders.
Responsible for identifying possible budget concerns and formulating courses of action necessary to ensure projects are completed within budget.
Responsible for identifying and resolving any issues with delayed deliveries.
Performs other duties as needed upon request by superiors.
Exhibits ability to choose the best course of action for executing corporate events and trade shows.
Accountable for ensuring that all assigned work is completed in a timely fashion and is delivered to the satisfaction of the client or vendor.
Completes work within the allotted budget.
Actively communicates with direct manager regarding the status of projects, and any issues that arise.

 

Skills and Experience:

2 or more years of event experience preferred
Advanced computer and software skills including Microsoft Office Suite products required
Excellent organizational, time management, communication, and leadership skills.
Proficient in project management and organizational skills
Strong attention to details, accuracy, and quality of work product, with few to no errors
Ability to act as a team player and be flexible to respond to changing needs
Ability to work with the utmost integrity, keeping proprietary information safeguarded
Ability to work independently within scope of job responsibilities

 

Salary: $45,000 annually
