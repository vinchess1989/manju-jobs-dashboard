Job Description:

At Grid & Power Quality Solutions and Service business at Hitachi Energy in Europe (HBUEU), Inclusion drives our culture of innovation and collaboration. Our leading technology and strong market position enable us to achieve growth targets in the energy transition across current and growing market segments such as data centers, hydrogen and e-mobility.

Join Our Team!

We are looking for a HSE Specialist to join our HSE Team in Vaasa, Finland. In this role, you will support our Finnish projects in the Nordic Operation Unit (OU) and focus on the HSE tasks in the HSE department and throughout the project’s life cycle. You will be ensuring that the highest standards of health, safety and environmental performance are documented, monitored and maintained. You will also be driving continuous improvement in all areas of HSE.

The Nordic OU HSE Team is a network of experienced health, safety and environmental professionals supporting the delivery of complex construction, energy and industrial projects across Finland, Sweden and Denmark. We support highly skilled site teams in delivering world-class projects through professionalism, quality and operational excellence, creating environments where safety is the natural outcome of doing the job right. Working closely with project management, customers and contractors, we provide expertise and practical support throughout the project lifecycle. Our focus is on enabling disciplined planning, effective risk management and high-quality execution that protects people, enhances performance and strengthens project outcomes. 

Our strength lies in our people. We bring together talented professionals with experience from the energy sector, industrial construction and major infrastructure projects. We value expertise, integrity and a willingness to take initiative. We are a team that shares knowledge openly, learns from each other and works together to solve challenges in demanding project environments. We welcome individuals from diverse professional backgrounds who are motivated by continuous development and a desire to make a meaningful impact. 

We believe that excellence comes from combining technical competence with personal accountability. We encourage constructive challenge, transparent communication and the confidence to speak up when improvements can be made. By maintaining high professional standards and supporting one another, we help project teams achieve results while protecting people, the environment and the long-term success of our projects. 

We challenge established practices and continuously look for better ways to manage risk, support project teams and improve performance. Through innovation, digitalization and intelligent use of technology, we advance how decisions are made and how projects are executed. We do not simply adapt to change; we actively shape the future of HSE by turning new ideas into practical solutions that enable safer, smarter and more efficient project delivery. 

If you are motivated by complex projects, enjoy working with skilled professionals and want to help shape the future of HSE in the energy and construction industries, you will fit right in with our team. 

How you’ll make an impact

Implement and work with continuous improvement of Hitachi Energy operating unit management system and local requirements in Finland for the operating unit of the Nordic OU.
Prepare and update HSE documentation in accordance with the internal process. Submit all preliminary HSE plans to the customer in accordance with the tender submission dates.
Attend clarification meetings and workshops with the customer (both in person and remotely).
Prepare the Project HSE Plan as well as other HSE-related documentation such as the Emergency Preparedness and Response Plan.
Ensure activities under the scope of Hitachi Energy are in compliance with legal and contractual HSE requirements as well as internal HSE requirements.
Produce reports for Project Reviews, both internal and customer, and attend the Project Review meetings to ensure the Hitachi Energy HSE Operating System remains suitable, adequate, and effective.
Review HSE documentation from all Hitachi Energy subcontractors and support subcontractors to meet internal and Project HSE requirements.

Your background

Previous experience of HSE tasks in projects
Professional HSE certification is seen as a plus
Knowledge of ISO 45001 and ISO 14001 certifications
Strong organizational and communication skills with the ability to operate within global teams
Self-driven and persistent attitude towards challenges. You work actively and professionally both independently and as a part of a team.
Excellent written and spoken communication skills in English and Finnish. Swedish is seen as a plus.
