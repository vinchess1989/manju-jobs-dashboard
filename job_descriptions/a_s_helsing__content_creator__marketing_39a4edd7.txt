We are looking for a hands-on Content Creator to manage and own our social media channels with +100 000 followers. You are a highly creative storyteller with a keen visual eye and a genuine interest in design. In this role, you will be responsible for managing and creating content for A.S.Helsingö’s international community.




Creative ideas come easily to you and you enjoy shooting and editing organic-feeling videos yourself. You love storytelling and live and breathe social media. You’ll be an integral team member in furthering our brand presence and building community around our unique brand proposition.
