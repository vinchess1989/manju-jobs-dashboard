Company Description LEONHARD WEISS OÜ is an experienced design, construction, and maintenance company specializing in railways, roads, and electrical networks across the Baltic States and Scandinavia. The company is part of the LEONHARD WEISS Group, a German family-owned enterprise founded in 1900 with a strong tradition in infrastructure projects. Working with LEONHARD WEISS OÜ means joining a stable, long-established organization that values quality, safety, and long-term partnerships. Team members participate in impactful infrastructure projects that support regional connectivity and development.
Role Description The Landowner Liaison Specialist is a full-time, on-site role based in Finland. This role focuses on building and maintaining constructive relationships with landowners affected by infrastructure projects, ensuring transparent communication and fair negotiations. Day-to-day tasks include contacting landowners, explaining project plans, coordinating site visits, and gathering feedback and approvals related to land use. The specialist will document agreements, track correspondence, and work closely with project managers, legal teams, and technical specialists to resolve concerns and ensure compliance with local regulations. The role also involves preparing reports, maintaining accurate records, and contributing to continuous improvement of stakeholder engagement processes.
Qualifications

Strong communication and interpersonal skills for liaising with landowners and project stakeholders.
Experience in negotiation, conflict resolution, or stakeholder management, preferably in construction, infrastructure, or utilities.
Ability to read and understand basic technical or legal documentation related to land use, easements, and infrastructure projects.
Solid organizational and documentation skills, including accurate record-keeping and report preparation.
Fluency in English and Finnish; additional language skills in Baltic or Scandinavian languages are an advantage.
Knowledge of local regulations and practices related to land ownership, property rights, and permitting is beneficial.
Comfort working on-site and traveling within Finland to meet landowners and attend project meetings.
Relevant educational background in law, land management, real estate, communications, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
