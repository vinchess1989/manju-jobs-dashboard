HSEQ Administrator – Finland | Hyperscale Data Centre




Location: Finland

Project: 150 MW Hyperscale Data Centre

Contract: 12-month rolling contract

Working Pattern: Full-time

Start Date: Early–Mid October 2026

Rate: Competitive day rate




The Opportunity

V7 Recruitment are currently looking for an experienced HSEQ Administrator to join a major 150 MW hyperscale data centre project in Finland.




This is an excellent opportunity for someone with a strong background in HSEQ administration within construction or a site-based environment to join a large-scale project and become part of an established project team.




The initial contract will be on a 12-month rolling basis, with the client also having a second hyperscale data centre project coming up following this programme. For the right person, there is therefore the potential to build a long-term pipeline of work in Finland.




The Role

Working as part of the project HSEQ team, you will provide administrative and coordination support across the day-to-day HSEQ function on site.




The role would suit someone who is organised, detail-focused and comfortable working in a busy construction environment, supporting HSEQ processes and ensuring documentation and records are maintained accurately.




Key Responsibilities

Providing day-to-day administrative support to the HSEQ team
Maintaining and updating HSEQ documentation and records
Supporting the management and tracking of HSEQ documentation
Assisting with inspections, audits and HSEQ reporting
Maintaining training, competency and certification records
Supporting incident, near-miss and safety reporting processes
Assisting with the coordination of HSEQ meetings and maintaining meeting records
Supporting the wider project team with HSEQ-related administration and document control
Ensuring HSEQ information is accurately recorded, organised and readily available
Working closely with site management, contractors and the wider HSEQ team




Requirements

Previous experience in an HSEQ/HSE administration role
Background working within construction or a construction site environment
Strong organisational and administrative skills
Excellent attention to detail
Comfortable managing large volumes of documentation and information
Good communication skills and the ability to work effectively with site teams and contractors
Proficient with Microsoft Office and standard administrative systems
Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced project environment
Right to work in Europe is essential




Previous data centre or mission-critical experience would be advantageous, but is not essential for the right candidate with a strong construction HSEQ background.

Long-Term Opportunity




Ideally, we're looking for someone who is able to remain in Finland for the duration of the project, providing the opportunity to continue onto the next project and build a long-term relationship with the client.




If you're an experienced HSEQ Administrator with a construction/site-based background, have the right to work in Europe, and are looking for a long-term opportunity in Finland, we'd be keen to hear from you




Apply now or contact V7 Recruitment for further information.
