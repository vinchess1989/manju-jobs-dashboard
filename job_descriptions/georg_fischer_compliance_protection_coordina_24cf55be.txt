Hae 
Tallenna
At GF, we develop market-leading solutions that ensure water, gas, and other essential fluids flow safely to support cities, industries, and everyday life. With people in our 40+ countries and impact going far beyond that, this is your chance to do work that millions of people depend on.

We are seeking a motivated, curious, and proactive individual with a passion for data protection to join GF as Data Protection Coordinator for a part-time (50%) 2 years fixed-term position. As the selected candidate, you'll get hands-on experience with a wide range of data protection tasks and projects. This global role is based in Helsinki, Finland, and reports to the Chief Compliance & Privacy Officer.

This Is What You Do


Advise stakeholders on Privacy and AI compliance matters and legal governance questions.
Promote privacy across the organization, build and maintain trust.
Investigate privacy incidents.
Produce, maintain and improve privacy and AI documentation.
Check Data Processing Agreements.
Collaborate with key stakeholders to assess whether data collection and usage practices are transparent, protect user privacy, and mitigate risk.
Draft privacy policies, procedures and training materials.
Evaluate effectiveness of privacy processes.


This Is What You Need


Bachelor’s or master’s degree (preferably in law, business administration, business informatics or similar).
Experience in the field of data protection, compliance, or information security.
Fluency in English, both written and verbal.
Team player attitude with good interpersonal communication skills.
Proactive, independent and structured way of working.
Reliable, open and transparent way of communicating.
Passion and curiosity to learn, improve and develop.
Very good MS Office skills.
Eligibility to work in Finland.


This Is What You Get


Interesting and versatile role in which you can develop further.
Competitive pay and comprehensive set of employee benefits.
International and dynamic work environment with brilliant colleagues.
Modern office facility in Ilmala, Helsinki.
