Job Title: Events Operations Assistant

Location: Remote, East Coast preferred

Duration: 12 months, with potential to extend/hire

Hours: Part-Time – 20 hours/week (5 days a week, 4 hours/day)




Schedule-Part-Time

This role is ideal for an experienced administrative professional who enjoys supporting the successful execution of conferences and events through exceptional organization, financial management, and operational excellence.




Position Overview

Seeking a highly organized and detail-oriented Part-Time Events Operations Assistant to support the Global Events team.
This role provides administrative and operational support for conferences, client events, and internal meetings.
The ideal candidate is an experienced administrative professional who thrives in a fast-paced environment, is proactive, highly organized, and capable of managing multiple priorities while ensuring event logistics and financial processes are executed accurately and efficiently.




Key Responsibilities

Event Operations & Logistics

Coordinate the shipment of conference booths, event materials, and branded assets by working with warehouse partners and expo teams to ensure timely delivery and return
Order and manage inventory of promotional items, giveaways, and event supplies for conferences and client events
Research venues, private dining rooms, restaurants, and other event-related resources for meetings, client dinners, and small-scale events
Provide administrative support for internal meetings and select client events, assisting with logistics as needed




Financial & Administrative Management

Process and track invoices and vendor payments, partnering with the Accounts Payable team to ensure timely processing
Perform corporate credit card reconciliation and expense management
Maintain accurate records of spend tracking and reporting
Support the Head of Global Events with expense reports and minor travel assistance
Maintain organized event files, contracts, and supporting documentation




Travel & Logistics Coordination

Partner with Corporate Travel to coordinate attendee travel logistics
Manage attendee manifests and travel-related documentation
Coordinate travel logistics for internal stakeholders, speakers, and event attendees, as appropriate
Maintain up-to-date event documentation, including guest lists, itineraries, confirmations, and event records




General Team Support

Assist with day-to-day administrative activities to support the Global Events department (printing, shipping, ordering, registration pulls)
Communicate with internal stakeholders, vendors, and event partners to ensure timely follow-up and coordination




Qualifications

3–5+ years of administrative, executive assistant, operations, or corporate events support experience, preferably within a professional services or financial services organization
Demonstrated experience managing invoices, expense reporting, travel coordination, and administrative support for meetings or events
Exceptional organizational skills with meticulous attention to detail and a commitment to accuracy
Proven ability to prioritize multiple projects, manage competing deadlines, and work independently with minimal supervision
Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to anticipate needs before they arise
Excellent written and verbal communication skills with a professional, client-service mindset
Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (Outlook, Excel, Word, and PowerPoint)
Experience working with expense management, travel, and invoice processing systems is preferred
Demonstrated ability to build strong relationships with internal business partners, vendors, and external stakeholders
Dependable, resourceful, and adaptable with a positive, team-oriented approach
Experience supporting conferences, meetings, or corporate events is strongly preferred
