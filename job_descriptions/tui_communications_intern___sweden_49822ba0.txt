Communications Intern - Finland

Are you pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Finland and passionate about storytelling, public relations, and impactful communication? Join our Nordic Communications team and gain hands-on experience in a dynamic, global organization where you'll help shape how TUI connects with employees, customers, and stakeholders.

The role will be published until 21 July 2026.

About Our Offer


Flexible working: Work is something you do, not somewhere you go. We encourage a healthy work-life balance with a dynamic working environment.
A career to shape: Access the TUI Learning Hub to level-up and reach your ambitions.
Broaden your network: We champion intercultural collaboration and provide opportunities to work on global projects and teams.
Community: Get involved with incredible local charity and sustainability initiatives like the TUI Care Foundation.


About The Job


You'll write and manage content for employee newsletters, intranet updates, and internal campaigns while supporting leadership communication across departments.
Drafting press releases, briefing materials, and articles for external publications will be part of your role, alongside building relationships with journalists to ensure positive media coverage.
We'll rely on you to monitor and analyze media coverage, helping protect and enhance TUI's reputation through strategic communication efforts.
You'll assist in preparing and executing crisis communication strategies, collaborating with teams to respond effectively to emerging issues.
Conducting research on communication trends and evaluating the effectiveness of our efforts will allow you to provide actionable recommendations.
At the end of your internship, you'll present your insights and recommendations to our team, ensuring we benefit from your unique contributions.


About You


You're currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's degree program in Finland, ideally in PR, Communications, Journalism, Economics, or a related field.
Fluency in Finnish and English is essential for this role.
You're available to start your internship in Fall 2026, in line with your university's internship requirements.
Your enthusiasm, thoughtful approach, and interest in exploring various facets of corporate communication make you a great fit.
