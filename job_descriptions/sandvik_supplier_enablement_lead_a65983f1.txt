Räätälöi ansioluetteloni
Do you thrive on creating structure in complex environments and driving supplier collaboration to the next level? Here’s your chance to take the lead in enabling global suppliers onto cutting-edge digital platforms.

Why join us?

At Sandvik and business area Mining, you join a forward-thinking team where your expertise shapes the future of our supplier ecosystem. You make a real impact in a role that bridges technology, supply chain, and partnerships.

Our offer:


Influence how suppliers connect and collaborate with us globally
Drive adoption of modern digital tools for transparent and efficient processes
Work in a truly international environment with diverse stakeholders


About The Job

You’re at the center of our supplier enablement initiatives, taking full ownership of onboarding suppliers to our SAP Ariba platform across multiple regions. You plan and execute end-to-end onboarding — from supplier registration and sourcing projects to ensuring smooth collaboration on purchase orders, invoices, and forecasts. Your mission is to create a scalable way of working and align stakeholders with different expectations, while boosting tool adoption both internally and externally.

What you’ll do:


Coordinate supplier enablement activities across global teams and systems
Define and roll out efficient, standardized processes for onboarding and collaboration
Engage actively with internal procurement teams, suppliers, and system providers
Monitor and analyze data to identify gaps and opportunities for improvement
Act as a trusted go-to person for all enablement issues and ensure business continuity


This position is located either in Sandviken, Stockholm, Turku, Tampere or Dublin.

About You

You’ve strong procurement or supply chain experience and understand B2B relationships. You bring analytical skills and know how to work with data insights, ideally with tools like Power BI. You thrive in environments where technology and process intersect and have experience with SAP Ariba. We see hands-on experience from leading or coordinating projects or large-scale system rollouts as a great plus.

You bring:


Solid knowledge of supplier onboarding or enablement processes
Hands-on experience with SAP Ariba in a procurement or supply chain context
Ability to navigate complex stakeholder networks and build strong collaborations
Experience with data analysis and deriving actionable insights
Familiarity with digital procurement tools and end-to-end sourcing processes


You’re proactive and structured, with the confidence to influence and push progress. You love being the connector – the person who spots when action is needed and makes it happen. Resilience, curiosity, and excellent communication skills keep you thriving in a dynamic, global environment.
