Roschier is looking to hire a part-time receptionist to join the Catering & Reception team.

Roschier is one of the leading law firms in the Nordic region. We are now looking for an energetic and service-minded part-time receptionist to join our team in Helsinki.

Our team takes pride in providing an exceptional guest experience and delivering first-class service in matters like reception and switchboard, and conference and catering services. We work closely with other teams in our Stockholm and Helsinki offices to meet the needs of the many internal and external customers we serve daily. The role is a diverse combination of responsibilities and requires above all initiative, a hands-on approach and a genuine interest to provide excellent customer service.

The role is a part-time and permanent position (1-4 shifts per week), starting preferably in September or October 2026. Work shifts are mainly during office hours (8-17), but flexibility is appreciated due to occasional evening work related to events, client meetings and other activities at our office. Catering & Reception is part of the broader Communications & Branding team.

Your main tasks will include


daily reception and switchboard routines, including customer service and welcoming visitors, answering their enquiries and referring them to appropriate contacts
assisting the catering team
providing general support to our employees, with a variety of administrative tasks
daily mail and courier services related routines
office safety, security and access control related matters, and assisting with other premises related matters
assistance in property maintenance coordination as needed


We Are Looking For Talents Who


share our values and commitment to providing first-class service
are service-minded, attentive and take pride in creating a welcoming experience for others
enjoy interacting with visitors and colleagues
have good organizing skills and a positive will-do attitude
have a keen eye for details and flexible mindset
have a good command of the MS Office programs
are able to work both independently and as part of a team
have excellent oral and written communication skills: fluency in Finnish and English, Swedish language skills are seen as an advantage


Apply today — and join the Roschier team

Please apply by 13 September through the application link and attach your CV and a short motivation letter. Kindly note that we only accept applications in English. Applications are reviewed on an ongoing basis.

Candidates are evaluated based on their professional qualifications and motivation towards the role. Reference checks will be conducted on shortlisted candidates.
