Meta seeks a highly motivated and experienced team player to serve as an Associate General Counsel, Competition and Regulatory. Working with a talented and supportive team, this position will advise the company on competition issues associated with mergers and acquisitions, product launches, investigations and inquiries, and help develop the company’s legal position and strategy on competition matters throughout the world. This is an outstanding opportunity for a talented individual to join a global legal team and work on cutting edge issues in an exciting, fast-paced environment with a broad and interesting remit.

Associate General Counsel, Competition & Regulatory Responsibilities:


Provide the company with counsel and advice on antitrust issues relating to mergers and acquisitions
and help develop and execute our legal strategy in connection with the review of mergers and acquisitions by regulators around the world.
Advise and counsel the company on competition issues relating to product launches and related business strategies.
Represent the company in connection with competition inquiries around the world.
Advise the company on global antitrust legislation, merger control updates, and emerging regulatory trends.
Work with cross-functional teams to develop and implement long-term competition strategy.


Minimum Qualifications:


5+ years of legal experience, including at a law firm, government, and/or in-house experience with companies working on antitrust matters
Flexibility and eagerness to work on legal matters globally
Interpersonal skills with a commitment to professionalism and collegiality


Preferred Qualifications:


Experience with global antitrust laws and regulators
Experience with internet or technology firms
Demonstrated ability to integrate AI tools to optimize/redesign workflows and drive measurable impact (e.g., efficiency gains, quality improvements)
Experience adhering to and implementing responsible, ethical AI practices (e.g., risk assessment, bias mitigation, quality and accuracy reviews)
Demonstrated ongoing AI skill development (e.g., prompt/context engineering, agent orchestration) and staying current with emerging AI technologies
