Xwander




Xwander is an Arctic group of companies based in Ivalo.
Xwander Nordic designs and operates high-end travel packages across Lapland.
Xwander Productions delivers location and production services for international film and TV productions in the Arctic — most recently a world-class prime-time series filmed in Lapland.
Xwander Academy runs versatile outdoor education and guide training. Operations are backed by our own vehicle, snowmobile and rental-equipment fleet, and the group runs AI-first in its daily work.




The Role

You run Xwanders operations. Winter is the high season and the core of the job with Summer growing. Permanent, year-round position. Based in Ivalo.




Responsibilities

Resourcing: guides, vehicles and fulfilment, day by day
Booking systems: Bokun, FareHarbor, HubSpot, ERP — integrations and data flow
Supplier network and logistics, Ivalo–Rovaniemi–Northern Norway
Finance operations: reconciliations, cost centres, weekly review
Leading teams; writing the SOPs




Requirements

Proven seasonal-operations track record and processes you wrote that others then ran
Real people-management experience
English as working language; Finnish and French a plus
AI tools in active daily use
Field work in the Lapland winter
This is an In-house role




This is not;

A guide, sales or marketing role




We offer;

A rare permanent, year-round leadership role in a seasonal industry
A defined growth path to company leadership with measurable gates
Staff use of the fleet, gear, gym and sauna
