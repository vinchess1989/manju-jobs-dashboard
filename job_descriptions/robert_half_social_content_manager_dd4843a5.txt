SENIOR CONTENT MANAGER




JOB SUMMARY

The Senior Content Manager leads complex, multi-platform content initiatives—balancing advanced editorial execution with strategic insight, mentorship, and cross-functional alignment. This role strengthens content rigor through analytics, SEO inputs, and audience understanding.




WHO WE’RE LOOKING FOR

Experienced content strategist with strong writing and editing skills
Confident communicator who can guide client and internal conversations
Organized, analytical thinker with strong project leadership skills
Mentor-minded contributor who supports team growth and consistency
Skilled collaborator across Creative, Media, Digital, and Account teams




RESPONSIBILITIES

Lead content planning and development for complex or high-priority programs
Align content with creative direction, campaign narratives, and brand guidelines
Write and edit long-form and short-form content across platforms
Present content strategies and performance insights to clients
Mentor Managers and Coordinators; provide structured feedback
Refine editorial processes, standards, and workflows
Support integrated planning and cross-functional deliverables
Exercise independent judgment on content approaches, prioritization, and execution decisions within assigned programs
Use approved AI tools while ensuring outputs remain accurate, on-brand, and aligned with editorial standards.




REQUIREMENTS

5–7 years in content, editorial, or digital storytelling roles
Strong understanding of SEO, analytics, and content performance drivers
Excellent writing, editing, and communication skills
