AVIXA is looking for an Associate Editor (Contract) to support the Digital Content Manager with editorial tasks ranging from writing SEO content to supporting digital production. 




This is a mid-level editorial role, suitable for someone with several years of content writing and editing experience. Familiarity with the AV, IT, or Tech industries is a bonus.  




Note: This position requires set working hours each week. Schedule is flexible, but availability must stay generally the same each week. Please read through to the end of the listing for application requirements.




Responsibilities 




Content Creation 

Research, pitch, and write original articles focused on trending AV topics in North America 
Translate complex AV concepts into clear, engaging, and accessible content 
Maintain awareness of industry developments, technologies, and market trends 




Editing & Quality Control 

Edit internal and freelance content for clarity, structure, accuracy, and voice 
Provide clear, constructive feedback and revision guidance to writers 
Ensure content aligns with editorial standards and brand voice 
Serve as the primary point of contact for assigned freelance writers throughout the content development process 
Review submissions and manage revisions to ensure content meets AVIXA editorial standards 




Editorial Coordination 

Create detailed content briefs, outlines, and assignments for writers 
Manage deadlines and track content through the editorial pipeline 
Communicate with contributors to keep projects moving smoothly 
Support the Content Manager in maintaining the editorial calendar 




SEO & Content Performance 

Conduct basic keyword and search trend research to inform content development 
Collaborate with the Content Manager to support organic traffic growth 
Identify opportunities for content refreshes, optimization, and internal linking 
Apply SEO best practices across editorial content while maintaining content quality 

 

Required Qualifications 

At least five years of experience in editorial content, journalism, or digital publishing 
Strong writing and editing skills, with a portfolio of published work 
Experience working with freelance writers and providing constructive feedback 
Comfort managing your own workload and proactively taking on new tasks 
Knowledge of SEO‑aware editing and headline writing 
Availability during established core business hours for team meetings and collaboration 

 

Preferred Qualifications 

Experience translating technical information into audience-friendly content. 
Experience working within a project management system 
Familiarity with professional AV, enterprise technology, or related industries 
Comfort working in a CMS-driven publishing environment 
Experience supporting newsletters or recurring editorial programs 




To apply, please send your resume and your strongest writing sample to djelter@avixa.org. In your email, tell us why you selected this piece, what you would improve if you were editing it today, and one technology or AV-related trend you think deserves more attention.
