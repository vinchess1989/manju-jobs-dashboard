About Servant



At Servant, we specialize in empowering leaders and organizations within faith communities through innovative digital transformation and change management solutions. We are dedicated to providing tailored strategies that align with our clients' values and missions, focusing on service, integrity, and excellence. Our team is passionate about driving impactful change by equipping our partners with the tools and technologies they need to thrive in a digital age.

Position Overview



The Operations Generalist is a highly organized, detail-oriented professional who helps keep Servant's People Operations running smoothly. Reporting to the Director of People, this role supports the employee and contractor lifecycle, talent acquisition, and administration of our core business systems.

This is a hands-on, execution-focused role responsible for coordinating operational processes, maintaining accurate data, administering systems, and delivering an exceptional employee experience. The ideal candidate enjoys creating structure, improving processes, and ensuring nothing falls through the cracks.

Key Responsibilities

People Operations


Coordinate the employee and contractor lifecycle from onboarding through offboarding
Maintain personnel records, compliance documentation, and contract administration
Support payroll and PEO administration, including state tax updates and employee changes
Coordinate performance review cycles, eNPS surveys, unemployment claims, employment verifications, and recurring People Operations processes
Maintain the annual HR calendar and ensure operational tasks are completed accurately and on time
Drive employee engagement and foster connection through company-wide communications, recognition programs, wellness challenges, and celebrations
Talent Acquisition


Coordinate interviews, scheduling, and candidate communications
Support hiring managers throughout the recruitment process
Prepare offer letters, onboarding documentation, and new hire logistics
Assist with recruiting operations, reporting, and process improvements
Systems & Operations


Administer and maintain core business systems including Rippling, Notion, Slack, Google Workspace, and other internal tools
Provision and reprovision user access and maintain system data integrity
Generate reports, perform data audits, and ensure data accuracy across systems
Document processes, improve workflows, and support special projects that enhance operational efficiency
Coordinate logistics for internal meetings, offsites, and company events



Requirements


Required
2+ years of experience in People Operations, or Human Resources
Strong organizational skills with exceptional attention to detail
Experience managing multiple priorities in a fast-paced, remote environment
Excellent written and verbal communication skills
Proficiency with Google Workspace or Microsoft Office
Ability to handle confidential information with professionalism and discretion
Preferred
Experience with Rippling or another HRIS/PEO platform
Experience supporting the employee lifecycle and talent acquisition
Familiarity with Notion, Slack, and modern workplace technology
Experience with HR reporting, data management, and systems administration
Experience supporting performance management and employee engagement initiatives
Familiarity with AI tools such as Claude to improve productivity and workflows
