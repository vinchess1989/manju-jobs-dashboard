Katso, kuka Actors Academy Finland on palkattu tähän rooliin

Actors Academy Finland is an international acting school based in Helsinki, dedicated to helping actors grow both artistically and personally. Born from a passion for the craft of acting and authentic self-expression, we bring leading acting coaches from London to Finland and across Europe, offering a Meisner Technique-based One Year Training programme, workshops, masterclasses, and events that support actors at every stage of their journey.

Over the past seven years, we have built a thriving international network of acting coaches and industry professionals, creating opportunities for actors to learn, connect, and grow within a supportive and inspiring community.

We are a small, passionate, and ambitious team where every contribution matters. As a person part of the team, you'll be trusted with meaningful responsibilities and have the opportunity to make a visible impact on a growing international organisation. We offer a flexible working environment, opportunities for remote work, and scope to develop your skills within the creative industries.

SOCIAL MEDIA AND MARKETING INTERN

Part-time

We're looking for a part-time Social Media & Marketing Intern to help grow our online presence, create engaging content, and support the continued development of our brand and community. This role is ideal for someone who is proactive, creative, organised, and excited about contributing to a purpose-driven organisation that values both professional excellence and personal growth.

What the internship includes

The core of this role is keeping our social media running consistently and at a high standard — creating posts, reels, and graphics that reflect who we are and speak to our audience.

Alongside the daily work, you'll be building and developing our brand book with the support of a design and marketing expert.

Role & responsibilities


Creating and scheduling content for Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok (posts, reels, stories)
Video editing for social media reels and short-form content
Graphic design using Canva
Copywriting for social media captions and newsletters
Building and maintaining Canva templates as part of our ongoing brand book development
Contributing to the social media content plan


Who is this role for?

We're Looking For Someone Who


Is studying or has a background in marketing, communications, media, or a related field
Has strong video editing skills and experience creating social media content
Has a good visual eye and feels confident working in Canva or similar tools
Understands how a brand maintains a consistent look, feel, and voice across platforms
Writes fluent, natural English (Finnish is a plus)
Is proactive, reliable, and able to work independently
Is interested in continuing as a part-time freelancer after the internship


A Strong Advantage


Photography and videography skills


What we offer


A creative, driven, and internationally minded working environment
Real responsibility from day one — your work will be seen
Hands-on learning in brand building and social media, guided by a marketing expert
Flexible, part-time and partially remote internship (studio near central Helsinki)
Possibility to continue in a part-time freelance role after the internship period
