Orchestrate Consulting's mission is to help job seekers access carefully coordinated opportunities that match their career goals. We are not a staffing firm or agency. Orchestrate Consulting does not hire for these roles—we curate and validate them from verified employers.

We are looking for a Remote Administrative Assistant to provide essential administrative support to our team and executives. Key responsibilities include managing calendars, communication, document management, travel coordination, and general administrative tasks.

Required Qualifications Include:


High school diploma (Associate's or Bachelor's preferred)
2+ years of administrative experience
Proficiency in Microsoft Office or Google Workspace
Strong communication skills and organizational abilities
Reliable internet and quiet workspace


Preferred Qualifications:

Include experience with project management tools, CRM systems, basic bookkeeping, and remote work history.

The work environment is fully remote with a full-time schedule (40 hours/week) and company-provided equipment.
