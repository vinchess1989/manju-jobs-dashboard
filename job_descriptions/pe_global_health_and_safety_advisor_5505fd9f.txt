Health and Safety Advisor

PE Global are delighted to be working with our client who are a dedicated data centre delivery partner providing turnkey solutions to our clients, headquartered in Dublin and delivering throughout Europe.

They are currently constructing multiple turnkey data centre projects in 7 different European countries, equating to over 430 MW of IT load in flight.

We are now actively sourcing for a Health and Safety Advisor on a permanent full time basis. We currently have 4 EHS Advisor requirements in Europe. Sandviken and Skelleftea, in Sweden and Helsinki and Vaasa in Finland. You will be based on one project only, not moving between multiple. Please highlight if you have a preference of location.




The successful candidate will be operating on a shift rotational roster basis with all flights, accommodation and transport all completely covered + a weekly NET subsistence income




Please note there is no visa sponsorship or relocation assistance on offer. Candidates must have an EU/UK Passport or the appropriate working visa to be considered.




Requirements

Support the implementation of Environmental, Health & Safety policies and procedures across the project lifecycle
Conduct regular site inspections to ensure compliance with health & safety standards and identify potential hazards
Assist in carrying out risk assessments and reviewing method statements (RAMS)
Monitor subcontractor activities to ensure adherence to safety requirements
Support incident investigations and assist in the implementation of corrective actions
Promote a strong safety culture through engagement with site teams and subcontractors
Deliver safety inductions, toolbox talks, and awareness sessions as required
Ensure all EHS documentation is maintained, accurate, and up to date
Attend Site Meetings, Safety Meetings, and Toolbox Talks
Support the preparation of EHS reports and safety performance metrics
Advise project teams on health & safety best practices and compliance requirements
Assist in ensuring compliance with environmental and sustainability requirements
Identify and report EHS concerns and support their resolution




Benefits

Opportunity to work on Europe’s largest and most advanced data centre projects.
Career growth and professional development in a fast-expanding sector.
Competitive salary package.
Supportive, dynamic, and collaborative team culture.
