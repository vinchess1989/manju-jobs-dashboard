GeBBS Consulting is a healthcare technology professional services and consulting firm based in Towson, Maryland servicing clients throughout the US. We are celebrating our 28th year in business. We have two divisions: 1) Hospital / Provider Practice 2) Managed Care / Payer Practice – we work directly with health plans (very strong in the Blue Cross Blue Shield space). GeBBS Consulting has over two decades of experience supporting healthcare clients throughout the US.




We are currently hiring an HR Generalist




Summary: One of our Blue Cross Blue Shield clients is looking for an HR Generalist to provide support for both the commercial and federal employee program divisions. The hired resource will serve as the first line of HR and will be responsible for answering basic inquiries on internal policies, handling and gathering information about internal disputes, understanding information related to corrective actions, helping internal managers on filing job openings, handling initial discussions on compensation inquiries, basic reporting, etc.




Need to Close:

1-3 years of experience as an HR Generalist
Experience supporting a mid-level to enterprise-level organization (300-1000 employees)
Experience with MS Office Suite (Word, Excel)
