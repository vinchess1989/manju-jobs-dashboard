At Hubexo, we help our customers make smarter business decisions by providing trusted insights into construction activity across Europe. We're looking for a Researcher to join our team and play a key role in collecting, verifying, and refining information about upcoming construction projects. 

If you're naturally curious, enjoy speaking with people, and have a talent for turning information into valuable insights, this could be the perfect opportunity for you. 

What you'll do 

As a Researcher, you'll combine research, communication, and analytical thinking to build a clear picture of construction activity in the market. 

Your responsibilities will include: 




Conducting telephone interviews with company representatives about upcoming construction projects 




Building and maintaining your own network of industry contacts 




Researching additional sources such as company websites, press releases, and industry news 




Verifying and validating information to ensure data quality and accuracy 




Transforming findings into clear, concise, and valuable project summaries for our database 




Producing market intelligence that helps customers identify business opportunities and make informed decisions 




Please note: This is not a sales role. Your focus will be on gathering and validating information rather than selling products or services.

The team 

You'll join a friendly and experienced team that values collaboration, knowledge sharing, and mutual support. 

We foster an open and relaxed working environment where questions are encouraged, learning is continuous, and successes are celebrated together. 

What we're looking for 

Essential 




Fluent Finnish language skills, both written and spoken 




Experience working in an office-based or specialist role 




Confidence communicating with people over the phone 




Good understanding of business operations and professional environments 




Good English language skills 




Nice to have 




Experience conducting telephone interviews or working in customer-facing phone-based roles 




Swedish language skills 




Who you are 

We're looking for someone who is: 




Organised and detail-oriented 




Self-motivated and comfortable working independently 




A confident communicator with a friendly and approachable style 




Naturally curious and eager to learn 





Skilled at finding, connecting, and understanding information 




Motivated by delivering accurate, high-quality insights 




What we offer 




Competitive salary of €2,900 - €3,500 per month 




Hybrid working model (4 days in the office, 1 day remote) 




Comprehensive onboarding and training programme 




Well-being benefit (Epassi) 




Supportive, inclusive, and encouraging work environment 




Opportunities for personal and professional development
