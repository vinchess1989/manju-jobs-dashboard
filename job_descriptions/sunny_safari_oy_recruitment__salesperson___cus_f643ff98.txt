Työn kuvaus

Company: Sunny Safari Customised Safaris & Destination Management (DMC)

Location: Rovaniemi, Finland

Season: Winter 2026-2027 (September 2026 April 2027)

About Us

Sunny Safari is a premier provider of bespoke safari experiences and destination management services in the Lapland region. Our head office is based in Rovaniemi the official hometown of Santa Claus and a world-famous winter tourism destination. We are looking for enthusiastic, reliable team members to join us for the upcoming winter season, supporting our office operations and sales outlet.

What We Are Looking For

We are seeking seasonal staff to join our team for the Winter 20262027 season, based primarily in Rovaniemi.

Key Requirements

* Good command of English; proficiency in additional languages is highly valued and will be prioritised

* Strong communication skills, with the ability to connect with people from all backgrounds

* Comfortable using computers and common office software independently

* Willingness to work flexible hours to match our seasonal business needs

* A valid driving licence permitting travel within the EU is NOT required but will be considered a strong advantage

* Most importantly: a strong sense of responsibility and commitment to your work

No prior sales experience or related qualifications? No problem! If you are motivated, eager to learn, and ready to give it your best, we warmly welcome you to apply.

What We Offer

* Hands-on experience in one of Finlands most exciting tourism sectors

* Insight into the operations of custom safaris and destination management

* The chance to build confidence and communication skills with guests and partners from across the globe

* A supportive, friendly team environment where you can grow both professionally and personally
