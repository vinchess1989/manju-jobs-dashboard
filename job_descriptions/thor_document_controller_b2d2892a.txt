DOCUMENT CONTROLLER - HYPERSCALE DATA CENTER CONSTRUCTION




Overview

We are seeking an experienced Document Controller to join a hyperscale data center project, supporting the successful delivery of all project documentation throughout the construction lifecycle.

Previous experience working on Data Center, Mission Critical, Pharmaceutical, Semiconductor, Industrial or Major Infrastructure projects is highly desirable.




Key Responsibilities

Manage the complete document control process from project mobilisation through to handover.
Maintain document registers, transmittals and workflows in accordance with project procedures.
Ensure all incoming and outgoing documentation is accurately logged, tracked and distributed.
Coordinate document submissions between subcontractors, consultants, design teams and the client.
Review documents for compliance with project naming conventions and quality standards before issue.
Manage Requests for Information (RFIs), Technical Submittals, Shop Drawings and Design Documentation.
Support the QA/QC team with inspection records, test documentation and handover packages.




Essential Requirements

Minimum 2–5 years' Document Control experience within construction or engineering projects.
Previous experience on Data Center, Mission Critical, Pharmaceutical, Semiconductor, Energy or Industrial projects is highly advantageous.
Excellent understanding of document control procedures and ISO standards.
Strong experience managing large volumes of technical documentation.
High level of attention to detail and organisational skills.
Ability to work within fast-paced project environments and manage competing priorities.
Excellent communication skills with both internal and external stakeholders.
Fluent English (additional Nordic languages are advantageous).
