Type: Contractor, Remote

Compensation: $50 - $101/hour

Location: United States

Role Responsibilities


Engage in simulated client-event planner dialogues, providing authentic, scenario-based conversations.
Document and articulate standard event planning workflows, challenges, and solutions.
Analyze client needs, propose event solutions, and outline step-by-step planning procedures.
Identify and describe common hurdles and creative problem-solving approaches in event coordination.
Deliver structured feedback on event planning conversations to improve AI system responses.
Contribute written materials exemplifying clear, professional communication for virtual interaction.
Collaborate remotely with the project team to refine project deliverables as needed.


Requirements


Demonstrated expertise in event planning, coordination, and client communication.
Strong problem-solving skills and ability to manage complex event scenarios.
Exceptional organization and time management capabilities.
Excellent written and verbal communication; ability to convey details clearly in virtual formats.
Experience with remote collaboration tools and virtual meetings.
Background in training, coaching, or contributing to professional development content is a plus.
