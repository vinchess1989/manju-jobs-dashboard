Associate to Our Banking & Finance Team

Borenius Asianajotoimisto Oy - Helsinki

Julkaistu 10.8. (3 päivää jäljellä)

Työpaikkakuvaus

Our Banking & Finance team is looking for an Associate to join our firm in a permanent position.

Borenius is a leading Finnish law firm. We employ over 230 specialists and deliver premier expertise across all areas of business law. We also have offices in London and New York.

Could this be your team?

Our top-tier Banking & Finance practice is known for its deep industry expertise and commitment to delivering innovative solutions tailored to our clients' needs. We have a broad team working across corporate finance, leveraged finance, project finance, debt capital markets and real estate finance. We also regularly collaborate on various restructuring assignments with our esteemed insolvency team. Our assignments often require cross-practice cooperation with M&A, capital markets, real estate, tax or projects teams, enabling us to deliver comprehensive and forward-thinking solutions. We are now looking to further strengthen our team to respond even better to the high demands of our clients.

You can learn more about our Banking & Finance team here.

What do you need to succeed?

We expect you to hold a master's degree in law and have the relevant qualifications to practise law in Finland. You may be newly graduated or already have a few years of work experience as a lawyer. Prior experience in banking and finance is not a requirement; however, such experience will be viewed as an advantage. What matters most is that you have a genuine interest in becoming a great finance lawyer, that you take pride in your work and always aim for an outstanding performance.

Proficiency in Finnish and English, both written and spoken, is required because of the nature of our work. Proficiency in other languages is considered an asset.

Why Borenius?

We offer an opportunity to work in a highly recognised and skilled team with some of the most demanding finance-related assignments in the market. This means working with clients that value quality, personal attention and an understanding of their commercial needs. Our team is committed to providing top-quality service in each assignment, and we want our clients to feel that our team is always on top of each assignment and provides best advice in any given situation. In order to meet this goal, we have made internal training and continuous development a top priority for our team.

Our team is built on a strong sense of commitment and genuine collegiality. Our core values of succeeding together, respecting others and taking pride in our work manifest in how we work. Our success is ultimately measured by the success and satisfaction of our people.

In addition to our commitment to applying our values in everything we do, our carefully selected pro bono partners and programmes reflect our commitment to promoting diversity and social inclusion.

Join our team now

Please submit your application in English by 23 August 2026. We will review applications as they are submitted – the sooner you apply, the sooner we can process your application. Please note that we may fill all available positions before the end of the application period.

For additional information, please contact:
Partner Riikka Eronen, riikka.eronen@borenius.com, tel. +358 50 422 6746
Partner Ben Fallström, ben.fallstrom@borenius.com, tel. +358 40 865 2030 

We champion equality and inclusivity in all our practices and encourage individuals from diverse backgrounds to apply. We welcome candidates of all ages, genders, cultural backgrounds and minority groups.
