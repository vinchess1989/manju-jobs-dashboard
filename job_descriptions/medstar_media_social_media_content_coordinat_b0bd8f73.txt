We’re Hiring: Social Media Content Strategist




Remote | Full-Time | Help Shape the Voice of Leading Aesthetic Practices and Medstar Media




We're looking for a creative, strategic, and thoughtful Social Media Content Strategist to join our growing team. This isn't a role for someone who simply schedules posts. We're looking for someone who understands how content supports business growth. Someone who can think beyond trends and create content that builds trust, generates engagement, educates audiences, and ultimately drives new patients. You'll help shape content strategy for both Medstar Media and our portfolio of medical aesthetic clients. You'll work closely with our account management, creative, paid media, and leadership teams to develop ideas that differentiate our clients and position them as leaders in their markets. If you're constantly analyzing what makes content perform, love turning ideas into compelling stories, and enjoy building brands through thoughtful strategy, we'd love to meet you.




What You'll Do - Client Content

Develop strategic monthly content plans aligned with each client's business goals, promotions, and growth initiatives.
Brainstorm original content ideas that educate, entertain, and convert.
Research competitors, industry trends, and emerging content opportunities.
Identify content gaps and recommend new campaigns, recurring series, and creative concepts.
Create detailed content briefs, filming guides, shot lists, and creative direction for clients.
Write engaging captions, hooks, video scripts, and light advertising copy.
Collaborate with designers, editors, and account managers to bring ideas to life.
Review content before publication to ensure quality, consistency, and alignment with each brand.
Analyze content performance and continuously refine future strategy based on results.




Medstar Content

Help grow Medstar Media's own social media presence.
Develop content that showcases our expertise, client successes, marketing insights, company culture, and thought leadership.
Bring fresh ideas that help position Medstar as the leading marketing agency in the aesthetics industry.
Collaborate with leadership on educational content, videos, and larger brand initiatives.




What We're Looking For

2–5 years of experience in social media, content marketing, or digital marketing.
Excellent understanding of Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and short-form video.
Strong strategic thinking with the ability to connect content to business objectives.
Outstanding copywriting skills and the ability to adapt to multiple brand voices.
Highly organized with excellent project management skills.
Naturally curious and proactive. You don't wait for ideas—you generate them.
Comfortable giving creative direction and collaborating across teams.
Strong understanding of what makes content perform organically and through paid promotion.
Passion for branding, storytelling, and creating content people actually want to consume.




Bonus

Agency experience managing multiple brands.
Experience in medical aesthetics, wellness, healthcare, or beauty.
Familiarity with Canva, CapCut, Adobe Creative Suite, or similar content creation tools.
Experience reviewing analytics and making data-informed content recommendations.




What You'll Get

Fully remote work environment.
Opportunity to work with some of the country's leading aesthetic practices.
Creative ownership and the ability to make a real impact.
A collaborative, fast-moving team that values initiative and new ideas.
Significant opportunities for growth as Medstar continues to scale.




How to Apply

Please send the following to ivonne@medstarmedia.com:

Your resume
A brief cover letter explaining why you're a great fit
3–5 examples of content you've created, accounts you've managed, or campaigns you're proud of
(Optional but encouraged) Tell us about a brand whose social media you admire and why.




We're looking for someone who combines creativity with strategy. Someone who understands that great content isn't just beautiful, it's intentional.
