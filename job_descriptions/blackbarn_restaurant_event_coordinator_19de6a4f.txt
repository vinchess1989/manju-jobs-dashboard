BLACKBARN is an American restaurant founded by John Doherty, former Executive Chef of Waldorf Astoria New York. Located at the north end of Madison Square Park in Manhattan's NoMad neighborhood, we are known for seasonal menus, warm hospitality, and versatile event spaces that host everything from corporate meetings to full-scale weddings.




We believe great events start with strong teamwork. Our environment is collaborative, fast paced, and supportive, with a focus on professional growth and genuine hospitality for both our guests and our team.




We are hiring an Event Coordinator to join our events team, reporting to our Director of Events. This person supports the daily operations and growth of our private events business, coordinating events from booking through execution and working alongside our marketing and PR teams on the outreach that brings new business in.




Event Coordinator Responsibilities

Coordinate logistics for events in our private and semi-private dining spaces
Create and manage Banquet Event Orders and maintain accurate event documentation
Process deposits and final payments
Conduct site tours for prospective clients and assist with follow up communication
Serve as a liaison between clients and culinary and operations teams to ensure seamless execution
Support event day execution as needed, including client greeting and on site coordination
Maintain CRM systems such as Tripleseat and assist with reporting and file organization
Assist with office administration including ordering supplies and maintaining sales materials
Support menu updates, proposal templates, and sales kit preparation




Marketing Support

Assist with events related marketing initiatives in collaboration with the PR and social media teams
Draft content for email campaigns and event promotions
Support targeted outreach initiatives and industry events
Be present onsite for select marketing activations and hosted planner events




What we ask of you

You value teamwork and communicate clearly with colleagues across departments
You approach clients and coworkers with professionalism, warmth, and respect
You are organized, detail oriented, and comfortable managing multiple projects at once
You take initiative and follow through
You are interested in building a career in hospitality, events, or sales




Experience

At least 1 year in events, hospitality, or a comparable client-facing role
Familiarity with platforms such as OpenTable, Resy, and Tripleseat preferred
Comfort drafting email and marketing content and familiarity with MailChimp is a plus




Schedule and location

On site at 19 E 26th St, New York, NY. Monday through Friday core schedule, with some evenings and weekends as our clients' events require.




Compensation and benefits

$65,000 to $75,000, paid hourly
Medical, dental, and vision insurance available after 90 days
401(k) eligibility after 90 days
Two weeks paid time off
Commuter benefits
Employee dining discounts
