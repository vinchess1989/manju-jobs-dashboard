Company

NestAI is a European AI lab for defence delivering the adaptive operating system for modern battlefield operations. We develop adaptive intelligence for unmanned and command systems: AI that continuously learns from operational data and adapts to changing conditions.




NestOS is our open, modular and interoperable platform that enables this at scale and keeps capability evolution under sovereign European control. Founded in 2025 by Peter Sarlin, whose previous company Silo AI became Europe's largest AI acquisition, NestAI brings together over 150 engineers and scientists and partners with leading European defence forces and industry.




Who we are looking for

We're looking for a People Partner who combines excellent operational execution with a passion for building a great employee experience as NestAI grows.




You'll partner closely with leaders and employees while helping define what community and employee experience should look like in a fast-growing company. This role combines hands-on People Partnering with ownership of community and workplace experience.




You'll have a significant opportunity to shape how this area develops as NestAI grows. We're looking for someone who enjoys building, improving and challenging existing ways of working, rather than just maintaining them.




You can come from versatility of backgrounds, but ideally you would bring:

Strategic & Operational People Partnering. The ability to partner directly with leaders and employees to build clarity, guide performance, and manage daily employee relations while actively translating high-level People strategies into operational practices.
Employee Experience & Community Building. Proven experience designing scalable community initiatives, company rituals, peer learning forums, and cross-office collaboration systems that connect teams and foster an environment where people can do their best work.
Practical expertise in owning core HR practices—including onboarding, buddy programs, probation milestones, offboarding, and documentation—with a strict eye on compliance and internal communication tools.
A continuous improvement mindset with a interest in simplifying and automating People workflows using modern tools and AI, collaborating cross-functionally with Talent Acquisition, IT, Security, Finance, and Marketing.
An empathetic and analytical approach to using employee feedback and workplace signals to surface organizational trends early and continuously refine the employee experience as the company scales.
Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to build trust, facilitate feedback sessions, and maintain alignment across cross-functional and multi-office environments.




What you'll do




People Partnering

Partner with leaders and employees to create clarity, support performance and help teams succeed.

Partner with leaders on performance, growth and day-to-day people topics
Own milestone and probation practices together with the People team
Support managers in feedback, coaching and employee relations
Drive onboarding and early employee experience within your teams
Ensure company-wide People practices land clearly and consistently
Handle core People processes, documentation and compliance topics
Use employee feedback and day-to-day signals to identify themes, improve practices and raise issues early




Community & employee experience (focus in beginning of employment)

Design and continuously improve the systems, rituals and everyday experiences that make NestAI a place where people can do their best work.




You'll help people connect, collaborate and feel part of one company by:

Developing our buddy experience and helping people build meaningful relationships beyond onboarding
Building scalable community initiatives, peer learning forums and company rituals
Creating opportunities for cross-team and cross-office collaboration
Improving the employee experience across all NestAI offices
Supporting internal communication and our knowledge base (Nestopedia)
Using employee feedback to continuously improve how people experience working at NestAI
Helping define what great community and employee experience look like as we scale

As discussed, the responsibilities are wide and evolve as the company grows.




Building the People team

Like everyone in our team, you'll also help us improve how we work.

Improve and scale People processes
Use AI to simplify and automate People work
Collaborate closely with Talent Acquisition, Marketing, IT, Security and Finance
Take ownership wherever you can make things better




What success looks like

Leaders trust you as a People Partner.
Employees have a consistently great experience from joining onwards.
People feel connected regardless of team or office.
Community grows through scalable systems and shared ways of working, rather than relying just on individual efforts or events.
You help make NestAI an easier, more connected and more enjoyable place to work.
You help define what great employee experience looks like as we continue to grow.




Why join NestAI

At NestAI, you’ll build meaningful, real-world technology as part of a world-class team of engineers, scientists, and experienced professionals. You’ll work on systems where reliability genuinely matters, contribute to Europe’s security and resilience, and solve problems that demand depth, trust, and craftsmanship. If you want to build with purpose — and with leading experts in the field — you’ve come to the right place.




We operate as a deliberately small, AI-augmented team built to move fast and punch well above our weight. You will have real ownership and the tools to operate at a scale that would normally require a much bigger team.
