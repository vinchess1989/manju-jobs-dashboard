WHO WE ARE:

Plug and Play Tech Center is the ultimate innovation platform, bringing together the best startups and the world's largest corporations to create the most meaningful connections that bring innovation to anyone, anywhere. We are on a mission to bring the power of global innovation to every corner of the world. Each year, hundreds of entrepreneurs join our vertical-specific accelerator programs to team up with our corporate members or receive a direct investment from Plug and Play.

WHO WE ARE LOOKING FOR:

An associate drives the execution of Plug and Play’s innovation path with ownership over client-facing projects, startup scouting, and investments. The Associate ensures excellence in delivery, builds trusted relationships with clients and startups, and contributes to measurable business impact.

Responsibilities

Startup Discovery & Market Insights


Lead startup scouting and selection aligned with client needs.
Deliver benchmarking, ecosystem, and trend analysis reports.
Ensure data accuracy and maintain a consistent pipeline of relevant startups.
Supporting deal teams on new investments.
Evaluate and recommend high-potential investment opportunities within the region, ensuring alignment with the global investment thesis.
Execute the regional due diligence, providing strategic insights and critical evaluation of startup performance to the Investment Committee.


Client & Project Management


Execute startup–client matchmaking sessions and follow-ups.
Support event logistics and ensure delivery excellence.
Coordinate startup participation and readiness for client meetings.


Team Support & Collaboration


Mentor Junior Associates and Interns in research and delivery tasks.
Share best practices and cross-vertical learnings within the pod.
Contribute to vertical growth initiatives and knowledge sharing.


TYPICAL BACKGROUND:


Education: Master's degree (MBA, or related field) is preferred, or a Bachelor's degree combined with significant relevant professional experience.
3+ years of experience in the industry, VC, consulting, innovation, or client-facing project management.
Proven ability to deliver measurable outcomes and manage multiple stakeholders.
Finnish language skills are required and an advanced level of English is essential; additional Nordic languages are a plus.
