About This Opportunity

This posting is not tied to a specific open role.

PMI US is building an Early Career Talent Community for individuals interested in entry-level, internship, and emerging talent opportunities that may become available across the business.

By joining this community, you may be considered for future opportunities and may be contacted by our recruiting team as roles align with your interests, skills, and availability.

Why Join Our Talent Community?

Members Of Our Early Career Talent Community May Receive


Early awareness of upcoming entry-level and internship opportunities
Consideration across multiple teams and functions
Invitations to recruiting events, information sessions, or early-career programming
Updates on our hiring process and future opportunities


Joining the community allows us to stay connected as business needs evolve.

Who Should Consider Joining

This community may be a good fit for individuals who:


Are exploring early careers, internships, or entry-level professional opportunities
Are interested in learning more about career paths at PMI US
Want to be considered for future opportunities as they become available


Opportunities may exist across a variety of functions, depending on business needs, including:


Sales & Commercial
Marketing & Brand
Operations & Supply Chain
Finance
IT
Manufacturing & Field Operations


Specific roles, locations, and requirements will vary.

Eligibility & Compliance Notice

Some positions at PMI US involve access to or exposure to age-restricted nicotine and tobacco products.

For These Roles


Candidates must be at least 21 years of age to be eligible for employment


Employment offers are contingent upon meeting all applicable legal, regulatory, and company requirements.

What Happens After You Apply


You will receive confirmation that you have joined our Early Career Talent Community
Your information will be reviewed and retained for consideration for future opportunities
Our recruiting team may reach out as roles become available that align with your background and interests
You will receive Early Careers specific communications on occasion which will vary in topic and purpose


Please note: Timing and frequency of outreach will vary based on business needs.
