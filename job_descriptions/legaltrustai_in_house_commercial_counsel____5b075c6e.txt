Major, Lindsey & Africa’s Interim Legal Talent group has an immediate need for an Interim Contracts Counsel.




Overview: Our client seeks a skilled transactional attorney to assist with a variety of commercial agreements.




Company: Our client is a well-respected global manufacturing organization.




Experience: Five or more years advising on contractual issues acquired in a top law firm and/or in-house environment.




Location: Fully remote; East Coast hours strongly preferred.




Responsibilities Include:

Review, negotiate, and draft myriad commercial agreements including, but not limited to, marketing agreements (including brand ambassador agreements), collaboration agreements, vendor agreements, joint venture agreements, supply chain agreements, and non-disclosure agreements.
Serve as a point of contact for the sales, human resources, and executive teams for questions arising in internal and client agreements.
Conduct legal research as necessary




Qualifications Include:

Excellent academic credentials, including a law degree from an accredited, national law school.
Five or more years acquired in a top law firm and/or in-house environment advising on commercial agreements. (Client had a strong preference for AmLaw training).
Experience advising on marketing agreements and/or brand ambassador agreements.




Pay rate: $95-$100




Information regarding benefits can be found on MLA’s Website on the Consultant Resources Page: https://www.mlaglobal.com/en/consultant-resources




All interested and qualified candidates should apply directly with Major, Lindsey & Africa for review and consideration.

Major, Lindsey & Africa does not discriminate against applications on the basis of age, sex, race or any other protected characteristics pursuant to applicable state or federal laws.
