Position Details

The Clackamas Soil and Water Conservation District (CSWCD) is seeking an innovative, collaborative, and community-minded individual to serve as our newly established Outreach and Communications Coordinator, leading and strengthening the District's public engagement and communications efforts. This position offers a unique opportunity to connect people with the conservation of soil, water, and related natural resources by developing compelling communications, marketing strategies, and fostering meaningful partnerships to expand awareness of the District's mission, programs, and impact throughout Clackamas County.

The deadline for receiving applications is 4 pm PST, August 31, 2026.

For questions regarding the position, please contact Christopher Lapp, District Manager. Phone: 503-210-6001
