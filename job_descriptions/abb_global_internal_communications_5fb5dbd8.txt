Your Role And Responsibilities

We are seeking a dynamic and experienced Global Internal Communications Manager for the Drive Products division in ABB’s Motion Business Area, to location Helsinki. This role is responsible for building and executing a strategic internal communications plan that delivers on business goals and priority programs in line with global internal corporate strategy and guidelines. You will be in a key position to foster employee engagement and build a positive internal culture of Drive Products global team with 3,000+ employees.

You Will Be Mainly Accountable For


Being accountable for developing and implementing the internal communications plan according to the division strategy and the ongoing business priorities
Being a process owner for input collection, writing, editing, and approval process for all internal communications content such as articles/stories, interviews, scripts, videos, news and other initiatives
Being a communications partner and advisor to support our internal stakeholders, including the division’s leadership and management teams. Collaborating with communications and marketing colleagues across organizations, functions and regions - to ensure full alignment between internal and external communication activities
Defining quality and delivery target KPIs, monitoring ongoing progress to track the effectiveness of the implemented tactics and channels and to drive continuous improvement
Ensuring compliance with Motion and Corporate directives and instructions related to internal communications
Being an innovation leader for internal communication processes and channels


Qualifications For The Role


Master’s degree in Communications, Public Relations, Marketing, or related field.
Demonstrated track record in communication roles with 10+ years of experience in an international environment
Self-driven performer and collaborative team member
Exceptional written and verbal communication skills in English
Confident in working with and coaching senior executives, experienced in change management communications
Hands-on experience with MS Office 365, Adobe Creative Cloud programs, AI-tools
Experience and analytical competence with communication metrics, measurement and KPIs


More About Us

ABB Drive Products serves the industries and infrastructure segments with world-class drives and programmable logic controllers (PLC). With its products, global scale and local presence, the Division helps customers to improve energy efficiency, productivity and safety.

Our culture and benefits reflect our values: courage, care, curiosity, and collaboration. The most appreciated benefits according to our personnel are at the moment e.g. an annual performance bonus, employee share acquisition program, continuous learning opportunities, bicycle and other wellbeing and family benefits. The work model for the role is hybrid, but presence at least a few days per week at the Helsinki office is required.

Interested in joining the crew? Please apply for the position by Aug 30, 2026. We look forward to receiving your application.
