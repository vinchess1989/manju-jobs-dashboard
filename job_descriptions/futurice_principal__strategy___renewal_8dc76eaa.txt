About the role

As a Principal Consultant, Strategy & Renewal, you work at the intersection of executive strategy consulting, AI-enabled transformation and business development. In addition to hands-on consulting work with clients, the role requires active involvement in sales, shaping opportunities, and growing client accounts as well as contributing to internal development within the service line.

In client work, you help organizations to lead an AI transformation by designing the strategies, building an impactful business and operations, and co-developing effective and sustainable operating models and the capabilities. Your work typically begins by understanding the client’s current state, defining an ambitious yet realistic target state, creating a feasible strategy and /or roadmap for transformation, and driving change to create impact. You act as a trusted advisor to senior stakeholders and lead multidisciplinary teams.

In this role you will work with Finland’s public and security sector organisations. Your home base will be Helsinki or Tampere, with a flexible hybrid setup and some travel to client sites in the future within the Nordics.

We’re a tech-native, people-centric, outcome-driven strategic advisory team. Our work blends business and technology, and typical project topics include:

AI & technology strategies

Vision and consulting on for how AI transforms business and operations

AI-ready Operating model renewals

Transformation planning and management

Digital and AI-driven business model creation

AI and data opportunity identification and value assessments

Enterprise architecture & capability development in AI era

You don’t need to master all of these areas, but we expect you to feel comfortable taking a leading role across several of them.

What helps you succeed:

Experience with the public sector and/or security sector in consulting delivery projects or in-house roles.

5–10+ years of experience in management consulting or in a senior in-house role driving digital, technology, data or AI strategy and transformation

Experience from the past two years in leveraging AI, and technology for business and process development.

Sales capability in strategic services, especially around AI strategy and business transformation driven by emerging technologies (opportunity shaping, proposals, executive storytelling)

Experience in client management and new business development

Confidence working with C-level stakeholders

Strong storytelling and PowerPoint skills — you can turn complex topics into clear, compelling executive-level narratives

Solid experience designing and facilitating workshops that bring structure, clarity, and alignment

Experience acting as a delivery lead, leading multidisciplinary teams and ensuring quality and outcomes

A relevant university degree (business, technology, industrial engineering, etc.)

Familiarity with business design, service design, lean startup or agile approaches is a plus

Strong communication skills in Finnish and English. Swedish is a benefit as we expand in the Nordics.

You are structured, curious and comfortable with complexity. You enjoy working closely with clients, can confidently engage with senior stakeholders, and are motivated by building both client impact and internal capabilities.

About Futurice

We help organizations unlock new value by combining data, AI, design, and strategy. Our promise is simple: faster, smarter delivery; AI-powered decisions; and exceptional, data-driven experiences. We deliver clarity, and clarity delivers results.

Our vision is to redefine organizations with data and AI. Large enterprises work with us to build smarter services, accelerate decision-making, and strengthen their competitive edge.

Founded in 2000, Futurice has grown into an 800+ person community across Europe. Our culture is built on trust, transparency, care, and continuous improvement. We offer a collaborative, international environment where ambitious people learn, grow, and make a real impact.
