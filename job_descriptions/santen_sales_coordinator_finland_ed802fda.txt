Company Description

Santen is a specialized company with a proud 130-year heritage focused exclusively on eye health. As a Japan-originated, global company with our footprint in over 60 countries and regions, our mission is to provide essential and significant value to patients and society through our products and services created from our expertise in ophthalmology and from the patient’s perspective.

Job Description

We're looking for a proactive and detail-oriented Sales Coordinator to provide operational and administrative support to our Finland Sales organization, working closely with the Commercial Manager and Key Account Managers. In this pivotal role, you'll ensure the smooth coordination of customer-related activities for ophthalmology clinics and pharmacies, organize major events from planning through execution, manage vendor and healthcare professional agreements, and oversee purchase orders and invoices in the Coupa system. If you enjoy coordinating multiple priorities, building strong relationships, and keeping operations running seamlessly, we'd love to hear from you.

What You Will Do


Operational and administrative support for the Country Manager and Key Account Managers
Coordination of large customer events and local exhibitions
Customer communication regarding product changes and product availability
Management of healthcare professional contracts, including collecting required information and coordinating publication on the website
Uploading marketing materials to Veeva PromoMats for compliance review and coordinating the review process with the Country Manager
Reporting medicinal product samples to Finnish Medicines Agency (Fimea)
Supporting the adaptation of marketing materials and coordinating technical aspects of localization projects
Coordination of internal meetings for the Finnish sales team
Supporting the implementation and use of compliance systems, such as Engagemate


Qualifications

What you will bring to the role


At least 1–2 years of experience in a similar coordination or support role, preferably supporting a Sales team and/or Country Manager
Fluency in Finnish and English, both written and spoken
Service-minded with a strong customer focus
Strong team player with excellent collaboration skills
Well-organized and systematic, with great attention to detail
Ability to plan, prioritize, and manage multiple tasks effectively
Self-motivated, proactive, and able to work independently
Excellent time management and organizational skills
Structured approach to work with the ability to meet deadlines
Strong communication and coordination skills


What We Offer


Competitive Salary: A market‑aligned base salary starting from €55,255.00 – €75,975.00, depending on experience and location.
