We are looking for an organized, proactive, and service-oriented Office Assistant for our client in Mäntsälä to support HR and administrative operations at a manufacturing site. In this role, you will work closely with employees, managers, payroll, occupational health services, and other stakeholders to ensure smooth and efficient daily operations.




Role Overview




The position combines HR administration, payroll support, reporting, training coordination, and general site administration. You will maintain employee records, support payroll processes, prepare HR and EHS reports, and serve as a key user for HR-related systems. You will also provide practical support to employees and managers and help ensure that HR processes are carried out accurately and in compliance with company policies.




A key part of the role is coordinating training activities and employee services. This includes arranging internal training sessions, managing registrations and logistics, supporting onboarding and offboarding processes, and coordinating activities with occupational health care providers. In addition, you will assist with visitor arrangements, employee benefits administration, and various site-related administrative tasks.




You will also contribute to employee engagement and workplace wellbeing by supporting personnel events, recognition programs, and wellbeing initiatives. Furthermore, you will help promote a strong culture of quality, hygiene, safety, and compliance throughout the site. The role requires on-site work in Mäntsälä, especially at the beginning to ensure a successful onboarding.




Main Responsibilities




Maintain HR records and personnel documentation
Support payroll administration and related reporting
Prepare HR, EHS, and other administrative reports
Coordinate training programs and employee development activities
Support onboarding, offboarding, and occupational health processes
Provide administrative support to employees and managers
Assist in organizing employee events and wellbeing initiatives
Promote compliance with company quality, safety, and hygiene standards




Qualifications




Relevant education in HR, Business Administration, or a related field
Minimum two years of experience in HR, administration, payroll support, or a similar role
Knowledge of employment legislation and HR processes
Ability to handle confidential information with discretion
Strong organizational and communication skills
Good computer and data management skills
Fluent written and spoken Finnish & English




Experience with SAP or other HR/ERP systems and previous experience in a manufacturing environment are considered an advantage.




What We Offer




We offer a versatile role in an international manufacturing environment where you will have the opportunity to contribute to both employee experience and business performance. You will be part of a collaborative team and have the opportunity to develop your skills across a broad range of HR and administrative activities. The salary range for this position is approximately €2,800-€3,600 per month, depending on the selected candidate’s experience and qualifications.




More information




This role is fixed-term for approximately 3 months, and the employment is done through Poolia Finland Oy. Please send us your resume and cover letter through Poolia’s website by clicking the Hae Työpaikkaa button. Please note that we don’t accept applications sent via email.

The position will be filled, and the job advertisement closed, as soon as the right candidate is found. If you have any questions, Poolia’s consultants, Vera (vera@poolia.fi) and Sakari (sakari@poolia.fi) will be happy to help!
