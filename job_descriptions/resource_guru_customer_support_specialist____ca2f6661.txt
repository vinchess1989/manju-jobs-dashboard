👋 Meet The Guru

Resource Guru is a blissfully simple scheduling tool that helps busy teams stay on track.

We boost efficiency, improve resource utilisation, and increase project profitability - while promoting a healthy work/life balance.

We're proud to be one of the leaders in our category, trusted by customers in over 100 countries - including top creative agencies like Publicis, TBWA, Grey, BBH, and M&C Saatchi, as well as household names like NASA, Accenture, CNN, Kraft Heinz, and the NHS. They rely on Resource Guru to manage one of their most valuable assets - their people's time.

We're a small, fully remote team based across the UK and Europe. We collaborate mainly via Slack, with occasional meetups to connect, plan, and celebrate. See if this is the right place for you.

⭐️ The role

We're looking for a Customer Support Specialist to join our team.

As more teams use Resource Guru and as we continue to support larger, more complex customers we want to make sure every customer receives fast, clear, thoughtful and genuinely helpful support.

This role is primarily about helping customers by email, understanding their questions, troubleshooting issues, and making sure they get the best possible experience from Resource Guru.

You'll work closely with Dana, our Senior Customer Support Specialist, and collaborate with our product and engineering teams when customer questions, bugs or feature requests need to be investigated further. We see Customer Support as a core part of building a great product, so you'll work closely with the rest of the team to help improve Resource Guru, not just answer support tickets.

This is a great role for someone with some experience in SaaS or online customer support who wants to keep developing their technical, communication and customer-facing skills in a friendly, remote-first team.

🤝 What You'll Be Doing

Learning Resource Guru inside out


In your first few months, you'll get to know Resource Guru inside out - how customers use the product, what common questions come up, and how to guide people towards the right answer


Responding to customer questions


Help customers with day-to-day support questions, including product usage, account setup, billing enquiries, troubleshooting and general "how do I...?" queries
Most support is handled by email, though you may occasionally join a phone or video call when that would help resolve something more quickly


Troubleshooting problems


Investigate customer issues carefully and methodically - asking the right follow-up questions, checking account settings, trying to reproduce a problem, looking for browser-related issues, or escalating a bug to engineering with clear notes


Working with the wider team


Work closely with Dana and the wider Resource Guru team to understand customer questions, resolve issues, and make sure useful feedback is shared with Product and Engineering
Help create clear internal reports when customers report bugs or technical issues, and tag/categorise feature feedback so the team understands what customers need


Improving our help content


Help keep the Help Centre useful and up to date - updating existing articles, writing new ones, recording simple walkthroughs, or suggesting improvements when you notice customers asking the same questions repeatedly


Using our support tools


Use tools such as Front, Recurly, Customer.io, HubSpot and other internal systems to manage customer conversations, billing questions and account information. We don't expect you to know all of these already, but you should be comfortable learning new software quickly


Helping customers feel looked after


A big part of this role is making sure customers feel heard, understood and helped. We care about support that is accurate and efficient, but also warm, human and thoughtful


💎 What We're Looking For

Essential Experience


1-3 years' experience in online customer support, ideally for a B2B SaaS or similar software company
Excellent written and spoken English and the ability to explain things clearly, simply and warmly
A calm, patient and empathetic approach to helping customers
Good attention to detail, especially when dealing with billing, account or technical questions
A practical approach to troubleshooting and problem-solving
Confidence learning new software tools and understanding how web-based products work
Some familiarity with basic technical concepts such as browsers, cache, cookies, internet connection issues and permissions
The ability to work independently in a remote team and manage your time well
A collaborative and conscientious attitude and willingness to share useful customer feedback with product, engineering and the wider team
Comfort with asynchronous communication and keeping written conversations clear, concise and on track


You don't need to be a developer, but you should be curious about technology and comfortable investigating how things work.

Bonus (not Required)

It would be a bonus if you have experience with any of the following:


Supporting a SaaS product
Helping customers with billing, subscriptions or invoices
Writing Help Centre articles or customer-facing guides
Using support tools such as Front, Intercom, Zendesk, HubSpot or similar
Working in a fully remote team
Recording short customer help videos or walkthroughs
Supporting customers across multiple time zones, especially APAC-friendly hours
Comfortable using AI tools to improve the quality and efficiency of your work


Working Hours

This is a remote role, for people based in either the UK or Canada (Atlantic Time Zone only). You'll also need regular overlap with the UK team for training, collaboration and handovers.

The exact working pattern will depend on your location and the coverage we agree, but you should be clear in your application about where you're based and what working hours you would expect to keep.
