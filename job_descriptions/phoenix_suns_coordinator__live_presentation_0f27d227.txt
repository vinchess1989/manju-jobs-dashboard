Job Summary

Player 15 Group is the sports and entertainment company behind the Phoenix Suns (NBA), Phoenix Mercury (WNBA), Valley Suns (G League), and Mortgage Matchup Center.

Headquartered in downtown Phoenix, Player 15 Group is driven by a culture built on passion, creativity, innovation, and care. Our people are at the center of everything we do, united by a shared commitment to create unforgettable memories for our fans, partners, and community.

We offer a fast-paced, collaborative environment where every team member contributes to the success of our teams, the experiences of our fans, and the impact we make throughout our community.

We are seeking an experienced and motivated Live Presentation Coordinator to join our team. In this role, you will support all facets of Live Presentation for Phoenix Suns, Phoenix Mercury, and Valley Suns, while playing a key role in broader department initiatives.

Your Role on the Team


Create and produce Phoenix Suns games. Responsibilities include developing game-night rundowns (ROS), writing scripts, and coordinating show execution
Serve as a Stage Manager for Phoenix Suns, Mercury, Valley Suns, and select external events, supporting activations, promotions, talent, and event logistics
Serve as an alternate Game Director for select Phoenix Suns games
Assist the Manager with hiring, training, and overseeing Game Night Staff across the Phoenix Suns, Phoenix Mercury, and Valley Suns. Maintain monthly schedules, game-day assignments, and payroll-hour processing
Oversee all promotional-item inventory, including organizing, maintaining, and curating items for game-day activations, promotions, and events
Act as the primary point of contact for Group Sales to ensure all fan engagement platform (FEP) activations are executed at a high level for Phoenix Suns games
Assist in managing and maintaining departmental invoicing, expense tracking, and budgeting for Phoenix Suns Live Presentation categories


Your Skills And Experience


Ability to take direction, show initiative, and remain flexible as gameday and event needs change
Comfortable working in an active, high-energy environment that may require extended periods of standing and moving throughout the venue
Strong attention to detail, excellent organizational skills and problem-solving abilities, with the ability to manage multiple priorities at once
Ability to work independently, take initiative, and remain proactive while also contributing effectively as part of a collaborative team
Proven ability to motivate, support, and build positive relationships with staff and performers
Enthusiasm for live sports, entertainment, events, and fan engagement, with a desire to gain hands-on experience
Able to work odd hours including weekends, evenings, and some holidays
High school diploma or GED
Two or more years of related experience in sporting events; previous NBA experience highly preferred
Ability to transit throughout the arena for long periods of time
This position works mainly indoors, removed from extreme weather; exposure to weather is
S-Sedentary Work – Exerting up to 10 pounds of force occasionally
Must be able to carry on a conversation both on the phone and in-person
Ability to Read, Write & Speak in English


The Team & Experience

We believe great organizations are built by great people. That’s why we foster an in-person collaborative, people-first work environment where team members feel supported, empowered, and connected. We value individuals who bring an ownership mindset, lead with initiative, and embrace an “I’ll do it” mentality in support of the team and our fans.

From career growth opportunities to everyday experiences around the office, we work hard to create an environment where people genuinely enjoy coming to work.
