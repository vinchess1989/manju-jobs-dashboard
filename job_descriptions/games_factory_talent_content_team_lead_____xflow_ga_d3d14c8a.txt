We are looking for an experienced leader on behalf of one of our clients, a fast-growing mobile games studio, casual genre, around 8 years on the market, top 10 worldwide in its category, profitable and self-sustained.




What you'll do

Contribute to and help shape the app's content strategy, aligned with product and business goals
Plan and coordinate the monthly release of seasonal, regional, and event-based visual content — holidays, celebrations, partner collaborations
Propose and develop new content ideas, formats, and initiatives to increase engagement
Own the end-to-end production cycle for content, from task setup to publication
Set up review and quality-control processes to ensure content meets product standards
Lead a small, growing content team: set goals, distribute responsibilities and resources
Prioritize across multiple concurrent content initiatives and coordinate closely with other departments, including design and product
Act as product owner for content-related features and internal tools used by the team, define requirements, manage the backlog, work with development and design




Requirements

Fluent Russian (C1/B2), internal documentation and communication run in Russian

Prior experience managing a content or creative team, even a small one; this is a people-management role, not an entry-level position

Strong visual literacy and taste:composition, image quality, layout, and how content reads within a product

This is a visual / product content role, distinct from marketing, creative, or user-acquisition content, not a fit for candidates from a pure UA or creative-ads background

You are based in the EU.




Benefits

Remote-first role with flexibility around office visits
Competitive compensation, based on experience
A direct voice in shaping the studio's content strategy and roadmap
