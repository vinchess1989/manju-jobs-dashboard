Role Responsibilities


Draft original multiple-choice questions on Finnish law for an AI evaluation dataset.
Create exam-style questions with ten answer options and a worked solution.
Work independently with flexible hours to meet deadlines.
Collaborate with AI research teams to ensure question quality and relevance.

Native-level Finnish.
