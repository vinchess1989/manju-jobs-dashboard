About The Job

Mercor connects elite creative and technical talent with leading AI research labs. Headquartered in San Francisco, our investors include Benchmark, General Catalyst, Peter Thiel, Adam D'Angelo, Larry Summers, and Jack Dorsey.

Position: Moonlight MCQA - Finnish Law

Type: Contract

Compensation: $39–$47/hour

Location: Remote

Role Responsibilities


Draft original multiple-choice questions on Finnish law for an AI evaluation dataset.
Create exam-style questions with ten answer options and a worked solution.
Work independently with flexible hours to meet deadlines.
Collaborate with AI research teams to ensure question quality and relevance.


Qualifications

Must-Have


Law degree or Finnish equivalent.
2+ years practising law.
Educated or based in Finland.
Native-level Finnish.
