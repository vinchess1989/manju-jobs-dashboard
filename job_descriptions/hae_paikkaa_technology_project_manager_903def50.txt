Technology Project Manager

More about the job

Your role in our team

Bidding Projects & Proposal Management (Pre-Sales)

Lead Technical Proposals: Own the end-to-end technical coordination for new licensing RFIs, and RFPs across the licensed technologies portfolio (e.g., incoming requests facilitated through internal commercial teams or external partners).
Technical Packages: Manage engineering resources to compile accurate technical proposals, cost estimations, and technical annexes required for bids and Technology Licensing Agreements.
Sales Support: Act as the operational technical support during bidding and customer due diligence processes, clarifying technical boundaries, and coordinating technical Q&A sessions.

Feasibility Studies & Technology Transfer (Execution)

Project Management: Plan, schedule, and execute technical feasibility studies for customers to validate the viability of specific Neste technologies under local conditions.
Licensor Delivery: Manage high-quality project delivery for technology licenses (e.g., traditional engineering packages or standardized equipment products), ensuring Neste fulfills its contractual duties as licensor.
Services: Plan and coordinate licensor support services, including site visits and technical test runs at the licensed plants.

Partner & Alliance Management

Alliance Technical Alignment: Manage day-to-day operational and engineering collaboration with strategic partners (such as product owners and developers) to align on productization packages and engineering delivery.
Operational Interface: Act as the primary technical point of contact for routine alignment and weekly/monthly operational calls with technology partners and joint ventures.
Data Package Ownership: Take ownership of creating, structuring, and maintaining shareable technical data packages (Pre/Post-NDA data rooms and marketing/technical materials) to ensure commercial teams always have updated productization data.

Technical Coordination & Internal Integration

Resource Coordination: Secure and manage engineering, research, and partner resources (internal engineering teams or EPCM partners) to ensure project milestones are met without creating organizational bottlenecks.
R&D and IP Implementation: Act as the execution link for technology roadmaps; implement defined technical priorities (such as feedstock validation, catalyst performance, side-stream handling, or upgrading criteria) into the practical project delivery packages and customer feasibility criteria.

The best combination to succeed in this role

Relevant university degree in the field of chemical engineering
Over 10 years of experience in process technology, process engineering, technology project management and/or process technology licensing to refinery, petrochemical, chemical and/or bio-industries
Knowledge and experience in preparing and negotiating sales and licensing contracts
Desire for long-term professional growth in a business where the learning curve takes several years
Full professional proficiency in English (written and spoken) is required. Proficiency in Finnish and Swedish is considered a strong asset, and additional language skills are highly valued.
Good presentation and communication skills
Responsible attitude and safety-oriented mindset

We value that you possess a strong sense of ownership and the ability to drive initiatives and projects, while maintaining a practical, results-oriented mindset. Project Management Professional (PMP) or other project management certification is a valuable asset in this position.

Neste in brief

Neste (NESTE, Nasdaq Helsinki) is the world's leading producer of renewable diesel and sustainable aviation fuel (SAF), with production on three continents. The company’s renewables production capacity is expected to reach 6.8 million tons annually in 2027. Neste also produces high-quality oil products at its Porvoo refinery in Finland. The company has a network of nearly 1,000 fuel stations with expanding service offering, such as EV charging, in Finland and in the Baltics.

Neste’s strategy focuses on growth in renewable fuels, which help its customers to reduce their greenhouse gas emissions. Neste is included in many international indices for its sustainability performance. In 2025, Neste's revenue stood at EUR 19.0 billion. Read more: neste.com
