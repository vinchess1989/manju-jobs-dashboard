Roschier Talent Academy
 Application Deadline: Sept. 29, 2026  Helsinki  On-site

Shape what’s next

Curious about where a career in business law could take you? Roschier Talent Academy gives you the opportunity to experience it firsthand. Put your studies into practice, gain insight into the legal profession and develop skills and perspectives that will help shape your future career.

Built for the curious

Roschier Talent Academy – for you who are curious about a career in business law.

Being the leading law firm by far doesn’t happen by chance. It comes from the same energy and ambition that drive this city forward – in people who stay curious, think big, and refuse to stand still. At Roschier, we believe in potential: in nurturing ambition, supporting continuous learning, and building something meaningful, together. Your legal future is full of possibilities. Make it yours. We're not just looking for the best – we're looking for the bold. And we think that's you.

Roschier Talent Academy offers an insider’s view into the world of business law. Over three days, you’ll experience life at a leading law firm through inspiring presentations, engaging discussions and hands-on learning. You’ll explore different areas of business law, take on a challenging case study, develop professional skills with industry experts and connect with both Roschier lawyers and fellow students. The program will give you a deeper understanding of how a law firm operates as a business, the complexity of client work and the dynamics of the consultant role.

Shape what's next – apply today.

When? 28–30 October @ Roschier's office, Helsinki

For whom? For law students who are curious about a career in business law and are at the mid-to-end stages of their studies, minimum of 90 study credits

When to apply? Send in your application by 29 September 2026 through the application link and attach your CV, short cover letter, and University transcript.
