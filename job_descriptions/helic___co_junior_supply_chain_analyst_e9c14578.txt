Job Summary


We are seeking a detail-oriented and analytical Junior Supply Chain Analyst to support our supply chain operations. In this role, you will assist in data analysis, inventory management, demand forecasting, and process optimization to ensure efficient and cost-effective movement of goods. This is an excellent opportunity for someone looking to build a career in supply chain management.



Key Responsibilities

Collect, analyze, and interpret supply chain data to identify trends and inefficiencies

Assist in demand forecasting and inventory planning

Monitor inventory levels and help prevent stock shortages or overstock situations

Support procurement and logistics teams with data insights and reporting

Prepare regular reports on supply chain performance (KPIs, costs, delivery times)

Collaborate with cross-functional teams including procurement, warehousing, and transportation

Identify opportunities for process improvements and cost savings

Maintain accurate records in supply chain systems and databases


Qualifications

Bachelor’s degree in Supply Chain Management, Business Administration, Logistics, or a related field

0–2 years of experience in supply chain, logistics, or data analysis (internships count)

Strong analytical and problem-solving skills

Proficiency in Microsoft Excel (pivot tables, VLOOKUP, etc.); knowledge of SQL or data tools is a plus

Familiarity with ERP systems is an asset

Excellent communication and organizational skills

Ability to work independently and in a team environment


Preferred Skills

Basic understanding of supply chain processes (procurement, inventory, distribution)

Experience with data visualization tools (e.g., Power BI, Tableau)

Attention to detail and ability to manage multiple tasks
