About Evondos Group

Evondos Group is the European market leader in medication management, with over 500 employees working across the Nordics, the UK, and the Netherlands. Our solutions help tens of thousands of people take their medication safely and independently in their own homes, improving patient safety and reducing the burden on healthcare services.

This is the role

We are looking for a Quality Manager to lead our Quality team in Finland and ensure the effective implementation, maintenance, and continuous improvement of the Evondos Quality Management System (QMS) across Finland operations.

This role is responsible for the day-to-day execution of quality activities covering manufacturing, supplier quality, service quality, and post-market quality processes. You will work closely with local stakeholders and Group Quality & Regulatory Affairs to ensure that quality processes are effective, compliant, and continuously improving.

This is a hands-on leadership role for someone who understands how quality works in practice and can translate quality requirements into efficient, scalable business processes. You will play a key role in developing quality performance, supporting continuous improvement, and leading a team that helps ensure product quality and patient safety.

What you will spend your time on


Leading and developing the Finland Quality team
Ensuring effective implementation, maintenance, and continuous improvement of the Evondos QMS across Finland operations
Supporting quality activities across manufacturing, supplier quality, service quality, and post-market quality processes
Overseeing nonconformance management, CAPA processes, change control, and quality investigations
Monitoring quality performance, trends, and continuous improvement initiatives
Supporting internal and external audits and ensuring a high level of audit readiness
Collaborating closely with Operations, Production, Supply Chain, Service Operations, R&D, Product Management, Customer Support, and Group Quality & Regulatory Affairs
Acting as the primary Quality partner for Finland operations and local management teams


Why you will enjoy it

Quality is at the core of everything we do at Evondos. In this role, your work will directly contribute to product quality, patient safety, and the continued growth of a leading medical technology company.

You will have the opportunity to shape how quality is executed in day-to-day operations while working closely with colleagues across multiple functions and countries. This is a visible role with real impact and the opportunity to build a strong quality culture throughout the organization.

Our headquarters and production facilities are located in Salo, Finland, and we support flexible hybrid working arrangements.

This could be you

You may have a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Engineering, Life Sciences, or another relevant field, combined with several years of experience in quality roles within a regulated industry, ideally medical devices.

Beyond that, we are looking for someone who:


Has strong knowledge of ISO 13485 and quality requirements related to EU MDR
Has experience with quality management systems, audits, CAPA, and change control
Has previous people leadership or team management experience
Supports quality performance through consistent execution and follow-through
Applies a pragmatic and structured approach to problem-solving
Can translate quality requirements into practical and effective ways of working that support both compliance and business performance
Builds strong relationships across functions and works effectively in a matrix organization
Demonstrates integrity, consistency, and a strong commitment to patient safety and product quality
Uses data to support decision-making and continuous improvement efforts


Fluency in English is required. Finnish language skills are highly beneficial.

What we offer


A key leadership role in a growing international medical technology company
The opportunity to influence and improve quality operations across Finland
Meaningful work that directly contributes to patient safety and independent living
An international and collaborative working environment
Flexible hybrid working arrangements
The opportunity to develop both people and processes while supporting continuous improvement
