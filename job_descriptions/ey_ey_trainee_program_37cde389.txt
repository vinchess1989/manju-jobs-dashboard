Työpaikkakuvaus

Introduction 

Imagine your first day at EY: you meet your own team, receive an introduction to new tools, and learn about the types of client projects you will soon be involved in. Perhaps already during your first week, you take part in a project meeting where the team discusses how to develop a client’s business, prepares the next steps of an audit, or looks into a tax-related question. 

EY’s trainee role is an opportunity designed for students and recent graduates to see what expert work looks like in practice. As a trainee, you are not an outside observer, but part of a team where you learn by doing, ask questions with confidence, and build your expertise step by step. Your interests may lie in consulting, audit, tax, law, or strategy. Regardless of where your career path is taking you, the trainee period offers an opportunity to explore, learn, and find your own way to make an impact. 

Key Responsibilities 

A trainee’s working days consist of varied tasks that allow you to combine learning new things with hands-on work. On one day, you might prepare materials for a client meeting; on another, analyze data to support the project team; and on a third, participate in an internal workshop where solutions to a client challenge are being developed. 

In practice, your work may include conducting background research, preparing reports and presentation materials, gathering and analyzing information, and supporting client projects and engagements. The tasks vary depending on the team and business area, but what they have in common is that you get to take part in real work and see how your contribution fits into a larger whole. 

Expectations for Applicants 

You may be in the final stages of your studies, recently graduated, or looking for your first work experience in your field. Perhaps you have already noticed that you enjoy problem-solving, working with numbers, collaborating with people, or absorbing new information. In our trainee roles, you will have the opportunity to develop all these skills in practice. 

The most important thing is not that you already know everything. What matters more is that you are ready to learn, ask questions, and take on new situations. At EY, you will be surrounded by colleagues who help you understand why things are done and how your work connects to the client’s goals. 

We value curiosity, initiative, collaboration skills, analytical thinking, and a willingness to grow. At EY, diverse backgrounds are seen as a strength, because our clients’ questions are diverse as well. That is why it is worth carefully reviewing each job description and considering which role would allow you to best use your strengths and learn something new. 

What We Offer 

At EY, the trainee period can be the moment when theory begins to turn into practice. The moment when you realize you understand the question behind a client meeting. When you receive your first area of responsibility in a project. When a colleague takes the time to explain a difficult topic in a way that finally makes it click. 

We offer you an environment where learning does not happen alone. You will be supported by experienced colleagues as well as peers who are learning and growing alongside you — in a culture where sharing ideas, asking questions, and learning together are part of everyday life. You will get involved in real client projects and see how experts from different fields solve questions that matter to our clients. 

With us, a trainee is not left on the sidelines. You will receive responsibility at a suitable pace, feedback to support your development, and the opportunity to explore the kinds of career paths that can be built at EY. At the same time, you will become part of a global organization where your own direction can start to take shape during the first months. 

Application Instructions

Explore our open trainee positions and apply by 20 September through EY’s careers website. Please read the job description carefully before applying, as it contains more detailed information about the role, the stages of the application process, and the required application documents. Once we have received your application, you will receive confirmation by email. In most of our application processes, the next steps include gamified ability tests and/or a video interview. The salary information can be found in each job posting.

If you have any questions about the application process, you can contact EY’s recruitment team at rekrytointi@fi.ey.com.
