Räätälöi ansioluetteloni
Corvid Technologies is seeking an organized, detail-oriented Engineering Document Coordinator to support our vehicle avionics engineering team. This position will support the preparation, control, and archival of technical documentation. The role plays a critical part in ensuring the accuracy, compliance, and traceability of avionics-related procedures, drawings, test reports, and integration records in support of flight and ground vehicle development programs.

The ideal candidate will have experience working in a fast-paced aerospace environment and be familiar with engineering documentation processes, especially those related to avionics systems, integration, and verification.

Responsibilities May Include


Collaborate with avionics engineers to assist in drafting, formatting, and finalizing technical documents including test procedures, wiring diagrams, interface control documents (ICDs), and system reports
Ensure that engineering procedures are completed correctly, reviewed, and approved according to program standards
Maintain document version control and ensure accurate tracking of changes throughout the engineering lifecycle
Assemble and manage comprehensive document packages for design reviews, integration milestones, and program closeout
Support configuration management and ensure alignment between physical and digital documentation
Coordinate the proper storage and archiving of all avionics-related documentation, ensuring accessibility and security
Act as the primary point of contact for document control within the vehicle avionics engineering team, interfacing with systems engineering, quality, and program management as needed
Ensure document consistency with organizational templates, style guides, and data management standards
Travel up to 15% of the time to customer sites for installations, testing, and support


Requirements


Candidate must be a US Citizen and be able to obtain (and maintain) a Secret Clearance
Position requires at least an Associates Degree in a related discipline (preferred candidate will have a Bachelor's Degree in a STEM related field)
2+ years of experience in document coordination or technical writing within the aerospace or defense industry
Familiarity with avionics systems, integration processes, and related documentation practices; preferred candidate will have experience with flight hardware or spacecraft avionics
Demonstrated proficiency with Microsoft Office Suite and PDF tools
Solid understanding of industry documentation standards such as AS9100, MIL-STD-31000, and IEEE standards
Effective communicator, comfortable working with engineers, technicians, and program stakeholders
Knowledge of electrical schematics and avionics architecture documentation as well as configuration management systems is a plus


About Corvid

Corvid Technologies is an engineering firm specializing in using high-fidelity, computational modeling and simulation to analyze, design and manufacture products for aerospace, DoD, and commercial customers. We offer a fast-paced and flexible work environment that encourages creativity and collaboration.
