Sustainability Specialist, Communications and Certification
 Application Deadline: Sept. 15, 2026  Mäntsälä  Hybrid

Do you want to help build a more sustainable everyday life for millions of customers?

We are looking for a Sustainability Specialist, Communications and Certification to join Tokmanni Group's Sustainability team.

In this expert role, you will combine sustainability, communications, and business understanding to strengthen our sustainability performance, compliance, and reputation. You will play a key role in making complex sustainability topics understandable, engaging, and credible for both internal and external stakeholders.

The position reports to the Group Head of Sustainability and involves close collaboration with colleagues across sustainability, marketing, communications, sourcing, and other business functions.

Your key responsibilities

Certification and Compliance

Manage third-party certification schemes, such as FSC, PEFC, Better Cotton, and RSPO.
Develop and implement policies and processes related to certified sourcing and high-risk raw materials.
Monitor compliance requirements and maintain documentation for audits and reporting purposes.

Sustainability Communications

Create clear and engaging sustainability-related content for websites, social media, campaigns, and stakeholder communications.
Support internal communications and training initiatives that increase awareness and understanding of Tokmanni Group's sustainability ambitions.
Prepare presentations and contribute to responses for sustainability-related questionnaires and stakeholder requests.

Marketing Integration

Collaborate with marketing teams to highlight sustainability initiatives and certified products in campaigns and product launches.
Ensure that sustainability claims are accurate, transparent, and aligned with brand messaging.
Develop compelling storytelling around sustainability achievements, product lifecycles, and certification programs.

What we are looking for

To succeed in this role, you have:

A relevant university degree in sustainability, communications, business, environmental sciences, social sciences, or a related field.
Experience in sustainability, communications, marketing, or related specialist roles.
A strong understanding of the sustainability landscape, relevant standards, and legislation.
Excellent communication and storytelling skills.
Fluent Finnish and English skills; Swedish is considered an advantage.
The ability to translate technical sustainability topics into clear and meaningful messages for different audiences.
Strong project management and stakeholder engagement skills.
A structured, proactive, and solution-oriented way of working.

This is a permanent position, starting as soon as possible by agreement. The position will be filled as soon as a suitable candidate is found. The weekly working hours are 37.5 h/week. The primary workplace is Tokmanni Group's Head Office in Mäntsälä, Finland, and the role follows the Group's hybrid working model. Occasional travel within the Group may be required.

What we offer

We offer you a unique opportunity to work at the intersection of sustainability, communications, and certification in one of Finland's leading retail groups. In this impactful role, you will contribute to strengthening Tokmanni Group's sustainability performance and reputation while collaborating with a wide range of stakeholders.

You will join a supportive team where collaboration and shared success are valued. We offer a flexible hybrid working model that supports both teamwork and focused individual work.

In addition, we provide a competitive package of employee benefits, including ePassi, staff discounts, and a wide range of partner benefits.

About the company

More than 6,000 employees in Finland, Sweden and Denmark make customers’ everyday life and special occasions easier by offering a versatile and up-to-date assortment of nordic and international brand-name products and other high-quality products at prices that are always affordable.

With more than 390 Tokmanni, Dollarstore, Big Dollar, Click Shoes and Shoe House stores and online stores, the Group is always close to its customers. In addition, the Tokmanni Group has had exclusive rights to sell SPAR products and operate the SPAR brand in Finland since 2025.

In 2025, the Group’s revenue was EUR 1,728 million and comparable EBIT amounted to EUR 85 million. The Tokmanni Group Corporation’s shares are listed on Nasdaq Helsinki.

For us at Tokmanni Group, it is essential to operate in accordance with our values, caring for both the environment and people. We encourage and support our staff in developing their skills and foster a positive team spirit.

We continuously open new stores and recruit talented individuals with diverse skill backgrounds for various job positions.
