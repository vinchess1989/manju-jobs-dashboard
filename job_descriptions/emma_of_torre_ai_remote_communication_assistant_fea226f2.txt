I’m helping Charles Perez LLC find a top candidate to join their team full-time for the role of Remote Communication Assistant.




You will drive operational clarity by managing professional communication and coordination.




Compensation:

USD 1.5K - 4K/month

+ Commissions (~USD300/month)

+ Bonuses (up to 10% of base compensation)




Location:

Remote (for Australia, United Arab Emirates, United Kingdom, Singapore, and Switzerland residents)




Mission of Charles Perez LLC:

"Empower businesses with innovative software solutions that drive efficiency, scalability, and sustainable growth."




What makes you a strong candidate:

You are proficient in Video calls, Online research, Online communication.
English - Conversational.




Responsibilities and more:

We are looking for a reliable Remote Communication Assistant who can support online communication, coordination, and daily administrative tasks. The ideal candidate should be a strong communicator, quick thinker, organized, professional, and available to work online with consistency.




Responsibilities:

- Communicate professionally with clients, team members, and external contacts through chat and online calls.

- Respond to messages clearly, politely, and in a timely manner.

- Organize follow-ups.

- Provide daily updates about completed tasks, pending items, and important conversations.

- Understand instructions quickly.

- Ask clear questions when something is unclear.

- Use good judgment when handling conversations and representing the company or individual professionally.

- Maintain confidentiality and handle sensitive information with care.




Requirements:

- Strong written and verbal communication skills.

- Good English speaking and writing ability.

- Reliable internet connection and ability to work remotely.

- Enough availability to respond during agreed working hours.

- Strong sense of responsibility, professionalism, and attention to detail.

- Ability to think clearly, solve simple problems, and make smart communication decisions.

- Friendly, respectful, and patient attitude.
