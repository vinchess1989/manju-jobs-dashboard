A rapidly growing manufacturing company is seeking a skilled Front-End Developer to join their dynamic team. They value a collaborative environment and are committed to delivering top-tier digital experiences. In this role, you will play a crucial part in developing and maintaining their web applications. You will work closely with their design and back-end teams to deliver high-quality, scalable, and user-friendly interfaces. This is a remote position, allowing you to work from anywhere while contributing to their fast-paced and innovative environment




Key Responsibilities:

Develop and maintain responsive and interactive user interfaces using React and TypeScript.
Collaborate with designers and back-end developers to create seamless and efficient web applications.
Optimize applications for maximum speed, scalability, and user experience.
Participate in code reviews and contribute to continuous improvement of the codebase.
Troubleshoot and debug issues to ensure the smooth operation of web applications.
Stay up-to-date with the latest industry trends and technologies to ensure our applications remain competitive and cutting-edge.
Contribute to the creation of technical documentation and guides for internal use.




Required Qualifications:

Proven experience as a Front-End Developer, with a strong portfolio of web applications.
Proficiency in React and TypeScript, with a deep understanding of their ecosystems.
Solid understanding of web technologies including HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript.
Experience with state management libraries (e.g., Redux) and front-end build tools.
Familiarity with RESTful APIs and integrating front-end applications with back-end services.
Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work effectively in a remote team environment.
