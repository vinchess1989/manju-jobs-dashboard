Who Are We Looking For?

Do you love social media, creative ideas, and telling stories that people actually want to hear?

Join Edunation as a Marketing Intern and help inspire students from around the world to discover Finland and Europe as study destinations.

You’ll be part of our international marketing team, working on real projects, creating content for a global audience, and getting hands-on experience in digital marketing. If you’re curious, creative, and ready to learn by doing, we’d love to meet you.

What You'll Do

You’ll get involved in different parts of our marketing work, including:


Create and assist with content for Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, and YouTube
Turn real student experiences and journeys into engaging stories for social media, campaigns, and blogs
Support marketing campaigns promoting Finland and Europe as top study destinations
Help create email newsletters, website content, and other marketing materials
Work closely with our international marketing team on campaigns and creative projects
Bring your own ideas to the table and help us find new ways to connect with our audience


What's In It for You?

This is a hands-on internship where you’ll have the opportunity to actually do marketing — not just watch from the sidelines.


1,500 € total allowance for the full 6-month internship, based on a 40-hour working week
Get real experience in social media, content creation, digital marketing, and communications
Work on real projects with real responsibility and real impact — you won't be fetching coffee
Learn from an international marketing team and get guidance along the way
Create content for an international audience and build your portfolio
Enjoy flexible working hours and a hybrid setup
Work from our office in Tampere and collaborate with an international team
Get a valuable first step into a career in marketing
