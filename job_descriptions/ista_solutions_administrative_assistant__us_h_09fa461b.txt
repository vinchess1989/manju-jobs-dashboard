ISTA Personnel Solutions South Africa is a dynamic and fast-growing BPO company based in South Africa, specializing in providing top-tier operational support to U.S.-based companies. We are not a recruitment agency - we are a dedicated extension of our clients' teams, delivering high-quality service with precision, efficiency, and a personal touch.

We are seeking a detail-oriented Administrative Assistant for our U.S.-based client, a healthcare technology company that helps skilled nursing and assisted living facilities coordinate smoother, connected care transitions, using automated alerts and real-time data to close care gaps and keep patients and care teams aligned throughout the discharge process. In this role, you will support the team with data entry, administrative tasks, and day-to-day operations as the business continues to grow.

PLEASE NOTE:


Working Hours: Monday to Friday, 8AM - 5PM EST (14:00 PM - 23:00 PM South African time - subject to daylight savings)
Internet Requirements: A fixed fibre line with a minimum speed of 25 Mbps (upload & download) and support for a wired Ethernet connection is mandatory
Power Backup: A reliable power backup solution is required to manage load shedding and power outages. Applicants without a power backup cannot be considered
Public Holidays: You will be required to work on all South African public holidays (compensated as per BCEA)
Work Environment: This is a fully remote role


Responsibilities:

This role is dynamic, and day-to-day tasks may evolve as our client's systems and processes continue to develop. Typical responsibilities include:


Perform daily data entry and maintain accurate records (approx. 2 hours per day, varying based on workload)
Assist with setting up facilities and backend systems ahead of launch
Draft emails, create documents, and follow up on outstanding items
Provide general administrative support, including electronic filing and maintaining organized records
Assist with compiling and capturing data for reports as needed
Support scheduling and calendar coordination
Communicate professionally with internal stakeholders and external contacts
Provide light user support as needed (no prior training or experience required)
Complete other ad hoc administrative tasks as required


Requirements


Previous experience in an administrative role preferred
Highly detail-oriented with strong time management skills
Excellent written and verbal English proficiency
Ability to write clean, well-structured customer emails in English, with excellent grammar and spelling
Strong attention to detail, time management and good judgement skills
Basic computer literacy, including MS Office and Outlook (no advanced Excel skills required)
Ability to work independently in a fast-paced, remote environment
Adaptable and receptive to constructive feedback


If you are not contacted within 14 working days, please consider your application unsuccessful.
