1. About Our Client:

Notchwood Landscape operates in the landscape design, installation, and maintenance industry, serving both residential and commercial clients. The company addresses the need for customised outdoor spaces that improve property aesthetics and usability. It focuses on quality and customer satisfaction to deliver tailored landscaping solutions.




2. About the Opportunity:

The Remote Administrative Assistant role provides comprehensive administrative support to ensure smooth and efficient operations. This position is critical for maintaining organisation within the team, enabling landscape professionals to concentrate on delivering high-quality results for clients.




3. Responsibilities:

• Act as the primary contact for client enquiries via phone and email.

• Manage schedules, calendars, and appointments for design and installation teams.

• Assist with financial administration, including invoicing, payment tracking, and expense processing.

• Maintain accurate and organised company records such as client information and project files.

• Coordinate with suppliers and vendors to order materials and track deliveries.

• Provide general administrative support including document and report preparation.

• Facilitate communication between the team, clients, and suppliers to support project coordination.




4. Requirements:

• Experience as an Administrative Assistant, Virtual Assistant, or comparable role.

• Strong organisational, planning, and time-management skills.

• Excellent written and verbal communication skills with a professional demeanour.

• Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite and high IT literacy.

• Ability to work independently with self-motivation and discipline.

• Attention to detail and commitment to quality.

• Experience in landscaping, construction, or related industries is preferred but not required.




5. Pay Range and Compensation Package:

• Competitive hourly wage of $25 - $35 USD.




6. Benefits & Perks:

• Fully remote working arrangement offering flexibility and convenience.

• Supportive, collaborative, and friendly team culture.
