Social Media Content Creator (Project-Based)




🧩 About METIC Global

Metic Global Oy is a Helsinki-based company working with businesses in Finland and internationally. We help companies grow, communicate effectively, and build a strong professional presence.

We are looking for a creative individual to help produce engaging social media content for both METIC Global or selected client companies.







💬 Your Role

We are looking for a Social Media Content Creator for an initial two-month project.

Your main responsibility will be creating and publishing engaging daily social media content using each company's existing visual identity, brand assets, and ready-made templates. Occasionally, you will also create new visuals that follow the company's branding guidelines or use your own creative ideas when suitable.

This is a project-based position with the possibility of continuing into a longer-term collaboration.







🎯 Key Responsibilities

Create and publish daily social media content for Metic Global and/or assigned client companies.
Use existing Canva templates, key visuals, and brand materials to produce high-quality content.
Create new visuals in Canva when needed while following company's brand guidelines.
Write engaging captions and adapt content for different social media platforms. (LinkedIn, Meta and X)
Help maintain a content calendar and ensure content is published consistently.
Suggest new content ideas and creative concepts.
Work together with the team to ensure content is accurate, professional, and aligned with each brand.







🧠 What We're Looking For

Good written English.
Comfortable using Canva to create social media content.
Interest in social media, content creation, branding, and visual communication.
Creative mindset with attention to detail.
Reliable, organised, and able to work independently.
Experience managing social media accounts.

Nice to have (not required):

Basic photography, video editing, or animation skills.
Experience using AI tools to assist with content creation.







🌟 What We Offer

A paid two-month project with a possibility of continuing into a longer-term role based on performance and future projects.
Freedom to contribute your own creative ideas alongside established brand guidelines.
A collaborative and international working environment.







📋 Project Details

Project Duration: 2 months

Start Date: 20th of July, 2026

Location: Helsinki

Employment Type: Project-based
