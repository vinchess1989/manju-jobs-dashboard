Company Description

Junior Startup Academy Finland is an educational initiative focused on inspiring and equipping young people with practical entrepreneurial skills. The academy organizes programs, workshops, and events that introduce participants to startup culture, innovation, and creative problem-solving. It collaborates with mentors, founders, and industry partners to provide hands-on learning experiences. The team values curiosity, collaboration, and initiative, and offers interns the opportunity to contribute to meaningful projects while learning about event operations in a dynamic environment.




Role Description

As an Event Coordinator Intern, you will support the planning and execution of startup and entrepreneurship-focused events and workshops. This full-time, on-site role is based in the Helsinki Metropolitan Area, where you will assist with scheduling, venue coordination, logistics, and preparation of event materials. Your day-to-day tasks will include managing registrations, communicating with participants and partners, and providing on-site support before, during, and after events. You will help maintain event documentation, track attendance and feedback, and contribute ideas to improve future events. The role offers exposure to startup networks and practical experience in event coordination and youth-focused programming.




Qualifications

Candidates should possess strong Event Planning and Event Management skills, including organizing timelines, logistics, and resources.
Candidates should possess clear and professional Communication skills for interacting with participants, partners, and team members (English and/or Finnish).
Candidates should possess reliable Customer Service skills, with a focus on creating welcoming, supportive event experiences.
Candidates should possess basic Sales skills, such as promoting events, engaging potential participants, and supporting partnership outreach.
Additional beneficial qualifications include organizational skills, attention to detail, and the ability to handle multiple tasks in a fast-paced environment.
Comfort working with common office and event tools (e.g., spreadsheets, presentation software, online registration platforms) is preferred.
Interest in startups, entrepreneurship, or youth education, and readiness to work on-site in the Helsinki Metropolitan Area.
