Sales Trainee




Location: Finland




At In 1 Percent, we help founders, CEOs and B2B companies grow through LinkedIn by combining personal branding, thought leadership and modern sales strategies.




We're looking for an ambitious Sales Trainee to join our growing team.




This role is designed for someone who wants to build a long-term career in sales. You'll learn directly from experienced entrepreneurs and sales professionals while gaining hands-on experience in modern B2B sales from day one.




Your responsibilities:




Build relationships with potential customers through LinkedIn, email and phone
Start conversations with founders, CEOs and other business decision-makers
Identify and qualify new business opportunities
Schedule meetings and support the sales process
Maintain CRM records and follow-up activities
Work closely with the team
Continuously develop your sales and communication skills through coaching and feedback




Depending on your strengths and interests, you'll also have opportunities to contribute to customer onboarding, sales development and other commercial initiatives.




We're looking for someone who:




Speaks fluent Finnish and has strong English communication skills
Is ambitious and motivated to build a successful career
Learns quickly and enjoys continuous development
Communicates confidently and enjoys connecting with new people
Has a positive attitude and strong work ethic
Is coachable and open to feedback
Takes ownership and enjoys working in a high-performance environment




Previous sales experience is an advantage but not essential. We're primarily looking for people with the right mindset, ambition and willingness to learn.




What we offer:




Comprehensive onboarding and continuous coaching
The opportunity to work directly with experienced entrepreneurs and leaders
A supportive, ambitious and fast-growing team
Plenty of responsibility and opportunities to grow as the company grows




Join our journey:

In less than a year, we've built a €500,000 online business and we're now scaling rapidly towards €1M+ in annual revenue. Our ambition is to build Finland's leading LinkedIn growth company before expanding globally.




You'll learn from some of the world's top LinkedIn creators, work alongside ambitious people and work directly with some of Finland's leading CEOs, founders and business leaders.

Welcome to the 1%.




During your onboarding, we hope you'll be able to work with us from our Oulu office. After that, the role can be performed remotely from anywhere in Finland.
