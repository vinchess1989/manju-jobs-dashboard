Are you the SNOW ACTIVITY PARK EMPLOYEE we are looking for?

Snowhotel Family is a family-owned business in Lehtojärvi, Rovaniemi, offering unique experiences in snow and ice. Arctic Snow Hotel & Glass Igloos with its restaurants guarantee a memorable overnight experience by the shores of Lehtojärvi, surrounded by the Northern Lights and nature. Located in Santa Claus Village, Snowman World invites the whole family to winter fun at a snowy playground made of snow and ice. In Santa Claus Village also locates charming gingerbread houses of Piparina and a cozy restaurant where customers can savor authentic Lapland flavors and relax in private gingerbread cottages throughout the day.

Snowman World is the most fun playground in Santa Claus Village, where the day is filled with exciting winter activities. The area features snow and ice slides, an ice rink, a snow maze, a snow restaurant, and an ice bar. The snow restaurant, ice bar, and café restaurant offer a wide variety of food, drinks, and café products daily.

We are now looking for snow activity park employees for the busy Christmas season at Snowman World. As an amusement park worker, your diverse tasks will begin with snow construction in November, and once the amusement park opens, your duties will include assisting customers in areas such as the slide and skating rink, monitoring safety, checking tickets, serving as a guide, performing mascot duties, and snow removal in outdoor areas.

Full-time work (120 hours/3 weeks) is available for the christmas season from the end of November until 6.1.2027. Part-time work is also possible according to your availability. We follow the collective agreement for the adventure services, and the salary is based on skills and experience. The employment relationship will be established between Bondata and the employee, with accommodation in Rovaniemi arranged if needed.

We offer you:

- A diverse job in the best workplace in Lapland

- A good orientation to your tasks

- Competitive salary

- Shared accommodation for a reasonable rent

We expect from you:

- Excellent customer service skills

- Proactive and responsible work attitude

- Proficiency in English, as our workplace and customers are international

- A confident attitude

Recruitment process

If you are interested in this position, please send us your CV and application as soon as possible! We will start interviews during the application period, and selections for hires will begin in August 2026.
