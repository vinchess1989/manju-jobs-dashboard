Job Summary

We are seeking a highly organized and proactive Executive Assistant to provide comprehensive administrative and operational support to senior executives in a fully remote environment. The ideal candidate will manage schedules, coordinate meetings, handle confidential information, and ensure the efficient execution of executive priorities while maintaining a high level of professionalism.




Key Responsibilities

Manage executive calendars, appointments, and meeting schedules
Coordinate virtual meetings, prepare agendas, and record meeting minutes
Handle email correspondence and prioritize communications on behalf of executives
Arrange domestic and international travel, including flights, accommodation, and itineraries
Prepare reports, presentations, spreadsheets, and other business documents
Maintain confidential records, files, and executive documentation
Track action items and follow up with internal and external stakeholders
Assist with project coordination and administrative support for strategic initiatives
Organize virtual events, team meetings, and executive briefings
Support day-to-day office administration and process improvements




Required Skills & Qualifications

Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Management, Communications, or a related field (preferred)
2–5 years of experience as an Executive Assistant, Personal Assistant, Administrative Assistant, or similar role
Excellent verbal and written communication skills
Strong organizational, time management, and multitasking abilities
Proficiency in Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook) and Google Workspace
Experience with scheduling and collaboration tools such as Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Slack, Calendly, or similar platforms
High level of discretion and ability to handle confidential information
Strong attention to detail and problem-solving skills
Ability to work independently in a remote environment




Preferred Qualifications

Experience supporting C-level executives or senior leadership teams
Familiarity with project management tools such as Asana, Trello, Monday.com, or Jira
Experience coordinating international travel and managing complex calendars
Knowledge of expense management and document management systems
Experience working in a remote or global team environment
