Agility Partners is seeking a qualified Content Curation Strategist to fill an open position with a Fortune 15 company based in the greater Seattle area. This is an exciting opportunity to influence content experiences used by millions of customers across productivity and AI-powered experiences. The ideal candidate will combine content strategy, digital merchandising, data analytics, and visual design judgment to optimize customer engagement and drive business outcomes. This role offers high visibility, cross-functional collaboration, and the opportunity to shape how users discover and interact with content across multiple product experiences.




Responsibilities

Curate, optimize, and manage large-scale digital content collections, ensuring customers receive relevant, engaging, and high-performing content experiences.

Analyze content performance metrics, customer engagement data, and experimentation results to identify trends and develop actionable recommendations.

Partner with content, design, product, and business teams to execute content strategies, evaluate experiments, and improve customer-facing experiences.

Manage content publishing workflows, metadata, lifecycle management, reporting, and CMS operations while maintaining high standards for quality and consistency.




Qualifications

5+ years of experience in content curation, content strategy, digital merchandising, or content lifecycle management.

5+ years of experience managing large-scale content collections using CMS platforms, publishing tools, metadata frameworks, and content governance processes.

3+ years of experience analyzing performance metrics, customer engagement data, dashboards, reporting tools, and business insights.

Bachelor's degree in Design, Communications, Data Analytics, Marketing, or a related field.

Strong understanding of visual design principles, content quality evaluation, customer experience optimization, and content performance measurement.

Experience creating data-driven recommendations that influence business decisions, content investments, and product strategy.

Excellent communication, storytelling, stakeholder management, and presentation skills.




Preferred Qualifications

Experience supporting digital content ecosystems, content marketplaces, productivity platforms, or AI-powered content experiences.

Experience with experimentation frameworks, A/B testing methodologies, and customer engagement analytics.

Familiarity with recommendation systems, personalization technologies, or content discovery platforms.

Experience partnering with design, research, product, and business stakeholders to drive customer-focused outcomes.

Passion for combining analytics, creativity, and customer insights to improve content experiences at scale.




This role offers a unique opportunity to shape content experiences across widely used productivity and AI-driven platforms. Candidates will influence content strategy through a combination of analytics, experimentation, design judgment, and customer insights while helping drive engagement, adoption, and long-term business impact across a global customer base.
