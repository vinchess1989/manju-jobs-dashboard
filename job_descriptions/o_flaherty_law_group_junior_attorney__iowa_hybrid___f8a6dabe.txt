Job Description

Remote

Cedar Rapids

$75,000.00 - $110,000.00 per year

We are seeking a Junior Attorney (0–3 years experience) to join our growing team handling criminal defense, family law, and civil litigation matters in Iowa.

This is a remote-first role with local court appearances as needed, and includes hands-on mentorship and training from experienced attorneys.

Why This Role Is Ideal for Early-Career Attorneys


Hands-on experience across multiple practice areas
Strong mentorship — not a sink-or-swim environment
Remote flexibility with real courtroom experience
Consistent case flow — focus on learning, not business development
Clear path for growth and increased responsibility


What You’ll Do


Assist with and gradually take ownership of cases in criminal, family, and civil matters
Attend court with guidance and support
Draft pleadings, communicate with clients, and build litigation skills
Grow into independent case management


We Are Seeking


Licensed in Iowa or awaiting bar results
Interest in litigation (criminal, family, or civil)
Strong work ethic and willingness to learn
Good communication and organizational skills
