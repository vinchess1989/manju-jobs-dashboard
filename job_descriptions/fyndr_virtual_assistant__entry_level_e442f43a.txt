We are seeking an exceptionally organized and proactive Virtual Assistant to join our team. This is an excellent opportunity for a fresh graduate or early-career professional who excels at anticipating needs, managing multiple priorities, and taking initiative. You will provide comprehensive administrative support while working remotely with a UK-based team during UK business hours (7:00 AM - 7:00 PM UK Time).




Key Responsibilities:

* Provide comprehensive administrative support across multiple projects and tasks

* Proactively identify potential issues and solve problems before they escalate

* Manage calendars, schedules, and coordinate meetings across time zones

* Handle email management and correspondence via Gmail

* Coordinate communication across teams using Slack

* Utilise AI tools (Claude) to optimise workflows and enhance productivity

* Organise and maintain digital files, documents, and systems

* Anticipate needs and prepare materials in advance

* Conduct research and compile information as needed

* Handle ad-hoc tasks with minimal supervision




Required Qualifications:

* Fresh graduate or 0-1 years of professional experience

* Exceptional organisational skills with meticulous attention to detail

* Proactive mindset - ability to think ahead and anticipate problems

* Strong multitasking abilities - comfortable juggling multiple priorities

* Excellent contextual awareness and ability to adapt quickly

* Proficiency in Slack for team communication

* Proficiency in Gmail for email management

* Experience using Claude AI or similar AI tools

* Available to work during UK business hours (7:00 AM - 7:00 PM UK Time)

* Reliable high-speed internet connection and quiet workspace




Ideal Candidate Traits:

* Detail-oriented perfectionist who takes pride in accuracy

* Self-starter who doesn't need constant direction

* Quick learner who can pick up new tools and processes rapidly

* Strong written and verbal communication skills in English

* Problem-solver who sees challenges as opportunities

* Calm under pressure with excellent time management

* Trustworthy and discreet with confidential information




Technical Requirements:

* Equipment: Computer/laptop with webcam and microphone

* Internet: Stable connection (minimum 10 Mbps)

* Software: Familiarity with cloud-based collaboration tools

* Learning: Comfortable adopting new software and platforms




What We Offer:

* Fully remote work arrangement with flexibility

* Opportunity to work with a UK-based team and clients

* Exposure to cutting-edge AI-powered productivity tools

* Professional development and skill-building opportunities

* Collaborative and supportive work environment




How to Apply:

Please submit your CV/resume along with a brief cover letter (200-300 words) addressing the following:

1. Why do you consider yourself exceptionally organised

2. An example of when you anticipated and prevented a problem

3. Your experience with Slack, Gmail, and Claude (or similar AI tools)

4. Your availability and workspace setup for UK business hours
