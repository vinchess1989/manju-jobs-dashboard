We are looking for a Data Product Owner to join our Data & Digital Enablement (DDE) team to focus on Sales and Operations Planning (S&OP) domain.

Greetings from your future manager 

At UPM, we believe data is one of the key drivers of better decisions, business performance, and future growth. In the Data & Digital Enablement (DDE) team, we work closely with our businesses to turn data into sustainable solutions that create value and support transformation across UPM.

"In DDE, we're building the data capabilities that support UPM's transformation and future growth. If you're excited about creating tangible business value, driving change, and developing yourself along the way, I'd be happy to welcome you to the team.” — Jukka Laitinen, Director, IT Data & Digital Enablement

What you will do 

Own and drive the data product vision, roadmap, and backlog for assigned domains
Translating business needs into well-defined, prioritized data product requirements
Ensure data products are designed according to data management and information architecture principles
Work closely with business stakeholders, data engineers, architects, and governance teams to deliver high-value data assets
Ensure alignment with enterprise data models, metadata standards, and architecture guidelines
Drive data quality, consistency, and lifecycle management across data products
Promote reuse, standardisation, and interoperability across domains
Contribute to UPM data governance and information management

In this role, you will design and drive the development of high-quality, reusable data products that support business operations, analytics, and AI use cases. You ensure that data is structured, governed, and delivered in a way that enables scalability, reusability and long-term value.

Who you are 

You have 5+ years of experience in Information Architecture and data modelling
You possess a strong understanding of data management principles, including data modelling, metadata, master data, and data quality
You have solid understanding of information architecture and enterprise data frameworks
You have experience working with data platforms, data engineering teams, and analytics solutions
Experience in Sales or Operations Planning domains is beneficial
You demonstrate strong communication skills and can translate business needs into data requirements
You are fluent in English, both spoken and written
