We’re Hiring!

Join the Willmar Area Community Foundation (WACF) and help strengthen our community through meaningful relationships and impactful philanthropy.

Who are we: WACF, a partner of CommunityGiving (CG), connects people and resources to make lasting change in the Willmar area.

We believe in preserving donor legacies, inspiring philanthropy across generations, and using financial resources to create lasting, positive change. Our values—community, integrity, commitment, innovation, optimism, diversity, and generosity—guide everything we do.

What You’ll Do: Provide exceptional service to donors and community partners, while coordinating communications, marketing, and community outreach efforts that promote the Foundation’s impact, strengthen stakeholder relationships, increase awareness of philanthropic opportunities, and maintain the Foundation’s community presence across various platforms.

What we need: Someone with strong communication, organizational, and relationship building skills who can be self-directed and practice the core mission and values of the Foundation with professionalism.

Time commitment to the organization of 32-40 hours per week. Preferred candidate will have a four year college degree, 2-4 years of experience in communications, marketing, administration, or a related field (nonprofit experience a plus).

What we offer: Health and dental insurance, 13 paid holidays, potential for 4 weeks accrued paid time off, generous retirement contribution and match, and a chance to make a difference. Pay range $22-$25 per hour.
